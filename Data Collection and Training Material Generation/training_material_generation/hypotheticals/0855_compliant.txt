Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Arbor Ridge Securities, a broker-dealer offering a proprietary mutual fund family called Arbor Ridge Core Funds, implemented a formal conflict mitigation program in January 2022 following SEC examination findings. Under the program, when a representative recommends any Arbor Ridge proprietary fund, the firm's supervisory system automatically generates a side-by-side comparison of the proprietary fund against at least two third-party funds with similar investment objectives, displays expense ratios and 3- and 5-year returns, and requires supervisor sign-off before the order is processed. Representative Lisa Fong recommended the Arbor Ridge Core Growth Fund (expense ratio 0.68%) to a 38-year-old client with a $95,000 taxable account and a 15-year time horizon, over two Vanguard alternatives (expense ratios 0.04% and 0.14%), documenting that the client had an existing relationship with Arbor Ridge, valued consolidated account reporting, and that the performance differential over the prior 5 years was less than 10 basis points annually. The supervisor reviewed and approved the recommendation with notes on file.

QUESTION
--------
Did Lisa Fong and Arbor Ridge Securities satisfy the Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation when recommending a proprietary fund despite lower-cost alternatives being available?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The SEC's Conflicts Staff Bulletin and the 2022 Reg BI compliance review findings distinguish between firms that merely disclose conflicts and those that have structural mitigation procedures that affirmatively address the conflict at the point of recommendation. Arbor Ridge's automated comparison tool, mandatory supervisor sign-off, and documented client-specific rationale demonstrate that the conflict was identified, managed, and mitigated rather than simply disclosed. The documented cost differential analysis and client preference rationale provide an auditable record showing the recommendation was placed in the client's best interest rather than driven by the firm's financial incentive.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iii) – Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation; SEC 2022 Staff Bulletin on Standards of Conduct for Broker-Dealers and Investment Advisers Regarding Conflicts of Interest

KEY FACTOR
----------
A structural conflict mitigation program with automated comparison tools, supervisor review, and documented client-specific rationale elevated this proprietary fund recommendation from a potential conflict violation to a compliant best-interest recommendation.
