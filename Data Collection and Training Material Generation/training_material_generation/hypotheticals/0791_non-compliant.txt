Source Folder: 15_FINRA_Regulatory_Notices
Topic: complex_products
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Ridgeline Securities, a mid-sized broker-dealer, allows its 85 registered representatives to recommend alternative mutual funds (Alt Funds) including the BlackRock Multi-Strategy Alternatives Fund and the JPMorgan Hedged Equity Fund. The firm's written supervisory procedures for Alt Funds were last updated in 2017 and reference only traditional mutual fund suitability criteria such as expense ratios and historical returns. The firm has no specific training requirement for representatives selling Alt Funds, no product-specific suitability questionnaire addressing strategies like managed futures or long/short equity, and no heightened review process for concentrated Alt Fund positions. Over the past 18 months, 23 representatives recommended Alt Fund allocations exceeding 40% of total portfolio value for 147 retail customers, many of whom had moderate risk tolerances. A FINRA examination identified that the firm's supervisory system did not differentiate Alt Funds from conventional mutual funds in any respect.

QUESTION
--------
Did Ridgeline Securities maintain a reasonably designed supervisory system for recommendations of alternative mutual funds as required by FINRA rules?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a supervisory system failure regarding alternative mutual fund sales practices.

FINRA Regulatory Notice 22-11 explicitly reminds firms of their obligation to establish and maintain a reasonably designed supervisory system for recommendations of alternative mutual funds, noting that FINRA has taken enforcement action against firms failing to do so. The notice identifies as deficiencies the precise failures present here: lack of Alt Fund-specific training, absence of product-specific suitability criteria addressing the unique strategies and risks of Alt Funds, and no heightened supervisory review for concentrated positions. A supervisory system that treats Alt Funds identically to conventional mutual funds is not reasonably designed given the materially different risk profiles.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 3110 (Supervision); FINRA Regulatory Notice 22-11 (Alternative Mutual Funds Sales Practice Obligations)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm failed to establish any Alt Fund-specific supervisory procedures, training, or heightened review processes, treating complex alternative mutual funds identically to conventional mutual funds in its compliance program.
