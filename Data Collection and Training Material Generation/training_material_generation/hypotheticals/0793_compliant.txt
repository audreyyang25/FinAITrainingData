FACTS
-----
Sarah Chen, age 52, is a high-net-worth client with $2.1 million in investable assets at Clearwater Advisors. Her investment profile indicates an aggressive risk tolerance, a 15-year time horizon to retirement, and experience investing in hedge funds and private equity through prior employer plans. Her representative, David Park, recommends allocating 20% of her portfolio ($420,000) across three alternative mutual funds: the PIMCO TRENDS Managed Futures Strategy Fund, the AQR Long-Short Equity Fund, and the Blackstone Alternative Multi-Strategy Fund. Before recommending these Alt Funds, Park completed the firm's mandatory Alt Fund training module, documented a product-specific suitability analysis noting each fund's strategy, liquidity profile, fee structure, and correlation benefits relative to Sarah's existing equity-heavy portfolio, and provided Sarah with the funds' prospectuses and a firm-prepared educational comparison sheet. Park's recommendations were reviewed and approved by Clearwater's designated Alt Fund principal reviewer before execution. Park receives the same compensation grid rate for Alt Funds as for conventional mutual funds, and Clearwater does not maintain any revenue-sharing agreements with the three fund families.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation of alternative mutual funds to an aggressive, experienced investor comply with FINRA sales practice obligations and Reg BI?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The representative completed firm-required Alt Fund training, conducted and documented a product-specific suitability analysis addressing strategies, risks, and portfolio fit, and provided the client with detailed disclosures — all effective practices identified in FINRA Regulatory Notice 22-11. The recommendation was subject to heightened principal review by a designated Alt Fund supervisor, satisfying FINRA Rule 3110. The 20% allocation is reasonable for an aggressive investor with a long time horizon and prior alternative investment experience. The absence of differential compensation and revenue-sharing arrangements mitigates conflict-of-interest concerns under Reg BI's Conflict of Interest Obligation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability); FINRA Rule 3110 (Supervision); FINRA Regulatory Notice 22-11 (Alt Fund Sales Practices); SEC Rule 15l-1 (Reg BI)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm implemented all effective practices identified in Regulatory Notice 22-11 — product-specific training, documented suitability analysis, heightened principal review, and full disclosure — for a client whose aggressive profile and experience supported the Alt Fund allocation.
