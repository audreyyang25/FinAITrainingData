Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Granite Peak Financial Advisors, a registered investment adviser, manages $85 million in client assets. During a routine SEC examination, staff discover that the firm has not updated its Form ADV Part 2A brochure in over three years. During that period, the firm added a new fee-based wrap program with a 1.75% annual fee, hired two new portfolio managers with disciplinary histories, and began receiving 12b-1 fees from a mutual fund family whose funds are recommended to clients. None of these material changes were disclosed to existing or prospective clients. The firm's CCO states they 'planned to update the brochure at the next annual amendment filing but kept missing the deadline.'

QUESTION
--------
Did Granite Peak Financial Advisors comply with its fiduciary duty and Form ADV disclosure obligations under the Investment Advisers Act?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of the Investment Advisers Act of 1940, Sections 206(1) and 206(2) (fiduciary duty / anti-fraud) and Rule 204-1 (Form ADV annual updating amendment requirements).

Under the Investment Advisers Act, registered investment advisers owe a fiduciary duty that includes the obligation to provide full and fair disclosure of all material facts, particularly those relating to conflicts of interest such as 12b-1 fee receipt. Rule 204-1 requires advisers to file an annual updating amendment to Form ADV within 90 days of the end of the fiscal year and to promptly update the brochure for material changes. Failing to disclose a new fee program, disciplinary histories of personnel, and 12b-1 fee conflicts for three years is a clear breach of both the updating requirement and the fiduciary duty of disclosure.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940, Sections 206(1), 206(2); SEC Rule 204-1 (Annual Updating Amendment to Form ADV)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser failed for three years to update its Form ADV to disclose material changes including new fee arrangements, personnel disciplinary histories, and 12b-1 fee conflicts, breaching both its regulatory filing obligations and fiduciary duty of full disclosure.
