Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A mid-sized broker-dealer, Hargrove Securities LLC, employs 45 registered representatives and has a written supervisory procedure (WSP) that nominally requires branch managers to review all trades for excessive activity. However, a GAO review of the firm's 2022–2023 records reveals that branch manager Kevin Plourde approved 312 trade confirmations over 18 months for a single account belonging to a 68-year-old retiree with $240,000 in assets, generating an annualized cost-to-equity ratio of 34% and a turnover rate of 9.2, without ever documenting a review or flagging the account for heightened scrutiny. The firm's WSPs had not been updated since 2019 and made no reference to Reg BI's Care Obligation cost-analysis requirements. GAO reports on Reg BI implementation have identified weak supervisory infrastructure at smaller and mid-sized broker-dealers as a systemic gap in investor protection.

QUESTION
--------
Did Hargrove Securities LLC's supervisory system satisfy Reg BI and FINRA Rule 3110 obligations with respect to the retired client's account?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a supervisory system violation under FINRA Rule 3110 and Reg BI's Compliance Obligation, constituting a failure to supervise excessive trading.

GAO reports on Reg BI implementation have specifically identified failure to update WSPs and inadequate review of high-turnover accounts as systemic supervisory deficiencies. A 34% annualized cost-to-equity ratio far exceeds thresholds that courts and FINRA have used to establish a presumption of churning, and a turnover rate of 9.2 independently satisfies quantitative churning benchmarks. The branch manager's approval of 312 transactions without documented review and the firm's failure to update WSPs to incorporate Reg BI cost-analysis standards constitute independent violations of both FINRA Rule 3110 and Reg BI's Compliance Obligation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 3110 – Supervision; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iv) – Reg BI Compliance Obligation; Dodd-Frank Act §913 (GAO implementation review authority)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm's failure to update its WSPs since 2019 to reflect Reg BI standards, combined with zero documented supervisory reviews of a chronically over-traded account, is the pivotal supervisory infrastructure failure highlighted in GAO Reg BI implementation findings.
