ID: 936
Source Folder: 21_FINRA_OHO_Decisions
Topic: undisclosed_outside_business_activity
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Registered representative Priya Nandakumar incorporated a private LLC, 'Nandakumar Capital Advisors,' and began providing fee-based investment advisory services to seven clients outside her member firm, collecting approximately $84,000 in fees over two years. Nandakumar did not disclose the outside business activity to her firm on Form U4 or through any internal notification process, and affirmatively stated 'none' on the firm's annual compliance questionnaire when asked about outside business activities. Two of the seven advisory clients were also brokerage customers of her member firm, and she directed them to purchase specific securities through her firm account while simultaneously collecting advisory fees from them through the LLC. Her firm had no knowledge of the arrangement until a client complaint triggered a regulatory inquiry.

QUESTION
--------
Did Nandakumar's operation of an undisclosed outside investment advisory business while serving firm customers comply with FINRA's outside business activity and conduct standards?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of FINRA Rule 3270 (outside business activities), FINRA Rule 2010, and potentially constitutes selling away.

Rule 3270 requires registered persons to provide prior written notice to their member firm before engaging in any outside business activity, and affirmatively misrepresenting the absence of such activity on a compliance questionnaire constitutes an aggravated violation of Rule 2010's commercial honor standards. The overlap between her brokerage clients and private advisory clients creates an undisclosed conflict of interest and raises parallel concerns about receipt of undisclosed compensation in connection with securities transactions. The books and records implications under Rule 4511 are also triggered because the firm's customer records did not reflect the dual compensation arrangement.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 3270 (Outside Business Activities of Registered Persons); FINRA Rule 2010 (Standards of Commercial Honor); FINRA Rule 4511 (Books and Records)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Affirmatively concealing an outside advisory business generating $84,000 in fees, particularly where firm clients overlapped with private advisory clients, elevates this beyond a technical notice failure to a serious Rule 2010 violation.
