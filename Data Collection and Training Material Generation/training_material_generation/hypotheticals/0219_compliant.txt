FACTS
-----
Advisor N manages $3.2M in AUM for a 58-year-old semi-sophisticated client (MBA, prior stock analyst experience, net worth $4.8M). The client requested exposure to interval funds as part of a diversification strategy; standard liquid alternatives were insufficient for the client's specific goal (illiquidity tolerance for enhanced yield in a rising-rate environment). Advisor N recommended a BlackRock Interval Fund (BIF) at 1.25% ER, 1-year liquidity window, 8.2% average annual distribution yield, with documented quarterly redemption caps at 5%. The advisor created a 12-page suitability memo analyzing: (1) the client's stated risk/return objectives; (2) liquidity needs across all accounts (60% in liquid assets, 15% in interval fund, 25% in real estate); (3) fee comparison to closed-end funds (1.9% ER) and illiquid hedge funds (2% + 20%); (4) three-year redemption stress-test scenarios; (5) written acknowledgment of redemption risk. The advisor disclosed all material conflicts, including Platform revenue (15 bps annually) and distribution fees. The file was reviewed and approved by the advisor's Chief Compliance Officer.

QUESTION
--------
Did Advisor N comply with Reg BI's care obligation for recommending an interval fund to a semi-sophisticated retail client?

ANSWER
------
Yes. Advisor N satisfied all three prongs of the care obligation: (1) Reasonable Basis: comprehensive documented analysis of client's needs, liquidity profile, and product characteristics; (2) Suitability: the 15% allocation fits a sophisticated investor's diversification goals and is supported by stress testing; (3) Disclosure: material terms (redemption caps, liquidity windows, fees vs. alternatives, conflicts) were clearly documented and acknowledged. The semi-sophisticated client profile and explicit written acknowledgment are fact-intensive mitigators under Reg BI and SEC guidance on complex products. CCO approval adds institutional rigor. This resembles SEC no-action guidance on interval fund sales (IA Bulletin 2008-01) when accompanied by tailored suitability analysis.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Reg BI Care Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i)–(iii); SEC IA Bulletin 2008-01 (Complex Products); Institutional-grade suitability documentation

KEY FACTOR
----------
Comprehensive, contemporaneous documentation of reasonable basis, client-specific suitability analysis, and transparent disclosure of material terms and conflicts.
