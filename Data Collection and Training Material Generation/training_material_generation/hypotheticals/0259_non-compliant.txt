Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
DualServe Holdings is both a FINRA-registered broker-dealer and a SEC-registered investment adviser (dual registrant). On its Form CRS, Item 3, the firm stated: 'We provide investment advice and execute securities transactions. We may act as a broker or an adviser depending on the engagement.' The language did not explicitly explain that in broker-dealer capacity, DualServe is NOT subject to a fiduciary duty, but in investment adviser capacity, it IS subject to a fiduciary duty under the Investment Adviser Act of 1940. For retirement accounts (IRAs), the firm provided advice recommending Class A mutual funds with 5.75% front-end loads, generating $8,700 in commissions on a $151,000 IRA rollover, while simultaneously charging 1.00% AUM advisory fees. The client, Robert Hassan (age 62), was not clearly informed that the broker-dealer recommendation was not subject to a fiduciary duty analysis and was structured for commission generation, not retirement income optimization.

QUESTION
--------
Did DualServe's Form CRS adequately disclose the difference between broker-dealer and investment adviser capacities in compliance with Reg BI?

ANSWER
------
No. SEC staff guidance (IA Exam Priorities 2022–2023) and Form CRS Instructions (Items 2–3) require dual registrants to clearly explain the material differences in duties and economic incentives between broker-dealer and adviser capacities. Vague language like 'depending on the engagement' fails to communicate that broker-dealer recommendations are subject only to suitability (FINRA Rule 2111), not fiduciary duty. Failing to disclose this distinction is a Form CRS deficiency because retail clients cannot understand how recommendations differ or recognize the conflict inherent in recommending high-load products in a retirement account. This mirrors SEC enforcement patterns against dual registrants (e.g., Morgan Stanley, 2016).

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Reg BI Disclosure Obligation, 17 CFR § 240.15l-1(g)(1); Form CRS Instructions Item 2 and Item 3; SEC staff guidance on dual registrant disclosure

KEY FACTOR
----------
Dual registrants must clearly distinguish fiduciary duty in adviser capacity from suitability standard in broker-dealer capacity; generic language is insufficient.
