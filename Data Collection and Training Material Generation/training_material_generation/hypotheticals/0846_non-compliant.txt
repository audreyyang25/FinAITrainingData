ID: 846
Source Folder: 17_SEC_Staff_Bulletins
Topic: disclosure_requirements
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Maria Chen, a 67-year-old retired schoolteacher with a $280,000 IRA, contacts her broker-dealer representative at Keystone Financial Group in August 2021 to open a new brokerage account and roll over her IRA funds into a variable annuity product. The registered representative completes the transaction and recommends a portfolio of mutual funds within the annuity wrapper without delivering a Form CRS to Maria prior to or at the time of the recommendation. The rep later explains he believed Form CRS only needed to be delivered once per firm relationship and that Maria had received one two years prior when she opened a prior account at a different broker-dealer affiliated with the same holding company. No documentation of prior Form CRS delivery exists in Maria's file.

QUESTION
--------
Did the registered representative satisfy Reg BI's disclosure obligations when recommending the IRA rollover and annuity product without delivering a new Form CRS?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of Reg BI's Disclosure Obligation and Form CRS delivery requirements.

Reg BI requires broker-dealers to deliver Form CRS to retail customers before or at the time of making a recommendation, including recommendations involving account types such as IRA rollovers. The FAQ guidance excerpted from Cambridge Investment Research expressly identifies QRP or IRA rollover/transfer as a triggering event requiring Form CRS delivery if not previously received. Because no documented prior delivery existed and the prior account was at a separate entity, the representative could not rely on an assumed prior delivery, and the omission constitutes a clear Disclosure Obligation failure.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation; Form CRS Delivery Requirements (17 CFR § 240.17a-14)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Form CRS was not delivered prior to or at the time of the IRA rollover recommendation, a specifically enumerated triggering event under Reg BI guidance.
