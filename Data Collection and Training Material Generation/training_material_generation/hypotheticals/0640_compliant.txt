Source Folder: 08_State_Fiduciary_Rules
Topic: state_fiduciary_bd
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
An investment adviser representative in a state that has adopted the NASAA Model Rule 102(a)(4)-1 recommends that her client, James Whitfield, age 45, a high-income software executive earning $350,000 annually, invest $50,000 into a private REIT that the adviser's firm also recommends to other clients. The adviser fully discloses in writing that her firm receives a 2% placement fee from the REIT sponsor, provides James with the private placement memorandum detailing all risks and fees, documents that James meets accredited investor qualifications, and obtains James's written acknowledgment of the conflict. The investment represents less than 5% of James's $1.1 million portfolio, and the adviser documents how the REIT's income-generating profile aligns with James's stated goal of diversifying into real assets for long-term income.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation comply with the NASAA Model Rule's fiduciary standard given the adviser's firm receives a placement fee?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Under NASAA Model Rule 102(a)(4)-1, investment advisers are fiduciaries who must act primarily for the benefit of clients. However, receiving compensation from a product sponsor is not per se prohibited if the conflict is fully disclosed in writing and the client provides informed consent. Here, the adviser disclosed the placement fee, obtained written acknowledgment, documented the client's suitability and accredited investor status, and ensured the allocation was proportionate to the client's overall portfolio and investment objectives. The fiduciary duty was satisfied through transparency, documentation, and alignment with client goals.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NASAA Model Rule 102(a)(4)-1 – Unethical Business Practices of Investment Advisers, Investment Adviser Representatives, and Federal Covered Advisers

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser fully disclosed the compensation conflict in writing, obtained informed client consent, and documented the suitability rationale, satisfying the NASAA fiduciary standard despite receiving a placement fee.
