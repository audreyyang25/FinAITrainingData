ID: 571
Source Folder: 05_SEC_Exam_Findings
Topic: share_class_selection
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A dually-registered investment adviser and broker-dealer recommends Class A mutual fund shares with a 5.75% front-end sales load to 145 retirement plan rollover clients during 2023-2024, generating approximately $2.1 million in upfront commissions. Lower-cost institutional share classes and clean shares of the same funds were available on the firm's platform with no load and expense ratios averaging 45 basis points less per year. The firm's Form ADV Part 2A disclosed that 'the firm may receive compensation from mutual fund companies,' but did not specifically disclose the conflict arising from the financial incentive to recommend higher-cost share classes. No individualized analysis was documented comparing share class costs for any of the 145 clients.

QUESTION
--------
Did the firm comply with its fiduciary duty and Reg BI's Care Obligation by recommending Class A shares without documenting a share-class comparison or adequately disclosing the compensation conflict?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of both the Investment Advisers Act fiduciary duty and SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation, as well as the Disclosure Obligation.

The SEC Division of Examinations has repeatedly identified share class selection as a priority exam area, citing widespread failures by advisers to recommend the lowest-cost share class when identical fund exposure is available. The Care Obligation under Reg BI requires a reasonable basis to believe the recommendation is in the client's best interest, which includes evaluating reasonably available alternatives such as lower-cost share classes. The firm's generic disclosure about receiving compensation from fund companies is insufficient under the Disclosure Obligation, which requires disclosure of material facts about the specific conflict of interest. The complete absence of documented cost comparisons across 145 clients indicates a systemic compliance failure.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940 §206(1)&(2) fiduciary duty; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation; SEC Share Class Selection Disclosure Initiative (2018)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm systematically recommended higher-cost share classes without documenting any individualized cost comparison or adequately disclosing the specific compensation conflict driving the recommendations.
