Source Folder: 22_PIABA_Materials
Topic: expungement_without_state_regulator_notice
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Broker-dealer representative Sofia Carrano sought expungement of a customer complaint from her CRD record through a FINRA arbitration panel. The arbitration panel, after a one-hour telephonic hearing, recommended expungement based on a finding that the claim was clearly erroneous because the customer's account statements showed the product performed as described. Neither the FINRA arbitration panel nor Carrano's counsel provided notice of the expungement proceeding to the state securities regulator in the state where the underlying customer complaint originated, and the regulator had no opportunity to object or present evidence regarding the public interest in retaining the disclosure. The award was subsequently confirmed by a federal district court without any sua sponte inquiry into the notification gap.

QUESTION
--------
Was the expungement proceeding conducted in compliance with FINRA's requirements for state regulator participation?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of FINRA Rule 2080(b)(1)(C) and associated FINRA guidance requiring notice to state regulators in expungement proceedings.

Under FINRA's expungement framework, particularly as strengthened by rules effective June 2023, state securities regulators must be notified of expungement proceedings so they may participate and present the public interest perspective on whether disputed customer complaint information should be removed from the CRD. PIABA has extensively advocated for robust state regulator participation as a structural safeguard against inappropriately low expungement standards, noting that regulators hold investigative information about brokers that arbitrators may not otherwise consider. The failure to provide notice to the relevant state regulator deprived the proceeding of a critical investor protection check and renders the expungement procedurally defective.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2080(b)(1)(C); FINRA Regulatory Notice 17-42; FINRA Dispute Resolution Services Expungement Reform Rules (effective June 15, 2023)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Omitting mandatory state securities regulator notice from the expungement proceeding violated FINRA's procedural requirements designed to protect the integrity of the CRD public disclosure system.
