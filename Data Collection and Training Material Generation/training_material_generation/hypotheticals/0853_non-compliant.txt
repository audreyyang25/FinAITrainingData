Source Folder: 17_SEC_Staff_Bulletins
Topic: care_obligation_detail
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Representative James Hollenbeck at Pinnacle Securities recommended that his 71-year-old client, a widowed retiree named Eleanor Vasquez with $415,000 in a rollover IRA and a self-described 'conservative' risk tolerance, purchase a variable annuity with a 7-year surrender charge period and a 1.85% mortality and expense fee. Hollenbeck completed a Best Interest Analysis form noting that the annuity's guaranteed minimum income benefit addressed the client's stated concern about outliving her savings, but he did not document consideration of any alternative products — including lower-cost immediate annuities or bond ladders — that might have served the same stated objective. The variable annuity generated a 6.5% commission to Hollenbeck on the $415,000 investment, totaling $26,975. The firm's compliance review flagged the recommendation but took no follow-up action.

QUESTION
--------
Did James Hollenbeck satisfy the Care Obligation under Reg BI when recommending the variable annuity to Eleanor Vasquez?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of the Reg BI Care Obligation.

The SEC's 2022 Reg BI compliance alert emphasized that firms were failing to document genuine consideration of reasonably available alternatives and were over-relying on boilerplate Best Interest Analysis forms without tailoring analysis to the specific client. For a 71-year-old conservative investor, a 7-year surrender period is presumptively unsuitable absent documented justification, and the 1.85% M&E fee imposes a material cost drag that must be weighed against alternatives. The high commission — $26,975 — further signals a conflict that demands heightened scrutiny and explicit documentation, neither of which was present here.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Staff Bulletin on Reg BI Care Obligation (2022)

KEY FACTOR
----------
No documentation of consideration of reasonably available lower-cost alternatives to satisfy the same investment objective for a conservative elderly client.
