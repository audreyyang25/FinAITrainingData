ID: 557
Source Folder: 04_RegBI_Compliance_Guidance
Topic: reg_bi_disclosure
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Regulus-West Financial Group, LLC, a FINRA member firm headquartered in Michigan with 70 active branches and 120 registered representatives, primarily services retail investors. From June 2021 through early 2024, the firm's Form CRS and Reg BI disclosure documents failed to disclose that the firm's control affiliate, a registered investment adviser sharing common ownership, had been the subject of multiple regulatory actions, including a state consent order and a FINRA censure. The firm also did not disclose in its Form CRS that its registered representatives received higher compensation for selling proprietary advisory products managed by the control affiliate. Approximately 2,400 retail customers received the deficient Form CRS during this period, and the firm made no corrective filing or amended delivery until confronted by FINRA examiners during a cycle examination.

QUESTION
--------
Did Regulus-West satisfy its Reg BI Disclosure Obligation and Form CRS requirements by omitting its control affiliate's disciplinary history and material compensation conflicts from its customer-facing disclosures?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation and Form CRS requirements under Rule 17a-14.

The Reg BI Disclosure Obligation requires full and fair disclosure, prior to or at the time of a recommendation, of all material facts relating to conflicts of interest associated with the recommendation. Form CRS specifically requires disclosure of the firm's disciplinary history, including that of control affiliates, and must describe material conflicts such as differential compensation arrangements that could incentivize representatives to favor certain products. The failure to disclose the control affiliate's disciplinary history and the higher compensation for proprietary products over a multi-year period mirrors the Regulus Financial Group AWC (No. 2023077077001), where FINRA found violations for failing to disclose the firm's and its control affiliate's disciplinary history in required disclosures.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation; SEC Form CRS (Rule 17a-14); FINRA Rule 2267

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm systematically omitted its control affiliate's disciplinary history and material compensation conflicts from Form CRS and Reg BI disclosures delivered to approximately 2,400 retail customers over nearly three years.
