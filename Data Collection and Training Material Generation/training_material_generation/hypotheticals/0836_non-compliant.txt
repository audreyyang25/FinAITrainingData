Source Folder: 17_SEC_Staff_Bulletins
Topic: disclosure_requirements
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A broker-dealer registered representative at a regional firm opens a new brokerage account in August 2021 for a 58-year-old retail customer, Margaret Hollis, who is rolling over a $215,000 401(k) from a former employer into an IRA. The representative recommends a portfolio of commissioned mutual funds without delivering Form CRS prior to or at the time of the account opening. The representative later claims Form CRS was 'available on the firm's website' and that he verbally described the firm's services during the meeting. No written or electronic delivery confirmation of Form CRS exists in the client file, and the Best Interest Analysis form was not completed in connection with the rollover recommendation.

QUESTION
--------
Did the representative satisfy Reg BI's disclosure and Form CRS delivery obligations in connection with opening Ms. Hollis's IRA and recommending the mutual fund portfolio?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of Reg BI's Disclosure Obligation and Form CRS delivery requirements.

Reg BI's Disclosure Obligation requires broker-dealers to deliver Form CRS to retail customers before or at the time of making a recommendation or opening a new account. Website availability does not substitute for affirmative delivery, and verbal descriptions do not satisfy the written disclosure requirement. The SEC's staff guidance and Cambridge's FAQ documentation confirm that QRP or IRA rollovers independently trigger Form CRS delivery, and a Best Interest Analysis must be documented for new accounts receiving investment recommendations. The absence of any delivery confirmation renders the representative non-compliant on multiple independent grounds.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation; Form CRS Delivery Requirement (Rule 17a-14)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Form CRS was not affirmatively delivered prior to or at account opening and the rollover recommendation, as required by Reg BI's Disclosure Obligation.
