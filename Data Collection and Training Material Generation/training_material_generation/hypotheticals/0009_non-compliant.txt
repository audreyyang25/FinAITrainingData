FACTS
-----
A 52-year-old client with $320,000 in investable assets, moderate risk tolerance, and a 15-year time horizon to retirement is recommended a variable annuity (VA) with: (1) 1.35% annual MER (mortality & expense risk charge); (2) 0.75% sub-account expense ratio; (3) 3% contingent deferred sales charge (CDSC) if surrendered before year 7; and (4) guaranteed income rider adding 0.45% annually. Total annual cost: 2.55%. The advisor documents that the VA provides 'lifetime income guarantee' and complies with FINRA Rule 2330 suitability requirements (questionnaire, principal approval). However, the advisor fails to document: (1) analysis of whether the guaranteed income rider is necessary given the client's pension and Social Security, or (2) comparison to a diversified portfolio of mutual funds (ER ~0.25%) + a simple deferred income annuity (cost ~1.0%) as an alternative.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation satisfy the Reg BI care obligation, even though it complies with FINRA Rule 2330?

ANSWER
------
No, this violates the care obligation under Reg BI §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i), despite technical compliance with FINRA Rule 2330.

While FINRA Rule 2330 suitability for VAs focuses on the customer's age, financial situation, needs, and risk tolerance, Reg BI's care obligation is narrower but more stringent: it requires active, documented consideration of costs and alternatives at the time of recommendation. The VA's 2.55% annual cost is 10x the cost of a portfolio alternative (mutual funds + deferred income annuity = ~1.25% total). For a client with a 15-year time horizon who already has pension/Social Security income, the advisor did not document why the high cost and surrender charges (illiquidity risk) were justified. The FINRA Rule 2330 questionnaire is a suitability screen, not a care analysis. Reg BI requires affirmative documentation that the specific product (with its specific costs) was chosen over available lower-cost alternatives. The advisor's failure to analyze the rider's necessity or compare to lower-cost alternatives breaches the care obligation, even if the product is 'suitable' under FINRA rules.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Regulation Best Interest §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i) — care obligation (higher standard than FINRA suitability) requires consideration of costs, risks, rewards, and alternatives; Reg BI is the controlling standard for broker-dealer recommendations to retail customers after June 30, 2020.

KEY FACTOR
----------
2.55% total annual cost with no documented analysis of whether the product's features (guaranteed income rider, lifetime income guarantee) justified a cost differential of 1.3+ percentage points annually versus reasonable alternatives.
