Topic: ia_fiduciary_duty
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
An RIA with $150M AUM is approached by a prospective client, age 58, with $800K in liquid assets and a conservative risk tolerance. The RIA recommends a portfolio of 80% investment-grade bond funds (including a small holding in a no-load bond fund with 0.15% ER managed by a competitor) and 20% dividend-paying equity ETFs. The RIA's AUM fee is 0.70%, flat across all account sizes. The RIA provides a detailed written investment policy statement, explains that the recommended bond fund manager is not affiliated with the RIA, discloses the fee structure, and explains why bonds are appropriate for the client's age and risk profile. The client accepts the recommendation and signs the IPS.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation satisfy the fiduciary duty of care and loyalty?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this is compliant with the fiduciary duty of care and loyalty.

The RIA satisfies the duty of care by recommending a portfolio that matches the client's age (58), risk tolerance (conservative), and asset level, with a recognized analytical basis (80/20 allocation is conservative and widely accepted for pre-retirees). The RIA satisfies the duty of loyalty by: (1) recommending a competitor's bond fund (no-load, low expense ratio) when that fund is objectively superior to any affiliated alternative, demonstrating disinterest; (2) disclosing its flat fee structure upfront; (3) providing a detailed, written IPS showing the analytical foundation for the recommendation; and (4) obtaining the client's informed consent. The recommendation of a non-affiliated fund is particularly strong evidence of compliance because it shows the RIA is not favoring proprietary products. The UPIA 'prudent investor' standard (adopted in all 50 states and incorporated into adviser law) requires diversification, cost-consciousness, and alignment with client circumstances—all present here. The written documentation and client acknowledgment further evidences compliance.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Adviser Act Section 206; Uniform Prudent Investor Act (UPIA) standard of care; SEC v. Capital Gains; SEC Form ADV disclosure requirements

KEY FACTOR
----------
Recommendation of non-affiliated, lower-cost alternative product combined with transparent fee disclosure and written investment policy documentation.
