Source Folder: 23_International_Comparative
Topic: singapore_faa_balanced_product_presentation
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A Singapore-licensed financial adviser representative meets with a 45-year-old professional client seeking investment-linked insurance policies. Before making any recommendation, the representative conducts a comprehensive fact-find covering the client's income, liabilities, dependants, existing coverage, investment experience, and risk appetite, documenting responses in the firm's CRM system. The representative presents three product options with a balanced explanation of features, fees, surrender charges, and risks associated with each, and provides the client with product highlights sheets and the policy illustration. The recommendation letter explicitly links the recommended product to the client's documented objectives and explains why the alternatives were not preferred.

QUESTION
--------
Does the adviser's process of conducting a thorough fact-find, presenting multiple balanced options, and documenting the rationale for the recommendation comply with the Singapore Financial Advisers Act?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Section 27 of the FAA requires financial advisers to have a reasonable basis for recommendations, which necessitates collecting sufficient client information and ensuring the recommended product is suitable having regard to that information. The adviser's comprehensive fact-find, balanced multi-product presentation, and documented rationale directly satisfy the reasonable basis requirement and the balanced presentation obligations under FAA-N16. The provision of product highlights sheets and policy illustrations further demonstrates compliance with MAS disclosure expectations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Singapore Financial Advisers Act (FAA) §27; MAS Notice FAA-N16 (Recommendations on Investment Products); MAS Guidelines FAA-G11

KEY FACTOR
----------
Thorough needs analysis, documented suitability rationale, and balanced product disclosure collectively satisfy Singapore FAA's reasonable basis for recommendation requirement.
