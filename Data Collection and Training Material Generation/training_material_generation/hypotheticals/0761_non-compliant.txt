ID: 761
Source Folder: 13_FINRA_Rulebook
Topic: variable_annuity_rule
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A registered representative at a mid-sized broker-dealer recommends that Margaret Chen, a 78-year-old retired schoolteacher with $180,000 in liquid assets and a conservative risk tolerance, surrender her existing deferred variable annuity — which has a remaining surrender period of 3 years and a $9,400 surrender charge — and purchase a new deferred variable annuity with a 7-year surrender period. The new annuity has slightly different subaccount options but materially similar features, including an identical living benefit rider. The representative documents the transaction as 'client requested' but does not document any specific analysis of why the exchange would be beneficial compared to keeping the existing contract. The firm's principal approves the exchange the same day without requesting any comparative analysis.

QUESTION
--------
Did the registered representative and the firm comply with FINRA Rule 2330 when recommending this deferred variable annuity exchange?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates FINRA Rule 2330 (Deferred Variable Annuities).

FINRA Rule 2330(b) requires that, before recommending a deferred variable annuity exchange, the representative must have a reasonable basis to believe the transaction is suitable. Specifically, Rule 2330(b)(1)(A) mandates consideration of whether the customer would incur a surrender charge, whether the customer would be subject to a new surrender period, and whether the exchange provides a materially different benefit. Here, Margaret incurs a $9,400 surrender charge and begins a new 7-year surrender period for a product with materially similar features, and no documented comparative analysis supports the exchange. Additionally, the principal's same-day approval without requesting such analysis violates Rule 2330(c)'s requirement for a registered principal to review the exchange with reasonable diligence before approving it.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2330(b) – Suitability and exchange obligations for deferred variable annuities

KEY FACTOR
----------
The exchange subjected a 78-year-old conservative client to a new surrender charge and longer surrender period for a materially similar product with no documented benefit, and the principal failed to conduct meaningful review.
