FACTS
-----
Summit Private Fund Advisors, an SEC-registered adviser, manages a $600 million private credit fund. The fund's GP and adviser are affiliates. The adviser proposes to charge $1.8 million in compliance infrastructure costs back to the fund as a fund-level expense. Before doing so, the adviser engages an independent audit firm to conduct a fairness review of the allocation methodology. The fund's LPA explicitly permits charging compliance and regulatory expenses to the fund, and the annual audited financial statements distributed to all LPs itemize the expense. The adviser also provides a quarterly detailed expense report to the fund's LPAC, which reviews and does not object. A new investor conducting due diligence receives a copy of the current audited financials, the LPA provisions, and the expense allocation policy before committing $25 million to the fund.

QUESTION
--------
Is the adviser's practice of allocating $1.8 million in compliance infrastructure costs to the private fund compliant with the SEC's Private Fund Adviser Rules and fiduciary obligations?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The SEC's Private Fund Adviser Rules require transparency regarding fees and expenses charged to private funds, including through quarterly statements and annual audits. Here, the expense is authorized by the LPA, independently reviewed for fairness, itemized in audited financial statements, and reported quarterly to the LPAC. The adviser's fiduciary duty under Section 206 requires full and fair disclosure of fees and potential conflicts—obligations that are satisfied by the multi-layered disclosure and governance process. New investors are provided with all relevant documentation prior to their commitment, satisfying the Preferential Treatment Rule's transparency requirements and the adviser's duty of care regarding prospective investor disclosure.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Private Fund Adviser Rules – Rule 211(h)(2)-1 (Quarterly Statement Rule); Investment Advisers Act Section 206 – Fiduciary Duty; SEC Fiduciary Interpretation (2019)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The combination of LPA authorization, independent fairness review, audited itemized disclosure, LPAC oversight, and pre-commitment disclosure to new investors demonstrates compliance with both the Private Fund Adviser Rules and the adviser's fiduciary obligations.
