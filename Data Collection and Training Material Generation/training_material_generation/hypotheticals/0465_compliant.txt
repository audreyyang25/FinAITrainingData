Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Advisor Kim advised 67-year-old Robert Brennan, a former pharmaceutical executive holding 28,000 shares (59% of his $620,000 portfolio) of his former employer's stock, to maintain the concentrated position despite significant downside risk. Brennan's cost basis was $3.20/share; current market price was $68/share. Kim documented in writing: (1) Brennan's explicit instruction that he wished to 'hold for tax reasons' and pursue a gradual 10-year tax-loss harvesting plan; (2) a tax analysis showing that immediate diversification would trigger $1.292M in long-term capital gains tax (~33% rate = ~$427K liability); (3) Brennan's substantial net worth of $4.2M (the stock concentration represented 14.8% of his total liquid net worth) and his ability to absorb a 30% stock decline (~$572K loss); (4) a written alternative diversification schedule at different price points to manage risk while preserving tax-efficient realization. Brennan signed a document acknowledging the concentration risk and confirming his tax-driven preference.

QUESTION
--------
Did Advisor Kim comply with the fiduciary duty of care and loyalty in maintaining a 59% concentrated position in a single stock despite apparent suitability concerns?

ANSWER
------
Yes. Compliant. The fiduciary duty of care was satisfied because: (1) Kim documented a specific client-directed reason (tax minimization) for the concentrated position; (2) Kim analyzed the client's financial capacity to absorb the risk (net worth of $4.2M and only 14.8% total exposure); (3) Kim provided a gradual diversification plan to manage risk over 10 years while achieving tax efficiency; (4) the concentration was a client-directed decision, not an adviser-imposed or fee-driven choice; (5) the tax savings ($427K) substantially outweighed the concentration risk for this specific client. The written acknowledgment of concentration risk by the client demonstrates informed consent. This pattern is consistent with compliant concentrated position cases where tax considerations and documented client direction justify departures from standard diversification guidance.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Adviser Act §206 (fiduciary duty of loyalty and care); Reg BI Conflict of Interest, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(e)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Client-directed tax strategy + documented analysis of tax liability ($427K) + financial capacity (net worth $4.2M, 14.8% total exposure) + signed acknowledgment = compliant concentration.
