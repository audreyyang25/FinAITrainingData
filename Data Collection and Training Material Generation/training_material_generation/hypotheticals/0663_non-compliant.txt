Source Folder: 09_Law_Firm_Memos
Topic: regulatory_change_impact
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A municipal securities broker-dealer registered with the MSRB recommends that a retired 72-year-old schoolteacher with $180,000 in savings and a conservative risk profile invest $120,000 in long-duration, unrated revenue bonds issued by a small hospital district, yielding 5.8%. The representative documents only that the client 'wants income' and notes the higher yield compared to Treasury securities. The firm's supervisory procedures reference the old MSRB Rule G-19 suitability framework and have not been updated to reflect the MSRB's proposed alignment with Regulation Best Interest. The representative does not evaluate reasonably available alternatives such as highly rated general obligation municipal bonds or diversified municipal bond funds, nor does the representative document any cost or risk comparison.

QUESTION
--------
Did the broker-dealer satisfy its obligations under Regulation Best Interest and aligned MSRB rules when recommending the unrated hospital revenue bonds to this retail investor?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of the Reg BI Care Obligation and the MSRB's aligned suitability framework under Rule G-19.

Regulation Best Interest's Care Obligation requires a broker-dealer to exercise reasonable diligence, care, and skill in evaluating the recommendation in light of the retail customer's investment profile, including risk tolerance, time horizon, and financial situation. The MSRB's proposed amendments to Rule G-19 align municipal securities suitability standards with Reg BI, requiring consideration of reasonably available alternatives. Concentrating two-thirds of a conservative retiree's savings in unrated, long-duration revenue bonds without documenting any alternatives analysis or cost-risk comparison fails both the customer-specific and reasonable-basis components of the Care Obligation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation; MSRB Rule G-19 (as proposed to be aligned with Reg BI per SR-MSRB-2020-02)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The representative failed to evaluate reasonably available alternatives and concentrated the majority of a conservative retiree's assets in a high-risk, unrated municipal bond without adequate documentation.
