Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Marcus Dillon, a registered representative at a dually-registered broker-dealer, recommends that Helen Torres, a 72-year-old retired school teacher with $310,000 in savings and a moderate risk profile, invest $240,000 (approximately 77% of her liquid net worth) in a non-traded municipal bond fund with a 10-year holding period and significant early redemption penalties. Marcus earns a 4.5% upfront commission on the sale. He does not document any consideration of lower-cost municipal bond ETFs or shorter-duration alternatives. Marcus provides Helen with a Form CRS at account opening but does not discuss his financial incentive or the availability of less costly, more liquid alternatives.

QUESTION
--------
Does Marcus's recommendation of the illiquid, high-commission municipal bond fund to a retired client with moderate risk tolerance comply with the Care Obligation under Regulation Best Interest?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – the Reg BI Care Obligation.

The Care Obligation under Regulation BI requires a broker-dealer to exercise reasonable diligence, care, and skill in making a recommendation, including evaluating reasonably available alternatives. Concentrating 77% of a retired, moderate-risk client's liquid assets in a 10-year illiquid product with heavy early redemption penalties and a 4.5% commission, without documented consideration of lower-cost, more liquid municipal bond alternatives, fails the care and cost analysis requirements. The MSRB's proposed alignment of Rule G-19 with Reg BI further reinforces that municipal securities recommendations must satisfy best interest standards.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Regulation Best Interest Care Obligation; MSRB Rule G-19 (suitability of recommendations and transactions)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The advisor failed to consider or document reasonably available lower-cost, more liquid alternatives before recommending a high-commission illiquid product to a retiree.
