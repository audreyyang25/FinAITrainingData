ID: 856
Source Folder: 17_SEC_Staff_Bulletins
Topic: conflict_mitigation
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Marcus Delgado, a broker-dealer registered representative at Pinnacle Brokerage Services, recommends that his 58-year-old retail client, Sandra Kowalski, roll her $280,000 401(k) into a variable annuity product for which Pinnacle receives an elevated 6.5% commission compared to a 0.75% trail commission on comparable low-cost mutual fund alternatives. Delgado's branch manager has flagged that the firm earns significantly higher revenue on annuity sales, and internal training materials acknowledge this as a material conflict of interest requiring mitigation. Rather than disclosing this conflict to Kowalski or taking any steps to mitigate it, Delgado merely checks a box on the firm's pre-recommendation form indicating 'conflict reviewed' without documenting any analysis or adjustment to his recommendation process. The firm has no written supervisory procedure requiring representatives to affirmatively address compensation-driven conflicts beyond the checkbox exercise, and Kowalski's Form CRS does not specifically describe the differential compensation structure between annuity and fund products.

QUESTION
--------
Does Delgado's checkbox-only conflict review process satisfy Reg BI's Conflict of Interest obligation when recommending a higher-commission annuity to a retail customer?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of Reg BI's Conflict of Interest obligation through failure to establish and follow policies and procedures to identify, disclose, and mitigate material conflicts of interest.

The August 2022 SEC Staff Bulletin explicitly states that identifying and addressing conflicts of interest should not be a 'check-the-box' exercise but rather a robust, ongoing process tailored to each specific conflict. Reg BI's Conflict of Interest obligation requires broker-dealers to establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to identify and at a minimum disclose, and in some cases mitigate or eliminate, material conflicts including those arising from differential compensation. Delgado's firm failed to implement any meaningful mitigation process, and the Form CRS omission of the specific differential compensation structure further compounds the disclosure failure. The elevated 6.5% commission versus a 0.75% trail represents the type of financial incentive that regulators have identified as capable of consciously or unconsciously inclining a representative toward a non-disinterested recommendation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iii) – Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation; SEC Staff Bulletin on Conflicts of Interest for Broker-Dealers (Aug. 3, 2022)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm's reliance on a checkbox process without substantive conflict analysis or mitigation directly contradicts SEC staff guidance that conflict management must be a tailored, robust process rather than a perfunctory administrative step.
