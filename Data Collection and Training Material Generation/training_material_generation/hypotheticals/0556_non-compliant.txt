Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Murray Lane Securities, a small FINRA member firm based in East Texas with four registered representatives, has been operating since June 30, 2020 without establishing, maintaining, or enforcing any written supervisory procedures specifically addressing Reg BI compliance. The firm services approximately 120 retail customers and offers general securities products including mutual funds, variable annuities, and equities. The firm's principal, who also serves as its sole compliance officer, states that the firm's pre-existing suitability procedures from 2018 are 'functionally equivalent' to Reg BI requirements and that no updates were necessary. No policies address the identification or mitigation of conflicts of interest, and no Form CRS has been filed or delivered to any retail customer.

QUESTION
--------
Did Murray Lane Securities satisfy its Reg BI Compliance Obligation by relying on its pre-existing suitability procedures without establishing Reg BI-specific written supervisory procedures?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iv) – Reg BI Compliance Obligation and FINRA Rules 3110(a) and (b).

The Reg BI Compliance Obligation requires broker-dealers to establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with Reg BI as a whole, including the Care, Conflict of Interest, and Disclosure Obligations. Pre-existing suitability procedures under the former FINRA Rule 2111 standard do not address the heightened and distinct requirements of Reg BI, such as conflict identification, mitigation, and elimination, nor do they address Form CRS delivery obligations. This pattern mirrors the Murray Securities AWC (No. 2021069350301), where FINRA found violations for failing to establish and maintain a supervisory system and written procedures addressing Reg BI since its June 30, 2020 effective date.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iv) – Reg BI Compliance Obligation; FINRA Rules 3110(a) and 3110(b)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm completely failed to create or implement any Reg BI-specific written supervisory procedures after the June 30, 2020 compliance date, relying instead on outdated suitability-era policies.
