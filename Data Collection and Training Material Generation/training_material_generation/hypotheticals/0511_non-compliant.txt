Source Folder: 02_SEC_Enforcement_Actions
Topic: sec_enforcement_ia
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Greenfield Capital Advisors, LLC, a registered investment adviser managing three private equity funds with combined AUM of $1.2 billion, routinely directed fund portfolio companies to use a consulting firm owned by Greenfield's co-founder for operational improvement services. The consulting firm charged the portfolio companies fees totaling $4.8 million over four years. Greenfield's Form ADV Part 2A and the fund offering documents contained no disclosure of the co-founder's ownership interest in the consulting firm or the related-party nature of the arrangement. Fund investors, primarily pension funds and university endowments, were unaware of the conflict until a routine SEC examination uncovered the payments.

QUESTION
--------
Did Greenfield Capital Advisors violate its fiduciary obligations under the Investment Advisers Act by failing to disclose the co-founder's financial interest in the consulting firm receiving fees from fund portfolio companies?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is not compliant. This constitutes a violation of Sections 206(1) and 206(2) of the Investment Advisers Act for failure to disclose material conflicts of interest.

Under Sections 206(1) and 206(2) of the Advisers Act, an investment adviser owes its clients a fiduciary duty that includes the obligation to disclose all material conflicts of interest. The co-founder's ownership of the consulting firm receiving $4.8 million in fees from portfolio companies is a financial conflict that directly parallels the SEC's findings in In the Matter of First Reserve Management, L.P. (IA Release No. 4529), where the Commission found that undisclosed financial conflicts of interest related to service providers constituted a breach of fiduciary duty. The absence of any disclosure in the Form ADV or offering documents deprived fund investors of the ability to evaluate and consent to the conflict.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940, Sections 206(1) and 206(2) – Anti-Fraud Provisions; fiduciary duty of disclosure

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser's complete failure to disclose a material financial conflict arising from a principal's ownership interest in a service provider receiving fees from fund portfolio companies is a textbook Section 206 violation.
