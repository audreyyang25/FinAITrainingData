ID: 802
Source Folder: 15_FINRA_Regulatory_Notices
Topic: senior_investor_protection
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Margaret Collins, an 81-year-old widow with early-stage dementia documented in her account notes, has a $620,000 portfolio at Summit Wealth Advisors. Her registered representative, David Park, recommends she liquidate her diversified bond portfolio to invest $410,000 in a portfolio of Alt Funds including the PIMCO TRENDS Managed Futures Strategy Fund and the AQR Long-Short Equity Fund. Park tells Collins these funds 'work like regular mutual funds but with better returns.' He does not explain the use of derivatives, leverage, or the higher expense ratios (averaging 1.85% versus 0.45% on her existing bond holdings). When Collins' adult son contacts the firm to express concern, the compliance department takes no action and does not place a temporary hold on the transactions. The trades settle three days later.

QUESTION
--------
Did Summit Wealth Advisors and its representative comply with their obligations regarding senior investor protection and suitability for complex product recommendations?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates FINRA Rules 2111 (Suitability), 2165 (Financial Exploitation of Specified Adults), and the Care Obligation under Reg BI.

FINRA Rule 2165 authorizes and encourages firms to place temporary holds on disbursements and transactions when there is reasonable belief of financial exploitation of a senior investor. The firm was on notice of Collins' cognitive decline and received a direct complaint from her trusted contact, yet failed to act. Additionally, recommending complex Alt Funds that use derivatives and leverage to an 81-year-old client with documented cognitive impairment and a conservative bond portfolio, without adequate disclosure of material risks and costs, fails both the suitability obligation under FINRA Rule 2111 and the Reg BI Care Obligation's requirement to consider reasonably available alternatives.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rules 2111 (Suitability), 2165 (Financial Exploitation of Specified Adults), 3110 (Supervision); SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Care Obligation; FINRA Regulatory Notice 22-11

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm failed to place a temporary hold under FINRA Rule 2165 despite documented cognitive impairment and a family member's complaint, while simultaneously recommending unsuitable complex Alt Funds to a vulnerable senior investor without adequate risk disclosure.
