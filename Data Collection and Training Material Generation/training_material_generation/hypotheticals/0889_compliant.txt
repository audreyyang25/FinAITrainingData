Source Folder: 19_CFTC_Guidance

Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Harborview Capital Partners LLC is a registered CPO that operates a commodity pool structured as a private fund with ten limited partner investors. Eight of the ten investors are institutional QEPs—pension funds and endowments—each with portfolios exceeding $5 million in commodity interests and futures margin deposits sufficient to satisfy CFTC Regulation 4.7's Portfolio Requirement. The remaining two investors are high-net-worth individuals who were QEPs at the time of their original investment three years ago but whose liquid portfolios have since declined to approximately $1.85 million each due to market conditions, marginally below an elevated threshold contemplated in the CFTC's October 2023 proposed amendments. Harborview declines to force-redeem these investors, relying on the fact that the proposed amendments are not yet final rules and the investors remain QEPs under current operative Rule 4.7 thresholds. Harborview provides these two investors with updated risk disclosures and enhanced quarterly reporting voluntarily.

QUESTION
--------
Is Harborview's decision to retain the two individual limited partners who no longer meet the proposed but not-yet-finalized elevated QEP thresholds compliant with current CFTC Regulation 4.7?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

CFTC Regulation 4.7's QEP financial thresholds are assessed at the time of an investor's initial participation in the pool, and proposed rulemakings published in October 2023 had not been finalized as of the relevant period, meaning the existing operative thresholds remain controlling law. Harborview correctly applies the currently effective Portfolio Requirement rather than anticipatory compliance with proposed but unadopted amendments, and there is no CFTC rule imposing a continuing obligation to force-redeem existing investors who remain QEPs under the effective regulatory standard. The firm's voluntary provision of enhanced disclosures and quarterly reporting to the borderline investors demonstrates prudent risk management exceeding minimum requirements. Retaining investors qualified under operative rules while monitoring for final rulemaking constitutes reasonable and compliant CPO conduct.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
CFTC Regulation 4.7 – Qualified Eligible Person Threshold Requirements; CFTC Proposed Rulemaking (October 2, 2023) – QEP Threshold Amendments (not yet final)

KEY FACTOR
----------
QEP status under CFTC Regulation 4.7 is determined at initial participation using currently effective thresholds, and a CPO is not obligated to force-redeem existing investors based solely on proposed but not-yet-finalized amendments to financial eligibility criteria.
