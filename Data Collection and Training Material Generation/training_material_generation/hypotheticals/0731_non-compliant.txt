ID: 731
Source Folder: 12_FINRA_Arbitration_Awards
Topic: arbitration_liability
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Fortune Financial Services, a FINRA member firm headquartered in Monaca, Pennsylvania with approximately 280 registered representatives across 200 branch offices, allowed multiple deferred variable annuity exchanges for elderly clients between July 2016 and July 2021 without adequate supervisory review. A 74-year-old retired schoolteacher, Margaret Downs, was switched from one deferred variable annuity to another three times in four years, each time incurring new surrender charges averaging $8,200 per exchange. The registered representative earned new commissions on each exchange totaling $14,500 while no meaningful change in investment features or riders benefited the client. The firm's written supervisory procedures did not require a documented cost-benefit analysis for variable annuity exchanges, and no principal reviewed whether the transactions were suitable.

QUESTION
--------
Did the firm's failure to implement supervisory procedures requiring documented cost-benefit analyses for variable annuity exchanges violate its supervisory obligations?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is not compliant. The firm failed to establish and maintain a supervisory system reasonably designed to detect and prevent unsuitable variable annuity exchanges.

FINRA Rule 3110 requires member firms to establish and maintain written supervisory procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with applicable securities laws and regulations. The firm's failure to require any cost-benefit analysis for variable annuity exchanges—particularly for elderly clients vulnerable to surrender charge losses—mirrors the findings in the Fortune Financial Services AWC (No. 2020065435403), where the firm's supervisory system was found deficient from July 2016 through July 2021 regarding deferred variable annuity transactions. Serial exchanges generating new commissions without documented client benefit constitute a textbook supervisory failure.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rules 3110 (Supervision) and 2111 (Suitability); FINRA Regulatory Notice 12-25 (Deferred Variable Annuities)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm had no written supervisory procedure requiring cost-benefit analysis for variable annuity exchanges, enabling repeated unsuitable switches that harmed an elderly client while generating commissions for the representative.
