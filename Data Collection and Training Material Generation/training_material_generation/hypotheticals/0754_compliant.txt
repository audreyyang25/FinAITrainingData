Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Samantha Reeves, a registered representative, receives an order from Apex Capital Partners, an institutional account as defined under FINRA Rule 4512(c) with $2.3 billion in assets under management. Apex's chief investment officer directs Samantha to purchase $15 million in high-yield emerging market bonds for a portfolio that Apex's internal investment committee has designated for aggressive growth. Apex has previously provided Samantha's firm with an Institutional Suitability Certificate affirming that it is capable of evaluating investment risks independently and that it will exercise independent judgment in evaluating recommendations. Samantha's firm has documented the certificate and has no information suggesting that Apex has withdrawn or modified these affirmations. Samantha does not perform an independent customer-specific suitability analysis before executing the trade.

QUESTION
--------
Does Samantha's reliance on the Institutional Suitability Certificate to satisfy her customer-specific suitability obligation under FINRA Rule 2111 comply with the rule?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

FINRA Rule 2111(b) provides that the customer-specific suitability obligation can be satisfied for institutional accounts if the firm has a reasonable basis to believe that the institutional customer is capable of evaluating investment risks independently and the customer affirmatively indicates it is exercising independent judgment. Apex has provided a written Institutional Suitability Certificate consistent with FINRA Rule 2111 and Rule 4512(c), affirming both conditions. The firm has no contrary information. Under these circumstances, Samantha is not required to perform an independent customer-specific suitability analysis for the recommended transaction.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111(b) — Institutional Account Exemption from Customer-Specific Suitability

KEY FACTOR
----------
The institutional customer provided a valid affirmative indication of independent judgment and capability to evaluate risks, satisfying the conditions under FINRA Rule 2111(b) for the institutional account exemption from customer-specific suitability.
