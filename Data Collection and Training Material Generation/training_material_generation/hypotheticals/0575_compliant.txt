Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A newly SEC-registered investment adviser with $110 million in AUM receives its first examination notification from the Division of Examinations in early 2025. The firm, registered for 14 months, had proactively engaged an independent compliance consultant upon registration to review its policies and procedures. The firm's Form ADV accurately discloses all material conflicts of interest, including that three of its five IARs hold insurance licenses and may receive commissions on annuity sales outside the advisory relationship. The firm maintains a conflicts committee that reviews and pre-approves any outside insurance transactions by IARs, and clients receive written disclosure of the specific conflict and sign an acknowledgment before any annuity recommendation. The firm's compliance manual was updated within 60 days of Reg BI's compliance date to include Reg BI procedures for the firm's broker-dealer affiliate. Despite the potential appearance of a conflict with dual-hatted representatives, the firm can demonstrate a complete paper trail of disclosures, client acknowledgments, conflicts committee minutes, and suitability documentation for each transaction.

QUESTION
--------
Is the firm's handling of dual-registered representatives who earn insurance commissions outside the advisory relationship compliant with SEC fiduciary standards and examination expectations?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The SEC Division of Examinations has identified newly registered advisers as an exam priority, particularly focusing on whether these firms have adequate compliance infrastructure, conflict disclosures, and supervisory controls from the outset. This firm has proactively addressed the inherent conflict created by dual-hatted representatives through multiple layers of compliance controls: full Form ADV disclosure, a pre-approval conflicts committee, individualized client disclosures with signed acknowledgments, and complete documentation. While the existence of IARs earning outside insurance commissions creates an inherent conflict, SEC fiduciary standards do not prohibit conflicts—they require full disclosure, informed consent, and management of conflicts through appropriate policies and procedures.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act §206(1)&(2) fiduciary duty; Form ADV Part 2A disclosure requirements; SEC Rule 206(4)-7 compliance policies and procedures; SEC FY 2025 and FY 2026 Examination Priorities (newly registered advisers, conflicts of interest)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm's proactive, multi-layered approach to disclosing, managing, and documenting conflicts arising from dual-hatted representatives satisfies fiduciary and compliance obligations despite the inherent appearance of conflict.
