Source Folder: 12_FINRA_Arbitration_Awards
Topic: arbitration_defenses
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
David Kimura, a registered representative formerly associated with LPL Financial, was named in a customer complaint alleging that he recommended an unsuitable variable annuity with a living benefit rider to a 58-year-old client with a $520,000 IRA rollover. The client alleged $40,000 in damages based on surrender charges after withdrawing from the annuity 14 months post-purchase. Kimura's documentation showed that the client had stated a 10-year investment horizon, desired guaranteed lifetime income starting at age 65, had a moderate risk tolerance, and specifically requested protection against market downturns after experiencing significant 2008 losses. The variable annuity selected had a 7-year surrender period, a guaranteed minimum withdrawal benefit rider, annual all-in costs of 2.85%, and was from a carrier rated A+ by AM Best. The client's early withdrawal was prompted by an unforeseen medical expense. Kimura sought expungement of the complaint from his CRD record through FINRA arbitration after the underlying claim was denied.

QUESTION
--------
Was the variable annuity recommendation suitable given the client's stated investment horizon, income objective, and risk tolerance, supporting the advisor's expungement request?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Under FINRA Rule 2330, a variable annuity recommendation must consider the client's age, investment horizon, liquidity needs, and stated objectives. A 58-year-old client with a 10-year horizon seeking guaranteed lifetime income and downside protection is a textbook appropriate candidate for a variable annuity with a living benefit rider. The surrender period (7 years) falls within the stated investment horizon, and the early withdrawal was driven by an unforeseeable medical event, not a liquidity mismatch at the time of recommendation. As seen in Kimura v. LPL Financial (FINRA Case No. 21-02526), expungement is appropriate under FINRA Rule 2080 when the claim is factually impossible, clearly erroneous, or false.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability—Customer-Specific); FINRA Rule 2330 (Deferred Variable Annuities); FINRA Rule 2080 (Expungement of Customer Dispute Information)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The recommendation aligned with the client's documented 10-year horizon and guaranteed income objective, and the early surrender resulted from an unforeseeable event rather than a suitability deficiency.
