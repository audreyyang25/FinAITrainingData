Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Blue Ridge National Bank's trust department manages a $22 million charitable remainder unitrust (CRUT) for a 75-year-old grantor who is also the income beneficiary, with a university endowment as the remainder beneficiary. The trust document grants the trustee broad investment discretion. After a comprehensive asset allocation review, the trust investment committee recommends concentrating 28% of the portfolio ($6.16 million) in a single diversified real estate investment trust (REIT) index fund, citing the grantor's need for inflation-protected income and a 25-year investment horizon for the remainder interest. The investment committee's written analysis documents consideration of both the income beneficiary's current distribution needs (5.2% unitrust payout) and the remainder beneficiary's long-term growth interest, the REIT fund's historical correlation to inflation, its 0.12% expense ratio, and comparable manager alternatives reviewed and rejected on cost and performance grounds. The bank's board-approved investment policy statement for fiduciary accounts expressly permits REIT allocations up to 30% when supported by documented analysis.

QUESTION
--------
Does Blue Ridge National Bank's 28% REIT index fund concentration in a CRUT, supported by documented investment committee analysis and consistent with the board-approved fiduciary investment policy, comply with OCC trust investment management standards?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

OTS Trust Handbook Section 200 requires that boards of directors and management establish clear investment guidance and that trust officers operate within board-approved risk parameters, which Blue Ridge satisfied through its policy statement permitting REIT allocations up to 30% when documented. The OCC Asset Management Handbook requires fiduciaries to consider the interests of both income and remainder beneficiaries in making investment decisions — a duty the investment committee fulfilled by explicitly analyzing the 5.2% payout obligation alongside long-term growth needs. The Uniform Prudent Investor Act evaluates investments in the context of the total portfolio rather than in isolation, and a 28% REIT allocation within a documented, diversified strategy supported by formal committee analysis and manager alternatives review satisfies the prudent investor standard. The low-cost index fund selection (0.12% expense ratio) further demonstrates loyalty to beneficiaries by minimizing friction costs.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
OCC Comptroller's Handbook – Asset Management (June 2023) – Trust Investment Management; OTS Trust Handbook Section 200 – Management and Fiduciary Investment Oversight; Uniform Prudent Investor Act (as incorporated into applicable state trust law)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The concentration was permissible because it fell within board-approved policy limits, was supported by documented investment committee analysis balancing both beneficiaries' interests, and reflected a prudent process under the Uniform Prudent Investor Act rather than an imprudent isolated decision.
