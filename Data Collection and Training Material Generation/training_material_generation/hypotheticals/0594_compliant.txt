ID: 594
Source Folder: 06_DOL_Fiduciary
Topic: ira_advice
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Advisor Natalie Ferris at Clearwater Advisory meets with James Dalton, age 63, who is retiring and has $520,000 in his employer's 401(k). The 401(k) plan offers only six investment options, charges 40 basis points in administrative fees, and will no longer allow James to make partial withdrawals after separation from service — only lump-sum distributions. Natalie recommends rolling the funds into an IRA at her firm, where James will be invested in a diversified portfolio of ETFs with a weighted expense ratio of 0.12%, and she will charge a 0.75% advisory fee (total cost approximately 0.87%). Natalie documents a detailed comparison showing the 401(k)'s limited withdrawal flexibility, its restricted fund menu, and the net cost differential. She provides James with a written disclosure of her compensation, her firm's conflicts of interest policy, and a side-by-side analysis of staying in the plan versus rolling over. Clearwater's compliance team reviewed and approved the recommendation as part of its PTE 2020-02 retrospective review process.

QUESTION
--------
Does the advisor's rollover recommendation comply with PTE 2020-02 despite the fact that the IRA's total cost is higher than the 401(k)'s administrative fee?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

PTE 2020-02's best interest standard does not require that a rollover always result in lower costs; it requires a prudent evaluation of all relevant factors, including investment options, services, withdrawal flexibility, and costs. Natalie documented that the 401(k) plan's rigid withdrawal rules and limited investment menu were significant disadvantages for James's retirement income needs, and the IRA's total cost differential was reasonable in light of the broader advisory services and flexibility provided. Full disclosure of compensation and conflicts, along with institutional retrospective review, satisfies the exemption's conditions.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
DOL PTE 2020-02 – Section I(c) Care Obligation; Section II(b) Disclosure Requirements; Section II(d) Policies and Procedures

KEY FACTOR
----------
The advisor documented a comprehensive comparative analysis showing legitimate non-cost advantages of the IRA rollover, satisfying PTE 2020-02's care and documentation requirements despite slightly higher total costs.
