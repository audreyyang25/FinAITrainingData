Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Sandra Reyes, age 58, is a senior hospital administrator with $1.1 million in a 401(k) plan who is separating from her employer and seeking advice on her distribution options. Her registered representative at Meridian Brokerage Services recommends a direct rollover into a Meridian IRA, which will generate a $650 commission from the sale of an initial recommended mutual fund portfolio. The rep's Best Interest Analysis Form documents consideration of four alternatives: leaving assets in the plan, rolling to a new employer's plan, taking a lump-sum distribution, and the IRA rollover. The form notes that Sandra's former employer's plan has limited investment options (12 funds, no ETFs), higher average expense ratios of 0.82% versus 0.41% for the recommended IRA portfolio, no access to personalized investment advice, and that Sandra expressed a preference for ongoing advisory contact. The rep discloses the commission in writing prior to the recommendation and the Form CRS is delivered at the initiation of the rollover discussion.

QUESTION
--------
Does the registered representative's IRA rollover recommendation satisfy the Care Obligation and Disclosure Obligation under Reg BI despite the existence of a commission incentive?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The 2022 SEC Staff Bulletin and Cambridge Investment Research FAQ guidance make clear that IRA rollover recommendations are a specific triggering event requiring both Form CRS delivery and a documented best interest analysis comparing the rollover to available alternatives. Here, the representative satisfied the Care Obligation by conducting and documenting a multi-alternative analysis that identified specific, quantifiable advantages of the rollover—lower expense ratios, broader investment options, and access to personalized advice—that align with Sandra's stated preferences and financial profile. The commission conflict was fully and timely disclosed in writing before the recommendation, satisfying the Disclosure Obligation. The presence of a commission does not automatically render a recommendation non-compliant under Reg BI provided the conflict is disclosed and the analysis reflects the client's genuine best interest.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) and (ii) – Reg BI Disclosure and Care Obligations; Reg BI FAQ Guidance on IRA Rollover Recommendations (2022 SEC Staff Bulletin)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The representative's documented four-alternative comparison with specific cost and feature differentials, combined with timely written conflict disclosure and Form CRS delivery, demonstrates genuine Care Obligation compliance notwithstanding the commission incentive.
