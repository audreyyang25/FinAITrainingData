Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Raymond Stoll, a registered investment adviser representative with 18 years of experience and a clean disciplinary record, managed accounts for 47 retail clients with aggregate assets of $9.8 million. In 2021, Stoll recommended variable annuity products issued by Protective Life and Nationwide Financial to six clients aged 58–67 who had identified guaranteed lifetime income as their primary retirement objective. For each client, Stoll prepared a written needs analysis documenting the client's stated income objectives, existing pension and Social Security income, tax-deferred accumulation goals, and 20-year time horizon. The annuities carried a 1.35% mortality and expense fee and a 6-year declining surrender schedule beginning at 7%, with the surrender period ending before each client's projected retirement date. The firm's compliance department reviewed and approved each recommendation, and clients received the applicable prospectus and a separate disclosure of surrender charge risks prior to signing.

QUESTION
--------
Does Stoll's recommendation of variable annuities with a 6-year surrender schedule and 1.35% M&E fee to clients aged 58–67 with documented long-term income objectives, supported by written suitability analyses and firm compliance approval, comply with applicable standards?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Variable annuities are not categorically unsuitable for clients in their late 50s and 60s; their appropriateness depends on the client's specific financial profile, objectives, and time horizon, as highlighted in regulatory commentary on variable annuity disclosure standards. Here, Stoll conducted individualized written suitability analyses documenting that each client's 20-year income objective and tax-deferred accumulation goal aligned with the product's guaranteed lifetime income feature. The 6-year surrender schedule was structured to conclude before each client's projected retirement date, eliminating liquidity risk at the point of anticipated withdrawal. Firm compliance review, prospectus delivery, and a separate surrender charge disclosure satisfy the informed consent requirements applicable to complex products under the Advisers Act and relevant SEC guidance.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940 §206; SEC Cardozo Clinic Comment Letter (File No. S7-23-18) – variable annuity suitability and disclosure standards for retail investors

KEY FACTOR
----------
Written individualized suitability documentation, surrender schedule alignment with each client's retirement date, and dual-layer pre-sale disclosure together establish that variable annuity recommendations to pre-retirees with documented income objectives are compliant despite the products' fee complexity.
