Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Brian Keller is a registered representative dually registered with a broker-dealer and an investment adviser. He recommends that Margaret Olsen, a 72-year-old retired schoolteacher with $340,000 in retirement savings and a conservative risk profile, liquidate her diversified bond portfolio and invest the entire proceeds into a non-traded REIT with a 7-year lock-up period and a 6.5% upfront commission. Brian's firm receives enhanced compensation from the REIT sponsor. Brian provides Margaret with a CRS Form but does not discuss the lock-up risk, the illiquidity of the product, or how the enhanced compensation creates a conflict of interest. Brian documents only that the client 'wanted higher yield.'

QUESTION
--------
Did Brian satisfy the Regulation Best Interest Care and Disclosure Obligations when recommending the non-traded REIT to Margaret?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of Regulation Best Interest's Care Obligation and Conflict of Interest Obligation.

Under Regulation Best Interest, the Care Obligation requires a broker-dealer to exercise reasonable diligence, care, and skill in making a recommendation, including understanding the risks and rewards of the product and having a reasonable basis to believe the recommendation is in the retail customer's best interest given her specific investment profile. Concentrating a 72-year-old conservative retiree's entire savings in an illiquid, long-lock-up product is inconsistent with this standard. Additionally, the Conflict of Interest Obligation requires the broker-dealer to fully and fairly disclose the material conflict created by the enhanced compensation; delivery of a CRS Form alone does not satisfy this obligation when specific transaction-level conflicts are not addressed.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) and (ii) – Reg BI Care Obligation and Conflict of Interest Obligation; Release No. 34-86031

KEY FACTOR
----------
The recommendation of an illiquid, high-commission product to a conservative elderly retiree, combined with failure to disclose the specific compensation conflict, violated both the Care and Conflict of Interest Obligations under Reg BI.
