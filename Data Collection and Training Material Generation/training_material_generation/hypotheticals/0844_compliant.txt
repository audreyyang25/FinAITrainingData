ID: 844
Source Folder: 17_SEC_Staff_Bulletins
Topic: care_obligation_detail
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A 45-year-old orthopedic surgeon with $1.2 million in an existing commission-based brokerage account approaches her representative requesting to concentrate $300,000 into a single-issuer biotech stock she has researched independently. The representative completes a Best Interest Analysis documenting the client's 20-year investment horizon, high risk tolerance, $800,000 in other diversified assets held at other institutions, her professional sophistication, and the speculative nature and volatility profile of the specific biotech issuer. The representative also documents a verbal warning given to the client regarding concentration risk and the potential for total loss, confirms the investment represents 25% of the accounts he can observe, and notes the client's unsolicited initiation of the request. He then executes the transaction and retains the completed analysis in the client file.

QUESTION
--------
Does the representative's handling of the client's request to concentrate $300,000 in a single biotech issuer satisfy Reg BI's Care Obligation?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Reg BI's Care Obligation applies to recommendations, and while the client initiated the request, the representative still made a recommendation by agreeing to execute. However, the Care Obligation is satisfied when the representative conducts a thorough, documented analysis of the specific client's full financial profile, including assets held away, risk tolerance, and time horizon. The documentation of the client's professional sophistication, other diversified holdings, long investment horizon, and the explicit risk warning satisfies the requirement to have a reasonable basis to believe the recommendation is in this specific client's best interest. The SEC's guidance acknowledges that concentration in higher-risk securities may be appropriate for certain high-net-worth, sophisticated clients when properly documented.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Staff Bulletin on Care Obligation (2020)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The representative's thorough Best Interest Analysis documenting the client's full financial picture, including significant diversified assets held away and a long investment horizon, provides a reasonable basis for the concentrated recommendation consistent with the Care Obligation.
