Source Folder: 18_OCC_Bank_Trust_Guidance
Topic: bank_fiduciary
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Commonwealth Trust Bank serves as both trustee and investment manager for a $6.1 million revocable living trust established by a 68-year-old retired physician. The bank's affiliated brokerage subsidiary executes equity trades for the trust account, generating $14,200 in commissions annually. Prior to account opening, the bank provided the grantor with a written conflicts-of-interest disclosure identifying the affiliated brokerage relationship, the commission structure, and a comparison of costs versus third-party execution alternatives. The bank's trust investment committee documented a formal best-execution analysis on a semi-annual basis demonstrating that the affiliated broker's execution quality and total transaction costs were equivalent to or better than three unaffiliated alternatives reviewed during the same period. The grantor provided informed written consent to the affiliated brokerage arrangement at account inception and annually thereafter.

QUESTION
--------
Does Commonwealth Trust Bank's use of its affiliated brokerage subsidiary for trust account trade execution, with documented disclosure and best-execution analysis, satisfy OCC fiduciary conflict-of-interest standards?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The OCC Conflicts of Interest handbook permits affiliated brokerage arrangements in fiduciary accounts when the bank provides full written disclosure of the conflict, documents that the affiliated service is provided on terms at least as favorable as those available from unaffiliated providers, and obtains appropriate authorization. Commonwealth satisfied all three elements: it delivered pre-account written disclosure identifying the commission structure and alternatives, conducted and documented semi-annual best-execution analyses confirming competitive pricing, and obtained the grantor's informed written consent both at inception and annually. The documented fiduciary process — including formal committee review and contemporaneous written records — demonstrates the bank managed rather than ignored the conflict.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
OCC Comptroller's Handbook AM-CI – Conflicts of Interest (January 2015), Section: Dealings With Affiliated Service Providers and Allocation of Brokerage Services to Affiliates

KEY FACTOR
----------
The affiliated brokerage arrangement is permissible because it was supported by documented best-execution analysis demonstrating competitive terms, full written conflict disclosure, and the grantor's informed written consent.
