Source Folder: 14_NASAA_State_Enforcement
Topic: state_registration_violations
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Marcus Fontaine operates a small advisory firm, Fontaine Wealth Partners LLC, out of Roswell, Georgia, managing approximately $18 million in assets for 42 clients. Fontaine has never registered as an investment adviser with the Georgia Secretary of State or filed Form ADV through the IARD system. He has been providing personalized investment advice and charging a 1.25% annual management fee since 2021. When a client asked whether he was properly licensed, Fontaine stated that registration was 'only required for firms managing over $100 million' and that state registration was voluntary for smaller firms.

QUESTION
--------
Does Fontaine's operation of an advisory firm without state registration violate applicable state securities laws?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is not compliant. This constitutes an unregistered investment adviser violation under state securities law.

Investment advisers who manage less than $100 million in AUM and are not otherwise exempt are required to register with their state securities regulator, not the SEC. Fontaine's claim that state registration is voluntary is false. As illustrated in the California registration guidance requiring completion of Form ADV through the IARD system, state-registered advisers must affirmatively file and maintain current registrations. Operating without registration is a violation of state securities law and subjects the adviser to enforcement action.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
State securities registration requirements; Uniform Securities Act § 403; California Corp. Code § 25230 (analogous Georgia provisions); NASAA Model Rule requiring Form ADV filing through IARD

KEY FACTOR
----------
Fontaine operated as an investment adviser without filing Form ADV or registering with the state, which is required for advisers below the SEC registration threshold.
