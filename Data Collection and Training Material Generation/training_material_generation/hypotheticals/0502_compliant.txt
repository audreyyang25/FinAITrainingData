Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Redwood Advisory LLC advises a publicly traded BDC ('Redwood BDC') and also serves as general partner to Redwood Growth Partners LP, a private fund. A limited partner of Redwood Growth Partners LP, Lakeview Investments, holds no voting securities in Redwood BDC, has no board representation, and has no contractual right to participate in or influence the BDC's investment decisions. Redwood Advisory identifies a $22 million senior secured loan opportunity and proposes that Redwood BDC and Redwood Growth Partners LP co-invest on a pro rata basis. The CCO conducts a detailed analysis under IM Guidance Update 2014-12, concludes Lakeview qualifies as a remote affiliate because it has no control relationship with the BDC beyond its limited partnership interest in the common-control fund, and obtains approval from a required majority of the BDC's disinterested directors before proceeding with the transaction.

QUESTION
--------
Was Redwood Advisory's classification of Lakeview as a remote affiliate and its process for approving the co-investment transaction compliant with SEC guidance?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

IM Guidance Update 2014-12 explains that a second-tier affiliate of a BDC—such as a limited partner of a fund under common control with the BDC—may be treated as a remote affiliate where the limited partner lacks voting securities in the BDC, board representation, or other control relationships. In such cases, co-investment transactions are permissible subject to approval by a required majority of the BDC's directors. Redwood Advisory conducted the requisite factual analysis, properly identified the remote affiliate status, and obtained disinterested director approval as required under Section 57(b)(2).

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Company Act of 1940, Section 57(b)(2); IM Guidance Update No. 2014-12 (BDC Transactions with Certain Second-Tier Affiliates)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser performed the detailed multi-factor analysis contemplated by IM Guidance 2014-12, correctly determined the limited partner was a remote affiliate lacking any control relationship with the BDC, and obtained the required board approval before executing the co-investment.
