FACTS
-----
Stifel Nicolaus Advisory Services, a registered investment adviser, amended its Form CRS on November 1, 2023, to reflect a material change: effective November 15, 2023, the firm transitioned from a flat annual retainer model ($15,000 minimum) to an assets-under-management (AUM) fee of 0.75% on portfolios under $2 million and 0.50% on portfolios over $2 million. The firm identified this as a material change because the fee structure now directly incentivizes larger account sizes. Stifel notified all existing retail clients (847 clients with $1.2 billion AUM) via email with a link to the updated Form CRS on October 25, 2023 (21 days before effective date), with explicit consent language stating: "Your consent to this fee change is required. Click to accept the new fee schedule or contact your adviser to discuss alternative fee arrangements." Clients who did not respond within 14 days received a follow-up phone call from their adviser offering a call to discuss impact. The updated Form CRS clearly explained that AUM fees create incentives to increase assets and disclosed the specific breakpoint thresholds and a worked example showing fee impact for a $1.5 million portfolio ($11,250 annually vs. $15,000 retainer).

QUESTION
--------
Did Stifel Nicolaus comply with Form CRS update and disclosure obligations under Reg BI §240.15l-1(e) and SEC guidance on material changes?

ANSWER
------
Yes. Stifel complied with the Form CRS update obligation on five points: (1) the firm identified the fee-structure change as material because it alters incentive structures, per SEC guidance; (2) advance notice occurred 21 days before the effective date, permitting clients adequate time to evaluate and respond; (3) the updated Form CRS disclosed the specific incentive conflict (AUM incentive to increase assets) with numerical breakpoints and a concrete example; (4) the firm obtained affirmative consent before implementing the change; (5) follow-up contact was offered to clients who did not respond, demonstrating good-faith effort to ensure informed consent. The worked example (calculating impact on a $1.5M account) directly addresses retail client understanding without legal jargon, meeting SEC clarity standards.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Reg BI Disclosure Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(e); SEC Form CRS Instructions (material changes); SEC Compliance Outreach (2023) on AUM incentive disclosure

KEY FACTOR
----------
Timely advance notice, clear conflict disclosure with specific examples, and affirmative consent demonstrate compliance with Form CRS material change requirements.
