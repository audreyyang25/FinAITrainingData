Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
An adviser manages an account for a 40-year-old client with $300,000 in assets and a stated objective to maximize tax-efficient growth over a 25-year horizon with moderate risk tolerance. The adviser recommends: 60% total U.S. stock index (held in tax-deferred 401(k) portion), 20% international stock index, 15% high-yield bond fund (in tax-deferred IRA), 5% emerging market bonds (in taxable account with tax-loss harvesting overlay). The adviser documents the specific rationale for asset location strategy (placing tax-inefficient bonds and high-yield in tax-deferred accounts), the choice of low-cost index vehicles for tax efficiency, and the use of tax-loss harvesting in taxable accounts. The portfolio is reviewed annually; allocations remain appropriate for the 25-year horizon.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation satisfy the duty of care?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this is compliant.

The adviser demonstrated a reasonable basis for the recommendation by: (1) documenting the client's stated objective of tax-efficient growth; (2) developing a specific asset location strategy (bonds in tax-deferred accounts, index funds in taxable) with documented rationale; (3) selecting low-cost index vehicles consistent with tax efficiency; (4) implementing tax-loss harvesting to reduce tax drag; and (5) documenting why the 60/20/15/5 allocation matches the client's 25-year horizon and moderate risk tolerance. The adviser's approach shows sophisticated understanding of the client's needs and proactive investigation into how to achieve stated objectives efficiently. The recommendation is proportionate, diversified, and supported by documented reasoning that ties specific product choices and account location decisions to the client's stated tax-efficiency objective. Periodic review ensures the strategy remains appropriate. This satisfies the duty of care's requirement for reasonable investigation and basis.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940, Section 206(2); duty of care includes obligation to make recommendations consistent with client's objectives using investment strategies appropriate to those objectives; tax efficiency may be a material objective requiring documented consideration.

KEY FACTOR
----------
Documented asset location strategy with specific rationale linking account types (401(k), IRA, taxable) to product suitability (tax-inefficient bonds in tax-deferred accounts) in support of client's stated tax-efficiency objective.
