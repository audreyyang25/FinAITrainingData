Source Folder: 06_DOL_Fiduciary
Topic: pte_2020_02
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Alicia Fontaine is a financial advisor at Heritage Wealth Advisors who serves as an investment advice fiduciary under PTE 2020-02. Her client, Robert Yamamoto, age 55, is leaving his employer and has a 401(k) with $210,000 invested across four target-date funds with a total expense ratio of 0.12%. Robert's plan does not offer financial planning services, in-plan Roth conversion options, or access to individual bond laddering that Robert has expressed interest in for future retirement income planning. Alicia recommends Robert roll over to an IRA at Heritage, which charges a 0.50% advisory fee and will invest in a diversified portfolio of ETFs with a blended expense ratio of 0.06%, resulting in a total all-in cost of approximately 0.56%. Alicia prepares a written rollover analysis documenting the cost differential, the additional services available through the IRA (comprehensive financial planning, tax-loss harvesting, Roth conversion strategy, and individual bond access), and explains why the incremental cost is justified by the enhanced services aligned with Robert's stated goals. She provides Robert with a written disclosure of all compensation, conflicts of interest, and her fiduciary acknowledgment before the rollover is executed. Heritage's compliance team reviews the rollover documentation within 30 days as part of its ongoing retrospective review program.

QUESTION
--------
Is Alicia's rollover recommendation compliant with PTE 2020-02 even though the IRA has higher overall costs than the 401(k)?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

PTE 2020-02 does not mandate that a rollover recommendation always result in the lowest-cost option; rather, it requires that the recommendation be in the retirement investor's best interest considering the totality of circumstances, including services, investment options, and the investor's specific needs. Alicia documented a thorough comparative analysis acknowledging the cost increase but justifying it with concrete additional services—financial planning, Roth conversion strategy, individual bond laddering—that directly address Robert's stated goals and are unavailable in the existing plan. She satisfied disclosure obligations, provided the fiduciary acknowledgment, and Heritage's retrospective review program timely assessed the recommendation. All conditions of the exemption are met.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
DOL PTE 2020-02, Section II(a)–(d) – Best Interest, Care, Disclosure, and Policies & Procedures Conditions

KEY FACTOR
----------
A rollover to a higher-cost IRA can satisfy PTE 2020-02 when the advisor documents that additional services and investment options unavailable in the plan justify the incremental cost relative to the client's specific needs and goals.
