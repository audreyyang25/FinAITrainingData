Topic: form_crs
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Pinnacle Wealth Advisors is dually registered as a broker-dealer and investment adviser. The firm files and delivers a Form CRS that clearly distinguishes between its brokerage and advisory services on page one, using the SEC's prescribed comparison format. The Form CRS states that brokerage clients pay transaction-based commissions while advisory clients pay a 1.10% annual wrap fee, and it includes the required 'conversation starter' questions in bold. In Item 4, the firm discloses that two of its 30 financial professionals have reportable disciplinary events and directs clients to BrokerCheck and the SEC's IAPD for details. When a new client, a 55-year-old engineer with $750,000 in retirement savings, opens an account, the firm delivers the Form CRS electronically before making any recommendation, obtains an electronic acknowledgment of receipt, and retains the acknowledgment in its books and records. Six months later, the firm updates its Form CRS within 30 days after a material change (the addition of a new fee schedule for financial planning services) and delivers the updated version to existing clients. A competitor alleges the firm's Form CRS is deficient because it does not list every individual disciplinary event in full narrative detail within the document itself.

QUESTION
--------
Is the firm's Form CRS compliant despite not including full narrative descriptions of each financial professional's disciplinary history within the document itself?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Form CRS Item 4 requires firms to disclose whether they or their financial professionals have legal or disciplinary history and direct customers to BrokerCheck and/or the SEC's IAPD website for specific details. The SEC's instructions and adopting release do not require full narrative descriptions of each disciplinary event within the Form CRS itself, which is intended to be a brief, plain-language relationship summary document (limited to two pages for standalone broker-dealers or four pages for dual registrants). Pinnacle's approach—disclosing the existence of disciplinary events, identifying the number of affected professionals, and directing clients to the appropriate databases—satisfies the requirement. The firm also properly delivered the Form CRS before making recommendations, obtained acknowledgment, and timely updated it upon a material change.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Form CRS (17 CFR 249.2) General Instructions and Item 4; SEC Rule 17a-14; SEC Reg BI Adopting Release (34-86031)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Form CRS requires disclosure that disciplinary history exists and direction to BrokerCheck/IAPD for details, not full narrative recitation of each event within the document, and the firm satisfied all delivery, timing, and updating requirements.
