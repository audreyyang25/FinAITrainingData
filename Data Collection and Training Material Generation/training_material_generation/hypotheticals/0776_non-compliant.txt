Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Marcus Thelen operates a financial planning practice in Sacramento, California, managing approximately $18 million in assets for 42 clients. He completed his Form ADV and submitted it through the IARD system but never received confirmation of approval from the California Department of Financial Protection and Innovation (DFPI). Despite this, Thelen began soliciting new clients, entering into advisory agreements, and collecting quarterly fees of 0.75% of AUM. When a client complained about poor performance, a DFPI examiner discovered that Thelen's application had been rejected six months earlier due to incomplete disclosure of a prior FINRA disciplinary action, and Thelen had never followed up or resubmitted.

QUESTION
--------
Did Thelen violate state securities laws by conducting investment advisory business without a valid registration certificate from California?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is not compliant. This constitutes an unregistered investment adviser violation under California Corporations Code Section 25230.

California Corporations Code Section 25230 makes it unlawful for any person to conduct business as an investment adviser unless holding a valid certificate from the DFPI. Thelen's application was rejected, meaning he never obtained lawful registration. Collecting advisory fees and entering into advisory contracts without a valid certificate is a direct violation regardless of whether the initial application was submitted in good faith. The DFPI registration process, as outlined in California's How to Register guide, requires a complete application including full disclosure of disciplinary history.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
California Corporations Code Section 25230 – Requirement to register as an investment adviser before conducting advisory business; NASAA Model Rule on IA Registration

KEY FACTOR
----------
Thelen conducted advisory business and collected fees after his registration application was rejected, operating without a valid investment adviser certificate.
