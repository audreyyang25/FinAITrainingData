Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Clearwater Wealth Advisers, a mid-sized RIA with $340 million AUM, manages a $4.8 million portfolio for Thomas R., age 56, a hedge fund manager with $2.3 million in liquid net worth and annual income of $650,000. Clearwater's Form ADV Part 2A Item 5 clearly states: 'We offer performance fees only to qualified clients meeting SEC Rule 205-3(c) criteria: net worth ≥$750K (excluding residence), income ≥$200K for 2+ years, or AUM with us ≥$1M.' Thomas received a separate 'Qualified Client Certification' form, signed and acknowledged, confirming he meets the income test ($650,000 over prior two years, well above $200K threshold). Clearwater charged 0.5% AUM fee plus 15% of net gains above a 5% hurdle rate. The brochure disclosed the fee structure, the hurdle mechanism, and that performance fees apply only to new account gains (not pre-existing gains). Thomas's performance fee in year one totaled $28,500 based on $190,000 in gains above the hurdle. All disclosures were provided in writing before account opening.

QUESTION
--------
Did Clearwater Wealth Advisers comply with Rule 205-3 in charging Thomas a performance fee?

ANSWER
------
Yes, fully compliant. Clearwater satisfied all Rule 205-3 requirements: (1) Thomas is a qualified client (income ≥$200K for 2+ years per signed certification), (2) the adviser obtained written acknowledgment (Item 5.G) that Thomas meets the definition, (3) Form ADV Part 2A clearly disclosed the performance fee terms and the qualified client restriction, (4) the fee calculation methodology (15% of gains above 5% hurdle) was transparent, and (5) the hurdle was objectively verifiable. The signed Qualified Client Certification satisfies Rule 205-3(c)(5). Clearwater's practice mirrors compliant adviser arrangements validated in SEC no-action letters and exam guidance.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 205-3(c)(1)–(5) — Compliant Performance Fee Arrangement

KEY FACTOR
----------
Adviser obtained documented qualified client certification, clearly disclosed performance fee terms in brochure, and limited fees to qualified clients per Rule 205-3(c).
