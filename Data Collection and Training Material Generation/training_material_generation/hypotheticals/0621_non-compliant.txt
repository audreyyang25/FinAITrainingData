Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Greenfield Capital, a dual-registered broker-dealer and investment adviser, was required to deliver Form CRS to all retail investors by the June 30, 2020 compliance date. The firm updated its website with the Form CRS document but did not deliver the relationship summary to its approximately 4,200 existing retail customers at the beginning of any new account openings or upon material changes to its advisory services. When FINRA examined the firm in Q4 2020, it found that only 15% of existing retail customers had received the Form CRS, and the document provided to new customers omitted the required link to investor.gov/crs and failed to disclose whether the firm's financial professionals had reportable disciplinary history.

QUESTION
--------
Did Greenfield Capital satisfy its Form CRS delivery and content obligations under SEC rules?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of Form CRS delivery and content requirements.

Form CRS must be delivered to retail investors at the beginning of the relationship and whenever material changes occur. The document must include all required content items, including a link to the SEC's investor education page at investor.gov/crs and disclosure of reportable legal or disciplinary history. Greenfield's failure to deliver to 85% of existing retail clients upon triggering events, combined with omitting mandatory content fields, constitutes a clear failure to meet both delivery and content requirements.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Form CRS (Relationship Summary) – 17 CFR 275.204-5 / 17 CFR 240.17a-14; SEC Release No. 34-86032

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm failed both to timely deliver Form CRS to the vast majority of retail investors and omitted mandatory content including the investor.gov/crs link and disciplinary history disclosure.
