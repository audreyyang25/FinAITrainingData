Source Folder: unknown
Topic: reg_bi_conflict_expanded
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Raymond James advisor manages $3.2M portfolio for 52-year-old self-directed engineer with high risk tolerance. Firm recommends $1.8M allocation to managed futures strategy offered through affiliated hedge fund manager, Cypress Capital (Raymond James owns 45% equity stake). Cypress charges 1.50% management fee + 20% performance fee; unaffiliated alternatives: WisdomTree Managed Futures ETF (0.71% ER, no performance fee) or DBMF (0.85% ER, no performance fee). Client receives detailed disclosure: 'Cypress Capital is 45% owned by Raymond James. We earn incremental revenue from Cypress management fees.' However, advisor documented no comparative analysis of fee structures or net returns over 3–5 year periods. Raymond James compliance approved the recommendation based solely on suitability (appropriate for risk tolerance), without requiring conflict mitigation documentation. No analysis of whether Cypress's 1.50% + 20% performance structure could net-outperform the low-cost ETFs after fees.

QUESTION
--------
Did Raymond James's disclosure of affiliate ownership and Cypress fees, absent comparative fee/return analysis, satisfy Reg BI's Conflict of Interest Obligation?

ANSWER
------
No. Reg BI §240.15l-1(a)(2)(ii) requires identification, disclosure, AND affirmative mitigation—not suitability alone. While the disclosure of affiliate ownership and fee structure was appropriate, the recommendation failed the mitigation prong. The material conflict (Raymond James's 45% ownership of fee-generating entity) required documented analysis of whether Cypress's 1.70% net cost (1.50% + estimated 0.20% performance allocation assuming moderate positive returns) justified its selection over 0.71–0.85% unaffiliated alternatives. Absence of comparative net-return analysis over relevant time horizons meant the firm did not demonstrate the conflict did not materially influence the recommendation. Suitability (appropriate for high-risk client) and conflict of interest management are separate Reg BI requirements. The firm conflated compliance with suitability, missing the mitigation obligation. This mirrors SEC and FINRA enforcement patterns on affiliated manager recommendations without documented fee/performance justification.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(ii); disclosure + mitigation (comparative analysis)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Affiliate fee-generating conflict requires documented comparative fee and net-return analysis to demonstrate mitigation; disclosure alone is insufficient.
