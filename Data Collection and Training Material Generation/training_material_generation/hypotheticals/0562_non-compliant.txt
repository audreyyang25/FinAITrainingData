ID: 562
Source Folder: 04_RegBI_Compliance_Guidance
Topic: reg_bi_disclosure
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Regulus Capital Advisors, LLC, a FINRA member firm headquartered in Michigan with 60 branches and over 100 registered representatives, primarily services retail investors in general securities products. From mid-2021 through early 2024, the firm recommended various securities products to retail customers but failed to disclose in its Form CRS or any other customer-facing document that the firm and a control affiliate had material disciplinary history, including prior regulatory actions and customer complaints. The firm's Form CRS listed generic language about conflicts of interest but omitted any reference to disciplinary events involving the firm or its affiliated entities. Customers were directed to the firm's website for additional disclosures, but the website similarly contained no disciplinary history information.

QUESTION
--------
Did the firm violate Reg BI's Disclosure Obligation by failing to disclose its own and its control affiliate's disciplinary history to retail customers?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is not compliant. The firm violated the Reg BI Disclosure Obligation and Form CRS requirements.

The Reg BI Disclosure Obligation requires broker-dealers to provide retail customers, prior to or at the time of a recommendation, full and fair disclosure of all material facts relating to the scope and terms of the relationship, including conflicts of interest. Form CRS specifically requires disclosure of disciplinary history involving the firm and its financial professionals, including a reference to BrokerCheck. A multi-year omission of the firm's and its control affiliate's disciplinary events deprives retail customers of material information relevant to evaluating the firm's trustworthiness. This fact pattern closely parallels the Regulus Financial Group AWC (No. 2023077077001), where FINRA found the firm failed to disclose its own and its control affiliate's disciplinary history from May 2021 to February 2024.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation; Form CRS (17 CFR 249.2)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm systematically omitted its own and its control affiliate's disciplinary history from customer disclosures and Form CRS for nearly three years.
