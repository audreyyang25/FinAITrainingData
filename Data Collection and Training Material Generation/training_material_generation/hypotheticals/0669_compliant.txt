Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Ridgeview Advisors, a SEC-registered investment adviser, manages a growth equity private fund with $450 million AUM. As part of its annual compliance review, Ridgeview identifies that its co-investment allocation process could create the appearance of preferential treatment. The firm drafts and distributes a detailed written notice to all current limited partners and includes the notice in its updated offering documents for prospective investors, describing (a) the specific co-investment allocation methodology, (b) which investor categories may receive co-investment opportunities first, and (c) the material economic terms, including fee offsets, associated with co-investments. The firm also updates its Form ADV Part 2A to disclose the potential conflict. No investor receives preferential redemption or portfolio transparency rights that would materially disadvantage other investors.

QUESTION
--------
Does Ridgeview Advisors' proactive disclosure of its co-investment allocation methodology and related preferential terms to all investors comply with the SEC's Private Fund Adviser Rules?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The Preferential Treatment Rule under the Private Fund Adviser Rules requires that preferential terms not involving prohibited categories (such as preferential redemption terms materially harming others) be disclosed to all current and prospective investors via specific written notice. Ridgeview's detailed written disclosure to all LP classes—covering allocation methodology, prioritization criteria, and fee terms—satisfies the rule's transparency requirements. The proactive updating of Form ADV Part 2A further demonstrates the firm's compliance with the IA Act fiduciary duty of disclosure.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Private Fund Adviser Rules – Rule 211(h)(2)-3 (Preferential Treatment Rule); Investment Advisers Act Section 206

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser satisfied the disclosure-based requirements by providing specific, written notice of all preferential co-investment terms to every current and prospective investor.
