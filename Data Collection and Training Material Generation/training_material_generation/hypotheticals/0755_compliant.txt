Source Folder: 13_FINRA_Rulebook
Topic: variable_annuity_rule
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Robert Dellacroce, a registered representative, recommends that Linda Ferraro, a 62-year-old recently retired nurse with $520,000 in retirement savings, a moderate risk tolerance, and a stated need for guaranteed lifetime income starting at age 67, purchase a deferred variable annuity with a 5-year surrender period, a total annual expense ratio of 2.10%, and a guaranteed lifetime withdrawal benefit (GLWB) rider. Robert documents Linda's investment profile comprehensively under FINRA Rule 2090, including her income needs, tax status, existing insurance coverage, risk tolerance, time horizon, and liquidity requirements. He also documents that Linda has $185,000 in liquid savings outside the annuity and will not need to access the annuity funds during the surrender period. The firm's registered principal, Karen Whitfield, reviews the transaction documentation, confirms the suitability analysis, verifies that the product features match Linda's stated needs, and approves the transaction in writing two business days before the contract is issued. A competing lower-cost product exists but lacks the GLWB rider that addresses Linda's primary income objective.

QUESTION
--------
Does this deferred variable annuity recommendation and the firm's processing of the transaction comply with FINRA Rules 2111, 2330, and 3110?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

FINRA Rule 2330(b) requires that recommendations involving deferred variable annuities account for the customer's age, annual income, financial situation, investment objectives, intended use of the product, existing insurance holdings, liquidity needs, and whether the product as a whole benefits the customer. Robert's documentation addresses each of these elements, and the product's GLWB rider directly serves Linda's stated need for guaranteed lifetime income. The 5-year surrender period aligns with her planned withdrawal timeline at age 67, and her $185,000 in outside liquid savings mitigates liquidity concerns. The existence of a cheaper alternative without the GLWB rider does not render the recommendation unsuitable because the selected product's features specifically address the client's primary investment objective. Rule 2330(c)'s principal review requirement is satisfied by Karen Whitfield's documented, individualized review and written pre-approval before the contract was issued.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2330(b) (suitability for deferred variable annuities), FINRA Rule 2330(c) (principal review and approval), FINRA Rule 2111(a) (suitability), and FINRA Rule 3110 (supervision)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The recommendation was thoroughly documented, aligned with the client's specific income needs and time horizon, and received individualized principal review and pre-approval — satisfying all elements of Rules 2330, 2111, and 3110 despite the existence of a lower-cost alternative lacking the critical product feature.
