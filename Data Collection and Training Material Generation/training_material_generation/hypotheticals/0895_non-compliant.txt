ID: 895
Source Folder: 19_CFTC_Guidance
Topic: cta_cpo_suitability
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A registered CTA, operating under CFTC Rule 4.7, solicits a 58-year-old retired schoolteacher with $340,000 in total savings to participate in a managed futures trading program. The client's portfolio consists entirely of certificates of deposit and a fixed annuity; she has no prior derivatives trading experience. The CTA onboards her without conducting any individualized suitability assessment, relying solely on her self-certification that she meets the Qualified Eligible Person (QEP) threshold under Rule 4.7. In her first four trades—consistent with the median retail futures trader pattern identified in CFTC research—she sustains losses of approximately $28,000, representing over 8% of her life savings, and terminates her account.

QUESTION
--------
Did the CTA satisfy its suitability obligations by relying exclusively on the client's QEP self-certification without conducting an independent suitability analysis?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of NFA suitability obligations and CFTC Rule 4.7 disclosure and eligibility requirements.

CFTC Rule 4.7 provides exemptions from certain disclosure requirements only where all participants are genuinely Qualified Eligible Persons; it does not eliminate the CTA's independent obligation under NFA Compliance Rule 2-30 to evaluate whether the trading program is suitable for the specific client. A client meeting a financial threshold does not automatically render a high-risk managed futures program appropriate for her individual circumstances, risk tolerance, and investment objectives. The CTA's failure to document any suitability analysis beyond the QEP self-certification, combined with placing a retiree's primary savings into a speculative futures strategy, constitutes a clear suitability failure.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NFA Compliance Rule 2-30; CFTC Regulation 4.7 – QEP Eligibility and CTA Suitability Obligations

KEY FACTOR
----------
QEP status under Rule 4.7 does not substitute for an individualized suitability determination required under NFA Compliance Rule 2-30.
