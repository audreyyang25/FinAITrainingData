Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
On June 15, 2024, Advisor Martinez recommended a 20% allocation to Bitcoin to her client, Margaret Chen, age 74, a retired teacher with $450,000 in a brokerage account. Margaret indicated her risk tolerance was 'conservative' and she needed portfolio stability for living expenses. She had no prior cryptocurrency experience and did not request crypto exposure. Martinez did not conduct a written suitability analysis, did not document Margaret's investment objectives, and did not discuss the extreme volatility of Bitcoin (which had fluctuated ±40% in prior 12-month periods). The 20% allocation ($90,000) was purchased within one week of the recommendation.

QUESTION
--------
Did Advisor Martinez comply with Reg BI's care obligation and FINRA Suitability Rule 2111 in recommending a 20% Bitcoin allocation to this conservative elderly client?

ANSWER
------
No. Under Reg BI's care obligation (17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i)), an adviser must consider the client's age, financial situation, investment experience, and risk tolerance. Margaret, age 74, conservative risk tolerance, and zero crypto experience, presents an acute mismatch with a 20% Bitcoin allocation—an asset class exhibiting 40%+ annual volatility unsuitable for a retiree dependent on portfolio stability. FINRA Rule 2111 requires a reasonable basis for suitability before recommending a security. The absence of written suitability documentation and failure to address volatility risk constitutes negligence. SEC guidance (Reg BI Release, 85 FR 82510) and FINRA Notice 21-25 both emphasize that digital asset recommendations require heightened scrutiny for retail clients, particularly seniors.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Reg BI Care Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i); FINRA Rule 2111 Suitability

KEY FACTOR
----------
Recommending 20% Bitcoin to a 74-year-old conservative investor with no crypto experience and portfolio-dependence needs violates the care obligation due to extreme volatility mismatch with client's age and risk profile.
