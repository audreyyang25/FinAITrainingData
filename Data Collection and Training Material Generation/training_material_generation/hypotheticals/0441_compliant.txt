FACTS
-----
Patricia, age 74, retired teacher with $420,000 in savings and $1,800/month pension, approaches Advisor Lee. Patricia's daughter Sarah is named as trusted contact per FINRA Rule 4512; Patricia confirmed this arrangement in writing. Patricia's goals: preserve capital, generate $2,200/month income supplement (combined with pension = $4,000 total), maintain 3-year emergency reserve ($54,000 liquid). Lee recommends: $100,000 short-term bonds (2-year ladder, 4.5% YTM); $150,000 dividend-aristocrat ETF (3.2% yield, low turnover, 0.08% ER); $120,000 municipal bond fund (tax-exempt, 3.8% YTM); $50,000 FDIC-insured money market (emergency reserve). Total expected income: $2,240/month; portfolio volatility: 8% annual standard deviation; total annual cost: $340 (0.08% of AUM). Lee documents suitability analysis: age-appropriate risk, income goal matched to withdrawals, liquidity preserved. Six months later, Patricia calls saying her health may decline; Lee immediately reviews and proposes no changes (income strategy remains appropriate). In year 2, a market dip occurs; Sarah (trusted contact) calls Lee expressing concern. Lee proactively reassures Sarah, explains the strategy's resilience, and confirms Patricia remains satisfied.

QUESTION
--------
Did Advisor Lee comply with FINRA Rule 4512 (trusted contact) and Reg BI Care Obligation in managing Patricia's account?

ANSWER
------
Yes. Compliance is demonstrated by: (1) Rule 4512 - Lee documented establishment of trusted contact (Sarah) and obtained Patricia's written confirmation, satisfying the rule's requirement to 'identify, obtain contact information for, and document consent' by the customer. (2) Reg BI Care Obligation - Lee conducted reasonable inquiry (age 74, fixed income need, health uncertainties) and recommended portfolio aligned with stated goals: income target ($2,240) matches Patricia's stated need; liquidity preserved (20% in liquid reserves); volatility appropriate for retiree (8% σ); cost low (0.08% ER + advisory = <0.09% all-in). (3) Rule 4512 usage - when Sarah (trusted contact) raised concerns post-decline, Lee appropriately communicated with her while respecting Patricia's autonomy. (4) Ongoing monitoring - Lee reviewed strategy when Patricia disclosed health change, demonstrating vigilant care. SEC Senior Investor Report emphasizes that trusted contact designation + low-cost age-appropriate recommendations + proactive communication constitute best practices. No suitability red flags (high cost, illiquidity, concentration risk, poor fit) are present.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 4512 (Trusted Contact); Reg BI Care Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i); SEC Senior Investor Report 2018

KEY FACTOR
----------
Advisor documented trusted contact consent, recommended age-appropriate low-cost portfolio matching stated income and liquidity needs, and proactively managed account when life circumstances changed.
