Source Folder: 11_Case_Law
Topic: reg_bi_litigation
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Marcus Webb, a registered representative at Pinnacle Securities, recommends that his client Dorothy Chen, a 72-year-old retired schoolteacher with $285,000 in savings and a moderate risk tolerance, invest $190,000 (67% of her liquid assets) into a non-traded REIT with a 7-year lockup period, a 12% upfront commission, and limited liquidity provisions. Dorothy's stated investment objective is capital preservation with income to supplement her pension and Social Security. Webb earns a $22,800 commission on the transaction. Webb does not discuss alternative income-producing investments such as investment-grade bond funds or dividend-paying equity funds, and the transaction documentation contains no written rationale for why this concentrated, illiquid position serves Dorothy's stated objectives.

QUESTION
--------
Does Marcus Webb's recommendation of a concentrated, illiquid non-traded REIT to a retired, income-oriented client comply with Regulation Best Interest's Care Obligation?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is not compliant. Webb's recommendation violates the Reg BI Care Obligation by failing to exercise reasonable diligence and placing his compensation interest ahead of the customer's investment profile.

The Care Obligation requires a broker-dealer to exercise reasonable diligence, care, and skill in making a recommendation, considering the customer's investment profile including age, financial situation, liquidity needs, risk tolerance, and objectives. Recommending a 7-year lockup illiquid product consuming 67% of a 72-year-old retiree's liquid savings directly contradicts her capital preservation and income objectives. The $22,800 commission and failure to consider reasonable alternatives indicate that compensation incentives drove the recommendation rather than the customer's best interest. The absence of any documented rationale further demonstrates a failure of the required diligence.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Care Obligation; Exchange Act § 15(l)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Recommending an illiquid, high-commission product consuming the majority of a retired client's liquid assets, when her stated objective is capital preservation and income, is a clear violation of the Reg BI Care Obligation.
