FACTS
-----
A New York-registered independent broker-dealer and its adviser advise a 68-year-old Manhattan widow with $890,000 in liquid assets, $1.2M in a 401(k) rollover IRA (governed by ERISA/DOL fiduciary rules via PTE 2020-02), and stable $65,000 annual pension. The client's stated goal: 'income with inflation protection and minimal fees.' The BD recommends: (1) a $400,000 diversified dividend aristocrat ETF portfolio (0.08% expense ratio, 3.2% yield); (2) TIPS ladder of $200,000 (Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities, 0.00% fees); and (3) $150,000 in a low-cost variable annuity with 0.65% annual M&E, no surrender charge, and 3.8% income guarantee (addressing longevity risk). Total compensation: $3,200 (0.36% AUM fee) from the taxable account and no commission on the IRA rollover advice (acknowledging DOL fiduciary duty). The BD explicitly documents: (a) suitability under NY Reg 187; (b) analysis of why the variable annuity's longevity guarantee serves the client better than straight investment alternatives despite the 0.65% fee; (c) disclosure that the BD earns no commission on IRA advice; and (d) explanation that the strategy balances NY Reg 187's best interest standard with DOL fiduciary requirements across multiple account types.

QUESTION
--------
Does the BD's multi-account recommendation comply with both NY Reg 187 and DOL fiduciary standards across the taxable account and IRA rollover?

ANSWER
------
Yes. The BD demonstrates compliance with both NY Reg 187 (for the taxable account) and DOL fiduciary standards (for the IRA rollover under PTE 2020-02) by: (1) recommending low-cost, diversified solutions aligned with stated income and inflation-protection goals; (2) explicitly analyzing why a 0.65% variable annuity addresses longevity risk better than pure investment alternatives, satisfying NY Reg 187's comparative analysis requirement; (3) disclosing that it earns no compensation on IRA advice, thus avoiding prohibited transaction risk under DOL rules; and (4) documenting suitability across both accounts with acknowledgment of different legal standards. The strategy prioritizes client benefit (low-cost core investments, inflation protection, longevity insurance) while managing conflicts. This approach satisfies NY's heightened best interest standard and DOL's fiduciary duty by treating the client's entire financial picture holistically.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NY Regulation 187 § 227.2(a) and DOL Fiduciary Rule (PTE 2020-02) — convergence on best interest and fiduciary standards across account types

KEY FACTOR
----------
Explicitly documenting suitability under both NY Reg 187 and DOL fiduciary standards, with transparent disclosure of compensation across account types, satisfies multi-jurisdictional fiduciary obligations.
