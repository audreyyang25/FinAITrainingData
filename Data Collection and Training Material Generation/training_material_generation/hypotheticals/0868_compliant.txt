Source Folder: 18_OCC_Bank_Trust_Guidance
Topic: trust_investment_management
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Heritage Federal Savings Bank serves as trustee of a $3.2 million charitable remainder unitrust (CRUT) established by retired physician Dr. Anand Krishnamurthy, age 67, with a state university as remainder beneficiary. The bank's trust investment committee, following a thorough review of the trust document and Dr. Krishnamurthy's stated income objectives, approves a portfolio allocation of 55% equities, 30% investment-grade fixed income, and 15% in the bank's proprietary balanced mutual fund, which carries a 0.62% expense ratio. Before executing the allocation, the bank provides Dr. Krishnamurthy and the university's planned giving officer with a written conflict-of-interest disclosure identifying the proprietary fund, the bank's financial interest in its selection, a comparison to three comparable third-party funds ranging from 0.48% to 0.71% expense ratios, and a written statement that the investment committee independently determined the proprietary fund's risk-adjusted performance history supports its inclusion. Both parties execute written consent forms, and the decision is documented in trust committee minutes.

QUESTION
--------
Does the bank's inclusion of its proprietary mutual fund in the CRUT portfolio, supported by documented conflict disclosure, comparative cost analysis, independent investment committee review, and written beneficiary consent, comply with OCC fiduciary conflict-of-interest standards?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

OCC fiduciary standards permit a bank trustee to invest in affiliated products where the conflict is fully disclosed, the affected parties provide informed written consent, and the trustee independently documents that the investment serves the trust's purposes on terms comparable to alternatives. Here, the bank satisfied each element: it identified and quantified the conflict, provided a comparative cost analysis demonstrating the proprietary fund's fees were within the market range, conducted an independent investment committee review of performance history, and obtained written consent from both the income beneficiary and remainder beneficiary. The documented process reflects exactly the authorization framework contemplated by OCC conflict-of-interest guidance.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
OCC Comptroller's Handbook – Conflicts of Interest (AM-CI), Section: Self-Dealing Between Related Parties and Interests and Fiduciary Accounts; 12 CFR 9.12(c) (Exemptions with Consent); Common Law Duty of Loyalty

KEY FACTOR
----------
Full written disclosure of the affiliated-product conflict, a documented comparative cost and performance analysis, independent committee review, and informed written consent from all affected parties rendered an otherwise conflicted transaction permissible under OCC fiduciary standards.
