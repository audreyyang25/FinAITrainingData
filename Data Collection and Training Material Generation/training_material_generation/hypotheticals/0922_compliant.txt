Topic: compliant_conflict_disclosure_and_consent
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Registered investment adviser Meridian Capital Advisors recommended a proprietary mutual fund to clients after disclosing in writing, at the point of recommendation, that Meridian's parent company manages the fund and that Meridian receives a 0.25% revenue-sharing payment from the fund's distributor. The disclosure letter quantified the dollar impact for each client's proposed investment, explained alternatives available at lower cost, and obtained signed informed-consent acknowledgments before executing any transactions. Meridian's compliance officer conducted quarterly reviews of all proprietary product recommendations and documented that the funds were competitively priced relative to peer products with equivalent risk profiles.

QUESTION
--------
Did Meridian Capital Advisors satisfy its fiduciary disclosure obligations under the Investment Advisers Act when recommending its proprietary fund?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Section 206(2) fiduciary obligations are satisfied when an adviser makes full, fair, and specific disclosure of material conflicts in a manner that allows clients to provide meaningful informed consent before any transaction is executed. Meridian's point-of-recommendation written disclosure, which quantified the revenue-sharing amount and presented lower-cost alternatives, meets the standard articulated in SEC guidance and ALJ decisions requiring that disclosures be understandable and actionable rather than generic. The quarterly supervisory review further demonstrates good-faith compliance infrastructure consistent with an adviser's ongoing fiduciary obligations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act §206(2); SEC Guidance Update 2019-02 (Form CRS and conflict disclosure best practices)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Specific, quantified, point-of-recommendation conflict disclosure with documented informed consent satisfies the §206(2) fiduciary standard.
