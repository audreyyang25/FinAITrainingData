Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Bridgewater Capital Services executes a block trade of investment-grade corporate bonds on behalf of Lakeshore Pension Fund, an institutional account with $450 million in assets under management. The fund's investment committee has provided Bridgewater with a signed Institutional Suitability Certificate pursuant to FINRA Rule 2111.07 and FINRA Rule 4512(c), affirming that Lakeshore (1) is capable of independently evaluating investment risks both generally and with regard to specific transactions, and (2) will exercise independent judgment in evaluating Bridgewater's recommendations. Bridgewater's representative discussed the credit quality, duration risk, and yield characteristics of the bonds with Lakeshore's portfolio manager before executing the trade. The certificate is on file and remains current, with Lakeshore having confirmed within the past 12 months that nothing in the certificate has changed.

QUESTION
--------
Has Bridgewater satisfied its customer-specific suitability obligation under FINRA Rule 2111 for the institutional account recommendation?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Under FINRA Rule 2111.07, the customer-specific suitability obligation for institutional accounts is modified such that a broker-dealer fulfills its obligation if it has a reasonable basis to believe the institutional customer is capable of independently evaluating investment risks and the customer affirmatively indicates it will exercise independent judgment. The Institutional Suitability Certificate on file — consistent with the industry template referenced in FINRA guidance — satisfies this affirmative indication requirement. Bridgewater also engaged in substantive discussion with the portfolio manager regarding the transaction's characteristics, further supporting a reasonable basis belief in the institution's independent evaluation capacity.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111.07 – Institutional Suitability; FINRA Rule 4512(c) – Institutional Account Definition

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm obtained and maintained a current Institutional Suitability Certificate confirming the customer's capability and willingness to exercise independent judgment, satisfying the modified institutional suitability standard.
