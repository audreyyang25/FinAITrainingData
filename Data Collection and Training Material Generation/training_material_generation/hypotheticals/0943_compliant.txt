Topic: suitability_analysis_complex_product_institutional_client
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Senior registered representative Daniel Park recommended a portfolio allocation to inverse volatility exchange-traded products to a corporate treasury department that had provided written acknowledgment of its status as an institutional account under FINRA Rule 4512(c), confirmed its own independent review capability, and affirmatively indicated it did not require Park's suitability determination. Park documented the institutional client's written representations, provided detailed product disclosure materials including the products' daily reset mechanics and volatility decay characteristics, and retained copies of all communications pursuant to his firm's WSPs. The client's investment committee independently reviewed and approved the allocation after consulting with its own outside investment advisors.

QUESTION
--------
Did Park's recommendation of inverse volatility products to this institutional client satisfy FINRA's suitability and conduct obligations?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Rule 2111(b) provides that the customer-specific suitability obligation is modified for institutional clients when the institution affirmatively indicates it is exercising independent judgment and does not rely on the registered representative's suitability determination, shifting responsibility for the investment decision to the institution itself. Park's documentation of the written representations, delivery of detailed product disclosures addressing the specific risk characteristics of inverse volatility products, and retention of all communications satisfied both the institutional suitability safe harbor and his obligations under Rule 2010 and 4511. The client's use of independent outside advisors further reinforces the legitimacy of the institutional client exception.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111(b) (institutional suitability); FINRA Rule 2010; FINRA Regulatory Notice 12-25 (complex products); FINRA Rule 4511

KEY FACTOR
----------
The properly documented institutional client exception under Rule 2111(b), combined with comprehensive product disclosure and independent client decision-making, satisfies all applicable suitability and conduct of business standards.
