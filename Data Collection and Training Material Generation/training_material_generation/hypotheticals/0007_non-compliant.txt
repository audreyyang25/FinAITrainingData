Source Folder: unknown
Topic: reg_bi_care
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A 68-year-old retired investor with $450,000 in liquid savings and a stated moderate risk tolerance is recommended to invest $200,000 in a non-traded REIT with a 7% upfront sales load and 2% annual management fee. The advisor documents no analysis of lower-cost traded REIT alternatives (e.g., VGSLX at 0.12% expense ratio) or bond/equity ETF combinations that would provide similar diversification at a fraction of the cost.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation satisfy the Reg BI care obligation to consider costs, risks, rewards, and alternatives?

ANSWER
------
No, this violates the care obligation under Reg BI §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i).

The advisor failed to document consideration of substantially lower-cost alternatives (a 6.88% cost differential on upfront fees plus 1.88% annual ongoing fee difference). Non-traded REITs carry liquidity risk and the advisor provided no comparative analysis of costs or risks versus traded alternatives. For a 68-year-old with moderate risk tolerance, the 7% load is particularly difficult to justify given the availability of low-cost traded vehicles. Under Reg BI's care obligation, the recommendation must demonstrate that the product was suitable relative to available alternatives; this fact pattern suggests the advisor prioritized commission (likely 5.5% of the load) over the client's interests.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Regulation Best Interest §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i) — care obligation to consider the costs, risks, rewards, and alternatives in light of the retail customer's profile.

KEY FACTOR
----------
Complete absence of documentation comparing costs and alternatives; 6.88% upfront cost difference with no reasoned analysis of why the higher-cost product was in the client's best interest.
