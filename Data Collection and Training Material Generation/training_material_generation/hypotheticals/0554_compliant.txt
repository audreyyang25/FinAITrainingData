Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Apex Securities, LLC, a mid-size FINRA member broker-dealer with 85 registered representatives, receives revenue-sharing payments from three mutual fund families whose products appear on the firm's recommended list. Under its Reg BI conflict of interest policies, the firm (1) fully discloses the revenue-sharing arrangements and the specific basis-point amounts in its Reg BI disclosure document delivered to all retail customers prior to any recommendation, (2) has eliminated sales contests and differential compensation tied to the revenue-sharing fund families, (3) maintains a product governance committee that independently evaluates whether revenue-sharing products remain on the recommended list based solely on performance, cost, and suitability criteria, and (4) conducts quarterly compliance reviews comparing recommendation patterns to ensure representatives are not disproportionately steering customers toward revenue-sharing products. A 68-year-old retiree with $400,000 in savings and a moderate risk profile is recommended a diversified allocation including a revenue-sharing fund, but the representative documents that the fund was selected because it had the lowest expense ratio (0.45%) and strongest 5-year risk-adjusted return in its Morningstar category.

QUESTION
--------
Does the firm's receipt of revenue-sharing payments from mutual fund families whose products are recommended to retail customers violate the Conflict of Interest Obligation under Reg BI?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The Conflict of Interest Obligation does not prohibit all conflicts; rather, it requires firms to establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to identify, disclose, and mitigate—or in some cases eliminate—conflicts of interest associated with recommendations to retail customers. Apex has implemented a robust framework: full disclosure of revenue-sharing amounts, elimination of compensation incentives tied to conflicted products, independent product governance review, and ongoing compliance monitoring. The specific recommendation was documented as based on objective merit (lowest expense ratio, best risk-adjusted return), demonstrating that the conflict was effectively mitigated rather than driving the recommendation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iii) – Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm identified, disclosed, and mitigated the revenue-sharing conflict through comprehensive policies, eliminated compensation incentives tied to conflicted products, and documented that the specific recommendation was driven by objective product merit rather than the conflict.
