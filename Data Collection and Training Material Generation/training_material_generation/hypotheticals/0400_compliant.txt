FACTS
-----
A multi-state registered investment adviser (registered in NY, MA, CT, NJ, and VA) serving clients across all five states has adopted a compliance policy that applies the highest applicable state fiduciary standard to all clients, regardless of domicile. The policy explicitly references NY Regulation 187 (best-interest, strict conflict rules), Massachusetts fiduciary rule (broker-dealer fiduciary duty for all client interactions), and Nevada NRS 645A.240 (planner fiduciary duty). For a client in Stamford, CT, with $520,000 in investable assets, the adviser recommends a diversified portfolio of low-cost index ETFs (blended cost: 0.12%), documents the recommendation in writing with specific reference to each applicable state standard, provides an updated Form CRS identifying the adviser's fiduciary status under MA, NY, and NJ law, and discloses all conflicts (none material in this case). Annual fee: 0.85% flat. The adviser turns over only 6% annually and maintains soft-dollar arrangements limited to research-only services with full disclosure.

QUESTION
--------
Does the adviser's multi-state approach, applying the highest state standard to all clients, comply with state fiduciary rules?

ANSWER
------
Yes. The adviser's compliance strategy—applying the most stringent state standard across all jurisdictions—is compliant and best-practice. By adopting NY Reg 187, MA fiduciary rule, and Nevada NRS 645A.240 for all clients, the adviser ensures no client falls below the highest applicable standard. This approach: (1) Eliminates confusion about which state rule applies; (2) Protects the adviser from conflicting state requirements; (3) Provides uniform, client-protective treatment; (4) Complies with NASAA Model Rule principles (transparency, conflict disclosure, best-interest obligation); (5) Exceeds federal Reg BI baseline; (6) Demonstrates reasonable care under all state laws. The specific implementation—low-cost index strategy, written best-interest documentation, conflict disclosure in Form CRS, low turnover, transparent fees—satisfies each state's highest standard. Courts and regulators favor 'race to the top' compliance strategies in multi-state contexts. This adviser demonstrates compliance sophistication.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NY Regulation 187 § 227.0(b), MA Fiduciary Rule, NJ Fiduciary Rule, Nevada NRS 645A.240, NASAA Model Rule principles

KEY FACTOR
----------
Proactive adoption of highest-applicable state standard across all clients, combined with transparent documentation and low-cost strategy, demonstrates compliance with multi-state fiduciary obligations.
