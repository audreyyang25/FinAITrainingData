ID: 215
Source Folder: unknown
Topic: reg_bi_care_expanded
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A hybrid robo-advisor platform (algorithm + human advisor oversight) recommends a $120,000 allocation to a non-traded REIT (Inland Western Retail Properties) to a 44-year-old client with $380,000 in liquid savings and a stated 5-year time horizon. The platform's algorithm flags illiquidity risk but outputs a 'BUY' based on a projected 8.5% annual return. The human advisor, assigned after algorithm recommendation, does not override the algorithm. Client receives a boilerplate disclosure stating 'non-traded REITs involve liquidity constraints and are illiquid investments' but the advisor does not communicate the tension between a 5-year time horizon and a typical 7-10 year REIT hold recommendation. The advisor's documented review consists of checking a box: 'reviewed algorithm output—NO OVERRIDE.' The REIT has a 7% distribution fee and 1.2% ongoing expense ratio.

QUESTION
--------
Did the advisor comply with Reg BI's care obligation (including reasonable basis and conflicts-of-interest prongs) when recommending a non-traded REIT with a 5-year time horizon?

ANSWER
------
Non-compliant on multiple grounds. First, the advisor failed to establish reasonable basis: the algorithm's projection of 8.5% return and the human advisor's passive reliance on algorithm output without independent analysis of liquidity mismatch violates the care standard. Reg BI requires the advisor to exercise independent judgment and document a reasonable basis, not defer to an algorithm. Second, the 5-year time horizon is materially misaligned with typical non-traded REIT holding periods (7-10 years), creating liquidity risk the boilerplate disclosure does not adequately address. Third, the 7% distribution fee + 1.2% ER = 8.2% total cost creates conflicts of interest (advisor/platform may benefit from AUM-based fees on the REIT investment); these were not disclosed or mitigated. The passive checkbox review demonstrates failure to exercise care. Non-traded REIT recommendations require heightened care because of illiquidity—a point the algorithm flagged but the advisor ignored.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Reg BI Care Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i), and Conflict-of-Interest Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(ii); duty to exercise independent judgment and document reasonable basis

KEY FACTOR
----------
Advisor's passive reliance on algorithm output without independent analysis of the material mismatch between 5-year time horizon and non-traded REIT's 7-10 year typical hold breaches care obligation.
