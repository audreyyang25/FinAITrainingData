Source Folder: 04_RegBI_Compliance_Guidance
Topic: reg_bi_compliance
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Murray-Briggs Securities, a small FINRA member firm in East Texas with four registered representatives, has been operating since June 30, 2020 without establishing or maintaining written supervisory procedures specifically addressing Regulation Best Interest obligations. The firm conducts a general securities business with approximately 200 retail customer accounts, recommending mutual funds, variable annuities, and individual equities. The firm's compliance manual has not been updated since 2018 and contains no references to Reg BI's Care, Conflict of Interest, or Disclosure Obligations. When FINRA examined the firm in 2023, the CCO stated that the firm 'followed suitability standards which are basically the same thing.'

QUESTION
--------
Did the firm satisfy its obligations under Regulation Best Interest's Compliance Obligation by continuing to apply pre-existing suitability procedures without adopting Reg BI-specific written policies and supervisory systems?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates the Reg BI Compliance Obligation.

The Compliance Obligation under Reg BI requires broker-dealers to establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with the Care, Conflict of Interest, and Disclosure Obligations. Retaining pre-Reg BI suitability procedures without updating them to address the heightened Reg BI standards—including the obligation to consider reasonably available alternatives—fails this requirement. This mirrors the Murray Securities AWC (No. 2021069350301), where FINRA sanctioned a small Texas firm for failing to establish and maintain a supervisory system and written policies and procedures addressing Reg BI since its June 30, 2020 effective date.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iv) – Reg BI Compliance Obligation; FINRA Rules 3110(a) and 3110(b) – Supervisory System and Written Supervisory Procedures

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm failed to establish any written supervisory procedures addressing Reg BI obligations after the rule's June 30, 2020 compliance date, relying solely on outdated suitability standards.
