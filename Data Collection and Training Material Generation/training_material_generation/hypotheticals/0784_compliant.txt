Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Clearview Financial Planning, LLC is a newly formed investment advisory firm in Michigan with $12 million in AUM and 30 clients. Before commencing business, the firm's principal, Angela Torres, completed all six steps required for state registration: she created an IARD account, funded the daily account, completed Form ADV Parts 1 and 2, submitted Form ADV through the IARD system, registered all investment adviser representatives with required examinations (Series 65), and submitted the complete application package with the applicable state filing fee. Torres also filed notice of custody on Form ADV Item 9 because the firm has authority to deduct fees from client accounts. The firm was examined for the first time by Michigan regulators during the 2025 NASAA coordinated examination cycle, which ran from February 1 through July 31, 2025. The examination revealed no deficiencies.

QUESTION
--------
Is Clearview Financial Planning's registration and operational setup compliant with state securities law and NASAA model rules?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Torres followed the required registration steps as outlined in state registration guidance, including completing and filing Form ADV, registering representatives, and properly disclosing custody on Form ADV. The 2025 NASAA coordinated examination program, which examined 1,086 investment advisers between February 1 and July 31, 2025—with 365 of those being first-time examinations—confirmed Clearview's compliance. The firm's proactive disclosure of custody authority on Form ADV satisfies NASAA Model Rule 102(e)(1)-1(a)(1). No deficiencies were found.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
State investment adviser registration requirements; NASAA coordinated examination standards; California DFPI registration guidance (analogous Michigan provisions); NASAA Model Rule 102(e)(1)-1(a)(1) custody notice requirement

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm completed all required registration steps, properly disclosed custody, and passed its first state coordinated examination without deficiencies.
