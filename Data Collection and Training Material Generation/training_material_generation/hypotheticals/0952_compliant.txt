Topic: compliant_discovery_response_in_finra_arbitration
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
In a FINRA customer arbitration involving alleged unsuitability in a leveraged ETF portfolio, claimant's counsel submitted a timely and properly formatted discovery request seeking the broker's internal suitability assessment documents, supervisory exception reports, and all customer communications related to the account over a 24-month period. Respondent brokerage firm, through outside counsel, produced all responsive documents within the 30-day production period specified by the FINRA arbitration panel's scheduling order, provided a privilege log for the three documents withheld on attorney-client privilege grounds, and filed a declaration of completeness attesting that the production was comprehensive. The firm did not seek any extensions and proactively supplemented the production when additional responsive emails were identified.

QUESTION
--------
Did the respondent brokerage firm's discovery response comply with FINRA Code of Arbitration Procedure requirements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

FINRA Rules 12507 and 12508 require parties to produce documents listed in the Discovery Guide and to respond to additional document requests within timeframes established by the arbitration panel, with privilege assertions supported by a privilege log identifying withheld documents. The firm's timely full production, properly formatted privilege log, declaration of completeness, and voluntary supplementation all reflect compliance with both the letter and spirit of FINRA's discovery rules. PIABA's concerns about discovery abuse focus on delay tactics, incomplete productions, and bad-faith privilege assertions—none of which are present in this fact pattern.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Code of Arbitration Procedure Rules 12507, 12508, 12509; FINRA Discovery Guide (Customer and Industry Codes)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm's complete, timely, and transparent production with a proper privilege log fully satisfied FINRA Code of Arbitration Procedure discovery obligations.
