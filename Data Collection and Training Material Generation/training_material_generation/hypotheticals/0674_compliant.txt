Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Ridgeline Advisors, a dual-registered broker-dealer and investment adviser, recommends that David Patel, a 45-year-old executive earning $350,000 annually with $1.5 million in investable assets and a moderate-to-aggressive risk profile, roll over $400,000 from his former employer's 401(k) plan into a fee-based advisory IRA. The new IRA charges a 1.10% annual wrap fee and holds a diversified portfolio of domestic and international equity ETFs with average expense ratios of 0.08%. Ridgeline documents a detailed cost comparison showing the 401(k) plan charged 0.95% in combined administrative and fund expense fees with limited investment options and no personalized advice. The firm also documents its consideration of keeping funds in the 401(k), rolling to a brokerage IRA, or rolling to the advisory IRA, and concludes the advisory IRA best serves the client's stated goals of consolidated account management and ongoing tax-loss harvesting. The Form CRS and Reg BI disclosures were delivered at the time of the rollover recommendation.

QUESTION
--------
Does the rollover recommendation from a 401(k) to a fee-based advisory IRA comply with Regulation Best Interest given the slightly higher all-in cost?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Regulation BI does not require the lowest-cost option; it requires that the recommendation be in the retail customer's best interest after evaluating costs, benefits, and reasonably available alternatives. Here, the firm documented a thorough cost comparison, considered multiple alternatives including staying in the plan, and identified specific value-added services (personalized advice, tax-loss harvesting, consolidated management) that justify the marginal cost differential. The timely delivery of Form CRS and Reg BI disclosures further supports compliance. The SEC's Staff Bulletins on account-type recommendations and rollovers specifically contemplate this type of documented analysis.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i)-(ii) – Regulation Best Interest Care and Disclosure Obligations; SEC Staff Bulletin on Account Recommendations (Standards of Conduct)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm's documented cost-benefit analysis and consideration of reasonably available alternatives—including non-rollover options—demonstrate that the slightly higher cost was justified by additional advisory services aligned with the client's needs.
