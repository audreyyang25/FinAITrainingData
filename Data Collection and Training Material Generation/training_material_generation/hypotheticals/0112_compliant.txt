FACTS
-----
A 72-year-old retired client with $420,000 in a traditional IRA, highly risk-averse (stated risk tolerance: 'conservative'), poor health (life expectancy estimated at 8-10 years), and a strong need for guaranteed lifetime income seeks a recommendation. The advisor recommends a variable annuity with an immediate lifetime income rider (income guarantee 4.5% of purchase price guaranteed for life), M&E charges of 1.25%, sub-account fees of 0.65%, and a 5-year surrender charge declining from 5% to 0%. The advisor documents that: (1) the guaranteed income rider directly addresses the client's stated need for lifetime income security; (2) the total cost (1.9%) is reasonable for a guaranty rider product; (3) the client's short time horizon and conservative risk tolerance justify the income-focused structure; (4) the IRA context means ordinary income tax treatment applies regardless, so tax deferral is not the driver; (5) the client approved the surrender charge in writing.

QUESTION
--------
Does this variable annuity recommendation satisfy FINRA Rule 2330 and Reg BI?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this is compliant.

The recommendation is suitable under Rule 2330 because the advisor has a reasonable basis to believe the variable annuity is suitable for this client based on documented facts: (1) The client's stated investment objective (guaranteed lifetime income) aligns directly with the product's benefit (immediate income rider); (2) The client's age (72), risk tolerance (conservative), and health status (short life expectancy) support an income-focused annuity over growth-oriented securities; (3) The fee structure is reasonable for an income guaranty product (1.9% total, not excessive); (4) The IRA custodial context eliminates the tax-deferral argument, so the recommendation rests on suitability of the income guarantee itself, not tax arbitrage; (5) The advisor documented the suitability analysis and obtained written acknowledgment of surrender charges. Under Reg BI, the advisor acted in the client's best interest by recommending a product that directly addresses stated needs (lifetime income security) and is proportionate in cost to the guaranty benefit received. This scenario tracks favorably with FINRA Best Practice guidance on variable annuity suitability (e.g., in AWC settlement FINRA Case #2016051857, advisor suitability was found where income rider directly matched documented client objective).

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2330 (Variable Annuity Suitability); Reg BI duty of best interest; SEC Variable Annuity Suitability Guidance (2012); FINRA Rule 4512 (supervision and documentation)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Detailed documentation that the variable annuity's income rider directly satisfies the client's stated need for guaranteed lifetime income, combined with reasonable fees (1.9%), conservative client profile (age 72, risk-averse, short life expectancy), and written acknowledgment of terms, all of which demonstrate suitability and best interest.
