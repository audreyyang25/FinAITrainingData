FACTS
-----
Marcus Williams, age 38, explicitly told his financial advisor, 'I want my investments to reflect my values—no fossil fuels, no weapons manufacturers.' His advisor recommended a $420,000 allocation: 50% Vanguard ESG U.S. Stock (0.09% ER), 30% iShares MSCI ACWX ESG Select (0.20% ER), 20% Parnassus Core Equity Fund (0.76% ER). The advisor provided a written recommendation memo documenting: (1) Marcus's stated ESG preference with specific exclusions; (2) ESG fund performance data for the past 10 years (annualized return 9.2% vs. S&P 500 9.5%); (3) fee comparison showing the portfolio's blended ER of 0.24% vs. a conventional 0.08% benchmark; (4) statement: 'Higher fees reflect active management in ESG screening; you have accepted this trade-off to align with values'; (5) suitability analysis confirming Marcus's 27-year horizon and moderate risk tolerance support equity allocation. The advisor obtained Marcus's signed acknowledgment of the fee differential and ESG performance data.

QUESTION
--------
Did the advisor comply with Reg BI, the duty of loyalty, and ESG marketing disclosure obligations in recommending this ESG-focused portfolio?

ANSWER
------
Compliant. The advisor satisfied Reg BI §240.15l-1(a)(2) by: (1) documenting the client's explicit ESG preference; (2) conducting a reasonable investigation (10-year performance comparison, fee analysis); (3) disclosing material costs and benefits in writing; (4) obtaining client acknowledgment; and (5) demonstrating the recommendation is in the client's best interest given his stated values and time horizon. The duty of loyalty under Investment Adviser Act §206(1) is satisfied because the advisor prioritized the client's stated preferences (values-aligned investing) over maximum returns, with full transparency. The SEC Marketing Rule (IA-5653 §206(4)-1) compliance is met: ESG claims are supported by 10-year track record, and the advisor did not misrepresent ESG fund performance (noted the small underperformance vs. S&P 500, which is material). The documented preference eliminates the risk of the 'trending' recommendation pattern.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Reg BI Care Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i)-(ii); Investment Adviser Act §206(1) (duty of loyalty); SEC Marketing Rule IA-5653 §206(4)-1(a)(1) (substantiation of ESG claims).

KEY FACTOR
----------
Advisor documented explicit client ESG preference, disclosed material fee differential and performance comparison, and obtained written acknowledgment—satisfying both care and loyalty obligations.
