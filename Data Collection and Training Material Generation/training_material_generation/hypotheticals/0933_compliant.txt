Topic: suitability_documentation_conservative_portfolio
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Registered representative Camille Tran recommended a laddered bond portfolio consisting of investment-grade municipal bonds with maturities ranging from two to ten years to a 66-year-old retired physician who documented a conservative risk tolerance, a $1.2 million investment portfolio, no immediate liquidity needs beyond a separately maintained cash reserve, and a primary objective of tax-advantaged income generation. Before making the recommendation, Tran conducted a comprehensive suitability review using the firm's updated new account form, documented the client's tax bracket at 37%, confirmed the bonds were appropriate given state income tax exemption benefits, and presented a written rationale comparing three alternative portfolio structures. The client reviewed, acknowledged, and signed the recommendation rationale document prior to account funding.

QUESTION
--------
Did Tran's recommendation of a laddered municipal bond portfolio satisfy FINRA Rule 2111 suitability obligations?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Rule 2111 requires that recommendations be suitable based on reasonable diligence to understand the customer's investment profile, including financial situation, tax status, investment objectives, and risk tolerance; Tran satisfied all three suitability obligations — reasonable-basis, customer-specific, and quantitative — through documented analysis. Investment-grade municipal bonds are consistent with a conservative risk tolerance and align directly with the client's tax-advantaged income objective given her 37% marginal rate, and the written comparison of alternatives demonstrates that the recommendation was the product of genuine analysis rather than convenience. Documentation of the client's acknowledgment and the retention of records further satisfies Rule 4511 obligations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability); FINRA Rule 4511 (Books and Records)

KEY FACTOR
----------
A comprehensive documented suitability analysis, written recommendation rationale, and client acknowledgment demonstrate full compliance with Rule 2111's customer-specific suitability and Rule 4511's recordkeeping requirements.
