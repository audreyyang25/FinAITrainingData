ID: 592
Source Folder: 06_DOL_Fiduciary
Topic: pte_2020_02
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Ryan Gupta, a registered representative at Pinnacle Wealth Advisors, provides investment advice to IRA clients. Pinnacle has adopted written policies and procedures acknowledging its status as an investment advice fiduciary under PTE 2020-02 and requiring advisors to act in clients' best interest. However, Pinnacle's compliance department has not conducted any retrospective review of advisor recommendations for over 18 months. During this period, Gupta recommended fixed indexed annuities from a single carrier to 47 IRA clients ranging in age from 32 to 71, regardless of their individual risk profiles, time horizons, or liquidity needs. The annuity carries a 10-year surrender period with a 9% declining surrender charge and pays Gupta a 7% commission. Pinnacle's supervisory system flagged no alerts because the retrospective review function was never operationalized.

QUESTION
--------
Does Pinnacle Wealth Advisors' reliance on PTE 2020-02 satisfy the exemption's conditions regarding policies, procedures, and retrospective review?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of PTE 2020-02's requirements for written policies and procedures, including the obligation to conduct retrospective reviews to detect and prevent violations.

PTE 2020-02 conditions relief on the financial institution maintaining written policies and procedures that include a retrospective compliance review conducted at least annually to detect and prevent violations of the exemption's best interest standard. Pinnacle's failure to operationalize the retrospective review for 18 months means it cannot satisfy this condition. Furthermore, the pattern of recommending a single, high-commission, long-surrender annuity product to a broad range of clients without individualized analysis suggests a systemic failure to comply with the care obligation, which a functioning retrospective review should have identified.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
DOL PTE 2020-02 – Section II(d), Policies and Procedures; Section II(d)(3), Retrospective Review Requirement

KEY FACTOR
----------
The financial institution failed to conduct the required annual retrospective review under PTE 2020-02, allowing a pattern of unsuitable, conflict-driven recommendations to go undetected.
