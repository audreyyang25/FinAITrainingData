ID: 839
Source Folder: 17_SEC_Staff_Bulletins
Topic: conflict_mitigation
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A dually registered representative at a firm structured like Cambridge Investment Research recommends that 52-year-old physician Dr. Alan Park, who holds $1.2 million in advisory assets, move a discrete $75,000 new inheritance into a commission-based brokerage account to purchase individual municipal bonds rather than adding it to the fee-based advisory account. The representative completes a detailed Best Interest Analysis documenting that the municipal bonds carry a 0% sales load, generate tax-exempt income suitable for Dr. Park's 37% marginal tax bracket, and that the one-time transaction cost structure is more cost-effective than ongoing advisory fees of 0.85% annually on an asset class that requires no active management. The representative delivers Form CRS at the time of the new account opening, specifically discloses in writing that he earns a markup on the bond transaction, and documents in the file that the total markup is estimated at $562.50 (0.75% on $75,000), which is lower than the $637.50 in annual advisory fees that would have been charged in the fee-based account.

QUESTION
--------
Did the representative satisfy Reg BI's Care, Disclosure, and Conflict of Interest Obligations when recommending the commission-based brokerage account structure for Dr. Park's municipal bond purchase?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Reg BI does not prohibit commission-based accounts or recommendations of proprietary or higher-cost products per se; it requires that the recommendation be in the retail customer's best interest after considering costs, risks, and the customer's investment profile. Here, the representative conducted a documented cost comparison demonstrating that the brokerage account structure is objectively less expensive for this specific asset class and client tax situation, satisfying the Care Obligation. The material conflict — the markup — was specifically disclosed in writing with a dollar estimate, satisfying the Disclosure and Conflict of Interest Obligations. Form CRS was delivered at account opening, and the Best Interest Analysis was completed contemporaneously, meeting the procedural documentation standards identified in the SEC's Reg BI FAQ guidance.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i)(ii)(iii) – Reg BI Disclosure, Care, and Conflict of Interest Obligations

KEY FACTOR
----------
The representative satisfied all three core Reg BI obligations by documenting a genuine cost and suitability advantage to the client, specifically disclosing the markup conflict, and delivering Form CRS at account opening.
