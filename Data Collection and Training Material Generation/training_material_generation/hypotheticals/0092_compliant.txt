FACTS
-----
A FINRA broker recommends a series of municipal bond funds to a 62-year-old client in the 35% federal tax bracket, with $700,000 in investable assets, stated moderate income objectives, and residing in a high-income-tax state (California, 13.3% state rate). The broker recommends a portfolio of 40% California municipal bonds (6.2% tax-equivalent yield), 30% national municipal bonds (5.8% TEY), and 30% high-quality corporate bonds (4.2% yield). The broker documents the customer's tax situation, discusses tax efficiency, confirms understanding of municipal bond credit risk, and obtains written acknowledgment. The customer's 10-year time horizon and moderate risk tolerance are noted.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation satisfy FINRA Rule 2111 suitability?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this recommendation is compliant with FINRA Rule 2111 suitability requirements.

The broker satisfies all Rule 2111 suitability prongs. (1) Reasonable basis: municipal bonds are a recognized strategy for high-tax-bracket investors seeking tax-efficient income; the broker can rely on standard tax-efficiency analysis showing tax-equivalent yields of 6.2% for California munis versus 4.2% for corporate bonds in a 35% bracket. (2) Customer-specific suitability: the recommendation is tailored to a 62-year-old in the 35% tax bracket with state-level income tax exposure seeking moderate income; the allocation to California munis and national munis reflects the customer's specific state tax situation. (3) Documentation: the broker documents the customer's tax situation, yield analysis, and risk tolerance, and obtains written acknowledgment. (4) No quantitative violations: this is a buy-and-hold income strategy with no excessive trading. The broker's consideration of the customer's specific tax profile demonstrates customer-specific analysis required under Rule 2111(b).

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111(a) and (b): broker must have reasonable basis for recommendation and determine suitability based on customer's investment profile, including tax situation, income objectives, and risk tolerance.

KEY FACTOR
----------
Recommendation tailored to customer's specific tax bracket and state income tax situation, with documented tax-efficiency analysis.
