Topic: compliant_conflict_disclosure_and_consent
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Registered investment adviser Helena Voss managed a $45 million family office account and identified an opportunity in a private credit fund in which her firm held a 3% ownership interest. Before making any recommendation, Voss provided the client with a written conflict-of-interest disclosure specifically identifying the ownership interest, the nature of the economic benefit to the firm, and the potential for the recommendation to be influenced by that interest. The client acknowledged the disclosure in writing and provided informed consent. Voss also documented in the client file her independent investment thesis, comparable alternatives she considered, and her conclusion that the fund was consistent with the client's stated return objectives and liquidity needs.

QUESTION
--------
Did Voss's process for recommending the private credit fund satisfy her fiduciary obligations under the Investment Advisers Act?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

An investment adviser satisfies its §206 fiduciary duty with respect to conflicted transactions by making full and fair disclosure of all material conflicts and obtaining informed client consent, provided the underlying recommendation is otherwise consistent with the client's best interest. Voss's written transaction-specific disclosure, documented consideration of alternatives, and affirmative suitability analysis collectively demonstrate both the care and loyalty components of the fiduciary standard. The contemporaneous documentation further protects against an ALJ finding of negligence under §206(2).

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940 §206(1), §206(2); SEC Staff Bulletin on Standards of Conduct for Investment Advisers (April 2023)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Full written disclosure of the specific conflict, informed client consent, and a documented independent suitability analysis satisfy the §206 fiduciary standard for conflicted recommendations.
