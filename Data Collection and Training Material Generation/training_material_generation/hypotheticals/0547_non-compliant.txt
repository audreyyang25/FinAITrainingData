ID: 547
Source Folder: 04_RegBI_Compliance_Guidance
Topic: reg_bi_disclosure
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Regulus Capital Group, LLC, a FINRA member firm headquartered in Michigan with 70 registered representatives and 60 branch offices, primarily services retail investors in general securities products. From mid-2021 through early 2024, the firm's principal and a control affiliate each had multiple reportable disciplinary events, including customer complaints and regulatory actions, that were required to be disclosed on Form CRS. The firm filed an initial Form CRS in June 2020 but never updated it to reflect these subsequent disciplinary events. During this period, the firm distributed the outdated Form CRS to approximately 1,200 new retail customers and at account opening provided no supplemental disclosure of the firm's or its control affiliate's disciplinary history.

QUESTION
--------
Did the firm satisfy its Reg BI Disclosure Obligation and Form CRS requirements by distributing its original 2020 Form CRS without updating it to reflect material disciplinary events of the firm and its control affiliate?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of the Reg BI Disclosure Obligation and Form CRS filing and delivery requirements.

The Disclosure Obligation requires broker-dealers to provide retail customers, prior to or at the time of a recommendation, full and fair disclosure of all material facts relating to the scope and terms of the relationship, including disciplinary history. Form CRS General Instructions require firms to update the form within 30 days of any information becoming materially inaccurate and to communicate changes to existing customers. The Regulus Financial Group AWC (No. 2023077077001) specifically addresses a firm's failure to disclose its own and its control affiliate's disciplinary history on Form CRS over a multi-year period, resulting in FINRA sanctions.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation; Form CRS (17 CFR 249.2) – General Instructions, Item 9 (Disciplinary History)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm's failure to update and deliver an amended Form CRS reflecting its own and its control affiliate's disciplinary events over nearly three years deprived retail customers of material information required by the Disclosure Obligation.
