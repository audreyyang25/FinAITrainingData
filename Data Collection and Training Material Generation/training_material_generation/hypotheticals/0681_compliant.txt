Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Sarah Kim is a registered representative at a broker-dealer who recommends that Tom and Linda Patel, both age 48 with a combined household income of $275,000 and a long-term growth objective for retirement at age 65, invest $150,000 in a diversified portfolio of equity mutual funds across large-cap, mid-cap, and international categories, with a weighted-average expense ratio of 0.45%. Kim's firm offers both commission-based brokerage accounts and fee-based advisory accounts; after comparing total cost of ownership over the Patels' anticipated 17-year holding period and considering their preference for a buy-and-hold approach with infrequent trading, Kim recommends the commission-based brokerage account, which results in lower total costs. Kim documents her account-type analysis, delivers the firm's Form CRS to the Patels, and discloses that she will receive a one-time commission of $2,250. The firm's supervisory procedures require and confirm pre-trade review of account-type recommendations for retirement-oriented clients.

QUESTION
--------
Does Kim's recommendation of a commission-based brokerage account instead of a fee-based advisory account comply with Regulation Best Interest?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Regulation Best Interest requires that recommendations, including account-type recommendations, reflect reasonable diligence and be in the retail customer's best interest without placing the financial professional's interest ahead of the customer's. Kim performed a documented cost comparison demonstrating that the commission-based account would be less expensive over the Patels' long holding period given their buy-and-hold strategy, satisfying the Care Obligation's requirement to evaluate reasonably available alternatives. She also satisfied the Disclosure Obligation by delivering Form CRS and disclosing her compensation. The supervisory pre-review further supports the compliance infrastructure. The fact that Kim receives a commission does not per se violate Reg BI where the recommendation is supported by a reasonable basis analysis.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) and (ii) – Reg BI Disclosure and Care Obligations; Form CRS (17 CFR 249.2)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The representative documented a cost-of-ownership comparison showing the commission-based account was less expensive for the clients' buy-and-hold strategy, demonstrating a reasonable basis for the account-type recommendation.
