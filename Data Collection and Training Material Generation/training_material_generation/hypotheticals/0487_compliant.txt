Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Ashton Wealth Advisors, a dually registered investment adviser and broker-dealer, recommends that Margaret Chen, a 62-year-old pre-retiree with $1.2 million in retirement assets and a moderate risk tolerance, roll over her 401(k) into an IRA managed by Ashton's advisory platform. The advisory account charges a 1.05% annual wrap fee, which is lower than the 1.35% expense-weighted cost she was paying in her employer plan when accounting for sub-transfer agent fees and revenue sharing. Ashton's compliance department documented a comprehensive cost comparison, evaluated available investment options in the existing plan versus the IRA, considered the loss of creditor protections under ERISA, disclosed its financial interest in the rollover recommendation via its Form CRS and a written rollover-specific disclosure, and maintained records of the individualized analysis. The recommendation was reviewed and approved by the branch manager prior to execution.

QUESTION
--------
Did Ashton Wealth Advisors meet its fiduciary duty and Reg BI Care Obligation when recommending the IRA rollover to Margaret Chen?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Reg BI's Care Obligation requires that a recommendation be in the retail customer's best interest at the time it is made, considering reasonably available alternatives. Ashton conducted and documented a thorough cost comparison showing net savings, evaluated investment quality, considered the loss of ERISA protections, and disclosed its financial conflict. The SEC's final Reg BI release emphasizes that account-type recommendations such as rollovers require this type of individualized, documented analysis. The supervision and pre-approval by the branch manager further demonstrates reasonable policies and procedures.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940 fiduciary duty; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Release No. 34-86031 (Regulation Best Interest Final Rule)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm conducted and documented an individualized cost-benefit analysis including ERISA protections, disclosed its conflict of interest, and obtained supervisory pre-approval before recommending the rollover.
