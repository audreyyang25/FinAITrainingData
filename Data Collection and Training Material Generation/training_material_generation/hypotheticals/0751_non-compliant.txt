ID: 751
Source Folder: 13_FINRA_Rulebook
Topic: variable_annuity_rule
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A registered representative at a mid-sized broker-dealer recommends that Margaret Chen, a 78-year-old retired schoolteacher with $180,000 in liquid savings and a conservative risk tolerance, exchange her existing deferred variable annuity — which has exited its surrender period — for a new deferred variable annuity with a 7-year surrender schedule and an annual mortality and expense charge of 1.45%. The new annuity offers a guaranteed minimum income benefit rider that Margaret says she does not need because she already receives sufficient income from Social Security and a pension. The representative processes the exchange the same day the recommendation is made without forwarding the transaction to a registered principal for review. The branch's OSJ principal is on vacation and no designated alternate reviewer has been assigned.

QUESTION
--------
Did the representative and the firm comply with FINRA Rule 2330 requirements for the recommended deferred variable annuity exchange?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates FINRA Rule 2330 on multiple grounds.

FINRA Rule 2330(b) requires that a recommended exchange of a deferred variable annuity must be suitable, considering factors such as the customer's age, liquidity needs, whether the customer would incur a new surrender period, and whether the customer would benefit from the product features. Recommending a new 7-year surrender period to a 78-year-old with no need for the income rider fails this test. Additionally, Rule 2330(c) mandates that a registered principal review and approve the transaction before the purchase or exchange is processed. The same-day execution without any principal review is a direct violation of the principal review and approval requirement.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2330 (Members' Responsibilities Regarding Deferred Variable Annuities) — subsections (b) suitability and (c) principal review and approval

KEY FACTOR
----------
The exchange was executed without the required registered principal pre-approval, and the transaction was unsuitable given the client's age, liquidity profile, and lack of need for the new product's features.
