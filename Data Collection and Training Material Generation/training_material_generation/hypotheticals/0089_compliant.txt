FACTS
-----
A FINRA broker recommends a balanced mutual fund (60% equities / 40% fixed income, 0.55% ER) to a 68-year-old retired client with $320,000 invested, stated moderate risk tolerance, $2,400/month Social Security, and desire for steady income and capital preservation. The broker documents the customer's age, income needs, and moderate risk tolerance, discusses the fund's composition and volatility, and explains that the 40% fixed income component addresses income generation. The client acknowledges understanding and confirms suitability in writing. No further recommendations are made for 24 months.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation satisfy FINRA Rule 2111 suitability?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this recommendation is compliant with FINRA Rule 2111 suitability requirements.

The broker satisfies all Rule 2111 requirements. (1) Reasonable basis: a balanced 60/40 fund is a recognized, mainstream strategy appropriate for moderate-risk investors, and the broker can rely on the fund manager's prospectus analysis. (2) Customer-specific suitability: the recommendation is tailored to a 68-year-old retiree with stated moderate risk tolerance seeking income and capital preservation; the 40% fixed income component directly addresses the customer's stated income need. (3) Documentation: the broker documents the customer's age, objectives, and risk tolerance, and obtains written acknowledgment. (4) No quantitative violations: a single buy-and-hold recommendation with no subsequent trading avoids any churning concern. This straightforward recommendation structure reflects FINRA's longstanding view that balanced funds are suitable for moderate-risk retirees.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111(a) and (b): broker must have reasonable basis for recommendation and must determine suitability based on customer's investment profile including age, risk tolerance, and financial situation.

KEY FACTOR
----------
Balanced fund allocation directly matched to customer's stated income need, age, and moderate risk tolerance with proper documentation.
