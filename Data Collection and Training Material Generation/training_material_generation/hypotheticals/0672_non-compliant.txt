Topic: legal_risk_analysis
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Granite Peak Capital, an SEC-registered investment adviser managing three private funds totaling $1.2 billion in AUM, grants a side letter to a $50 million sovereign wealth fund investor providing preferential redemption rights allowing a 5-day notice period, while all other LPs in the same fund are subject to a 90-day redemption notice with a 12-month lock-up. The adviser does not disclose the existence or material terms of the preferential redemption side letter to any other current or prospective investors in the fund. The adviser's compliance team was aware of the SEC's Private Fund Adviser Rules under the Preferential Treatment Rule but took the position that because the side letter was executed before the compliance date, grandfathering applied—despite the fact that the fund accepted new subscriptions and the adviser issued a new PPM after the compliance date without updating the disclosure.

QUESTION
--------
Did the registered investment adviser violate the SEC's Private Fund Adviser Rules by failing to disclose the preferential redemption terms to other fund investors after the compliance date?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of the SEC's Preferential Treatment Rule under the Private Fund Adviser Rules.

Under the SEC's Private Fund Adviser Rules, preferential treatment regarding redemption terms must either be prohibited (if it would have a material negative effect on other investors) or, at minimum, disclosed to all current and prospective investors. The issuance of a new PPM and acceptance of new subscriptions after the compliance date triggers the obligation to disclose the material terms of all preferential side letters. The adviser's reliance on grandfathering was misplaced because ongoing fundraising activity and new offering documents created a fresh disclosure obligation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Private Fund Adviser Rules – Rule 211(h)(2)-3 Preferential Treatment Rule (Preferential Treatment Subject to Disclosure)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser's failure to disclose preferential redemption side letter terms to other investors after the compliance date—particularly following new subscriptions and an updated PPM—violated the Preferential Treatment Rule's disclosure requirements.
