Source Folder: unknown
Topic: compliant_nuanced
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Broker-dealer advisor Sarah recommended a variable annuity with a guaranteed minimum withdrawal benefit (GMWB) rider to 45-year-old manufacturing manager Kevin with $380K in a taxable brokerage account. Kevin had no immediate retirement plans, was enrolled in his employer's 401(k) with $520K balance earning 6.5% annual returns, and had $89K emergency fund. Sarah's documentation stated the annuity ($280K initial investment, 2.95% M&E fee, 0.75% investment advisory fee) would provide 'tax efficiency' and 'guaranteed income at age 65.' However, Sarah did not analyze: (1) Kevin's ability to achieve similar results via tax-loss harvesting in taxable account; (2) the 7-year surrender period conflict with Kevin's stated goal of accessing funds in 5 years for home renovation; (3) the GMWB rider's 1.15% annual cost versus simple bond ladder alternative; (4) early withdrawal tax penalties if Kevin needed funds before 59.5. Kevin signed the application without objection.

QUESTION
--------
Did Sarah comply with FINRA Rule 2330 and Reg BI care obligation in recommending this variable annuity to a 45-year-old non-retiree?

ANSWER
------
No, non-compliant. FINRA Rule 2330(b) requires advisers to consider 'the customer's age, investment objectives, financial situation and needs, and other relevant information' before recommending an annuity. Sarah's failure to document analysis of: (1) Kevin's 20-year accumulation horizon (age 45 to 65) where tax-deferral value is modest versus liquidity needs; (2) the 7-year surrender penalty conflict with Kevin's explicit 5-year renovation goal; (3) comparable tax-efficient alternatives in taxable accounts; and (4) the high rider cost—all violate the suitability standard. Reg BI care obligation requires advisers to have 'reasonable basis' that the product is in the client's interest; here the surrender period directly conflicts with stated needs. The absence of documented comparison to tax-loss harvesting or bond ladder alternatives, and failure to address early withdrawal penalties, mirrors FINRA AWC 2023-00038 (annuity sold without suitability analysis to non-retiree). Client silence does not cure the adviser's failure to conduct and document suitable analysis.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2330(b) Suitability; Reg BI Care Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Failure to document suitability analysis for non-retiree with explicit 5-year liquidity need, combined with 7-year surrender period, violates annuity suitability rule regardless of client's stated acceptance.
