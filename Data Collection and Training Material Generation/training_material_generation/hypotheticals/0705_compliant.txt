Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Greenfield Wealth Partners, a dually registered investment adviser and broker-dealer, retains a relationship with SHRM (Society for Human Resource Management) under which SHRM introduces its employer-members to Greenfield for 401(k) retirement plan advisory services. SHRM receives a flat $2,500 referral fee per client engagement from Greenfield, and this arrangement is prominently disclosed in the Form ADV Part 2A, in the 401(k) plan advisory agreement, and in a standalone written disclosure provided to each prospective employer-client before any advisory contract is signed. The disclosure states: 'SHRM receives cash compensation from Greenfield Wealth Partners when a SHRM member becomes a client of Greenfield. This creates an incentive for SHRM to market the investment advisory services of Greenfield. SHRM is not affiliated with Greenfield and does not offer investment advisory services.' Greenfield's investment committee independently evaluates each employer plan's needs and recommends low-cost index fund lineups averaging 6 basis points in expense ratios, consistent with ERISA fiduciary standards.

QUESTION
--------
Is Greenfield Wealth Partners' receipt of client referrals from SHRM in exchange for cash compensation compliant with fiduciary and conflict-of-interest disclosure requirements under the Investment Advisers Act and ERISA?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The referral arrangement between Greenfield and SHRM mirrors the type of compensated solicitation structure contemplated under the Advisers Act's solicitation framework (now governed by the reformed Marketing Rule). Full, written disclosure of the material conflict—that SHRM receives cash compensation creating an incentive to recommend Greenfield—is provided before the advisory relationship begins, satisfying both the Advisers Act §206 antifraud standard and the practical disclosure expectations for ERISA fiduciaries. The flat-fee structure (rather than asset-based) limits the magnitude of the conflict, and Greenfield's independent investment process recommending low-cost index funds demonstrates that the referral arrangement does not compromise the substantive quality of fiduciary advice provided to plan sponsors.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940 §206; SEC Rule 206(4)-3 (Cash Solicitation Rule) / reformed under Marketing Rule 206(4)-1; ERISA §404(a)(1) – Prudent Man and Loyalty Standards; DOL Advisory Opinion guidance on referral fee arrangements

KEY FACTOR
----------
The cash referral compensation arrangement with SHRM was fully disclosed in writing before engagement, and the advisor independently exercised prudent fiduciary judgment in recommending low-cost plan investments, ensuring the conflict did not compromise client outcomes.
