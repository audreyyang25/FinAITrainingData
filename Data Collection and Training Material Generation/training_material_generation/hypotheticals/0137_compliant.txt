FACTS
-----
A 58-year-old plan participant with $625,000 in a 401(k) plan at a mid-size employer meets with a fee-only fiduciary adviser (CFP, charging 1.0% AUM on IRA assets only). The plan's 401(k) offers limited investment options (8 mutual funds, average expense ratio 1.2%, no low-cost index alternatives) and plan-level fees of 0.65% all-in. The participant has a moderate risk tolerance and plans to retire in 7 years. The adviser documents that a rollover to a low-cost IRA (0.10% custodial fee) offering hundreds of fund options would allow implementation of a diversified portfolio with blended expense ratios of 0.55%. The adviser prepares a written analysis comparing total all-in costs (401(k): 1.85% vs. IRA post-rollover: 1.65%) and identifies her 1.0% AUM fee as a material conflict, disclosing it in writing and proposing a written BIC engagement letter that describes her fiduciary duty and right to terminate if fee not paid.

QUESTION
--------
Does this rollover recommendation satisfy DOL ERISA fiduciary duty and PTE 2020-02 requirements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this recommendation is compliant with DOL ERISA fiduciary duty and PTE 2020-02.

The adviser satisfied all elements of PTE 2020-02 and ERISA §404(a)(1): (1) she made a documented, reasoned best-interest determination showing that net all-in costs would be lower (1.65% vs. 1.85%) and the IRA would offer better investment flexibility for the client's moderate risk profile and 7-year horizon; (2) she clearly identified and disclosed her material conflict of interest (the 1.0% AUM fee) in writing; (3) she offered a written BIC engagement letter meeting 29 CFR §2550.404a-1(d)(3)(ii) and §2550.408g-2 requirements; and (4) she is a fee-only fiduciary with no sales compensation, reducing the likelihood of self-dealing. The documented cost comparison and articulated rationale (diversification, lower expense ratios) align with DOL's guidance in Advisory Opinion 2005-01A and the preamble to PTE 2020-02 stating that a rollover may be prudent when it allows better portfolio construction at lower net cost.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
DOL ERISA §404(a)(1)(A)–(B) (fiduciary duties of care and loyalty); PTE 2020-02 (Prohibited Transaction Exemption for rollover advice); 29 CFR §2550.404a-1(d) (fiduciary advice standard)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Adviser documented a cost and benefit analysis showing net savings, disclosed the material conflict of interest in writing, and offered a written BIC—the three requirements that PTE 2020-02 establishes for compliant rollover advice.
