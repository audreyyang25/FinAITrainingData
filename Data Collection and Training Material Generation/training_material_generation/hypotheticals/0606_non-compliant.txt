Source Folder: 07_Practitioner_Commentary
Topic: training_supervision
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Following the October 1, 2018 effective date of FINRA's restructured qualification examination framework under Regulatory Notice 17-30, a broker-dealer hires five newly registered representatives who passed the Securities Industry Essentials (SIE) exam and the Series 7 top-off exam. The firm's compliance department schedules continuing education (CE) Regulatory Element training for these representatives but repeatedly postpones the sessions due to budget constraints. Fourteen months after their initial registration, none of the five representatives have completed the Regulatory Element of CE. During this period, the representatives collectively generated $2.3 million in commissions from recommendations of complex structured products to retail customers, including several elderly clients with moderate risk tolerances.

QUESTION
--------
Does the firm's repeated postponement of required continuing education for newly registered representatives while allowing them to continue making recommendations to retail customers constitute a supervisory violation?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of FINRA CE requirements and the firm's supervisory obligations under FINRA Rule 3110.

FINRA Regulatory Notice 17-30 approved amendments to CE requirements alongside the restructured SIE and top-off examination framework, reinforcing the importance of ongoing education for registered representatives. FINRA Rule 1240 requires registered persons to complete the Regulatory Element of CE within prescribed timeframes, and failure to do so results in inactive status, meaning the representatives should not have been permitted to continue performing registered activities. The firm's decision to allow these representatives to continue recommending complex structured products without completed CE also violates FINRA Rule 3110's requirement for a supervisory system reasonably designed to ensure compliance with applicable securities laws and regulations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 1240 – Continuing Education Requirements; FINRA Rule 3110 – Supervision; FINRA Regulatory Notice 17-30

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm permitted representatives to continue making recommendations to retail customers despite their non-completion of mandatory continuing education requirements, reflecting a systemic supervisory failure.
