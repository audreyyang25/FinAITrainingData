ID: 552
Source Folder: 04_RegBI_Compliance_Guidance
Topic: reg_bi_disclosure
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Regulus-West Financial Group, LLC, a FINRA member firm headquartered in Grand Rapids, Michigan with 110 registered representatives and 60 branch offices, primarily services retail investors through general securities products including mutual funds, variable annuities, and private placements. From June 2021 through early 2024, the firm failed to disclose on its Form CRS and in its Reg BI disclosures that the firm and a control affiliate had reportable disciplinary history, including a prior FINRA censure and fine from 2019. The firm's Form CRS Item 4 ('Do you or your financial professionals have legal or disciplinary history?') answered 'Yes' but directed customers to BrokerCheck without disclosing the firm-level disciplinary events that were required to be affirmatively described. No updated Form CRS was filed when a new disciplinary event occurred in 2022.

QUESTION
--------
Did Regulus-West satisfy its Disclosure Obligation under Reg BI and Form CRS requirements by directing customers to BrokerCheck without affirmatively disclosing its own and its control affiliate's disciplinary history?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates the Reg BI Disclosure Obligation and Form CRS requirements.

The Disclosure Obligation requires broker-dealers to provide retail customers, prior to or at the time of a recommendation, full and fair disclosure of all material facts relating to the scope and terms of the relationship, including conflicts of interest. Form CRS Item 4 specifically requires firms to disclose their disciplinary history and that of their financial professionals, and Form CRS must be updated within 30 days of material changes. Merely directing customers to BrokerCheck without affirmatively disclosing firm-level and control affiliate disciplinary events does not satisfy the disclosure requirement. This pattern directly mirrors the Regulus Financial Group AWC (No. 2023077077001), where the firm failed to disclose its own and its control affiliate's disciplinary history from May 2021 through February 2024.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation; Form CRS (17 CFR 249.2) Item 4; SEC Rule 17a-14

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm failed to affirmatively disclose its own and its control affiliate's disciplinary history on Form CRS and in Reg BI disclosures for nearly three years, instead only providing a generic BrokerCheck referral.
