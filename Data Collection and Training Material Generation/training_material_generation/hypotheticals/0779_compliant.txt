Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Horizon Financial Partners LLC is a newly formed investment advisory firm in California seeking state registration. The firm's principal, Catherine Ng, completed all six steps outlined by the California DFPI: established an IARD account, funded the daily account with applicable filing fees, completed Form ADV Parts 1 and 2 including full disclosure of her prior employment termination from a broker-dealer due to a customer complaint (which was ultimately dismissed), submitted the Form ADV through IARD, ensured her two investment adviser representatives passed the Series 65 exam and filed Form U4, and submitted the complete application package. Before receiving confirmation of registration, Ng refrained from entering into any advisory agreements, soliciting clients, or collecting any fees. The DFPI approved the registration after a 45-day review, and only then did the firm commence business operations.

QUESTION
--------
Did Horizon Financial Partners comply with California's investment adviser registration requirements despite the principal's prior employment termination?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Horizon followed all required steps for California IA registration as prescribed by the DFPI, including proper IARD setup, complete Form ADV disclosure, representative qualification, and full application submission. Critically, the firm did not conduct any advisory business before receiving its registration certificate. The principal's prior termination and dismissed complaint were fully disclosed on Form ADV, satisfying the disclosure obligations. A prior employment termination that resulted in a dismissed complaint does not automatically disqualify an applicant from registration; what matters is full and accurate disclosure.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
California Corporations Code Section 25230; California DFPI Registration Procedures for Investment Advisers; IARD Filing Requirements

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm fully disclosed all material regulatory history, completed all registration steps, and refrained from conducting advisory business until the DFPI approved the registration.
