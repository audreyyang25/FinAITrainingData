Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Greenfield Advisory Group, a registered investment adviser managing $120 million in assets, recently received several positive reviews from existing clients on an independent third-party review site, WealthAdvisorReviews.com. Greenfield's marketing director copies and pastes three of the most glowing five-star client reviews — including specific claims such as 'Greenfield doubled my portfolio in two years' and 'Best adviser I've ever worked with, guaranteed returns' — directly onto the firm's own website and includes them in a PDF brochure emailed to 2,000 prospective clients. The firm does not add any disclaimers, does not disclose whether the reviewers were compensated, and does not present any reviews that are less favorable. The marketing materials were distributed in September 2021, prior to the November 4, 2022 effective date of the new SEC Marketing Rule (Rule 206(4)-1), meaning the prior testimonial prohibition under the old Advisers Act advertising rule was still in effect.

QUESTION
--------
Was Greenfield Advisory Group compliant when it republished selected client testimonials from a third-party review site onto its own website and marketing brochure in September 2021?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of the testimonial prohibition under the former Advisers Act advertising rule.

Under the pre-November 2022 advertising rule, investment advisers were prohibited from using testimonials in their advertisements. IM Guidance Update 2014-04 recognized that while third-party commentary may appear organically on independent social media sites, an adviser's affirmative act of republishing selected favorable testimonials onto its own website and distributing them in marketing materials constitutes the use of testimonials in an advertisement. Greenfield's cherry-picking of only the most favorable reviews, including one referencing 'guaranteed returns,' and distributing them without any context or balance, directly violated the then-operative testimonial prohibition.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act Rule 206(4)-1(a)(1) (pre-November 4, 2022 version) – Prohibition on Testimonials in Advertisements; SEC IM Guidance Update No. 2014-04

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser affirmatively republished and selectively curated client testimonials from a third-party site into its own marketing materials at a time when the testimonial prohibition was still in effect, transforming organic third-party commentary into a prohibited advertisement.
