Source Folder: 21_FINRA_OHO_Decisions
Topic: adequate_supervision_escalation_procedures
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Branch manager Sylvia Okonkwo at a mid-sized broker-dealer supervised a team of twelve registered representatives and maintained a written supervisory procedures manual that was updated annually and distributed to all staff. Upon receiving an automated exception report flagging one representative's account turnover rate as exceeding firm thresholds for two consecutive months, Okonkwo promptly reviewed the flagged accounts, interviewed the representative, documented her findings, and escalated the matter to the compliance department within three business days. The compliance department conducted an independent review, determined the trading reflected a documented short-term active trading strategy consented to in writing by the affected customers, and closed the review with a documented rationale. Okonkwo maintained all review records for the required period.

QUESTION
--------
Did Okonkwo's supervisory response to the exception report satisfy FINRA Rule 3110 obligations?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Rule 3110 requires member firms to establish and maintain a supervisory system reasonably designed to achieve compliance, including written supervisory procedures, review of exception reports, and timely escalation of red flags. Okonkwo's prompt review of the exception report, documented investigation, escalation within three business days, and retention of records collectively satisfy Rule 3110's substantive and procedural requirements. The compliance department's independent review and documented closure further demonstrate a functioning supervisory system operating as designed.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 3110 (Supervision); FINRA Rule 4511 (Books and Records)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Timely detection, documented investigation, and escalation of the exception report to compliance reflects a supervisory system that meets Rule 3110's reasonable-design standard.
