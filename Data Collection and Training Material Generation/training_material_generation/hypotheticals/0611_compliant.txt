Topic: finra_awc_suitability
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Maria Vasquez, a registered representative at Clearview Securities LLC since 2015, recommends that her client Thomas Hargrove, age 62, exchange his existing deferred variable annuity (current value $210,000, 2 years remaining on a 7-year surrender period, total annual expenses of 2.15%) into a new variable annuity with a guaranteed lifetime withdrawal benefit rider (total annual expenses of 2.55%, new 6-year surrender period). Hargrove, a recently retired municipal worker with a $48,000 annual pension, $390,000 in total investable assets, and a moderate risk tolerance, has expressed significant concern about outliving his retirement savings and specifically requested income guarantees. Vasquez documents a detailed comparison showing the existing annuity lacks any living benefit rider, that the 40-basis-point cost increase funds the guaranteed withdrawal benefit valued at 5% annual withdrawals for life beginning at age 65, and that Hargrove's surrender charge on the existing contract ($4,200) is substantially offset by the new carrier's enhanced death benefit. The firm's principal reviewer independently verifies the suitability analysis, confirms the client's stated objective of lifetime income, reviews the cost comparison, and documents approval with a written rationale referencing Hargrove's specific retirement income gap.

QUESTION
--------
Was Vasquez's recommendation to exchange the existing variable annuity for a new contract with higher fees and a new surrender period suitable and compliant with applicable standards?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Although the exchange involves higher annual costs and a new surrender period — factors that typically warrant heightened scrutiny — the recommendation is grounded in the client's specific, documented need for guaranteed lifetime income that the existing contract cannot provide. Under both FINRA Rule 2111's reasonable-basis and customer-specific suitability components, and Reg BI's Care Obligation, a recommendation may involve higher costs if the overall benefits to the particular customer justify the expense. The representative conducted and documented a thorough cost-benefit comparison, the client's income gap and stated objectives supported the guaranteed withdrawal feature, and the firm's principal independently reviewed and approved the transaction with written rationale — satisfying both the suitability and supervisory requirements.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability) and SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation

KEY FACTOR
----------
The higher-cost exchange was justified and documented based on the client's specific need for guaranteed lifetime income that was unavailable in the existing contract, with independent principal review confirming suitability.
