Topic: investor_harm_empirical
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A broker-dealer representative recommends that a recently retired 67-year-old couple with $520,000 in combined savings and Social Security income of $3,200/month concentrate 78% of their investable assets into a single non-traded REIT with a 7-year holding period and no liquidity provisions. The couple stated on their new account form that their primary objectives were income generation and capital preservation, and that they may need access to funds for medical expenses. The representative earned a 7% front-end commission totaling $28,392 on the transaction. No alternative income-generating products with greater liquidity were discussed or documented.

QUESTION
--------
Was this recommendation consistent with Reg BI's Care Obligation and the suitability requirements for a recently retired couple needing liquidity?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates the Reg BI Care Obligation and constitutes an unsuitable concentration in an illiquid product.

Reg BI's Care Obligation requires that a recommendation be in the retail customer's best interest and not place the financial professional's interest ahead of the customer's. Concentrating 78% of a retired couple's assets in a single illiquid, non-traded REIT with a 7-year holding period directly contradicts their stated need for potential liquidity and capital preservation. The 2025 State of Retirement Planning research shows that retirees who feel retirement is going 'as planned' rely on diversified, accessible portfolios—this recommendation undermines that outcome. The 7% commission creates a clear incentive conflict that was not mitigated by any documented analysis of alternatives.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Care Obligation; FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability); FINRA Regulatory Notice 09-09 (Non-Traded REITs)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Concentrating the vast majority of a retired couple's liquid assets in a single illiquid product contradicted their stated need for capital preservation and liquidity access, while generating an outsized commission for the representative.
