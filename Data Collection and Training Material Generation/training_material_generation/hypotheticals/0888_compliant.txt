Source Folder: 19_CFTC_Guidance
Topic: cta_cpo_suitability
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
AlphaStream CTA LLC is a registered commodity trading advisor operating a managed futures program focused on equity index and interest rate futures. A prospective client, a 52-year-old anesthesiologist with $4.8 million in liquid investable assets, a self-described 'aggressive growth' risk tolerance, and five years of prior experience trading listed equity options, requests discretionary CTA management of a $500,000 sub-account. AlphaStream collects a completed NFA-required customer information form documenting income of $750,000 per year, the liquid asset figure, investment objectives, and prior derivatives experience. The firm's written suitability determination memorandum concludes that the client's financial sophistication, asset base, income, and stated risk appetite are consistent with exposure to speculative futures strategies, and AlphaStream provides a current Disclosure Document including a CFTC-required performance record and risk disclosure. The client subsequently loses $88,000 in a six-week drawdown.

QUESTION
--------
Did AlphaStream CTA LLC satisfy its suitability and disclosure obligations under NFA rules notwithstanding the client's subsequent trading losses?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

NFA Compliance Rule 2-30 requires CTAs to make reasonable efforts to obtain relevant customer financial information and to determine suitability before providing trading advice, not to guarantee profitable outcomes. AlphaStream collected complete financial information, documented a written suitability determination, and provided a compliant Disclosure Document including required risk disclosures and performance data under CFTC Regulation 4.31. The client's $4.8 million liquid asset base, high income, prior derivatives experience, and aggressive risk tolerance collectively support a reasonable suitability conclusion for a speculative futures program. Subsequent losses do not retroactively render a properly documented suitability determination non-compliant.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NFA Compliance Rule 2-30 – Know Your Customer / Suitability; CFTC Regulation 4.31 – CTA Disclosure Document Requirements

KEY FACTOR
----------
Complete upfront customer information collection, documented suitability analysis, and delivery of a CFTC-compliant Disclosure Document prior to trading constitute full satisfaction of NFA suitability obligations regardless of subsequent performance.
