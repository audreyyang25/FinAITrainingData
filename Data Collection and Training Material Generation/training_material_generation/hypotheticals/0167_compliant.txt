Source Folder: unknown
Topic: undisclosed_conflict
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A dually registered broker-dealer and investment adviser recommends a variable annuity (MetLife VA with 1.35% M&E charges) to a 68-year-old widow with $180,000 in liquid savings, moderate income needs, and moderate risk tolerance. The firm receives a 5.5% upfront commission on the sale and 0.60% annual trail commission. Before the recommendation, the adviser provides the client a detailed Form ADV Part 2A supplement disclosing: (1) that it is dually registered; (2) the specific upfront commission (5.5%) and annual trail (0.60%); (3) that the annuity carries 1.35% M&E charges; (4) that it recommends annuities for clients meeting specific suitability criteria (age 65+, income needs, moderate-to-conservative risk); and (5) that it has compared the annuity to three alternative fixed-income ETFs (cost 0.15%–0.35% annually). The client acknowledges receipt and signs a conflict-of-interest consent form. A suitability memo in the file documents the widow's income needs, age, and estate planning goals.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation and disclosure process satisfy Reg BI conflict obligations and IA fiduciary duty?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this is compliant.

The firm's disclosure is specific and quantified: it identifies the exact upfront (5.5%) and trail (0.60%) commissions, the 1.35% M&E charges, and the alternative lower-cost options. The disclosure is provided in writing (ADV 2A supplement) before the transaction, satisfying the 'clear, prominent, and understandable' standard. The suitability analysis demonstrates that the recommendation is not driven solely by the conflict: the client's age (68), income needs, and moderate risk tolerance are genuinely consistent with annuity suitability per FINRA Rule 2330 standards. The firm's written policy limiting annuity recommendations to clients 65+ with documented income needs shows a conflict-mitigation control. The client's signed acknowledgment of the conflict disclosure—even though consent is not strictly required under Reg BI—strengthens the compliance posture.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Regulation Best Interest (Rule 15l-1(a)(2)) requires clear, prominent disclosure of material conflicts; IA Act Section 206 requires fiduciary to avoid self-dealing and disclose all material conflicts. FINRA Rule 2330 (variable annuity sales) requires suitability documentation and disclosure of compensation.

KEY FACTOR
----------
Specific quantification of compensation, written pre-transaction disclosure in Form ADV 2A, documented suitability analysis, and policy limiting recommendations to clients meeting objective criteria.
