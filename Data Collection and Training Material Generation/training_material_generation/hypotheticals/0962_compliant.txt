Topic: expungement_customer_notice_and_participation
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Broker Sandra Fielding filed a statement of claim in FINRA arbitration seeking expungement of a customer complaint that alleged unauthorized trading. The customer, Mr. Tran, was provided written notice of the expungement hearing at least 30 days in advance and was given the opportunity to participate and present evidence. At the hearing, a three-person arbitration panel reviewed the full customer file, the settlement agreement, and testimony from both parties. The panel issued a detailed written decision finding that Mr. Tran's account records conclusively demonstrated that written authorization existed for each trade, making the unauthorized trading claim factually impossible.

QUESTION
--------
Did the arbitration panel comply with FINRA's expungement requirements when it granted expungement based on a finding that the claim was factually impossible?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

FINRA Rule 2080 and Rule 12805 require that an arbitration panel make one of three affirmative findings before recommending expungement, one of which is that the claim is factually impossible or clearly erroneous. Here, the panel made an explicit factual finding supported by documentary evidence that the unauthorized trading claim was factually impossible given the existence of written trading authorization. Additionally, customer notice and opportunity to participate, as emphasized in FINRA Regulatory Notice 17-42, were properly observed, satisfying procedural requirements that PIABA has advocated for to protect investor interests in the expungement process.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2080; FINRA Code of Arbitration Procedure Rule 12805; FINRA Regulatory Notice 17-42

KEY FACTOR
----------
Panel made the required affirmative factual finding of impossibility and afforded the customer full procedural participation rights before ordering expungement.
