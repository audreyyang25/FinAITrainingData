Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
David Kimura, a registered representative formerly associated with LPL Financial LLC, sought expungement of a customer complaint from his CRD record through FINRA arbitration. The underlying complaint alleged that Kimura recommended unsuitable mutual fund investments in a customer's IRA, resulting in a $12,000 loss during a broad market downturn. LPL's records showed the customer had a moderate growth objective, a 10-year investment time horizon, and $450,000 in total assets. Kimura had recommended a diversified portfolio of four large-cap equity mutual funds and two intermediate-term bond funds, with an overall equity allocation of 60%. The firm's compliance department had reviewed and approved the recommendations at the time of the transactions. The arbitration panel reviewed the complete record and found that the customer's losses were attributable to general market conditions and that the recommendations were consistent with the customer's documented profile. The panel recommended expungement, finding the complaint was factually impossible or clearly erroneous.

QUESTION
--------
Was the arbitration panel's recommendation for expungement of the customer complaint appropriate under FINRA rules?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Under FINRA Rule 2080 and Rule 12805, an arbitration panel may recommend expungement of customer dispute information from a representative's CRD record if it finds that the claim is factually impossible or clearly erroneous, or that the registered person was not involved in the alleged conduct. Here, the panel reviewed the full record and determined that a 60/40 diversified portfolio was consistent with the customer's moderate growth objective and 10-year time horizon, and that the losses were attributable to market conditions rather than unsuitable recommendations. The firm's contemporaneous compliance approval further supports the finding that the recommendations met suitability standards under FINRA Rule 2111.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2080 (Obtaining an Order of Expungement of Customer Dispute Information) and FINRA Rule 12805

KEY FACTOR
----------
The diversified portfolio allocation was consistent with the customer's documented investment profile, and losses resulted from general market conditions rather than unsuitable recommendations, supporting the panel's expungement finding.
