Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A 62-year-old client with $250,000 in a traditional IRA, moderate risk tolerance, and a stated goal of current income plus modest growth asks the advisor about income options. The advisor recommends a variable annuity with a 5-year period-certain immediate annuity payout (guaranteed monthly income for 5 years, then systematic withdrawal plan). The advisor documents: (1) the client's stated income need and time horizon; (2) the guaranteed payment amount and comparison to non-annuity withdrawal strategies; (3) the total fee structure (1.8% M&E and sub-account fees); (4) that the client understood surrender charges do not apply to guaranteed withdrawal plan; and (5) that the product is held in the client's existing IRA custodial account. The recommendation is made in writing with a dated suitability statement.

QUESTION
--------
Does this variable annuity immediate income recommendation satisfy FINRA Rule 2330?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this is compliant.

This recommendation satisfies Rule 2330 because the advisor established a reasonable basis for suitability before recommending the variable annuity. The documented facts show: (1) The product directly addresses the client's stated objective (current income); (2) The client's age (62) and moderate risk tolerance support an income-focused strategy; (3) The fee structure (1.8%) is reasonable and disclosed; (4) The advisor provided written documentation of the suitability analysis, including the guaranteed payment amount; (5) The advisor explained that guaranteed withdrawal periods do not trigger surrender charges, reducing a key risk; and (6) The product remains in the IRA custodial account, preserving tax treatment. Rule 2330 requires only that the advisor have a reasonable basis to believe the recommendation is suitable given the client's circumstances—not that it be optimal or the lowest-cost option. Here, the variable annuity's guaranteed income rider directly matches the client's articulated need, and the costs are reasonable. The written suitability statement satisfies Rule 4512's documentation requirement.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2330 (Variable Annuity Suitability); SEC Variable Annuity Guidance (2012); FINRA Rule 4512 (documentation)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Complete written documentation of the suitability analysis, including the client's stated income objective, disclosure of fees and guarantee terms, and demonstration that the product directly addresses the client's needs, establishing reasonable basis for Rule 2330 suitability.
