Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Cornerstone Advisory Group, an SEC-registered investment adviser with $310 million in AUM, undergoes a routine Division of Examinations inspection. During the COVID-19 pandemic, the firm transitioned to a remote work environment and implemented enhanced compliance procedures including encrypted VPN access for all employees, recorded video supervisory check-ins with advisers conducting client meetings from home, daily reconciliation of client account activity by the CCO, and updated business continuity plans tested quarterly. The firm documented all policy changes in a compliance memorandum dated April 2020, communicated them to all IARs, and maintained logs showing 98% adherence to the new supervisory protocols over the review period. One minor exception was noted: a single adviser conducted a client call from a personal cell phone without recording, which was identified by the CCO within 48 hours and resulted in a written reminder and updated procedure.

QUESTION
--------
Did Cornerstone Advisory Group demonstrate adequate compliance and operational resiliency during the COVID-19 remote work transition consistent with SEC examination expectations?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The SEC's August 2020 COVID-19 Compliance Risk Alert specifically identified operational resiliency, supervision of remote employees, and protection of client information as key areas of concern during the pandemic transition. Cornerstone implemented documented, tested compliance procedures addressing each of these concerns, maintained high adherence rates, and promptly identified and remediated the single exception. The Division of Examinations has indicated that it does not expect perfection but rather reasonable policies, procedures, and a culture of compliance that identifies and corrects issues—all of which are evidenced here.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940 § 206(4); SEC Rule 206(4)-7 – Compliance Programs; SEC OCIE Risk Alert on COVID-19 Compliance Risks (August 12, 2020)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm proactively implemented comprehensive remote-work compliance policies, documented adherence at 98%, and promptly self-corrected the single identified exception, demonstrating the type of robust compliance infrastructure the SEC's COVID-19 Risk Alert encouraged.
