Source Folder: 03_FINRA_AWC
Topic: finra_awc_supervision
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Ridgeline Financial Services, Inc., a FINRA member firm since 1998 with 18 registered representatives and three branch offices, provides investment and retirement planning services to retail customers. Since the June 30, 2020 Reg BI compliance date, Ridgeline has failed to establish and maintain written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with Regulation Best Interest. The firm's compliance manual still references only the pre-existing FINRA Rule 2111 suitability standard. The firm has no written procedures addressing the Reg BI Disclosure Obligation, no process for identifying and mitigating conflicts of interest related to compensation incentives or product-level revenue sharing, and has not delivered Form CRS to any retail customers. The firm's chief compliance officer acknowledged during a FINRA examination that the firm 'intended to update its procedures but had not gotten around to it.' FINRA censured the firm, imposed a $25,000 fine, and required an undertaking to certify remediation.

QUESTION
--------
Did Ridgeline Financial Services' failure to establish Reg BI-compliant written policies, procedures, and supervisory systems constitute a violation?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is not compliant. This is a violation of Exchange Act Rule 15l-1(a)(1), FINRA Rules 3110 and 2010 for failure to establish and maintain a supervisory system and written procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with Reg BI.

Under Reg BI's Compliance Obligation, broker-dealers must establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with Reg BI as a whole. FINRA Rule 3110 independently requires member firms to establish and maintain a supervisory system, including written procedures, to supervise the activities of associated persons. As illustrated in the Alexander Investment Services AWC, a firm's complete failure to update its supervisory framework from the prior suitability standard to Reg BI—including the absence of conflict-of-interest mitigation procedures and Form CRS delivery—constitutes a clear and ongoing violation from the June 30, 2020 compliance date forward.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Securities Exchange Act Rule 15l-1(a)(1) – Reg BI Compliance Obligation; FINRA Rule 3110 (Supervision); FINRA Rule 2010 (Standards of Commercial Honor)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm's total failure to establish any written policies, procedures, or supervisory system addressing Reg BI since the June 30, 2020 compliance date is the pivotal violation.
