Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Apex Capital Partners, an SEC-registered investment adviser managing $2.1 billion across several private funds, grants one large institutional investor — a state pension fund committing $400 million — a side letter providing preferential redemption rights that allow full liquidity within 10 business days during a market dislocation event. The adviser does not disclose the existence or material terms of this preferential treatment to other limited partners in the same fund, several of whom are endowments and family offices with $20–$50 million commitments subject to a standard 90-day redemption notice period. The side letter was executed in March 2024, after the compliance date for the SEC's Preferential Treatment Rule under the Private Fund Adviser Rules. The adviser's chief compliance officer reviewed the letter but concluded that because the pension fund was a 'cornerstone investor,' no disclosure was required.

QUESTION
--------
Did the registered investment adviser violate the SEC's Private Fund Adviser Rules by failing to disclose the preferential redemption terms granted via side letter to other investors in the same fund?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of the SEC's Preferential Treatment Rule requiring disclosure of material preferential terms to other investors.

Under the Preferential Treatment Rule applicable to all SEC-registered private fund advisers, preferential treatment regarding redemption rights that is not outright prohibited must be disclosed to current and prospective investors in the same fund. Preferential liquidity terms are specifically identified as material economic terms requiring written disclosure. The CCO's rationale that 'cornerstone investor' status exempts the adviser from disclosure has no basis in the rule; no such exception exists. The adviser's failure to provide notice to other LPs of the side letter's material terms constitutes a clear violation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Private Fund Adviser Rules – Rule 211(h)(2)-3 (Preferential Treatment Rule), adopted August 2023

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser failed to disclose preferential redemption terms granted via side letter to other investors as required under the Preferential Treatment Rule, for which no 'cornerstone investor' exception exists.
