ID: 739
Source Folder: 12_FINRA_Arbitration_Awards
Topic: arbitration_liability
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A registered representative at a mid-sized FINRA member firm recommended that Hisako Tanaka, a 74-year-old retired nurse with $210,000 in savings and no prior investment experience, invest $185,000 — approximately 88% of her liquid net worth — in a single illiquid, non-traded REIT with a 7-year holding period and early redemption penalties of 10%. The representative earned a 7% upfront commission of $12,950 on the transaction. Tanaka's new account form indicated a 'conservative' investment objective and 'low' risk tolerance, and she told the representative she might need access to funds for potential assisted living costs within two to three years. No alternative, more liquid products were discussed or documented.

QUESTION
--------
Was the representative's recommendation of the non-traded REIT suitable for this client?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is an unsuitable recommendation in violation of FINRA Rule 2111.

FINRA Rule 2111(a) requires that a recommended transaction be suitable based on the customer's investment profile, including age, financial situation, liquidity needs, risk tolerance, and investment objectives. Recommending that a 74-year-old conservative investor with low risk tolerance and near-term liquidity needs concentrate 88% of her liquid net worth in a single illiquid, non-traded REIT with a 7-year hold period and steep redemption penalties directly contradicts every element of her stated profile. The representative's failure to discuss any alternative products further demonstrates a disregard for the customer-specific suitability obligation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111(a) — Customer-Specific Suitability Obligation

KEY FACTOR
----------
Concentrating 88% of a conservative, elderly investor's liquid assets in a single illiquid product with a 7-year holding period when the client expressed near-term liquidity needs is a clear customer-specific suitability violation.
