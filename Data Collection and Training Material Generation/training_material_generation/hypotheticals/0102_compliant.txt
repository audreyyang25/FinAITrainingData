Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A 35-year-old software engineer with $50,000 in savings and no prior investment experience requested help building a long-term retirement portfolio. The broker documented the client's 30-year time horizon, moderate risk tolerance, $120,000 annual income, and no near-term liquidity needs. The broker recommended a simple portfolio of 60% low-cost U.S. stock index ETF and 40% bond index ETF. The recommendation was documented in a suitability memo and reviewed annually. The broker charged 0.50% AUM and made no transaction-based commissions.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation satisfy FINRA Rule 2111 suitability?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this is compliant.

The broker's recommendation clearly satisfies Rule 2111. The reasonable-basis prong is met: a 60/40 stock/bond allocation is a standard, well-documented strategy appropriate for moderate-risk long-term investors, and index-based vehicles at 0.50% AUM are cost-efficient. The customer-specific prong is satisfied: the broker documented that the 35-year-old client has a 30-year time horizon, moderate risk tolerance, sufficient income, and no withdrawal needs, all supporting a balanced equity/bond allocation. The recommendation avoids excessive trading (annual review only), and the fee structure (AUM, not commissions) removes incentive for unsuitable portfolio turnover. This straightforward recommendation epitomizes compliant Rule 2111 suitability.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111(a) requires a reasonable basis and customer-specific analysis of the client's investment profile.

KEY FACTOR
----------
A 30-year time horizon, moderate risk tolerance, and adequate income make a balanced 60/40 index allocation plainly suitable, and documentation of the client's profile demonstrates compliance.
