ID: 596
Source Folder: 06_DOL_Fiduciary
Topic: dol_rollover
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Margaret Chen, a 62-year-old school teacher, retires with a $340,000 balance in her employer's 403(b) plan invested in low-cost Vanguard index funds with a total expense ratio of 0.04%. Her financial advisor, Ryan Torres of Beacon Wealth Group, recommends she roll the entire balance into an IRA at his firm, where he places her in a suite of proprietary variable annuities carrying a 1.35% annual M&E charge, a 7-year surrender schedule, and a $12,000 first-year commission to Torres. Torres does not document any comparison of the 403(b) plan's investment options, fees, or services against the IRA he recommends. He tells Margaret the rollover will give her 'more choices and better service' but provides no written analysis.

QUESTION
--------
Did the advisor satisfy the DOL's fiduciary obligations under PTE 2020-02 when recommending this rollover?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates PTE 2020-02's best interest standard and its specific rollover documentation requirements.

PTE 2020-02 requires that investment advice fiduciaries act in the retirement investor's best interest, which includes providing advice that reflects the care, skill, prudence, and diligence that a prudent person would exercise. For rollover recommendations specifically, the exemption requires the fiduciary to consider and document the alternatives to the rollover, including leaving assets in the existing plan, and to evaluate comparative fees, investment options, and services. Torres failed to perform or document any such comparison, and the recommended IRA products carry dramatically higher costs (1.35% vs. 0.04%) with significant surrender penalties, while generating a large commission that creates an obvious conflict of interest.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
DOL PTE 2020-02 – Improving Investment Advice for Workers & Retirees; ERISA §404(a)(1)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The advisor failed to document a comparative analysis of the existing 403(b) plan versus the recommended IRA rollover, as specifically required by PTE 2020-02 for rollover advice.
