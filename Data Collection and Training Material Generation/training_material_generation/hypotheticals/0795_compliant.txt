Topic: senior_investor_protection
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Robert Halloran, age 74, holds a $540,000 brokerage account at Pinnacle Securities. His registered representative, Lisa Moreno, notices that Robert has been making uncharacteristic requests: three wire transfers totaling $47,000 to unfamiliar recipients in a two-week period, and he appears confused during phone calls about the purpose of the transfers. Moreno escalates the matter to her firm's designated senior investor protection contact, who places a temporary hold on the disbursements pursuant to the firm's FINRA Rule 2165 procedures. The firm promptly notifies Robert's previously designated trusted contact person — his adult son, James — and also contacts Adult Protective Services. After a five-day review period, James confirms that Robert has been targeted by a phone scam. The hold is maintained for 15 business days while the matter is investigated, and the transfers are ultimately cancelled with Robert's and James's consent.

QUESTION
--------
Did the firm and representative act appropriately under FINRA's senior investor protection rules in placing a temporary hold on the disbursements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

FINRA Rule 2165 permits member firms to place a temporary hold on disbursements of funds or securities when there is a reasonable belief of financial exploitation of a customer age 65 or older or a specified adult. Moreno's observation of uncharacteristic transfer requests and apparent confusion provided a reasonable basis for the hold. The firm properly followed Rule 2165 procedures by escalating to the designated contact, notifying the trusted contact person identified under Rule 4512, and maintaining the hold within the permissible 15-business-day initial period. Contacting Adult Protective Services further demonstrates the firm's commitment to senior investor protection consistent with FINRA guidance.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2165 (Financial Exploitation of Specified Adults); FINRA Rule 4512 (Trusted Contact Person); FINRA Regulatory Notice 22-09 (Senior Investor Protections)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm appropriately utilized FINRA Rule 2165's temporary hold provisions based on a reasonable belief of financial exploitation, followed all required notification procedures, and acted within the permitted timeframes.
