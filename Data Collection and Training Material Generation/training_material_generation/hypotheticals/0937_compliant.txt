Topic: compliant_suitability_documentation_conservative_client

Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Registered representative Alicia Fontaine recommended a diversified portfolio of investment-grade municipal bonds and dividend-paying blue-chip equities to her 68-year-old client, a retired engineer with $900,000 in investable assets, a moderate-conservative risk tolerance, and a stated objective of income generation with modest capital appreciation. Before making the recommendation, Fontaine conducted a thorough needs analysis, updated the client's account profile to reflect recent changes in his health expenses, and documented her rationale in a written investment proposal reviewed by her branch manager. The client acknowledged the proposal in writing, and the recommended allocation was consistent with the firm's model portfolios for clients in his demographic and risk category.

QUESTION
--------
Did Fontaine's recommendation and documentation process comply with FINRA suitability requirements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Rule 2111 requires that recommendations be based on a reasonable assessment of the customer's investment profile, including risk tolerance, financial situation, tax status, and investment objectives, all of which Fontaine documented and updated prior to making her recommendation. The alignment of the recommended products with the client's income objective and moderate-conservative risk tolerance, supported by a written proposal reviewed by supervisory personnel, satisfies both the customer-specific and reasonable-basis prongs of Rule 2111. Supervisory review by the branch manager reflects compliance with the firm's Rule 3110 obligations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability); FINRA Rule 3110 (Supervision)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Thorough pre-recommendation profiling, updated account documentation, written rationale, and branch manager review collectively demonstrate a textbook-compliant suitability process.
