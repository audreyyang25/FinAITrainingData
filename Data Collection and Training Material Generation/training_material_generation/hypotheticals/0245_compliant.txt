FACTS
-----
Edward Jones advisor counsels 72-year-old widow, $680K liquid assets, seeking stable income and capital preservation. Advisor recommends $300K annualized income from variable annuity: Nationwide SecureSelect VA (3.2% annual fees; 7-year surrender charge; 6% annual withdrawal cap). Competitive option: DIY bond ladder (municipal + Treasury, 0.30% custodial fee; full liquidity; 3.5% yield). Edward Jones receives 6% front-end commission on VA sale ($18K) plus 0.85% annual trail. Advisor's written disclosure explicitly states: (1) Commission amount and percentage; (2) Annuity's surrender period and fee structure; (3) Liquidity restrictions vs. bond ladder; (4) Documented analysis that VA's guaranteed income and death benefit justify higher costs for widow's specific goal (avoid outliving assets, not maximize returns); (5) Offering of alternative (bond ladder) and client's written selection of VA after full explanation. Compliance documentation shows no sales contests or incentive bonuses for VA sales that month.

QUESTION
--------
Does Edward Jones's variable annuity recommendation with full commission disclosure and documented analysis satisfy Reg BI's Conflict of Interest Obligation?

ANSWER
------
Yes. Reg BI §240.15l-1(a)(2)(ii) requires identification + disclosure + mitigation. The advisor satisfied all three: (1) Identified the 6% commission conflict; (2) Disclosed the specific dollar amount ($18K) and trail percentage (0.85%); (3) Mitigated by documenting why the VA's guaranteed income and death-benefit features were material to the widow's stated goal (longevity risk management), justifying the higher 3.2% fee versus 0.30% bond ladder. The differentiation is key: when a product's unique features (guaranteed lifetime income, death benefit, insurance protection) align with client's specific risk profile and goals, higher fees are justified. The absence of sales contests or special incentive bonuses that month, plus the documented offer of a lower-cost alternative and client's affirmative choice, further support mitigation. FINRA and SEC have endorsed annuity recommendations when the conflict is disclosed and the unique benefits are documented as meeting specific client needs.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(ii); disclosure + mitigation through documented needs analysis

KEY FACTOR
----------
Commission conflict mitigated when unique product features (guaranteed income, death benefit) are documented as meeting specific client goal and disclosed in dollar terms.
