Source Folder: 18_OCC_Bank_Trust_Guidance
Topic: bank_fiduciary
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Lakewood National Bank serves as trustee for a $4.4 million revocable living trust for Patricia Okonkwo, age 66, who has named the bank as successor trustee upon her incapacity. The bank's trust department proposes to allocate 15% of the trust to Lakewood's proprietary balanced mutual fund. Before executing the transaction, the trust committee conducts and documents a formal conflict-of-interest review, determines the fund's 0.55% expense ratio is competitive with comparable third-party options at 0.52%–0.61%, discloses the affiliated fund relationship and the bank's indirect revenue benefit in a written notice delivered to Ms. Okonkwo, and obtains her written informed consent. The investment policy statement is updated to reflect the allocation rationale, and the committee minutes document the alternative products considered and rejected.

QUESTION
--------
Does Lakewood National Bank's investment of trust assets in its affiliated proprietary fund, following full disclosure and written client consent, comply with OCC fiduciary conflict-of-interest requirements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The OCC Conflicts of Interest handbook recognizes that affiliated product investments are permissible when the bank complies with applicable authorization requirements, which include full written disclosure of the conflict and the financial benefit to the bank, documented comparison of the affiliated product's terms against comparable alternatives, and informed client consent. Lakewood's process satisfied each of these elements: the expense ratio comparison demonstrated the affiliated fund was priced within a competitive range, the written disclosure and consent document addressed the conflict specifically, and the investment policy statement update evidenced a sound fiduciary process. The trust committee's documented deliberation and minute-keeping further demonstrates the procedural rigor OCC examiners look for under asset management operations and controls guidance.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
OCC Comptroller's Handbook – Conflicts of Interest (AM-CI), Version 1.0 (January 2015); 12 CFR 9.12(d) – Affiliated Product Authorization Exception

KEY FACTOR
----------
Full written disclosure of the affiliated fund relationship, documented competitive pricing analysis, and affirmative informed consent from the grantor converted what would otherwise be an impermissible self-dealing transaction into a compliant affiliated product allocation under OCC authorization exception procedures.
