Source Folder: 17_SEC_Staff_Bulletins
Topic: conflict_mitigation
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Coastal Wealth Advisors, a dually registered broker-dealer and investment adviser, employs representatives who receive a 40-basis-point revenue-sharing payment from Thornbridge Asset Management whenever they recommend Thornbridge mutual funds to retail clients. The firm discloses this arrangement on page 11 of a 34-page Form CRS addendum in dense legal language without quantifying the dollar impact on a typical account. In 2022, representative Dana Lourdes recommended the Thornbridge Balanced Growth Fund to a 62-year-old retired schoolteacher with a $280,000 IRA rollover, noting its '5-star Morningstar rating,' but did not discuss three comparably rated funds with lower expense ratios and no revenue-sharing arrangements. The firm's written supervisory procedures identify the revenue-sharing arrangement but instruct supervisors only to 'note the conflict' in file reviews rather than to evaluate whether alternative funds were considered.

QUESTION
--------
Did Coastal Wealth Advisors and representative Dana Lourdes satisfy the Conflict of Interest obligation under Reg BI and the investment adviser duty of loyalty when recommending the Thornbridge fund?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of the Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation and the investment adviser fiduciary duty of loyalty.

The SEC's 2023 Conflicts Staff Bulletin and the IAA Conflicts CLE guidance emphasize that mere disclosure of a conflict is insufficient — firms must identify, manage, and mitigate conflicts such that they do not place the firm's financial interest ahead of the retail customer's best interest. Burying the revenue-sharing disclosure in a lengthy addendum without quantification does not constitute full and fair disclosure, and the failure to evaluate comparable lower-cost alternatives with no revenue-sharing violates the care and conflict obligations simultaneously. The firm's WSPs that instruct supervisors only to 'note' conflicts without requiring affirmative evaluation of alternatives are facially deficient under SEC examination findings.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iii) – Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation; Investment Advisers Act Section 206 Fiduciary Duty of Loyalty as interpreted in the 2019 SEC Standard of Conduct Interpretation

KEY FACTOR
----------
Undisclosed financial incentive from revenue-sharing was not mitigated, and no analysis of comparable lower-cost alternatives was documented or performed.
