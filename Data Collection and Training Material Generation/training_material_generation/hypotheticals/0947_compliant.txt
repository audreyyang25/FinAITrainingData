FACTS
-----
Broker Sandra Nguyen filed for expungement of a customer complaint after a customer alleged unauthorized trading that, upon full arbitration on the merits, was found by a three-arbitrator panel to be factually impossible because brokerage records conclusively established that the customer had provided written authorization for all disputed transactions. FINRA was named as an additional party, and the customer received proper notice and was given a meaningful opportunity to participate in the expungement hearing. The arbitration panel issued a detailed written explanation citing specific documentary evidence supporting the factual impossibility finding before recommending expungement.

QUESTION
--------
Does this expungement proceeding satisfy FINRA's procedural and substantive requirements for recommending expungement of a customer complaint from the CRD?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant with FINRA Rule 2080, Rule 12805, and FINRA's expungement standards requiring a specific qualifying finding supported by documentary evidence.

The expungement recommendation satisfies FINRA Rule 2080's substantive threshold because the panel made an affirmative finding of factual impossibility supported by concrete documentary evidence in the form of written customer authorizations, which is among the enumerated qualifying grounds for expungement. Procedural compliance was achieved by naming FINRA as an additional party, providing the complaining customer with notice and a meaningful opportunity to be heard, and issuing a detailed written explanation as required by Rule 12805. The combination of a qualifying substantive finding, proper notice to affected parties, and documented reasoning reflects the procedural rigor PIABA and FINRA have advocated as the minimum acceptable standard for expungement recommendations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2080; FINRA Code of Arbitration Procedure Rule 12805; FINRA Regulatory Notice 17-42

KEY FACTOR
----------
The panel's documented finding of factual impossibility, supported by written authorization records and accompanied by proper customer notice, satisfies both the substantive and procedural requirements for a valid expungement recommendation.
