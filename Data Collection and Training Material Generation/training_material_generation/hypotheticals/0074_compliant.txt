FACTS
-----
A 62-year-old client with $1.2 million in investable assets stated his objective as 'preserve capital with inflation protection.' The adviser recommended a portfolio of 30% dividend aristocrat stocks, 40% investment-grade bonds, 20% Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS), and 10% commodities ETFs. The adviser documented the client's risk tolerance as 'conservative,' conducted a detailed financial plan showing the allocation would meet his retirement income needs, and established a quarterly rebalancing schedule with documented review memos.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation comply with the duty of care?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this recommendation complies with the investment adviser's duty of care.

The adviser has satisfied the duty of care through: (1) documented understanding of the client's stated objective ('preserve capital with inflation protection'); (2) a reasonable allocation (60% bonds/TIPS/dividends with 30% growth for inflation protection, 10% in commodities for diversification) that balances preservation with inflation protection; (3) explicit documentation that the allocation meets the client's retirement income needs based on a financial plan; (4) formal establishment of a quarterly rebalancing schedule; and (5) documented review memos. The 30% in dividend stocks and TIPS directly addresses the inflation-protection component of the objective, while the 60% in bonds and TIPS provides capital preservation. This demonstrates both reasonable investigation and a reasonable basis for the recommendation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940, Section 206(4); SEC IA compliance standards for reasonable basis and suitability.

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser's documented financial plan showing the allocation meets retirement objectives, combined with established periodic rebalancing schedule and documented reviews, demonstrates satisfaction of reasonable investigation and periodic review requirements.
