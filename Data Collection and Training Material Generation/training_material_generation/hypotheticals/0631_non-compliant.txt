Topic: compliance_program_design
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A dual-registered broker-dealer and investment adviser firm with 120 associated persons missed the June 30, 2020 Reg BI compliance date without having implemented any written procedures or training programs to assess recommendations under a best interest standard. The firm continued making recommendations to retail customers using its pre-existing suitability framework. The firm's CCO acknowledged in an internal email that the firm had not yet 'gotten around to' updating its compliance manual or training materials to address the Care, Disclosure, Conflict of Interest, and Compliance component obligations. During a subsequent OCIE examination in September 2020, examiners found no evidence of any Reg BI policies, procedures, or associated person training.

QUESTION
--------
Did the firm comply with its obligations under Reg BI by continuing to operate under its prior suitability framework after the June 30, 2020 compliance date?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of the Reg BI Compliance Obligation and the overarching best interest standard.

Reg BI's Compliance Obligation requires broker-dealers to establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with Reg BI as a whole. As the FINRA Reg BI Checklist specifically notes, firms must have 'procedures and training in place to assess recommendations using a best interest standard' and that the best interest standard 'is satisfied only if you comply with four component obligations: Care, Disclosure, Conflict of Interest and Compliance.' Operating under the prior suitability framework alone is insufficient; the firm failed to satisfy the foundational compliance infrastructure requirement by the mandated compliance date.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iv) – Reg BI Compliance Obligation; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(1) – General Best Interest Obligation

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm completely failed to establish any Reg BI policies, procedures, or training by the June 30, 2020 compliance date, leaving its associated persons without guidance on the new best interest standard.
