ID: 798
Source Folder: 15_FINRA_Regulatory_Notices
Topic: reg_bi_implementation
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A large broker-dealer recruits a top-producing team of four financial advisors from a competitor, offering an aggregate transition package of $2.8 million payable over four years. Upon the team's arrival, the firm delivers to each of the team's 380 transferring clients an educational communication that discloses the existence and amount of the recruitment compensation package, explains that the package creates a financial incentive for the advisors to recommend account transfers, and details specific conflicts of interest including the possibility that clients may incur costs or lose access to proprietary products from their prior firm. The firm also provides each client with its Form CRS and updated Reg BI disclosures. The firm's compliance department conducts enhanced monitoring of the transferred accounts for the first 12 months, specifically reviewing for unsuitable product changes, unnecessary transactions, or recommendations that appear driven by the advisors' compensation incentives rather than client interests.

QUESTION
--------
Does the firm's handling of the recruitment transition and associated conflict disclosures comply with Reg BI and FINRA requirements regarding recruitment practices?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

FINRA Regulatory Notice 15-19 specifically addresses the conflict of interest created by large recruitment compensation packages and the need for educational communications to transferring clients. The firm's disclosure of the recruitment package amount, the specific conflicts it creates, and the potential costs to clients satisfies both the Reg BI Disclosure Obligation and the spirit of FINRA's guidance on recruitment practices. The enhanced 12-month monitoring of transferred accounts demonstrates the firm's reasonable efforts to mitigate the identified conflicts under Reg BI's Conflict of Interest Obligation. The delivery of Form CRS and updated Reg BI disclosures further ensures full transparency.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iii) – Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation; FINRA Regulatory Notice 15-19 (Recruitment Practices)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm fully disclosed the recruitment compensation package and associated conflicts while implementing enhanced supervisory monitoring to mitigate the identified incentive-driven risks to transferred clients.
