Topic: ia_fiduciary_duty
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
An RIA managing a discretionary account for a 72-year-old retired client with $1.5M in assets and low risk tolerance recommends a diversified portfolio of low-cost index funds (weighted 60% stocks, 40% bonds) with a blended expense ratio of 0.18%. The RIA discloses its standard AUM-based fee of 0.75% in writing at account inception and has no conflicts of interest. The allocation matches the client's stated risk tolerance and time horizon.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation satisfy the Investment Adviser Act fiduciary duty of loyalty and care?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this recommendation complies with the fiduciary duties under the Investment Adviser Act.

The adviser satisfies both the duty of loyalty and duty of care. The recommendation prioritizes the client's interests (low-cost, diversified index funds aligned with a retired, risk-averse investor's profile) over the adviser's own interests. No undisclosed conflicts exist, and the standard AUM fee is disclosed and reasonable. The duty of care is satisfied because the adviser selected investments that align with the client's risk tolerance, time horizon, and demonstrated financial objectives. The index-based, low-cost approach reflects a reasoned investment process that avoids high-cost products that would unjustifiably reduce the client's returns. No self-dealing, cherry-picking, or hidden compensation arrangements are present.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Adviser Act §206 (fiduciary duty); SEC Regulation (IA) Rule 206(4)-7 (compliance rule); SEC Dodd-Frank guidance on adviser duty of loyalty and care (duty to act in client's best interest and exercise reasonable care).

KEY FACTOR
----------
Adviser prioritizes client's best interest (low-cost diversified portfolio suited to client's age and risk tolerance), discloses all material facts and conflicts, and charges a reasonable, transparent fee.
