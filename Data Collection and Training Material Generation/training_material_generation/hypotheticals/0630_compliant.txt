Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A private equity adviser manages a $1.2 billion flagship fund and a $300 million co-investment vehicle. When a $75 million acquisition opportunity collapses after extensive due diligence, the adviser allocates the $1.8 million in broken deal expenses pro rata based on committed capital across both vehicles: approximately $1.44 million (80%) to the flagship fund and $360,000 (20%) to the co-investment vehicle. The adviser's limited partnership agreements for both funds explicitly describe the pro rata allocation methodology for broken deal expenses, and Form ADV Part 2A includes a detailed discussion of potential conflicts of interest arising from expense allocation among affiliated funds, including the specific methodology used. The adviser's compliance manual includes a written expense allocation policy that is reviewed annually by the CCO, and the allocation decision is documented in a contemporaneous memorandum reviewed by the fund's advisory committee. The advisory committee, composed of independent limited partners, approves the allocation without objection.

QUESTION
--------
Does the adviser's allocation of $1.8 million in broken deal expenses pro rata across both funds, with detailed written policies and disclosure, satisfy its fiduciary duty under the Investment Advisers Act?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The Willkie memorandum on the KKR enforcement action emphasizes that private equity firms must 'maintain detailed expense allocation policies among the funds and accounts they manage' and ensure disclosures adequately describe allocation methodologies and associated conflicts. Here, the adviser uses a transparent, documented pro rata methodology that is disclosed in both the LPA and Form ADV Part 2A, maintained in a written compliance policy reviewed annually, and subject to advisory committee oversight. Unlike the KKR case where expenses were shifted entirely to the external fund without adequate policies or disclosure, this adviser's approach reflects the industry best practices identified by the SEC examination staff: clear policies, specific disclosure, contemporaneous documentation, and independent oversight.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940, Sections 206(1) and 206(2) – Fiduciary Duty; SEC Guidance on Private Fund Adviser Obligations

KEY FACTOR
----------
Pro rata allocation methodology consistently applied, fully disclosed in governing documents and Form ADV, documented contemporaneously, and approved by an independent advisory committee.
