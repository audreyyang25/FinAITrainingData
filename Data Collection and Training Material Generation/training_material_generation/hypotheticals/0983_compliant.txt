Topic: iosco_cross_border_suitability_compliant

Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A multinational asset management firm operating across EU, UK, and Asia-Pacific jurisdictions develops a unified cross-border suitability framework aligned with IOSCO's 2021 Suitability Principles to serve high-net-worth clients holding accounts in multiple regulatory domains. The framework requires jurisdiction-specific overlays: MiFID II suitability reports for EU clients, FCA Consumer Duty outcome monitoring for UK clients, and ASIC-compliant SOAs for Australian clients, with a central data governance system ensuring no client is recommended a product outside their assessed risk profile in any jurisdiction. The firm conducts annual reviews of client risk profiles and retains audit trails accessible to each relevant regulator.

QUESTION
--------
Does the firm's cross-border suitability framework satisfy international conduct standards applicable across its operating jurisdictions?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

IOSCO's 2021 Suitability Principles establish baseline standards requiring firms to collect adequate client information, maintain updated profiles, and ensure recommendations are appropriate to client circumstances, with jurisdiction-specific implementation left to local regulators. The firm's layered framework satisfies IOSCO's cross-border coherence expectations while meeting each domestic regime's prescriptive requirements through jurisdiction-specific overlays, and the annual review cycle with auditable records addresses both the ongoing suitability monitoring obligations under MiFID II and the ASIC periodic review expectations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
IOSCO Suitability Principles 2021 (Principles 1-9); MiFID II Art. 25; FCA Consumer Duty PRIN 12; ASIC Corporations Act §961B

KEY FACTOR
----------
Integrated cross-border framework with jurisdiction-specific compliance overlays, continuous monitoring, and full audit trail satisfying all applicable international and domestic suitability standards.
