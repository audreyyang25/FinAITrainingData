ID: 782
Source Folder: 14_NASAA_State_Enforcement
Topic: state_fraud
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Pinnacle Asset Advisors, LLC, a state-registered investment adviser in California with CRD No. 310822, manages $65 million for 120 clients. The firm's principal, Diana Kowalski, has custody of client funds through her authority to deduct advisory fees directly from client accounts held at a qualified custodian. However, Pinnacle never disclosed its custody status on Form ADV Part 1A, Item 9, and has not arranged for an annual surprise examination by an independent public accountant. Pinnacle also fails to send quarterly account statements to clients, relying instead on the custodian's annual summary. Multiple clients are retirees in their 70s with account balances ranging from $150,000 to $900,000.

QUESTION
--------
Does Pinnacle's failure to disclose custody and arrange for a surprise examination violate NASAA model custody rules?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is not compliant. This constitutes a violation of NASAA Model Rule 102(e)(1)-1 regarding custody of client funds.

Under NASAA Model Rule 102(e)(1)-1(a)(1), an investment adviser with custody must promptly notify the Administrator in writing, including disclosure on Form ADV. Pinnacle's failure to disclose custody on Form ADV Part 1A violates this requirement. Additionally, the rule requires that advisers with custody arrange for an annual surprise examination unless certain narrow exceptions apply. Pinnacle's reliance solely on the custodian's annual summary rather than ensuring clients receive at least quarterly statements further violates the notice-to-clients provisions of the rule. The firm's custody of elderly clients' retirement assets without these safeguards is deemed a fraudulent, deceptive, or manipulative act under the model rule.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NASAA Model Rule 102(e)(1)-1 – Custody of Client Funds or Securities by Investment Advisers, subsections (a)(1), (a)(3), and surprise examination requirements

KEY FACTOR
----------
Pinnacle failed to notify the state administrator of its custody status on Form ADV and did not arrange the required annual surprise examination, violating NASAA Model Rule 102(e)(1)-1.
