Source Folder: 08_State_Fiduciary_Rules
Topic: state_fiduciary_annuity
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A New York-licensed insurance producer recommends that David Kowalski, age 67, a recently retired engineer with $1.2 million in liquid assets and an annual pension of $65,000, allocate $300,000 into a fixed indexed annuity with a 5-year surrender period and a guaranteed lifetime withdrawal benefit rider. David has expressed a primary goal of ensuring guaranteed income to supplement his pension and has moderate risk tolerance. The producer documents David's full financial profile—including existing life insurance, a separate $400,000 IRA, $500,000 in taxable brokerage accounts, and his desire to protect a portion of savings from market downturns. The producer also discloses her 4.5% commission and compares three comparable products from different carriers, selecting the one with the lowest fees and most favorable withdrawal terms for David's situation.

QUESTION
--------
Does this annuity recommendation comply with NY Reg 187's best interest standard despite the producer earning a commission?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

NY Reg 187 requires that annuity recommendations be in the best interest of the consumer, evaluated at the time of the transaction based on the consumer's financial situation, needs, and objectives. The producer gathered comprehensive suitability information, documented the client's profile and needs, compared multiple products, selected the most favorable option, and disclosed the commission. The allocation represents only 25% of the client's liquid assets, preserving adequate liquidity. Earning a commission does not itself violate the standard when the recommendation is otherwise supported by documented best-interest analysis.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NY Reg 187 – Best Interest Standard for Annuity Transactions (11 NYCRR 224)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The producer conducted and documented a thorough best-interest analysis, compared multiple products, disclosed compensation, and recommended an appropriately sized annuity relative to the client's overall financial profile.
