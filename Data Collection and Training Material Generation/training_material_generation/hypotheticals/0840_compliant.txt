Source Folder: 17_SEC_Staff_Bulletins
Topic: care_obligation_detail
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A broker-dealer representative recommends that 71-year-old widow Frances Delacroix, who has $180,000 in a rollover IRA, a Social Security income of $1,850 per month, and no other liquid assets, purchase a fixed indexed annuity with a 7-year surrender schedule, a 0% commission in year one due to a firm promotional program, and a guaranteed income rider providing $1,100 per month beginning at age 73. The representative completes a Best Interest Analysis documenting that Ms. Delacroix's primary concern is longevity risk and income stability, that she has no anticipated need for liquidity beyond her Social Security income based on a detailed expense analysis, that the guaranteed income rider supplements her Social Security to cover essential expenses, and that three alternative income-focused products — a CD ladder, a bond mutual fund, and an immediate annuity — were evaluated and rejected with specific written rationale. Form CRS was delivered prior to the recommendation, and the firm's conflicts disclosure noted the representative earns no upfront commission under the promotional structure.

QUESTION
--------
Did the representative satisfy Reg BI's Care Obligation and Disclosure Obligation when recommending the fixed indexed annuity with a surrender schedule to Ms. Delacroix?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The Care Obligation requires that a recommendation be in the best interest of the retail customer based on their investment profile, including time horizon, liquidity needs, and financial situation — it does not categorically prohibit annuities with surrender schedules for elderly clients. The SEC's staff guidance acknowledges that annuities can be appropriate for retirement-income-focused customers when liquidity needs are demonstrably low and income guarantees address documented financial objectives. Here, the representative conducted a documented liquidity analysis, evaluated and rejected three alternative products with written rationale, and selected a product with no upfront commission conflict. Form CRS delivery and specific conflicts disclosure further support compliance with the Disclosure Obligation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i)(ii) – Reg BI Care and Disclosure Obligations; SEC Staff Bulletin on Care Obligation (Nov. 2021)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The representative's documented comparison of alternatives, liquidity analysis, and zero-commission promotional structure demonstrate genuine best interest analysis rather than self-interested product selection.
