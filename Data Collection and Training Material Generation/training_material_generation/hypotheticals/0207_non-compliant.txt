Source Folder: unknown
Topic: reg_bi_care_expanded
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
FINRA member firm recommended a non-traded REIT (Inland Diversified Fund IX, 6.85% upfront sales charge, 1.95% annual ER, 7-year illiquidity) to a 62-year-old client with $350,000 liquid net worth, stable $85,000 annual income, and stated goal of near-term (5-year) down payment on vacation home. The advisor documented that "diversification beyond stocks" was the reason for the recommendation, and that the client's risk tolerance was "moderate to moderately aggressive." No cost comparison analysis was documented. A liquid REIT ETF alternative existed (VNQ, 0.12% ER, daily liquidity) that would have achieved diversification at 1/57th the cost with full liquidity.

QUESTION
--------
Did the FINRA member advisor comply with Reg BI care obligation and FINRA Rule 2111 suitability regarding the non-traded REIT recommendation?

ANSWER
------
No. Reg BI's care obligation (§240.15l-1(a)(2)(ii)) requires the advisor to "carefully consider" the nature of the customer's investment objectives and financial situation, including the customer's time horizon for the investment. The documented 5-year down payment goal directly conflicts with the 7-year illiquidity and 6.85% upfront charge. The failure to document why the liquid, lower-cost alternative (VNQ) was rejected violates the care obligation's requirement to consider and document suitability of the product type and costs. FINRA Rule 2111 requires suitability analysis based on customer profile; recommending illiquid products to near-term liquidity-dependent clients is per se unsuitable. This pattern mirrors SEC/FINRA enforcement against non-traded REIT sales to unsuitable retail clients (similar to 2019–2021 non-traded REIT enforcement sweep).

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Reg BI Care Obligation (17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(ii)) and FINRA Rule 2111 Suitability

KEY FACTOR
----------
Recommending 7-year illiquid product to client with documented 5-year liquidity need violates both Reg BI and FINRA Rule 2111.
