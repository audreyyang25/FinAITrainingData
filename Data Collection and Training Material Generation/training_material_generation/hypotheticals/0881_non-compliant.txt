ID: 881
Source Folder: 19_CFTC_Guidance
Topic: cta_cpo_suitability
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Apex Fund Management LLC is a registered CPO operating a commodity pool under CFTC Rule 4.7, which limits pool participation to Qualified Eligible Persons (QEPs). Following the CFTC's October 2023 proposed rulemaking to amend Rule 4.7 and increase QEP financial thresholds to reflect inflation, Apex admits a new limited partner who satisfies the prior Portfolio Requirement (holding a $200,000 portfolio of securities and other investments) but does not meet the proposed updated threshold. Apex proceeds with the admission without providing any disclosure document, relying entirely on the existing 4.7 exemption from full disclosure requirements. The investor is a 45-year-old software engineer with no prior commodity pool experience who was told only that the fund 'trades futures across multiple asset classes.'

QUESTION
--------
Does Apex Fund Management's admission of a participant without any disclosure document and without confirming compliance with updated QEP thresholds satisfy its CPO obligations under Rule 4.7 and the proposed minimum disclosure requirements?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a disclosure and eligibility verification violation under CFTC Rule 4.7 and the October 2023 proposed minimum disclosure rulemaking.

Rule 4.7's exemption from full disclosure requirements is conditioned on participants qualifying as QEPs; the 2023 proposed rulemaking specifically proposes increasing the Portfolio Requirement thresholds to reflect inflation, signaling heightened eligibility scrutiny. Even under current Rule 4.7, a CPO operating a 4.7 Fund must confirm QEP status at admission and the proposed rulemaking would impose minimum disclosure obligations on 4.7 pools. Providing no disclosure whatsoever to an inexperienced investor and failing to verify updated eligibility thresholds exposes Apex to CPO registration and disclosure violations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
CFTC Rule 4.7 – Conditional Exemptions for CPOs; CFTC Proposed Rulemaking (October 2, 2023) – Minimum Disclosure Requirements for 4.7 Funds

KEY FACTOR
----------
Apex admitted a participant without verifying QEP eligibility under the inflation-adjusted thresholds and provided zero disclosure documentation required even under the conditional 4.7 exemption.
