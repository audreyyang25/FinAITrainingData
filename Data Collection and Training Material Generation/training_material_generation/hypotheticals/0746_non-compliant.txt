ID: 746
Source Folder: 13_FINRA_Rulebook
Topic: variable_annuity_rule
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A registered representative at a mid-sized broker-dealer recommends that Martha Jennings, a 78-year-old retired schoolteacher with $180,000 in liquid savings, a fixed income of $2,100/month from Social Security, and a conservative risk tolerance, exchange her existing deferred variable annuity (which is past its surrender period) for a new deferred variable annuity with a 7-year surrender schedule and an annual mortality and expense charge of 1.45%. The new annuity offers a guaranteed minimum income benefit rider that the representative highlights, but no comparison of the existing annuity's subaccount performance, fees, or existing riders is documented. The representative's branch manager approves the transaction the same day it is submitted without requesting any documentation of the rationale for the exchange.

QUESTION
--------
Did the registered representative and the firm satisfy their obligations under FINRA Rule 2330 (formerly Rule 2821) regarding the recommended exchange of a deferred variable annuity?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates FINRA Rule 2330's suitability and principal review requirements for deferred variable annuity exchanges.

FINRA Rule 2330 requires that before recommending an exchange of a deferred variable annuity, the registered representative must have a reasonable basis to believe the exchange is suitable after considering, among other factors, whether the customer would incur a new surrender period, the comparative costs and benefits of the existing and proposed annuities, and the customer's age, liquidity needs, and risk tolerance. Here, no documented comparison of the existing annuity's features, riders, or fees was performed, and the client's advanced age and limited liquid assets raise serious suitability concerns about locking funds into a new 7-year surrender period. Additionally, the principal's same-day approval without requesting exchange documentation violates the Rule's requirement that a registered principal review and approve the specific reasons for the exchange before the transaction is processed.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2330 (Deferred Variable Annuities) – Suitability and Principal Review Obligations

KEY FACTOR
----------
The representative failed to document any comparative analysis of the existing versus proposed variable annuity, and the principal approved the exchange without reviewing the required suitability factors, violating Rule 2330's exchange-specific obligations.
