Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Greenleaf Capital, a FINRA member firm, receives a recommended transaction from registered representative Alicia Torres to purchase $250,000 in investment-grade corporate bond funds for her client, National Teachers Pension Fund, an institutional account with $480 million in assets under management. Prior to executing the recommendation, Alicia confirms in writing that the Fund meets the definition of an institutional account under FINRA Rule 4512(c), having total assets of at least $50 million. The Fund's chief investment officer signs an Institutional Suitability Certificate affirming that the institution is capable of evaluating investment risks independently, both in general and with regard to the specific recommended strategy, and that it will exercise independent judgment in evaluating Alicia's recommendations. Alicia maintains this certificate on file along with documentation of the Fund's investment policy statement and her discussion of the recommendation's rationale with the CIO.

QUESTION
--------
Has the registered representative satisfied her suitability obligations under FINRA Rule 2111 with respect to this institutional customer recommendation?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Under FINRA Rule 2111(b), a member firm's customer-specific suitability obligation is deemed fulfilled for an institutional account if the firm has a reasonable basis to believe that the institutional customer is capable of evaluating investment risks independently and the customer affirmatively indicates that it is exercising independent judgment. Here, the Fund qualifies as an institutional account under Rule 4512(c), the CIO executed an Institutional Suitability Certificate affirming both independent evaluation capability and the exercise of independent judgment, and Alicia documented the recommendation rationale and maintained the certificate on file. This appropriately satisfies the institutional suitability safe harbor.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111(b) – Institutional Account Exemption; FINRA Rule 4512(c) – Institutional Account Definition

KEY FACTOR
----------
The representative properly obtained and documented a written affirmation from the qualified institutional customer that it was capable of independently evaluating investment risks and would exercise independent judgment, satisfying FINRA Rule 2111(b)'s institutional suitability exemption.
