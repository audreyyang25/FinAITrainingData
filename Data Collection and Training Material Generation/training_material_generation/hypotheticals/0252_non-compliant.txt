Source Folder: unknown
Topic: form_crs_disclosure
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
InvestAdvisor LLC, an SEC-registered investment adviser managing $850 million in assets, updated its Form CRS on September 1, 2023. The form disclosed that the firm charges 'tiered advisory fees ranging from 0.75% to 1.25% based on account size,' but omitted that in July 2023, the firm had begun offering proprietary mutual funds managed by an affiliate, earning 12 basis points in revenue-sharing payments per $1 million of client assets invested in those funds. The Form CRS stated: 'We recommend products in your best interest.' Existing client Thomas Chen, age 62, with a $2.1 million account, was never notified of this material conflict. On February 14, 2024, his relationship advisor (earning variable comp based on assets in proprietary funds) recommended moving 60% of his equity allocation ($756,000) into the affiliate's index funds, which carried expense ratios of 0.34% vs. comparable third-party funds at 0.18%.

QUESTION
--------
Did InvestAdvisor's Form CRS adequately disclose conflicts of interest in violation of Reg BI disclosure standards?

ANSWER
------
No. Form CRS must include clear disclosure of material conflicts of interest that could affect the firm's recommendations. SEC guidance (IA-5653) requires that all conflicts material to the client-adviser relationship be disclosed, including proprietary product conflicts and revenue-sharing arrangements. Here, the undisclosed revenue-sharing payments (12 bps × $2.1M = ~$2,520 annual conflict) and advisor variable compensation tied to proprietary fund sales constitute material conflicts omitted from Form CRS. The generic statement 'in your best interest' masks specific conflicts. The failure to update Form CRS after the July 2023 affiliate arrangement launch, and the subsequent biased recommendation (higher-cost proprietary funds), demonstrates that the omission was material and resulted in harm. This pattern is similar to SEC enforcement actions addressing inadequate conflict disclosure.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Reg BI, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(c)(1)(vi) (Form CRS must disclose conflicts of interest); SEC Marketing Rule (IA-5653), Form CRS Instructions, Question 5 (material conflicts disclosure)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Failure to disclose material revenue-sharing conflicts and advisor compensation tied to proprietary product sales in Form CRS, combined with failure to update after the conflict arose.
