ID: 969
Source Folder: 23_International_Comparative
Topic: mifid_ii_suitability_assessment_omission
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A UK-based EU-passported investment firm's adviser recommends a high-yield emerging market bond fund to a retail client without gathering any information about the client's financial situation, investment objectives, or risk tolerance. The adviser tells the client the product has 'strong recent returns' and processes the transaction the same day. No suitability report is prepared or provided to the client. The firm's compliance team is unaware the recommendation was made.

QUESTION
--------
Does the adviser's conduct satisfy MiFID II suitability requirements when making a personal recommendation to a retail client?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of MiFID II Article 25(2) suitability obligations.

MiFID II Article 25(2) requires investment firms to obtain necessary information regarding a client's knowledge, experience, financial situation, and investment objectives before making a personal recommendation. The adviser made a personal recommendation without conducting any suitability assessment and failed to provide the mandatory suitability report required under Article 25(6), rendering the entire transaction non-compliant.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
MiFID II Art. 25(2); MiFID II Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/565 Arts. 54-55

KEY FACTOR
----------
Complete failure to gather client suitability information and absence of a suitability report prior to a personal recommendation.
