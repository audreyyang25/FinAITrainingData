Topic: predispute_arbitration_clause_waiver_of_class_action
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Brokerage firm Meridian Securities included a predispute arbitration clause in its customer account agreement that waived the customer's right to participate in any class action lawsuit and required individual FINRA arbitration for all disputes, including claims arising under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934. A customer named Linda Torres sought to join a class action against Meridian for widespread fraudulent misrepresentation affecting hundreds of similarly situated investors. Meridian moved to compel individual arbitration under the clause, citing the Federal Arbitration Act, and the firm made no disclosure to Torres at account opening that the clause waived class action rights.

QUESTION
--------
Is the enforcement of a predispute arbitration clause that prospectively waives Exchange Act rights to class action participation in a FINRA member's customer agreement compliant with applicable securities law?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of Securities Exchange Act Section 29(a), which voids any provision waiving compliance with the Exchange Act, and conflicts with investor protection principles regarding predispute arbitration clauses.

Section 29(a) of the Securities Exchange Act renders void any contractual provision that purports to waive compliance with any provision of the Act, and courts have recognized that class action waivers embedded in predispute arbitration clauses may impair substantive Exchange Act rights when they effectively preclude vindication of those rights. FINRA Rule 2268 additionally requires member firms to disclose in predispute arbitration clauses that the customer is waiving the right to seek remedies in court, including the right to jury trial, and prohibits clauses that limit Exchange Act-based claims in ways inconsistent with FINRA rules. The absence of meaningful disclosure at account opening compounds the violation by depriving Torres of informed consent.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Securities Exchange Act §29(a); FINRA Rule 2268; PIABA advocacy on predispute arbitration clause limitations

KEY FACTOR
----------
Predispute arbitration clauses cannot prospectively waive Exchange Act rights or eliminate class action access where doing so would prevent effective vindication of statutory securities claims.
