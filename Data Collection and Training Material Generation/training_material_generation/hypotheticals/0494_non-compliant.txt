Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Denise Park is an investment adviser representative at Summit Wealth Advisors, a registered investment adviser. She manages a discretionary account for Thomas Chen, a 55-year-old engineer with $1.2 million in retirement assets and a moderate growth objective. Over the past 18 months, Denise executes 147 trades in Thomas's account, generating $38,000 in soft-dollar credits that Summit uses to pay for market data terminals and Bloomberg subscriptions used across the entire firm—not solely for the benefit of Thomas's account. Denise does not disclose the soft-dollar arrangement to Thomas, nor does Summit's Form ADV Part 2A contain any description of the firm's soft-dollar practices. Thomas only becomes aware of the arrangement when he receives a detailed transaction cost analysis from an independent consultant.

QUESTION
--------
Did Summit Wealth Advisors and Denise Park comply with their fiduciary obligations under the Investment Advisers Act regarding the undisclosed soft-dollar arrangement?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of the Investment Advisers Act fiduciary duty, specifically the duty of loyalty and the obligation to disclose material conflicts of interest.

Under the Investment Advisers Act's fiduciary duty, an adviser must disclose all material conflicts of interest to clients. Soft-dollar arrangements in which trading activity in a client's account generates benefits used firm-wide represent a material conflict because the adviser has an incentive to trade more frequently than necessary. The failure to describe the soft-dollar practices in Form ADV Part 2A and the complete absence of client-level disclosure violate both the duty of loyalty and the specific disclosure requirements of Item 12 of Form ADV Part 2A. The 147 trades over 18 months also raise questions about whether the trading level was consistent with the client's moderate growth objective.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940, Sections 206(1) and 206(2); SEC Interpretive Release IA-1092 (Soft Dollar Practices); Form ADV Part 2A Item 12

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser failed to disclose a material soft-dollar conflict of interest in either the Form ADV or direct client communications, while generating firm-wide benefits from a single client's trading activity.
