Topic: bank_fiduciary
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Meridian National Bank administers a $9.8 million employee benefit plan CIF investing primarily in Guaranteed Investment Contracts issued by highly rated, unaffiliated insurance carriers. The bank charges differential management fees to CIF participants: larger plan accounts contributing over $1 million pay 35 basis points annually, while smaller accounts under $250,000 pay 55 basis points, reflecting the substantially greater custody, reporting, recordkeeping, and participant communication services provided to smaller plans. The fee schedule is fully disclosed in the CIF plan document filed with the OCC, and the bank's fiduciary review committee documented that the fee differential is strictly commensurate with the differing scope of services rendered to each participant tier, with no cross-subsidization of bank proprietary products.

QUESTION
--------
Is Meridian National Bank's practice of charging differential management fees to CIF participants based on account size and service level permissible under OCC fiduciary standards?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

OCC Interpretive Letter #829 expressly confirms that a national bank may charge CIF participants different fund management fees commensurate with the amount and types of services provided to each participant, provided the fee differential reflects genuine service distinctions rather than arbitrary or self-serving distinctions. Here, the bank documented that smaller plan accounts require materially greater per-account administrative services, justifying the higher fee tier, and the arrangement is fully disclosed in the OCC-filed CIF plan document. The bank's fiduciary review committee's documented analysis satisfies the duty of care under OCC Interpretive Letter #734, which requires fiduciaries to exercise reasonable care and skill and act solely in the best interest of beneficiaries, as the fee structure serves participant accounts rather than generating improper bank revenue.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
OCC Interpretive Letter #829 – 12 C.F.R. 9.18; OCC Interpretive Letter #734, Part 2 – Duty of Care and Loyalty

KEY FACTOR
----------
The fee differential is permissible because it is strictly commensurate with documented service distinctions, fully disclosed in the CIF plan document, and supported by independent fiduciary committee review—meeting the conditions established in OCC Interpretive Letter #829.
