Topic: reg_bi_conflict
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A mid-sized broker-dealer with 110 registered representatives sells both proprietary mutual funds and third-party mutual funds to retail investors. The firm's proprietary funds carry an expense ratio of 0.65% and pay the firm a revenue-sharing fee of 15 basis points, while comparable third-party funds have expense ratios ranging from 0.55% to 0.70% with no revenue-sharing arrangement. The firm has identified this conflict of interest in its written policies, discloses the revenue-sharing arrangement and the differential compensation in its Form CRS and in a standalone conflict-of-interest disclosure provided at account opening. The firm has implemented a product-neutral compensation grid so that registered representatives receive identical payout percentages regardless of whether they recommend proprietary or third-party funds. The firm's compliance department conducts quarterly reviews of recommendation patterns to detect any skewing toward proprietary products.

QUESTION
--------
Does the firm satisfy its Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation despite receiving revenue-sharing payments on its proprietary mutual funds?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The Conflict of Interest Obligation does not prohibit all conflicts; rather, it requires firms to identify and disclose material conflicts, and to establish policies and procedures reasonably designed to mitigate or eliminate conflicts that create incentives for representatives to place their own interests ahead of customers'. Here, the firm has identified the revenue-sharing conflict, disclosed it prominently in Form CRS and at account opening, neutralized the representative-level incentive through a product-neutral compensation grid, and implemented ongoing surveillance to detect recommendation skewing. These measures collectively satisfy the Conflict of Interest Obligation's requirements to identify, disclose, and mitigate.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iii) – Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm's combination of transparent disclosure, product-neutral representative compensation, and ongoing surveillance of recommendation patterns constitutes a reasonably designed framework to mitigate the proprietary-product revenue-sharing conflict.
