Source Folder: 09_Law_Firm_Memos
Topic: compliance_advisory
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A dually registered financial advisor recommends that a 55-year-old corporate executive with $1.2 million in investable assets and a moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance roll over $650,000 from a former employer's 401(k) plan into an IRA held at the advisor's broker-dealer. The advisor documents a detailed comparison of the 401(k) plan's institutional share class funds (averaging 12 basis points) against the IRA's proposed allocation of low-cost ETFs (averaging 9 basis points) and a small allocation to actively managed funds (averaging 55 basis points, blended IRA cost of 22 basis points). The advisor's written analysis addresses the broader investment menu, tax implications, Roth conversion flexibility, estate planning benefits, and the loss of 401(k) creditor protections. The advisor discloses the 25-basis-point advisory fee applicable to the IRA and provides a Form CRS. The recommendation is reviewed and approved by the branch manager.

QUESTION
--------
Did the financial advisor satisfy Regulation Best Interest and the investment adviser duty of care in recommending the IRA rollover?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The advisor performed a thorough, documented cost-benefit analysis comparing the existing 401(k) plan to the proposed IRA, including cost comparisons, investment menu breadth, tax flexibility, and creditor protection trade-offs — precisely the type of analysis SEC staff bulletins and the Fiduciary Interpretation's duty of care framework contemplate for rollover recommendations. The blended cost in the IRA is reasonable, the Form CRS was delivered, compensation conflicts were disclosed, and supervisory review was obtained. This represents a well-documented, good-faith best interest determination.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Fiduciary Interpretation (IA-5248) – Duty of Care; SEC Staff Bulletin on Account Recommendations (Rollovers)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The advisor's detailed, documented cost-benefit analysis of the rollover — including fee comparisons, investment options, tax implications, and disclosure of conflicts — satisfied both the Reg BI Care Obligation and the investment adviser duty of care.
