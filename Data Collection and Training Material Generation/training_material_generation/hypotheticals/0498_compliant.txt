Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Pinnacle BDC, Inc. and Ridgeline Growth Partners LP are both managed by Pinnacle Investment Advisors LLC, which controls the BDC and serves as general partner of the private fund. Lakeshore Capital, a limited partner in Ridgeline Growth Partners LP, holds no voting securities in Pinnacle BDC and has no officer, director, or employee overlap with either the BDC or the adviser. Pinnacle BDC wishes to co-invest $8 million alongside Lakeshore Capital in a $50 million senior secured loan to a healthcare services company. Pinnacle's counsel conducts a thorough analysis and determines that Lakeshore qualifies as a 'remote affiliate' because its only connection to the BDC is through its limited partner interest in the commonly controlled private fund. The co-investment is presented to and approved by a required majority of Pinnacle BDC's independent directors, who review detailed terms showing the BDC receives the same price, terms, and conditions as Lakeshore.

QUESTION
--------
Is Pinnacle BDC's co-investment with Lakeshore Capital permissible under the Investment Company Act affiliate transaction framework as interpreted in IM Guidance Update 2014-12?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

IM Guidance Update 2014-12 provides that a limited partner of a commonly controlled private fund may be treated as a 'remote affiliate' of the BDC where its affiliation arises solely through its limited partner interest and no other close affiliate indicia exist. When a second-tier affiliate qualifies as a remote affiliate, co-investment transactions with the BDC are permissible under Section 57(f) provided they are approved by a required majority of the BDC's directors. Here, Pinnacle's counsel performed the proper multi-factor analysis, Lakeshore has no independent relationship creating close affiliate status, and the required board approval was obtained on fair terms.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Company Act of 1940 §§ 57(a), 57(f); IM Guidance Update No. 2014-12 (BDC Transactions with Certain Second-Tier Affiliates)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The BDC correctly analyzed the limited partner's affiliate status, confirmed it was a remote affiliate with no independent close-affiliate indicia, and obtained the required majority board approval for the co-investment.
