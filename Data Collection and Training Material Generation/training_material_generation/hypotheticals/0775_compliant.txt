Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Horizon Fiduciary Partners, a state-registered investment adviser in Michigan with $42 million in AUM, has custody of certain client funds because its affiliated entity serves as the general partner of a pooled investment vehicle with $6.2 million in assets from 19 accredited investors. Horizon promptly disclosed its custody status on Form ADV Item 9 and notified the state administrator. All pooled vehicle assets are held by a qualified custodian (a national bank) in an account under the name of the pooled investment vehicle as required. Horizon engages an independent public accountant to conduct an annual surprise examination of client assets and delivers audited financial statements to all fund investors within 120 days of fiscal year-end. During a 2025 coordinated NASAA examination—the firm's first state exam—examiners review the custody arrangement, confirm the qualified custodian relationship, verify the surprise examination documentation, and find all records in order. The examiners note the arrangement as a potential risk area but document no deficiencies.

QUESTION
--------
Does Horizon Fiduciary Partners' custody arrangement for the pooled investment vehicle comply with NASAA Model Rule 102(e)(1)-1?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

NASAA Model Rule 102(e)(1)-1 permits an investment adviser to have custody of client funds provided the adviser notifies the administrator via Form ADV, uses a qualified custodian that maintains funds in accounts under the pooled investment vehicle's name (as expressly permitted under subsection (a)(2)(B)), and provides required notices to clients. Horizon satisfied all three prongs: it disclosed custody on Form ADV, the qualified custodian (a national bank) held assets in the vehicle's name, and the firm engaged an independent accountant for the annual surprise examination while delivering audited financials to investors. The 2025 coordinated exam, which included 365 first-time state examinations, confirmed full compliance with custody protocols.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NASAA Model Rule 102(e)(1)-1(a)(1)-(3) – Custody of Client Funds or Securities by Investment Advisers; NASAA 2025 Coordinated Investment Adviser Examination Program

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser satisfied all three custody safeguards—administrator notification, use of a qualified custodian with properly titled accounts, and independent surprise examination—despite the inherently higher-risk arrangement of having custody through an affiliated pooled vehicle.
