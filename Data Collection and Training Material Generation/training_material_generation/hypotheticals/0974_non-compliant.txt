Source Folder: 23_International_Comparative
Topic: mifid_ii_suitability_assessment_omission
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A UK-based investment firm operating under MiFID II onboards a retail client seeking to invest £50,000 in complex structured products. The adviser collects basic KYC information but skips the formal suitability questionnaire, reasoning that the client appears financially sophisticated based on conversation alone. A personal recommendation is made and documented without any written suitability report. The client later suffers significant losses.

QUESTION
--------
Does the firm's conduct satisfy MiFID II suitability requirements when making a personal recommendation to a retail client without completing a formal suitability assessment?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of MiFID II Article 25(2), which mandates a documented suitability assessment prior to any personal recommendation.

MiFID II Article 25(2) explicitly requires investment firms to obtain information on a client's knowledge and experience, financial situation, and investment objectives before making any personal recommendation. Relying on subjective impressions rather than a structured assessment process fails this requirement. The absence of a written suitability report also violates the documentation obligation under Article 25(6).

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
MiFID II Art. 25(2); ESMA Guidelines on Suitability (ESMA35-43-1163)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Personal recommendation made without a completed, documented suitability assessment as required under MiFID II Art. 25(2).
