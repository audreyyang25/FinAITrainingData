ID: 896
Source Folder: 19_CFTC_Guidance
Topic: commodity_conflicts
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A dually registered investment adviser and CPO manages a registered investment company that, following the CFTC's February 8, 2012 rule amendments eliminating the prior exemption, is now required to register as a CPO. The adviser continues to operate the fund's commodity derivatives sleeve—investing approximately $4.2 million in commodity-linked swaps and exchange-traded futures—for 18 months post-April 24, 2012 without completing CPO registration or filing a claim for an alternative exemption. During this period, the fund's disclosure documents are not updated to reflect the new CFTC regulatory obligations or the associated risk disclosures mandated for registered CPOs.

QUESTION
--------
Did the adviser's continued operation of the commodity derivatives strategy without completing CPO registration or updating fund disclosures following the April 24, 2012 effective date of the CFTC rule amendments constitute a regulatory violation?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of CFTC registration requirements under the Commodity Exchange Act and the mandatory disclosure obligations applicable to registered CPOs.

The CFTC's February 2012 rule amendments explicitly required investment advisers operating registered investment companies with commodity derivatives exposure to register as CPOs by April 24, 2012, eliminating the prior regulatory division between SEC and CFTC oversight. Operating as an unregistered CPO after that date violates CEA Section 4m(1) and exposes both the adviser and fund participants to material undisclosed regulatory risk. The failure to update disclosure documents compounds the violation by denying investors information about the changed regulatory status and associated costs and risks of CPO compliance.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
CFTC Regulations 4.21, 4.22, and 4.24; CEA Section 4m(1) – CPO Registration Requirement

KEY FACTOR
----------
Post-April 24, 2012 operation of a commodity derivatives strategy within a registered fund without CPO registration violates the CFTC's amended rules that ended the prior SEC-only regulatory framework for such advisers.
