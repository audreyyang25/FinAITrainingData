ID: 934
Source Folder: 21_FINRA_OHO_Decisions
Topic: leveraged_etf_suitability_retiree
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Registered representative Dana Holloway recommended a 3x leveraged S&P 500 ETF to her 72-year-old client, a retired schoolteacher with a fixed income, low risk tolerance, and a stated investment objective of capital preservation. Holloway made the recommendation without reviewing the client's most recent account profile or discussing the daily reset mechanism and volatility decay inherent in leveraged ETFs. The client sustained losses of approximately $38,000 within four months. Holloway's branch manager had previously issued a written guidance memo cautioning representatives about recommending leveraged ETFs to conservative clients.

QUESTION
--------
Did Holloway's recommendation of a 3x leveraged ETF to this retired client comply with FINRA suitability requirements?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of FINRA Rule 2111 (suitability).

Rule 2111 requires that a representative have a reasonable basis to believe a recommendation is suitable for the particular customer based on the customer's investment profile, including risk tolerance, financial situation, and investment objectives. A 3x leveraged ETF with daily reset and volatility decay characteristics is fundamentally incompatible with a capital preservation objective and low risk tolerance. Holloway's failure to review the client's account profile further demonstrates a disregard for the customer-specific suitability obligation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability); FINRA Rule 2010 (Standards of Commercial Honor)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Recommending a high-volatility, leveraged product to a conservative retiree without reviewing her investment profile is a textbook suitability violation under Rule 2111.
