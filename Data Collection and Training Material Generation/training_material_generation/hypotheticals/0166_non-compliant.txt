ID: 166
Source Folder: unknown
Topic: undisclosed_conflict
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A registered representative at a FINRA member firm recommends a mutual fund (XYZ Growth Fund, 1.2% expense ratio) to a 52-year-old client with $250,000 in investable assets and moderate risk tolerance. The fund company pays the firm a 12 basis point revenue-sharing arrangement (annual trailer fees) that increases when AUM exceeds $50 million in client assets. The rep's compensation is directly tied to this revenue-sharing total. The firm's Form ADV Part 2A discloses only that it receives compensation from fund companies but does not identify XYZ Fund or quantify the 12 basis point arrangement.

QUESTION
--------
Does the firm's disclosure satisfy the Reg BI conflict-of-interest obligation and Investment Adviser Act fiduciary duty?

ANSWER
------
No, this violates the applicable standard.

The firm's generic disclosure that it 'receives compensation from fund companies' does not satisfy the materiality standard because it fails to identify the specific fund (XYZ), the specific basis-point amount (12 bps), or the revenue-sharing trigger tied to AUM thresholds. A reasonable client cannot assess the conflict without knowing which funds generate higher compensation or how the rep's incentives are skewed. SEC Reg BI guidance (Release No. 34-90717) explicitly states that conflicts tied to specific product recommendations must be disclosed with particularity. The arrangement directly incentivizes recommendation of XYZ Fund over potentially lower-cost alternatives, making it material to suitability. A generic disclosure fails the 'clear, prominent, and understandable' standard.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Regulation Best Interest (Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii)) requires disclosure of 'material conflicts of interest' in a 'clear, prominent, and understandable manner.' Investment Adviser Act Section 206(2) fiduciary duty requires disclosure of all material facts regarding conflicts. FINRA Rule 2111 (suitability) requires disclosure of material compensation arrangements.

KEY FACTOR
----------
Failure to identify the specific fund, the specific basis-point amount, and the AUM trigger in the conflict disclosure, rendering the disclosure inadequate under Reg BI and IA fiduciary duty.
