Source Folder: 03_FINRA_AWC
Topic: finra_awc_suitability
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Linda Cho, a registered representative at a regional broker-dealer, manages the account of Gerald Pittman, a 68-year-old retired engineer with a $1.2 million portfolio, a growth-and-income investment objective, moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance documented on his updated account profile, and no immediate liquidity needs due to a pension and Social Security income covering his living expenses. In September 2023, Cho recommended that Pittman allocate approximately 15% of his portfolio ($180,000) to a diversified high-yield bond fund with an expense ratio of 0.65% and an expected yield of approximately 6.5%. Cho disclosed the credit risk associated with below-investment-grade bonds, the fund's historical volatility, and the fact that her firm received 25 basis points in revenue sharing from the fund family. Pittman acknowledged the risks and the conflict in writing. Cho's firm's conflict-of-interest procedures required documentation of why the recommendation was in the client's best interest notwithstanding the revenue-sharing arrangement, and Cho completed a written rationale noting Pittman's income objective, his adequate liquidity from other sources, and the fund's diversified credit exposure as mitigating concentration risk. The firm's supervisory principal reviewed and approved the recommendation.

QUESTION
--------
Did Cho's recommendation of a high-yield bond fund to a 68-year-old retiree comply with Reg BI and suitability standards despite the revenue-sharing conflict?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Reg BI does not prohibit recommendations involving conflicts of interest; it requires that they be disclosed and mitigated. Cho disclosed the revenue-sharing arrangement, and the firm maintained procedures to identify and mitigate such conflicts. The Care Obligation was satisfied because the recommendation was consistent with Pittman's documented growth-and-income objective and moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance, represented a reasonable allocation (15%) of a diversified portfolio, and Pittman had no dependency on this capital for living expenses. The firm's supervisory principal reviewed and approved the trade, and Cho documented a written rationale addressing why the recommendation was in the client's best interest notwithstanding the conflict. A customer's age alone does not render a moderate-risk income-generating investment unsuitable when the customer profile supports it.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iii) – Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation; FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The presence of a revenue-sharing conflict does not render a recommendation non-compliant when the conflict is properly disclosed, mitigated through documented procedures, and the recommendation is otherwise consistent with the customer's documented investment profile and best interest.
