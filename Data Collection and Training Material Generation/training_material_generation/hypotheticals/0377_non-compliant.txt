Topic: dol_rollover_analysis
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
James, age 58, terminates employment and has $675,000 in a 401(k) plan. The plan offers six funds: a stable value option (3.8% annual return, 0.12% ER), and five equity/bond mutual funds (average 0.68% ER). James has moderate risk tolerance and a 25-year time horizon. Fee-only RIA Elena recommends rolling to an IRA and investing 40% in a low-cost bond ETF (0.05% ER) and 60% in a self-directed brokerage account where James will pay $125 per quarter in platform fees plus trading commissions. Elena charges a flat $4,200 annual fee (not AUM-based). Elena documents that the rollover provides "direct control" and "lower costs through ETF investing." However, Elena fails to analyze whether James could remain in the plan or access an IRA direct rollover to the plan's stable value option with lower volatility and guaranteed return.

QUESTION
--------
Does Elena's rollover recommendation comply with PTE 2020-02 when she recommends an IRA with trading account structure that bypasses lower-cost plan alternatives?

ANSWER
------
No. Although Elena's flat fee structure and ETF strategy appear cost-conscious, PTE 2020-02 requires documented analysis of all reasonable alternatives available to the client. The decisive facts are: (1) the 401(k) stable value option guaranteed 3.8% return with 0.12% cost; (2) Elena's recommended IRA structure imposes platform fees of $500 annually plus trading costs and 0.05% ETF expense on $675,000 (approximately $3,375), plus the $4,200 flat fee, totaling $8,075+ annually; (3) Elena failed to document whether James could remain in the plan post-employment or access the stable value option; (4) "direct control" is not a legally sufficient best-interest justification under PTE 2020-02(b)(1) when materially higher costs result. Elena violated PTE 2020-02(b)(4) (written policies requiring analysis of alternatives) and Part 5 (insufficient documentation of why remaining in plan or accessing stable value was inferior).

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
DOL PTE 2020-02 Best Interest Standard; Five-Part Test (Part 2: Reasonable Compensation; Part 4: Policies & Procedures; Part 5: Documentation)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Failed to document analysis of lower-cost alternative (plan stable value at 3.8% + 0.12%) versus higher-cost rollover ($8,075+ annually), violating Five-Part Test requirement to analyze and document all reasonable alternatives.
