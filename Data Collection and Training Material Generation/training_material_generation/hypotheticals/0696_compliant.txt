Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
An SEC-registered investment adviser managing $95 million in client assets recommends that a 58-year-old pre-retiree client allocate $60,000 of her $800,000 portfolio (7.5%) to a private credit fund in which the adviser's affiliated entity serves as a sub-adviser and receives a 0.35% sub-advisory fee. The adviser's Form ADV Part 2A Item 10 discloses the affiliate relationship and the sub-advisory fee arrangement in detail. The adviser provides the client with a separate written conflict disclosure specific to this recommendation, explaining the additional compensation the affiliate receives and that comparable non-affiliated private credit funds were evaluated. The adviser documents that the affiliated fund had a three-year track record showing risk-adjusted returns in the top quartile of its peer group, charged total fees 0.20% below the category median, and that the 7.5% allocation was consistent with the client's moderate risk tolerance, income needs approaching retirement, and desire for portfolio diversification beyond public markets. The client provides informed written consent acknowledging the conflict after a 10-day review period.

QUESTION
--------
Does the adviser's recommendation of an affiliated private credit fund satisfy its fiduciary duty of loyalty under the Advisers Act, given the inherent conflict of interest from the sub-advisory fee?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The SEC's proposed interpretation in Release No. IA-4889 clarifies that the fiduciary duty of loyalty does not categorically prohibit conflicted transactions but requires full and fair disclosure sufficient to obtain informed client consent. Here, the adviser disclosed the affiliate relationship in Form ADV, provided a transaction-specific supplemental conflict disclosure, documented a comparative analysis showing the affiliated fund's superior risk-adjusted performance and below-median fees, and obtained informed written consent after a reasonable review period. The allocation at 7.5% of the portfolio is a measured position consistent with the client's objectives. Academic research on conflicts, including the framework discussed in Wade's fiduciary duty analysis, supports that conflicts can be managed through transparency and demonstrated substantive justification rather than outright prohibition.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940 §206(1), §206(2); SEC Release No. IA-4889 (Proposed Interpretation – duty of loyalty requires full and fair disclosure and informed consent); Form ADV Part 2A Item 10

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser fully disclosed the affiliate conflict through multiple channels, documented that the affiliated fund was substantively justified on performance and cost metrics relative to alternatives, and obtained informed written consent from the client.
