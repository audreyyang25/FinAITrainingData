Source Folder: 15_FINRA_Regulatory_Notices
Topic: senior_investor_protection
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A 78-year-old retired teacher, Harold, with a $280,000 account requests an immediate $95,000 withdrawal to purchase a vacation property. His financial advisor, noting the large withdrawal relative to account size and Harold's age, contacts the trusted contact person listed on Harold's account—his adult daughter—to verify Harold's intent and confirm there are no concerns about undue third-party influence. The daughter confirms she is aware of the purchase and supports her father's decision. The advisor documents the conversation with the trusted contact person, notes Harold's clear capacity and independent decision-making during in-person meetings, and processes the withdrawal within three business days. The firm's compliance system logs the temporary hold review and trusted contact outreach.

QUESTION
--------
Did the advisor and firm comply with FINRA rules when they contacted the trusted contact person and briefly delayed the disbursement before processing the senior client's large withdrawal request?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

FINRA Rule 4512 requires firms to make reasonable efforts to obtain trusted contact person information, and Rule 2165 authorizes firms to place temporary holds on disbursements when there is a reasonable belief of potential financial exploitation of a senior. Here, the advisor acted prudently by contacting the trusted contact person to verify the legitimacy of a large withdrawal by an elderly client, documented the outreach and the client's capacity, and processed the withdrawal promptly once concerns were resolved. This is exactly the type of protective yet client-respecting conduct FINRA's senior investor rules are designed to encourage.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 4512(a)(1)(F) (Trusted Contact Person); FINRA Rule 2165 (Financial Exploitation of Specified Adults)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The advisor appropriately utilized the trusted contact person mechanism and documented the review before promptly processing the withdrawal, balancing senior investor protection with respect for the client's autonomy.
