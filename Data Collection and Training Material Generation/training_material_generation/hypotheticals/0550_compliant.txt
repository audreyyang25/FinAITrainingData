Topic: form_crs
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A dually registered firm operating as both a broker-dealer and an investment adviser prepares and delivers a two-page Form CRS to all retail investors. The Form CRS clearly describes both the brokerage and advisory relationships available, explains that brokerage customers pay transaction-based fees while advisory clients pay an ongoing asset-based fee of 1.25%, and includes the required conversation-starter questions such as 'Given my financial situation, should I choose a brokerage account, an investment advisory account, or both?' The form discloses that the firm's representatives receive higher compensation for selling certain variable annuity products, identifies one prior FINRA censure from 2019 related to a supervisory deficiency, and directs customers to FINRA BrokerCheck and the SEC's IAPD for additional information. The firm updates the Form CRS within 30 days whenever any information becomes materially inaccurate and files amendments with the SEC via EDGAR and with FINRA via the Firm Gateway. The firm delivers the current Form CRS to each new retail customer before or at the earliest of a recommendation, placing an order, or opening an account.

QUESTION
--------
Does the firm's Form CRS satisfy the SEC's content, filing, updating, and delivery requirements despite the firm being dually registered and disclosing a prior disciplinary event?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Form CRS requires dually registered firms to present both relationship types in a single document not exceeding two pages, include prescribed conversation-starter questions, disclose material fees and conflicts including differential compensation, report disciplinary history with a reference to BrokerCheck and IAPD, and update and refile within 30 days of material changes. The firm here meets all content requirements, maintains the two-page limit, properly discloses the prior FINRA censure, and timely delivers and updates the form. Having a disciplinary event does not create a violation—failing to disclose it would, as illustrated by the Regulus Financial Group AWC.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Form CRS (17 CFR 249.2); SEC Rule 17a-14 (Form CRS delivery); SEC Reg BI Adopting Release, Section II.B (Form CRS Requirements)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm's thorough, timely, and accurately updated Form CRS—including proper disclosure of a prior disciplinary event and differential compensation—demonstrates full compliance with Form CRS requirements despite the inherent complexity of dual registration.
