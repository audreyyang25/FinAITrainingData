ID: 792
Source Folder: 15_FINRA_Regulatory_Notices
Topic: senior_investor_protection
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Margaret Dawson, an 81-year-old retired schoolteacher in rural upstate New York, has a $320,000 IRA account with Beacon Financial Group. Her registered representative, Thomas Kline, recommends she move 65% of her portfolio into a non-traded REIT with a 7-year lock-up period and a leveraged closed-end bond fund. Margaret's account documents indicate a conservative risk tolerance, a need for income to cover assisted living costs of $4,800 per month, and a 3-to-5-year investment time horizon. Kline tells Margaret the products will 'generate the income she needs' but does not explain the illiquidity of the non-traded REIT, the leverage risk of the closed-end fund, or the fact that he receives a 7% upfront commission on the REIT. Margaret's daughter later contacts FINRA after discovering her mother cannot access the REIT funds to pay for escalating care costs.

QUESTION
--------
Did the representative satisfy his suitability and best interest obligations when recommending illiquid and leveraged products to this elderly client?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a suitability violation and a failure to meet the Reg BI Care Obligation with respect to a senior investor.

Recommending a 7-year lock-up non-traded REIT and a leveraged closed-end fund to an 81-year-old client with a conservative risk tolerance, a 3-to-5-year time horizon, and immediate income needs for assisted living costs is unsuitable on its face under both customer-specific suitability (FINRA Rule 2111) and the Reg BI Care Obligation. The representative failed to exercise reasonable diligence to understand the products' risks relative to this senior client's profile and failed to disclose material conflicts including the 7% commission. FINRA's focus on senior investor protection, including proposals to accelerate arbitration for elderly parties (Regulatory Notice 22-09), underscores the heightened duty owed to vulnerable older investors.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability); SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Care Obligation; FINRA Regulatory Notice 22-09 (Senior Investor Protections)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Illiquid, long-duration, and leveraged products were recommended to an elderly client whose conservative profile, short time horizon, and immediate income needs made these investments clearly unsuitable.
