Source Folder: 19_CFTC_Guidance
Topic: cta_cpo_suitability
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Pinnacle Futures Management, a registered CPO, operates a commodity pool structured under CFTC Regulation 4.7 and markets it exclusively to participants it designates as Qualified Eligible Persons (QEPs). After the CFTC's October 2023 proposed rulemaking to increase financial thresholds under the Portfolio Requirement of Rule 4.7 to reflect inflation, Pinnacle continues onboarding new investors who meet only the pre-amendment $200,000 net worth threshold rather than verifying compliance with the proposed elevated thresholds that industry participants were on notice of. Additionally, Pinnacle provides new 4.7 Fund participants with a one-page term sheet but no disclosure document addressing trading strategy, conflicts of interest, or fee structures, relying solely on the pre-2023 understanding that 4.7 pools carried broad disclosure exemptions. One new investor, a 45-year-old IT consultant with $225,000 in liquid assets, loses $61,000 within six months.

QUESTION
--------
Does Pinnacle's onboarding practice satisfy the disclosure and QEP qualification requirements applicable to 4.7 commodity pools in light of the CFTC's October 2023 proposed amendments to Rule 4.7?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of CFTC Regulation 4.7 disclosure obligations and involves potentially inadequate QEP qualification procedures.

The CFTC's October 2023 proposed rulemaking explicitly flagged that existing QEP financial thresholds required inflation adjustment and introduced minimum disclosure requirements for 4.7 Funds, signaling regulatory deficiency in prior no-disclosure practices. A CPO on notice of these proposals who continues onboarding investors at stale financial thresholds and provides only a bare term sheet fails to exercise reasonable diligence consistent with evolving regulatory expectations. Even under existing Rule 4.7, a one-page term sheet without strategy, conflict, or fee disclosure does not satisfy minimum disclosure obligations that the proposed amendments were designed to codify. The investor's financial profile was marginal even under pre-amendment standards, heightening the qualification concern.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
CFTC Regulation 4.7 – Exemptions for CPOs and CTAs; CFTC Proposed Rulemaking (October 2, 2023) – Minimum Disclosure Requirements and QEP Threshold Amendments

KEY FACTOR
----------
Pinnacle failed to update QEP financial threshold verification and omitted required minimum disclosures for 4.7 pool participants in the face of explicit regulatory notice of heightened standards.
