Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A 35-year-old employed client with $250,000 in a 401(k) and $150,000 in a taxable account stated an objective of 'long-term growth with moderate risk.' The adviser recommended a portfolio of 70% diversified U.S. equity index funds, 20% international equity index funds, and 10% investment-grade bond index funds. The adviser documented the client's risk tolerance as 'moderate,' conducted a suitability analysis, and reviewed the allocation against the client's stated time horizon (30+ years to retirement).

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation comply with the investment adviser's duty of care?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this recommendation complies with the investment adviser's duty of care.

The adviser has satisfied the duty of care by: (1) obtaining the client's stated objectives ('long-term growth with moderate risk') and time horizon (30+ years); (2) recommending a diversified portfolio allocation (70/20/10) that is reasonable and aligned with moderate-risk growth objectives; (3) using low-cost index funds appropriate for a long-term investor; and (4) documenting the suitability analysis. The 70% equity allocation is reasonable for a 35-year-old with a 30-year time horizon and moderate risk tolerance. The recommendation has a reasonable basis because the adviser conducted adequate investigation into the client's circumstances and the allocation is consistent with those circumstances.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940, Section 206; SEC Rule 206(4)-1; SEC Compliance and Disclosure Interpretations (C&DIs) regarding reasonable basis.

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser's documentation of client objectives, risk tolerance, time horizon, and documented suitability analysis demonstrates reasonable investigation and a reasonable basis for the recommendation.
