Topic: discovery_abuse_document_withholding
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
In a FINRA customer arbitration involving alleged churning of a retirement account, the respondent broker-dealer, Harmon & Associates, received a discovery request pursuant to the Discovery Guide for documents related to the broker's supervision, including written supervisory procedure manuals and exception reports flagging the claimant's account. Harmon produced an outdated supervisory manual and objected to producing exception reports on the grounds of proprietary business information, without seeking a protective order from the arbitration panel. The claimant's counsel raised the issue at the preliminary hearing, and the arbitrators took no immediate action.

QUESTION
--------
Does a respondent broker-dealer's unilateral withholding of exception reports and production of outdated supervisory manuals, without seeking a protective order, comply with FINRA arbitration discovery obligations?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a discovery violation under the FINRA Code of Arbitration Procedure and the FINRA Discovery Guide.

FINRA Code of Arbitration Procedure Rule 12505 requires parties to cooperate in good faith with discovery, and the Discovery Guide's Document Production List 2 expressly identifies supervisory exception reports as documents presumptively required to be produced in customer disputes. A party asserting a privilege or proprietary interest must seek a protective order from the panel under Rule 12508 rather than unilaterally withholding responsive materials, and failure to do so constitutes a discovery abuse that PIABA has identified as a systemic problem undermining investor access to relevant evidence.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Code of Arbitration Procedure Rules 12505, 12506, 12508; FINRA Discovery Guide Document Production Lists

KEY FACTOR
----------
Unilateral withholding of presumptively required discovery documents without seeking a panel-approved protective order violates FINRA Rules 12505 and 12508.
