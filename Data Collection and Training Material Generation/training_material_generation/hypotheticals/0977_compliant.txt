Topic: singapore_faa_balanced_product_disclosure
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A Singapore-licensed financial adviser recommends an investment-linked insurance policy to a 45-year-old professional client with a moderate risk tolerance and a 15-year investment horizon. Prior to making the recommendation, the adviser administers a comprehensive fact-find covering the client's income, liabilities, dependants, existing insurance coverage, and financial goals. The adviser prepares a written Customer Investment Profile and a Balanced Scorecard comparing three products, clearly identifying the recommended product's fees, risks, and surrender charges. The client signs an acknowledgement confirming they have read and understood the recommendation document.

QUESTION
--------
Does the adviser's process satisfy the Singapore Financial Advisers Act requirements for making a suitable product recommendation to a retail client?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Section 27 of the FAA requires financial advisers to have a reasonable basis for recommendations, which includes having regard to the client's financial situation, investment experience, and objectives obtained through an adequate fact-find process. The adviser's use of a comprehensive fact-find, a documented Customer Investment Profile, and a balanced scorecard comparing alternatives demonstrates a reasonable basis for the recommendation. Client acknowledgement of the recommendation document further satisfies the disclosure and communication requirements under MAS Notice FAA-N16.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Singapore Financial Advisers Act (FAA) §27; MAS Notice FAA-N16 (Recommendations on Investment Products)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Adviser conducted a thorough fact-find, comparative product analysis, and obtained informed client acknowledgement, satisfying FAA §27 suitability requirements.
