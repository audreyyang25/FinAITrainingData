Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
RIA Advisor Chen manages $2.8M portfolio for 58-year-old corporate executive Maria, who had requested aggressive growth (80/20 equity/fixed income). However, Advisor Chen discovered during comprehensive review that Maria had guaranteed a $1.2M loan to her son's startup in 2023. Maria explicitly requested the loan guarantee remain undisclosed to her spouse. Advisor Chen recommended and implemented a 55/45 conservative allocation instead, documented the liability in writing with Maria's signature, and noted in the investment policy statement: 'Client has material contingent liability reducing true risk capacity despite stated tolerance.' The recommended portfolio earned 6.2% annualized return over 18 months versus 11.8% for the stock market. Maria has expressed no dissatisfaction.

QUESTION
--------
Did Advisor Chen comply with the duty of care under the Investment Adviser Act by recommending a portfolio more conservative than Maria's stated aggressive risk tolerance?

ANSWER
------
Yes, fully compliant. Under Reg BI and IAA fiduciary duty, advisers must assess 'risk capacity' alongside 'risk tolerance.' The Dodd-Frank amendments and SEC guidance require advisers to consider financial obligations and liquidity needs that reduce true risk capacity. Advisor Chen's written documentation of the contingent liability, Maria's explicit consent to the more conservative allocation, and the documented analysis showing the liability justified deviation from stated preference all satisfy the duty of care prong of fiduciary duty. The adviser's conduct mirrors SEC guidance in IA Release 2774 (Fiduciary Duty) and FINRA guidance on comprehensive suitability review.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Adviser Act §206(1) fiduciary duty (duty of care); Reg BI Care Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Documented contingent liability that reduces risk capacity justifies and requires more conservative allocation than stated risk tolerance, provided client consents in writing.
