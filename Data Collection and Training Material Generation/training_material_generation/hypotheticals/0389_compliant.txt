FACTS
-----
Cornerstone Benefit Advisors was engaged by the trustees of the $145M Riverside County Teachers' Pension Plan (2,100 members, average age 51, $38M annual payouts) to select and monitor target-date funds (TDFs) for the plan's participant-directed investment option. Cornerstone's scope was defined as a §3(21) fiduciary adviser for investment menu design and selection, not discretionary asset management. Cornerstone conducted a rigorous TDFs analysis: (1) reviewed 28 TDF suites from major providers; (2) stress-tested each suite using 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations; (3) evaluated glide path construction (equity allocation by target year), fund expense ratios (0.52%–0.68%), and sub-fund quality; (4) benchmarked against Morningstar's target-date fund methodology. Cornerstone recommended Vanguard Target Retirement Funds (0.55% ER average) based on superior glide-path design and low costs. The plan sponsor ratified the selection in writing. Cornerstone received a flat $45,000 annual advisory fee, with no AUM percentage or revenue-sharing from Vanguard.

QUESTION
--------
Did Cornerstone comply with ERISA fiduciary duty of prudence as a §3(21) adviser when selecting the plan's target-date fund lineup?

ANSWER
------
Yes. Cornerstone satisfied ERISA §404(a)(1)(B) duty of care for §3(21) investment adviser fiduciaries. The fiduciary must act with prudence of an expert in selecting investments for participant accounts. Cornerstone demonstrated: (1) expertise—rigorous comparative analysis using recognized methodologies; (2) documentation—Monte Carlo modeling, glide-path analysis, ER benchmarking were fully documented; (3) objectivity—Vanguard selection was based on independent metrics (glide path, expense ratio, sub-fund quality), not on revenue-sharing incentives; (4) alignment—TDFs inherently serve participant objectives across life stages. DOL Fact Sheet (Jan. 2013) on target-date funds confirms that prudent selection requires analyzing fund design, fees, and performance against peers. Cornerstone's flat-fee arrangement eliminates conflicts. The distinction between §3(21) adviser (selecting menu) and §3(38) fiduciary (discretionary management) was respected.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
ERISA §404(a)(1)(B) — Prudence duty; ERISA §3(21) — Investment adviser fiduciary status; DOL Fact Sheet on Target-Date Funds (Jan. 2013)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Adviser conducted rigorous, documented comparative analysis; recommended low-cost option based on objective criteria; compensation structure eliminated conflicts of interest.
