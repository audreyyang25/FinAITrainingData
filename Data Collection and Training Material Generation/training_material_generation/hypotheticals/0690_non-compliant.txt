Topic: reg_bi_effectiveness

Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Kevin Marsh, a registered representative at a national broker-dealer, recommends that Angela Davis, a 34-year-old Millennial teacher earning $52,000 annually with $18,000 in savings, open a margin account and invest $15,000 in a leveraged ETF (ProShares UltraPro QQQ, 3x daily leveraged) for her primary emergency fund and short-term savings goal of a home down payment within 18 months. Marsh's account opening paperwork lists the client's risk tolerance as 'aggressive' and investment objective as 'speculation,' though Davis's intake questionnaire indicated 'moderate' risk tolerance and 'capital preservation for home purchase' as her goal. Marsh earns a higher commission on margin account activity than on standard brokerage accounts.

QUESTION
--------
Did the advisor meet Reg BI's Care Obligation when recommending a leveraged ETF in a margin account for a client's emergency fund and short-term savings goal?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Care Obligation.

Reg BI's Care Obligation requires that a recommendation be in the retail customer's best interest based on that customer's investment profile, including risk tolerance, time horizon, and investment objectives. A 3x leveraged ETF is designed for short-term sophisticated trading and can lose substantial value through daily compounding effects, making it fundamentally unsuitable for an 18-month home down payment goal. Additionally, altering the client's stated risk tolerance and objectives on the account documentation to justify the recommendation is a clear violation. Empirical research on investor harm demonstrates that mismatches between documented risk profiles and actual client needs are among the most common patterns in enforcement actions and FINRA arbitrations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Care Obligation; FINRA Rule 2111 – Suitability (customer-specific obligation)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The advisor recommended a highly speculative leveraged product for a short-term capital preservation goal and falsified the client's risk tolerance on the account documentation.
