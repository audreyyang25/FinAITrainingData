Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A dually-registered financial adviser recommends that a 55-year-old executive client with $1.8 million in investable assets and a high risk tolerance allocate $270,000 (15%) into a proprietary alternative investment fund managed by the adviser's affiliated firm. The fund charges a 1.5% management fee and 15% performance fee. Prior to the recommendation, the adviser provides the client with a detailed written Reg BI disclosure document identifying the conflict of interest arising from the affiliated fund, the specific fees, a comparison to two similar third-party alternative funds with lower fee structures, and a written explanation of why the proprietary fund's track record and strategy alignment justify the higher cost. The client signs an acknowledgment after a two-week review period. The adviser documents the rationale in the client file, including risk-adjusted return comparisons and the client's existing diversified portfolio allocation.

QUESTION
--------
Is the recommendation of a proprietary affiliated fund compliant with Reg BI and fiduciary standards given the inherent conflict of interest?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Reg BI does not prohibit recommendations involving conflicts of interest; rather, it requires full disclosure, reasonable diligence in evaluating alternatives, and mitigation of the conflict. Here, the adviser provided specific written disclosure of the affiliation and fees, conducted and documented a comparative analysis against third-party alternatives, and gave the client adequate time to review before consenting. The 15% allocation is reasonable relative to the client's $1.8 million portfolio and high risk tolerance. Academic literature on conflicts of interest recognizes that well-disclosed, documented, and reasonable conflicted recommendations can satisfy both Reg BI and fiduciary standards when the process is robust.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i)-(iii) – Reg BI Disclosure, Care, and Conflict of Interest Obligations; Investment Advisers Act Sections 206(1)-(2)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The pivotal compliance factor is the adviser's proactive, specific written disclosure of the conflict, documented comparative analysis, and the client's informed written consent after a meaningful review period.
