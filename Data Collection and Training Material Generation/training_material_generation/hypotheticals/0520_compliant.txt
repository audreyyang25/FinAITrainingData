Source Folder: 02_SEC_Enforcement_Actions
Topic: sec_enforcement_bd
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Atlantic Coast Securities, LLC, a dually registered broker-dealer and investment adviser, recommended that certain high-net-worth clients (minimum $2 million investable assets, all with 10+ year time horizons and documented aggressive growth objectives) allocate up to 15% of their portfolios to a series of private placement offerings managed by a third-party sponsor. Atlantic Coast received placement agent fees of 1.5% on invested capital—a fact that was disclosed in writing to each client in a standalone conflict-of-interest disclosure document provided prior to investment, in the Form CRS, and in a supplemental Reg BI disclosure. Each client signed an acknowledgment confirming receipt and understanding of the fee arrangement. Atlantic Coast's investment committee conducted independent due diligence on the private placements, including analysis of the sponsor's track record, fund terms, liquidity constraints, and fee structure, and documented that the investments were consistent with each client's stated objectives, risk tolerance, and overall portfolio diversification. The firm's compliance department reviewed each recommendation for Reg BI Care Obligation compliance and maintained records of the review. No client held more than 15% concentration in private placements, and all clients maintained sufficient liquid reserves outside these positions.

QUESTION
--------
Did Atlantic Coast Securities comply with Reg BI and its fiduciary obligations despite receiving placement agent fees on private placement recommendations to high-net-worth clients?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Receipt of placement agent compensation creates a conflict of interest but does not per se violate Reg BI or the Advisers Act fiduciary standard if the conflict is fully and specifically disclosed and the recommendation otherwise satisfies the care obligation. Atlantic Coast provided layered, specific written disclosure of the placement fees across multiple documents and obtained client acknowledgments, satisfying the Reg BI Disclosure Obligation and §206 fiduciary disclosure requirements. The firm's independent due diligence, documented suitability analysis aligned with each client's aggressive growth profile and long time horizon, concentration limits at 15%, and compliance department review demonstrate reasonable diligence and care in forming the basis for the recommendation. This distinguishes the conduct from enforcement matters like Saxony Capital Management (IA-5385) and BiscayneAmericas Advisers (IA-6119), where undisclosed or inadequately disclosed compensation conflicts drove violations. Here, the comprehensive disclosure and care framework rendered the conflict-laden recommendation permissible.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) and (ii) – Reg BI Care and Disclosure Obligations; Investment Advisers Act §206(1) and §206(2) for advisory accounts

KEY FACTOR
----------
Comprehensive, specific, multi-layered disclosure of the placement agent fee conflict combined with independent due diligence and documented suitability analysis transformed what could have been a conflict-driven violation into a permissible recommendation.
