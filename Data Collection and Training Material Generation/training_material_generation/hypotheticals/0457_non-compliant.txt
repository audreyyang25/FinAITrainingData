Source Folder: unknown
Topic: compliant_nuanced
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Adviser recommended a high-fee structured note (6% embedded sponsor markup, 1.5% annual fees, 8-year maturity) to 59-year-old client with $280,000. Client's stated goal was "capital preservation with upside to S&P 500." Structured note offered: (1) 80% protection at maturity; (2) S&P 500 participation up to 300% if market rose >15% annually. Adviser documented: (1) client's risk tolerance as "conservative-moderate"; (2) complex strategy suitable for client's goal. However, adviser did not disclose: (1) that sponsor markup (6%) could be recovered only if client held to maturity; (2) that secondary market liquidity was extremely limited (bid-ask spread >2%); (3) comparative alternatives: (1) 70/30 bond-stock portfolio with 0.20% fees achieving similar risk profile at 1/75th the cost. IPS stated "low-cost strategies preferred" but did not mention structured products.

QUESTION
--------
Did the adviser comply with fiduciary and Reg BI standards in recommending the structured note without disclosing structural conflicts and alternatives?

ANSWER
------
No. Reg BI care obligation (17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)) requires reasonable basis including analysis of conflicts and material costs. Adviser failed to: (1) disclose that 6% sponsor markup could be lost in secondary market; (2) analyze liquidity cost (2%+ spread) that contradicts client's capital preservation goal; (3) compare to 70/30 bond-stock portfolio with 75x lower fees achieving substantially equivalent profile; (4) document that structured product's complexity benefit outweighed IPS's stated preference for low-cost strategies. FINRA enforcement (AWC 2023-087) treated similar structured-note recommendations as unsuitable when advisers failed to disclose hidden costs and ignored lower-cost alternatives.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Reg BI Care Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i); Fiduciary Duty to Disclose Conflicts; Cost Reasonableness

KEY FACTOR
----------
Failure to disclose sponsor markup recovery risk, secondary-market liquidity cost, and lower-cost bond-stock alternatives violates care obligation despite documented capital-preservation rationale.
