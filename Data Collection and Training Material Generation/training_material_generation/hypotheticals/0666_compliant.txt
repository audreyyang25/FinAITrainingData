FACTS
-----
In September 2025, a mid-sized broker-dealer's compliance department reviews its communications policies in light of recent SEC leadership changes under Chairman Paul Atkins. The firm had previously restricted registered representatives from using any projected performance data in retail communications about alternative investments, based on FINRA Rule 2210 restrictions. After the SEC withdraws the stay on amendments to FINRA Rule 2210 that now permit the use of projected and target performance in certain institutional and accredited investor communications — subject to specified conditions including clear disclaimers, reasonable assumptions, and fair presentation alongside standardized performance — the firm updates its written supervisory procedures. It permits senior representatives to use target return illustrations only in communications to qualified purchasers and accredited investors, with mandatory compliance pre-review, standardized disclaimers, and documented assumption methodologies.

QUESTION
--------
Is the broker-dealer's updated policy permitting target performance illustrations in communications to accredited investors compliant with the amended FINRA Rule 2210 framework?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

As discussed in law firm memos analyzing Chairman Atkins's reform agenda, the SEC withdrew the stay on FINRA Rule 2210 amendments that had previously blocked broker-dealers from using projected and target performance in communications. The firm's updated policy restricts the use of such illustrations to accredited investors and qualified purchasers, requires compliance pre-review and standardized disclaimers, and mandates documented reasonable assumptions — all consistent with the conditions under the amended rule. The firm's careful implementation reflects a compliant adaptation to the regulatory change.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2210 (Communications with the Public), as amended per SEC facilitation of projected/target performance use; SEC common-sense reform guidance (September 2025)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm's updated policy appropriately limits target performance illustrations to eligible investor audiences with required disclaimers and compliance oversight, consistent with the amended FINRA Rule 2210 framework.
