Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Lisa Pham, a registered representative, recommends that her client Roberto Fuentes, a 52-year-old physician earning $350,000 annually with $1.8 million in diversified investments, allocate $150,000 (approximately 8% of his portfolio) to a high-yield bond fund. Roberto's new account form, updated within the last 12 months under FINRA Rule 2090, documents his investment objective as 'growth with income,' his risk tolerance as 'moderate to aggressive,' his time horizon as '10+ years,' and his investment experience as 'extensive' across equities, fixed income, and alternative investments. Lisa documents her rationale in a contemporaneous file memo, noting that the high-yield allocation complements Roberto's existing investment-grade bond holdings and is consistent with his stated desire for yield enhancement within his risk parameters. The firm's supervisory principal reviews and approves the recommendation the same day.

QUESTION
--------
Does the recommendation of a high-yield bond fund allocation satisfy the suitability requirements of FINRA Rule 2111(a)?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

FINRA Rule 2111(a) requires that a recommendation be suitable based on the customer's investment profile, including financial situation, risk tolerance, investment objectives, time horizon, and experience. Roberto is a high-income, experienced investor with a moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance and a long time horizon, and the recommended allocation represents only 8% of his diversified portfolio. The high-yield bond fund is consistent with his 'growth with income' objective and his stated desire for yield enhancement. Lisa's contemporaneous documentation of her suitability rationale and the prompt supervisory review further demonstrate compliance with both the suitability obligation and supervisory requirements under Rule 3110.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111(a) – Suitability (Reasonable-Basis, Customer-Specific, and Quantitative Suitability)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The modest 8% allocation to a high-yield bond fund is well-suited to the client's documented moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance, extensive experience, long time horizon, and income-oriented growth objective, with proper documentation and supervisory review.
