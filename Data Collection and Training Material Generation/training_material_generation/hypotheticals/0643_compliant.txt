Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Linda Morales, a New York-licensed insurance producer, recommends a fixed indexed annuity with a 5-year surrender period and a guaranteed lifetime withdrawal benefit rider to David Park, age 62, who is planning to retire at 67. Park has $950,000 in retirement assets, including $400,000 in a 401(k), $300,000 in a brokerage account, and $250,000 in bank CDs earning 1.2%. Park tells Morales he wants to allocate $200,000 from the maturing CDs into a product that provides some market-linked growth potential with principal protection and guaranteed future income. Morales documents Park's financial profile, compares three annuity products from different carriers on cost, surrender terms, index strategies, and rider fees, and selects the product with the lowest all-in cost and most favorable guaranteed withdrawal percentages. Morales discloses her 4% commission and that her agency has a volume bonus arrangement with the issuing carrier, noting in writing that the volume bonus did not influence the recommendation.

QUESTION
--------
Does the producer's recommendation of the fixed indexed annuity comply with NY Reg 187's best interest and disclosure requirements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

NY Reg 187 requires the producer to act in the best interest of the client by evaluating the client's financial situation, needs, and objectives, and to consider reasonably available alternatives. Morales documented a thorough comparison of multiple products, selected one aligned with the client's stated goals (principal protection, growth potential, and future income), and the product's 5-year surrender period aligns with Park's 5-year-out retirement timeline. Critically, Morales also disclosed her compensation and the volume bonus arrangement, satisfying Reg 187's requirement that material conflicts of interest be disclosed and that compensation not be the primary motivating factor in the recommendation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NY Reg 187 – 11 NYCRR § 224.4(b) and § 224.4(d) – Best Interest Standard and Producer Disclosure Obligations

KEY FACTOR
----------
Thorough documentation of product comparison, alignment with the client's retirement timeline and objectives, and full disclosure of compensation including volume bonus arrangements satisfy NY Reg 187's best interest and disclosure requirements.
