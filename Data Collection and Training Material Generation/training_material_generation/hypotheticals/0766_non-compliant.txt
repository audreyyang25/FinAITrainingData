Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Marcus Webb, a former bank financial planner in Sacramento, California, began offering personalized portfolio management services to twelve clients, collecting approximately $1,800 per quarter in advisory fees from each. Webb never registered as an investment adviser with the California Department of Financial Protection and Innovation (DFPI) through the IARD system, nor did he file a Form ADV. He operated for fourteen months before a client complaint prompted a state inquiry. Webb claimed he believed his prior Series 65 license from another state was sufficient and that he did not need to re-register in California.

QUESTION
--------
Did Marcus Webb violate California state securities law by providing investment advisory services without registering as an investment adviser with the DFPI?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is not compliant. This constitutes an unregistered investment adviser violation under California Corporations Code.

California law requires any person or entity acting as an investment adviser to register with the DFPI by completing the multi-step process including IARD enrollment, funding the daily account, completing Form ADV, and submitting the application. Webb's failure to complete any of these steps, as outlined in the DFPI's registration guidance, means he was conducting advisory business unlawfully. A license from another state does not transfer to California, and each state requires independent registration.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
California Corporations Code Section 25230 – Requirement to Register as an Investment Adviser; California DFPI Registration Requirements (Form ADV via IARD)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Webb provided investment advisory services to California clients for fourteen months without ever registering with the DFPI or filing Form ADV through the IARD system.
