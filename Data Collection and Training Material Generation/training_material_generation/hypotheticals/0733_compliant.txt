ID: 733
Source Folder: 12_FINRA_Arbitration_Awards
Topic: arbitration_defenses
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
David Kimura, a registered representative formerly associated with LPL Financial LLC, sought expungement of a customer complaint from his CRD records through FINRA arbitration (filed October 2021). The underlying customer complaint alleged that Kimura had recommended an unsuitable allocation of 60% equities for a 55-year-old client with a moderate growth objective and 15-year time horizon. Kimura presented documentary evidence that the client had completed a detailed risk questionnaire selecting 'moderate growth' with a 'willing to accept short-term losses for long-term gains' response, signed an investment policy statement authorizing a 55-70% equity allocation, and received quarterly performance reviews over a three-year period without objection. The customer's complaint was filed only after a market correction caused a 12% temporary portfolio decline. The arbitration panel, after an evidentiary hearing, found the claim was factually impossible or clearly erroneous and recommended expungement.

QUESTION
--------
Was the representative's 60% equity allocation for a moderate-growth client with a 15-year time horizon a suitable recommendation supporting the expungement request?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

A 60% equity allocation falls squarely within the client's own stated moderate-growth objective and documented authorization of 55-70% equities. The client's 15-year time horizon, completed risk questionnaire, signed investment policy statement, and years of quarterly reviews without objection all demonstrate that the recommendation was consistent with the customer's investment profile. This mirrors the Kimura v. LPL Financial (FINRA No. 21-02526) expungement case, where an associated person sought to clear CRD records of a complaint that lacked factual merit. Under FINRA Rule 2080, expungement is appropriate where the claim is factually impossible or clearly erroneous.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability – Customer-Specific Suitability); FINRA Rule 2080 (Expungement Standards under FINRA Rule 12805)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Comprehensive documentation—including the client's risk questionnaire, signed investment policy statement, and years of unrebutted quarterly reviews—demonstrated the recommendation was fully consistent with the client's stated objectives and risk tolerance.
