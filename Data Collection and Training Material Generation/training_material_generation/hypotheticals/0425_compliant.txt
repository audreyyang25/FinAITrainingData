FACTS
-----
On October 10, 2024, investment adviser Rockefeller Wealth Partners recommended a Bitcoin spot ETF (iShares Bitcoin Trust, ticker IBIT, approved by SEC June 2024) to client Jerome Wallace, age 58, with $1.2M investable assets and stated moderate-to-high risk tolerance. Jerome explicitly requested crypto exposure, stating 'I want some Bitcoin but through a regulated fund.' The adviser conducted a written suitability analysis documenting: (1) Jerome's 15-year investment horizon; (2) 4% allocation ($48,000), appropriate for a 58-year-old with diversified portfolio; (3) Bitcoin ETF regulatory status (SEC-approved, Nasdaq-listed, daily liquidity, expense ratio 0.20%); (4) volatility disclosure (Bitcoin's 1-year range ±35%); (5) comparison to spot vs. futures-based ETFs; (6) client acknowledgment of risks. The recommendation was placed in Jerome's brokerage account with SIPC protection.

QUESTION
--------
Did the adviser comply with Reg BI and FINRA suitability standards in recommending a Bitcoin spot ETF to this client?

ANSWER
------
Yes. The recommendation complies with Reg BI's care obligation (17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i)–(ii)) and FINRA Rule 2111. The adviser documented a reasonable basis: (1) Jerome's client profile (age 58, high risk tolerance, 15-year horizon, $1.2M assets) supports moderate crypto allocation; (2) the 4% allocation is proportionate and diversified; (3) the Bitcoin spot ETF is SEC-approved (June 2024), reducing regulatory uncertainty vs. unregistered tokens; (4) the adviser disclosed volatility (±35%), expense ratio (0.20%), and regulatory status; (5) Jerome explicitly requested crypto exposure via regulated vehicle. SEC guidance (IA Release No. 6518) explicitly permits adviser recommendations of SEC-approved crypto ETFs when properly documented and matched to client profile. The brokerage account structure and SIPC coverage address custodial protection.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Reg BI Care Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i)–(ii); FINRA Rule 2111 Suitability

KEY FACTOR
----------
SEC-approved Bitcoin spot ETF with written suitability documentation, client-requested allocation, proportionate position sizing (4%), and full risk disclosure complies with care and suitability standards.
