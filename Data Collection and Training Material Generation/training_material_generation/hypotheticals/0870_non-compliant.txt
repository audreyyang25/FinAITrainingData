ID: 870
Source Folder: 18_OCC_Bank_Trust_Guidance
Topic: bank_fiduciary
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
First National Savings Association serves as corporate trustee for a $2.3 million irrevocable testamentary trust established under the will of a deceased client, with the bank's adult children as remainder beneficiaries and the surviving spouse as income beneficiary. The trust department's investment committee, seeking to reduce administrative overhead, directs 85% of trust assets into the bank's own proprietary money market funds and certificates of deposit without obtaining independent authorization or disclosing that the bank earns additional fee income from these proprietary products. The trust document is silent on investment selection methodology, and no court order or beneficiary consent was obtained prior to the self-dealing transactions. The bank's internal policies do not require committee review of transactions involving affiliated products.

QUESTION
--------
Does the bank's trustee conduct of directing trust assets predominantly into its own proprietary products without disclosure or authorization violate OCC fiduciary standards?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is self-dealing in violation of the fiduciary duty of loyalty.

The OCC Conflicts of Interest handbook expressly identifies self-dealing between related parties and fiduciary accounts as a prohibited conflict type requiring disclosure and authorization. Under common law fiduciary principles codified in OCC guidance, a trustee owes an undivided duty of loyalty to the trust and its beneficiaries and may not profit from its position without proper authorization. The bank failed to comply with the will's governing provisions, obtain court approval, or secure informed beneficiary consent before engaging in self-beneficial transactions. The absence of an internal policy requiring committee review of affiliated-product transactions further reflects a breakdown in the governance responsibilities outlined in OTS Trust Handbook Section 200.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
OCC Comptroller's Handbook – Asset Management, Conflicts of Interest (AM-CI), Version 1.0 (January 2015); 12 USC 92a; OTS Trust Handbook Section 110 – Fiduciary Duties

KEY FACTOR
----------
The bank engaged in undisclosed self-dealing by investing trust assets in proprietary products generating additional bank revenue without beneficiary consent, court authorization, or governing-document authority.
