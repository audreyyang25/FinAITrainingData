Source Folder: 06_DOL_Fiduciary
Topic: pte_2020_02
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Angela Torres, a fee-based financial advisor at a dually registered firm, recommends that her client Robert Dunn, age 55, roll $190,000 from his former employer's 401(k) plan into an IRA managed by Angela on a fee-based advisory platform charging 0.90% annually. Robert's 401(k) plan offers only 12 investment options, has limited online tools, and charges a 0.85% total plan expense (including administrative fees and fund expenses). Angela prepares a detailed written rollover analysis comparing the 401(k) plan's investment menu, fees, and services with the IRA's broader investment options (including ETFs, individual bonds, and model portfolios), personalized financial planning services, and tax-loss harvesting capabilities. The analysis documents that the slight fee increase of 0.05% is offset by substantially broader investment choices, personalized advice, and integrated estate and tax planning. Angela's firm has current PTE 2020-02 compliant written policies, provides full disclosure of her advisory fee and material conflicts, and conducted its annual retrospective review three months prior.

QUESTION
--------
Is the advisor's rollover recommendation from a 401(k) to a slightly higher-cost fee-based IRA compliant with PTE 2020-02 given the marginal cost increase?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

PTE 2020-02's best interest standard does not require that the lowest-cost option always be recommended; rather, it requires that the fiduciary prudently analyze and document why the recommendation serves the retirement investor's best interest considering all relevant factors including investment options, fees, services, and the investor's specific needs. Angela prepared a thorough comparative analysis documenting that the marginal 0.05% fee increase is justified by materially broader investment options, personalized financial planning, and tax-optimization services unavailable in the 401(k). Her firm has also satisfied all procedural conditions — current written policies, conflict disclosures, and a recent retrospective review. This represents a well-documented, substantively justified rollover recommendation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
DOL PTE 2020-02 §II(a)-(d) – Impartial Conduct Standards, Documentation, Policies and Procedures, and Retrospective Review

KEY FACTOR
----------
The advisor thoroughly documented the comparative analysis showing that the marginal cost increase was justified by substantially broader investment options and personalized services, satisfying PTE 2020-02's best interest standard.
