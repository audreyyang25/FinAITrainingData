FACTS
-----
Triad Wealth Advisors, a Boston-based RIA registered in Massachusetts and 10 other states, serves 240 retail clients in Massachusetts, 180 in Connecticut, 320 in New Hampshire, and 155 in New York. The firm adopted a conflict-of-interest policy that applies the highest applicable state standard across all client accounts: Massachusetts fiduciary duty (2020), which requires all adviser recommendations prioritize client interest over firm profit and mandate disclosure of all material conflicts. The firm prohibits proprietary product recommendations in retail accounts and uses only third-party mutual funds and ETFs with expense ratios below 0.35%. For all clients regardless of state, the firm discloses all conflicts in writing, updates annually, and documents suitability in accordance with Massachusetts standards. A Connecticut retail client invested $420,000 with the firm; the firm recommended a diversified portfolio of low-cost index ETFs with a total expense ratio of 0.18%, based on the client's risk profile and 15-year time horizon.

QUESTION
--------
Did Triad comply with applicable state fiduciary obligations by applying Massachusetts's higher standard uniformly across all client accounts in multiple states?

ANSWER
------
Yes. Triad's approach satisfies all applicable state law obligations. Massachusetts fiduciary duty (enacted 2020) applies to all adviser recommendations to Massachusetts residents. Connecticut does not have a state-level fiduciary rule for retail clients beyond SEC standards. New Hampshire and New York have varying requirements (NY Reg 187 for annuities; no blanket fiduciary rule). By applying the Massachusetts standard uniformly, Triad ensures compliance with the highest applicable state rule. This 'highest standard' approach is recognized under NASAA Model Rule guidance and is explicitly permitted under multi-state practice. The firm's conflict disclosures, absence of proprietary products, and focus on low-cost, transparent investments satisfy the affirmative fiduciary duty. The Connecticut client received no less favorable treatment; in fact, Massachusetts standards provide additional protection beyond Connecticut law.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Massachusetts Fiduciary Rule (2020) §258D.1 et seq.; NASAA Model Rule for Financial Planners and Advisers; Multi-state fiduciary harmonization principle

KEY FACTOR
----------
A multi-state adviser's application of the highest applicable state standard to all clients uniformly complies with all state laws and reflects best practices in fiduciary governance.
