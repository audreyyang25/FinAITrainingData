Source Folder: 22_PIABA_Materials
Topic: expungement_standard_factually_impossible
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A broker named Derek Holloway settled a customer complaint for $45,000 alleging unsuitable recommendations in variable annuities. The customer did not oppose expungement at the subsequent hearing. The sole arbitrator granted expungement, noting only that the settlement did not constitute an admission of liability and that the broker had an otherwise clean record. No finding was made that the claim was factually impossible, clearly erroneous, or that the broker was not involved in the alleged conduct.

QUESTION
--------
Did the arbitrator comply with FINRA's expungement standards when granting the expungement without making one of the three required affirmative findings?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of FINRA Rule 2080 and the applicable expungement standards.

FINRA Rule 2080 requires that before a court or arbitration panel orders expungement, it must affirmatively find that the claim is factually impossible or clearly erroneous, the broker was not involved in the alleged conduct, or the claim is false. Granting expungement solely because a settlement included no admission of liability and the broker had a clean record does not satisfy any of the three required grounds. PIABA has consistently advocated that such awards undermine the integrity of BrokerCheck as a public investor protection tool.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2080; FINRA Code of Arbitration Procedure Rule 12805

KEY FACTOR
----------
Arbitrator failed to make any of the three affirmative findings required by FINRA Rule 2080 before ordering expungement.
