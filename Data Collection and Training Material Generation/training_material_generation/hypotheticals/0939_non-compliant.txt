ID: 939
Source Folder: 21_FINRA_OHO_Decisions
Topic: leveraged_etf_suitability_retiree
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Registered representative Marcus Delaney recommended a 3x leveraged S&P 500 ETF to a 71-year-old retired schoolteacher with a fixed income, moderate risk tolerance, and a stated investment objective of capital preservation. The client had no prior experience with leveraged products and relied on her portfolio for monthly living expenses. Delaney made the recommendation without reviewing the client's complete financial profile and without documenting any suitability analysis. The client subsequently lost 42% of her invested principal during a two-week volatility spike.

QUESTION
--------
Did Delaney's recommendation of a 3x leveraged ETF to this retired client satisfy FINRA's suitability requirements?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of FINRA Rule 2111 (suitability).

Rule 2111 requires that a registered representative have a reasonable basis to believe a recommendation is suitable based on the customer's investment profile, including age, risk tolerance, investment objectives, and financial situation. A 3x leveraged ETF resets daily and carries extreme short-term volatility risk fundamentally inconsistent with capital preservation objectives and fixed-income reliance. The failure to document any suitability analysis further compounds the violation under Rule 4511.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111; FINRA Regulatory Notice 09-31 (Leveraged and Inverse ETFs)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Recommending a high-risk leveraged product to a conservative retiree dependent on investment income, without documented suitability analysis, directly violates the customer-specific suitability obligation of Rule 2111.
