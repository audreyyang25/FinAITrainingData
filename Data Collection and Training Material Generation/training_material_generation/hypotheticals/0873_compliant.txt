Source Folder: 18_OCC_Bank_Trust_Guidance
Topic: bank_fiduciary
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Consolidated Trust Company, a national bank exercising fiduciary powers under 12 USC 92a, serves as investment trustee for a $6.8 million revocable living trust that became irrevocable upon the grantor's death. The trust document expressly authorizes the trustee to invest in affiliated mutual funds 'provided such investments are suitable for the trust and disclosed to beneficiaries.' The bank's trust committee conducts a documented suitability review concluding that the bank's internally managed 'Consolidated Balanced Fund' — which charges a 0.45% management fee — is appropriate for the trust's growth-and-income objective and moderate risk tolerance. The bank sends annual written disclosures to all three adult remainder beneficiaries itemizing the affiliated fund's fees, the bank's receipt of those fees, and the existence of comparable non-affiliated alternatives, and the beneficiaries raise no objection over three consecutive years.

QUESTION
--------
Is the bank trustee's investment of trust assets in its own affiliated mutual fund, with documented suitability review and annual beneficiary disclosure, compliant with OCC fiduciary standards?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The OCC Conflicts of Interest handbook recognizes that investment in affiliated products is permissible where the governing instrument expressly authorizes such investment, the investment is suitable for the account's objectives and risk profile, and the conflict is fully disclosed to beneficiaries. Here, all three authorization elements are satisfied: the trust document contains explicit authorization language, the trust committee conducted and documented a suitability analysis, and annual written disclosures itemizing affiliated fees and non-affiliated alternatives were provided to adult remainder beneficiaries. The 0.45% management fee is within market norms for a balanced fund and was affirmatively disclosed, negating any inference of hidden self-dealing. This scenario illustrates a permissible affiliated-product investment that meets the conditions the OCC has established as safe-harbor criteria for conflict management.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
OCC Comptroller's Handbook – Conflicts of Interest (AM-CI), Self-Dealing Between Related Parties and Interests and Fiduciary Accounts; OTS Trust Handbook Section 110 – Fiduciary Duties; 12 USC 92a

KEY FACTOR
----------
Explicit governing-document authorization, documented suitability analysis, and full annual fee disclosure to beneficiaries satisfy the OCC's conditions for permissible affiliated-product investment in a fiduciary account.
