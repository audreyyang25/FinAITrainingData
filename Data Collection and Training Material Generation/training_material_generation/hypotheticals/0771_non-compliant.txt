Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Marcus Thornton, a former bank financial consultant in Sacramento, California, begins providing personalized investment advice to 12 clients for a quarterly fee totaling $18,000 per year. He has not filed Form ADV with the California Department of Financial Protection and Innovation (DFPI) through the IARD system, has not funded a daily account, and has no investment adviser registration or notice filing in any state. He tells clients he is 'exempt' because he manages less than $25 million in assets.

QUESTION
--------
Does Marcus Thornton's conduct comply with California state securities registration requirements for investment advisers?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is an unregistered investment adviser violation under California Corporations Code Section 25230.

California requires any person acting as an investment adviser to register with the DFPI by completing the multi-step process including IARD enrollment, funding the daily account, completing and submitting Form ADV, and registering investment adviser representatives. There is no blanket exemption based solely on assets under management for state-registered advisers. Thornton's failure to complete any registration steps while actively providing investment advice for compensation constitutes a clear violation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
California Corporations Code § 25230 – Prohibition on Unregistered Investment Adviser Activity; DFPI Registration Steps (Form ADV via IARD)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Providing personalized investment advice for compensation without completing any step of the required state investment adviser registration process.
