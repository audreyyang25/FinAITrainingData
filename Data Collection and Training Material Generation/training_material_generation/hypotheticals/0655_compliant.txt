FACTS
-----
Karen Liu, an investment adviser representative registered under NASAA's model rule jurisdiction in Oregon, manages a $275,000 portfolio for James O'Brien, a 45-year-old engineer with a high risk tolerance and a 20-year investment horizon. Karen recommends allocating 15% ($41,250) of the portfolio to a private REIT that charges a 1.5% annual management fee and has a 5-year lockup period. Karen provides James with a written disclosure detailing the illiquidity risk, the fee structure, and the fact that her firm receives a 2% placement fee. She documents that James's remaining $233,750 is invested in diversified, liquid equity and bond funds, that the private REIT allocation aligns with his stated goal of diversification beyond publicly traded securities, and that she considered two lower-fee alternative REITs but determined the selected fund's track record and strategy best matched James's objectives. James signs an acknowledgment of the risks and fee disclosures.

QUESTION
--------
Does Karen's recommendation of an illiquid private REIT comply with the NASAA Model Rule 102(a)(4)-1 fiduciary standard?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

NASAA Model Rule 102(a)(4)-1 establishes that investment advisers and their representatives are fiduciaries who must act primarily for the benefit of their clients. The rule prohibits recommending investments without a reasonable basis that the recommendation is suitable given the client's financial situation and needs. Karen's conduct satisfies this standard: the 15% allocation to an illiquid REIT is proportionate given James's long time horizon and high risk tolerance, she maintained ample portfolio liquidity in the remaining 85%, fully disclosed the placement fee conflict and illiquidity risks, documented consideration of alternatives, and obtained the client's written acknowledgment.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NASAA Model Rule 102(a)(4)-1 – Unethical Business Practices of Investment Advisers, Investment Adviser Representatives, and Federal Covered Advisers

KEY FACTOR
----------
Full disclosure of compensation conflicts, proportionate allocation relative to the client's risk tolerance and time horizon, documented consideration of alternatives, and client acknowledgment satisfy the NASAA fiduciary standard despite the illiquid product recommendation.
