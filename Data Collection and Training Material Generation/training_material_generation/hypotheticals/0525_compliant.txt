Source Folder: 02_SEC_Enforcement_Actions
Topic: disgorgement_remedies
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Clearview Wealth Advisors, Inc., a registered investment adviser with $520 million in AUM, historically invested client assets in Class A shares of certain mutual fund families for which it received 12b-1 fees averaging 25 basis points annually. In early 2022, following an internal compliance review that identified the conflict, Clearview's newly hired Chief Compliance Officer conducted a share class analysis and determined that lower-cost institutional share classes were available for 87% of client accounts. Clearview promptly converted all eligible accounts to institutional share classes, eliminating approximately $940,000 in annual 12b-1 fee revenue. Clearview updated its Form ADV Part 2A to remove all references to 12b-1 fee eligibility and added disclosure language explaining its prior receipt of 12b-1 fees. The firm then self-reported the historical conflict to the SEC's Division of Examinations and voluntarily reimbursed affected clients a total of $2.7 million, representing the excess fees paid over the prior three years plus interest calculated using the SEC's prejudgment interest rate. Clearview also retained an independent compliance consultant to review and enhance its policies and procedures regarding share class selection.

QUESTION
--------
Does Clearview Wealth Advisors' voluntary self-remediation of its prior share class conflict, including client reimbursement and self-reporting, satisfy SEC expectations for compliance remediation?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Clearview's conduct exemplifies the remedial approach the SEC has encouraged through initiatives like the Share Class Selection Disclosure Initiative, under which the Division of Enforcement offered favorable settlement terms to advisers who self-reported share class conflicts and reimbursed affected clients. By proactively identifying the conflict through internal compliance review, promptly converting to lower-cost share classes, self-reporting to the SEC, reimbursing clients with interest, and engaging an independent compliance consultant, Clearview undertook the type of comprehensive self-remediation that the SEC views favorably. This stands in contrast to cases like In the Matter of Saxony Capital Management, LLC (IA-5385) and Paradigm Wealth Advisory, LLC (IA-5852), where advisers maintained undisclosed conflicts for extended periods without voluntary remediation, necessitating SEC enforcement action. The completeness and timeliness of Clearview's response—including prejudgment interest and prospective compliance enhancements—demonstrates good faith compliance with fiduciary obligations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940, Sections 206(2) and 206(4); SEC Share Class Selection Disclosure Initiative (February 2018); SEC Rule 206(4)-7

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser's prompt self-identification of the share class conflict, voluntary self-reporting to the SEC, full client reimbursement with interest, and engagement of an independent compliance consultant collectively demonstrate the remedial response the SEC expects to avoid enforcement action.
