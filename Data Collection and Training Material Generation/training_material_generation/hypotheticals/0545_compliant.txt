Source Folder: 03_FINRA_AWC
Topic: finra_awc_suitability
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Denise Morales, a registered representative at a regional broker-dealer, recommended that her client Gerald Hutchinson, a 69-year-old retired engineer with $1.8 million in investable assets, a growth-and-income investment objective, and a moderate risk tolerance, allocate $180,000 (10% of his portfolio) to a non-traded REIT with a 7-year liquidity horizon. Morales documented that Hutchinson had a $4,200/month pension, $2,800/month in Social Security benefits, no debt, and $350,000 in liquid savings outside the recommended investment. She provided Hutchinson with the prospectus, discussed the illiquidity risk and 7-year hold period, disclosed the 7% upfront commission she would receive, and documented his acknowledgment. Her firm's Reg BI supervisory principal reviewed and approved the recommendation before execution.

QUESTION
--------
Was Morales's recommendation of a non-traded REIT to a 69-year-old retiree with moderate risk tolerance compliant with Reg BI and suitability requirements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The Care Obligation under Reg BI requires that the recommendation be in the customer's best interest based on the customer's investment profile, including financial situation, needs, and objectives. The $180,000 allocation represents only 10% of Hutchinson's $1.8 million portfolio, and he retains $350,000 in liquid savings plus $7,000/month in guaranteed income, making the illiquidity risk manageable. Morales satisfied the Disclosure Obligation by providing the prospectus and disclosing her 7% commission, and the recommendation was reviewed and approved by a supervisory principal. The concentration level, client wealth, guaranteed income streams, and thorough documentation support the conclusion that the recommendation was suitable and in Hutchinson's best interest.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Care Obligation; FINRA Rule 2111 – Suitability

KEY FACTOR
----------
A non-traded REIT recommendation to a senior client can be compliant when the allocation is a modest portfolio percentage, the client has substantial liquid reserves and guaranteed income, and full disclosure and supervisory review are documented.
