Topic: reg_bi_compliance
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Murray Creek Securities, a small FINRA member firm in East Texas with four registered representatives, has been operating since June 30, 2020, without establishing written supervisory procedures addressing Regulation Best Interest. The firm services approximately 200 retail customer accounts, recommending a mix of mutual funds, variable annuities, and municipal bonds. The firm's compliance manual still references only the legacy suitability standard under FINRA Rule 2111 and contains no policies addressing the Care, Conflict of Interest, or Disclosure Obligations under Reg BI. The firm's principal reviews trade blotters weekly but applies no Reg BI-specific criteria when evaluating recommendations made to retail customers.

QUESTION
--------
Does the firm's failure to establish and maintain written supervisory procedures specifically addressing Reg BI obligations constitute a violation?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is not compliant. The firm has violated the Reg BI Compliance Obligation and FINRA supervisory rules.

The Reg BI Compliance Obligation requires broker-dealers to establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with Reg BI as a whole, including the Care, Conflict of Interest, and Disclosure Obligations. FINRA Rules 3110(a) and 3110(b) independently require member firms to establish a supervisory system and written supervisory procedures addressing each applicable securities law and regulation. A firm that continues to reference only the legacy suitability standard after June 30, 2020, has failed both requirements. This mirrors the Murray Securities AWC (No. 2021069350301), where FINRA found the firm failed to establish and maintain a supervisory system and written policies and procedures to comply with Reg BI.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iv) – Reg BI Compliance Obligation; FINRA Rules 3110(a) and 3110(b)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm had no written policies or procedures addressing Reg BI despite the rule being effective since June 30, 2020, directly violating the Compliance Obligation.
