Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Meridian Financial Partners, a dually registered broker-dealer and investment adviser with $780 million in advisory AUM, identifies through an internal review that 23 advisory clients had been placed in higher-cost mutual fund share classes between 2019 and 2022, resulting in approximately $67,000 in excess fees paid collectively. Before receiving any SEC examination notice, the firm self-reports the issue to the Division of Examinations, calculates individualized restitution amounts for each affected client, remits full restitution plus 3.5% interest within 90 days, updates its compliance policies to require automated share class screening at the point of trade entry, retrains all 14 investment adviser representatives, and amends its Form ADV Part 2A to include enhanced conflict-of-interest disclosure regarding share class economics. The firm also engages an independent compliance consultant to review and certify its remediated procedures.

QUESTION
--------
Despite the historical share class selection errors, did Meridian Financial Partners' self-reporting and remediation satisfy SEC examination standards and demonstrate conduct consistent with fiduciary obligations?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The SEC's Share Class Selection Disclosure Initiative explicitly encouraged self-reporting and voluntary remediation of share class conflicts, offering favorable settlement terms to firms that proactively identified and corrected share class selection failures. Meridian's conduct mirrors the framework the Division of Examinations has endorsed: self-identification of the issue before examination, full client restitution with interest, systemic policy remediation including automated controls, personnel retraining, enhanced disclosure, and independent verification. While the original share class placements constituted fiduciary breaches, the comprehensive and proactive remediation program—undertaken without regulatory compulsion—is precisely the type of conduct the SEC has treated as mitigating and compliant going forward.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940 §§ 206(1), 206(2), 206(4); SEC Rule 206(4)-7; SEC Share Class Selection Disclosure Initiative (SCSD Initiative, 2018); SEC Division of Examinations FY 2025/2026 Exam Priorities

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm voluntarily self-reported historical share class errors, paid full restitution with interest, and implemented systemic automated controls and enhanced disclosures before any SEC examination, consistent with the framework endorsed by the SEC's Share Class Selection Disclosure Initiative.
