ID: 616
Source Folder: 06_DOL_Fiduciary
Topic: dol_rollover
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Maria Gonzalez, age 58, has a 401(k) plan with her employer containing $340,000 in low-cost index funds with an all-in expense ratio of 0.04%. Her financial advisor, Greg Paulson, recommends she roll the entire balance into an IRA at his broker-dealer, where he places her in a portfolio of actively managed mutual funds with an average expense ratio of 1.35% and a 5.25% front-end sales load. Greg earns a $12,750 commission on the rollover transaction. Greg does not document any comparison of the fees, investment options, or services available in Maria's existing 401(k) plan versus the proposed IRA, nor does he consider that Maria could leave her assets in the 401(k) plan at no additional cost. Greg's firm has no written rollover analysis policy.

QUESTION
--------
Did the advisor satisfy DOL fiduciary obligations and PTE 2020-02 requirements when recommending this 401(k)-to-IRA rollover?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of PTE 2020-02's Impartial Conduct Standards and the requirement to document a comparative analysis of the rollover recommendation.

PTE 2020-02 requires that investment advice fiduciaries recommending rollovers must act in the retirement investor's best interest and provide recommendations that reflect the care, skill, and prudence of a knowledgeable professional. The exemption specifically requires documentation of the bases for a rollover recommendation, including a comparison of fees, services, and investment options between the existing plan and the proposed IRA. Greg failed to perform or document any such comparative analysis, and the dramatically higher costs in the IRA (1.35% expense ratio plus a 5.25% load versus 0.04%) strongly suggest the recommendation was driven by his $12,750 commission rather than Maria's best interest.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
DOL PTE 2020-02 – Impartial Conduct Standards; ERISA §404(a)(1) – Duty of Loyalty and Prudence

KEY FACTOR
----------
The advisor failed to perform and document the required comparative analysis between the existing 401(k) plan and the proposed IRA rollover, as mandated by PTE 2020-02.
