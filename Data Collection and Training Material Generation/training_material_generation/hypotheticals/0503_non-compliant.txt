Source Folder: 01_SEC_Guidance_NoAction_Interpretive
Topic: no_action_reliance
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Brighton Wealth Management, a dually registered broker-dealer and investment adviser, recommends that Margaret Chen, a 72-year-old retired schoolteacher with $340,000 in retirement savings, liquidate her diversified bond portfolio to purchase a proprietary non-traded REIT paying a 7.5% stated distribution. Brighton's CRS (Form CRS) discloses that it offers proprietary products but does not specifically disclose the 4.5% upfront commission Brighton receives on the REIT or the revenue-sharing arrangement with the REIT sponsor. Brighton's compliance policies reference Reg BI but contain no written procedures for identifying, disclosing, or mitigating conflicts arising from proprietary product sales. The registered representative documents only that the client 'expressed interest in higher income' as the basis for the recommendation.

QUESTION
--------
Did Brighton Wealth Management satisfy Reg BI's Conflict of Interest and Care Obligations in recommending the proprietary non-traded REIT to Ms. Chen?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation and SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation.

Reg BI's final rule (Release 34-86031) explicitly states that the standard of conduct 'cannot be satisfied through disclosure alone' and requires broker-dealers to establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to identify and disclose material conflicts, and where disclosure is insufficient, to mitigate or eliminate them. Brighton failed to disclose the specific 4.5% commission and revenue-sharing arrangement, and maintained no written conflict mitigation procedures for proprietary product recommendations. Additionally, the Care Obligation requires a reasonable basis for believing the recommendation is in the retail customer's best interest considering the customer's investment profile; recommending that a 72-year-old retiree liquidate a diversified bond portfolio for a concentrated illiquid product based solely on a desire for 'higher income' without evaluating liquidity needs, concentration risk, or alternatives falls short of this standard.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation; Regulation Best Interest Release No. 34-86031

KEY FACTOR
----------
Brighton failed to specifically disclose material compensation conflicts, maintained no written conflict mitigation procedures for proprietary product sales, and did not perform a reasonable-basis analysis of the suitability of an illiquid concentrated product for an elderly retiree with limited assets.
