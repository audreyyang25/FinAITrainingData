ID: 841
Source Folder: 17_SEC_Staff_Bulletins
Topic: disclosure_requirements
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A registered broker-dealer at a mid-sized firm onboards a 68-year-old retired schoolteacher with $420,000 in a rollover IRA originating from a pension buyout. The representative recommends a variable annuity with a 7-year surrender period and a 1.85% annual expense ratio. The firm's Form CRS was filed with the SEC in July 2020, but the representative never delivers it to the client at account opening, nor does he provide any Reg BI-specific disclosure regarding material conflicts, including that the firm earns a $21,000 upfront commission on the sale. The client signs a new account form that references 'applicable disclosures available upon request' without any delivery confirmation.

QUESTION
--------
Does the failure to deliver Form CRS and conflict disclosures at the time of the rollover IRA account opening violate Reg BI's Disclosure Obligation?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of Reg BI's Disclosure Obligation.

Reg BI's Disclosure Obligation requires broker-dealers to provide retail customers with full and fair disclosure of all material facts relating to the scope of the relationship and material conflicts of interest prior to or at the time of the recommendation. Form CRS must be delivered to each retail investor at or before the earliest of the opening of an account or a recommendation. A reference to disclosures 'available upon request' does not satisfy the affirmative delivery requirement. The $21,000 upfront commission constitutes a material conflict that must be specifically disclosed under the Care and Disclosure Obligations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation; Form CRS Delivery Requirements (Rule 17a-14)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm failed to affirmatively deliver Form CRS and material conflict disclosures at account opening, relying instead on a passive 'available upon request' reference, which does not satisfy Reg BI's affirmative disclosure mandate.
