Source Folder: 15_FINRA_Regulatory_Notices
Topic: senior_investor_protection
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Robert, age 74, a retired engineer with $1.2 million in investable assets, a $6,500 monthly pension, and a moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance documented and confirmed annually, asks his registered representative about diversifying his portfolio with a 10% allocation to an alternative mutual fund employing a long/short equity strategy. The representative provides Robert with the fund's prospectus, explains the use of short selling and derivatives, discusses the fund's higher expense ratio of 1.85% compared to traditional equity funds, and documents Robert's stated understanding and desire to proceed. The firm's supervisory principal reviews and approves the recommendation, noting Robert's substantial assets, documented risk tolerance, and the modest allocation percentage. The firm also confirms that Robert's trusted contact person information is current on file pursuant to FINRA Rule 4512.

QUESTION
--------
Is the recommendation of a 10% allocation to a long/short alternative mutual fund to this 74-year-old investor compliant with FINRA suitability and senior investor protection requirements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The recommendation satisfies all three components of FINRA Rule 2111 suitability: the representative understands the Alt Fund's characteristics (reasonable-basis), the modest 10% allocation is appropriate given Robert's $1.2 million portfolio, pension income, and documented moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance (customer-specific), and there is no pattern of excessive or unsuitable transactions (quantitative). The firm followed Regulatory Notice 22-11's guidance by ensuring the representative explained the product's unique risks, the supervisory principal reviewed and approved the transaction, and the client's trusted contact information was verified per Rule 4512. The client's age alone does not preclude suitable recommendations of Alt Funds when all other factors support the allocation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability); FINRA Rule 4512 (Trusted Contact Person); FINRA Regulatory Notice 22-11 (Alt Funds Obligations)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The modest Alt Fund allocation was appropriate for the client's substantial assets, documented risk tolerance, and income, and the firm followed proper disclosure, supervisory review, and senior investor protection procedures.
