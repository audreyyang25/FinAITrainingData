Source Folder: 16_Congressional_Materials
Topic: gao_findings
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A dually registered firm's advisor recommends that a 44-year-old nurse with $310,000 in a rollover IRA and a moderate risk tolerance invest $75,000 (24% of the account) in the firm's proprietary balanced mutual fund carrying an expense ratio of 0.92%, rather than a comparable Vanguard fund at 0.14%. At the time of the recommendation, the advisor provides the client with a written Form CRS and a separate conflict disclosure document explicitly stating that the firm receives 12(b)-1 fees of 0.25% annually from the proprietary fund and that comparable lower-cost alternatives exist. The disclosure names three specific index fund alternatives with their respective expense ratios. The advisor's written rationale documents that the proprietary fund offers active tax-loss harvesting within the fund structure and a lower minimum investment threshold that aligns with the client's stated preference for managed diversification, and the client signs an acknowledgment confirming she reviewed the cost comparison and elected the proprietary fund.

QUESTION
--------
Does the advisor's recommendation of the higher-cost proprietary fund satisfy Reg BI's Disclosure and Care Obligations despite the existence of lower-cost alternatives?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

GAO reports on Reg BI implementation acknowledged that recommending a higher-cost proprietary product is not per se non-compliant provided the firm fully discloses the conflict and the advisor articulates a reasonable basis for why the product nonetheless serves the client's best interest. Here, the advisor satisfied the Disclosure Obligation by providing Form CRS and a conflict-specific written disclosure naming the 12(b)-1 fee arrangement and identifying comparable lower-cost alternatives with specific expense ratios. The Care Obligation is satisfied because the written rationale identifies product-specific features—active tax-loss harvesting and a lower investment minimum—that are reasonably related to the client's documented preference for managed diversification, and the 0.78% expense ratio differential is disclosed and acknowledged in writing by the client. The SEC's investor-protection framework under the Exchange Act, as amplified by Dodd-Frank §913, is served rather than undermined when conflicts are fully disclosed and client choice is preserved through informed written acknowledgment.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Form CRS delivery requirements; Dodd-Frank Act §913

KEY FACTOR
----------
Comprehensive written disclosure of the 12(b)-1 conflict, identification of specific lower-cost alternatives, a documented product-specific rationale, and a signed client acknowledgment collectively satisfy Reg BI's Disclosure and Care Obligations even where a higher-cost proprietary product is recommended.
