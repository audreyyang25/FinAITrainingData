Source Folder: 15_FINRA_Regulatory_Notices
Topic: reg_bi_implementation
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Ridgeway Financial Group recruits a top-producing team of four financial advisors from a competing wirehouse, offering a recruitment package totaling $2.8 million in forgivable transition loans over seven years. Upon onboarding, the firm delivers each affected customer an educational communication disclosing the recruitment compensation arrangement, including the total dollar value of transition payments, the forgiveness schedule, and a clear statement that this compensation creates a conflict of interest that could incentivize the advisors to recommend proprietary products or increase transaction volume. The firm also files updated Forms U4 and updates CRS disclosures. Ridgeway's compliance department implements a 12-month enhanced surveillance program on the recruited team's recommendations, comparing product mix, turnover rates, and fee structures against the team's historical patterns and firm-wide benchmarks. During review, two instances of elevated turnover are flagged and investigated; both are determined to reflect legitimate client-directed rebalancing in response to market volatility.

QUESTION
--------
Did Ridgeway Financial Group comply with its conflict of interest disclosure and supervisory obligations when onboarding recruited advisors who received substantial transition compensation?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

FINRA Regulatory Notice 15-19 addresses the conflict of interest inherent in large recruitment signing bonuses and transition packages, identifying them as posing serious conflicts between the advisor's financial incentive and the client's interest. Ridgeway addressed this by providing the educational communication with specific dollar amounts and conflict descriptions contemplated by the proposal, implementing enhanced surveillance tied to objective metrics, and investigating flagged activity. The Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation requires firms to establish policies to identify, disclose, and mitigate conflicts—all of which the firm did through disclosure, monitoring, and documented investigation of anomalies.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iii) – Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation; FINRA Rules 3110 (Supervision), 2210 (Communications); FINRA Regulatory Notice 15-19 (Recruitment Practices)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm proactively disclosed the specific dollar value and nature of the recruitment conflict, and implemented a risk-based enhanced surveillance program with documented investigation of flagged activity, satisfying both Reg BI conflict obligations and FINRA supervisory standards.
