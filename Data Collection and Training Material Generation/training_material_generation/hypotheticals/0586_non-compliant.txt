ID: 586
Source Folder: 06_DOL_Fiduciary
Topic: dol_rollover
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Marcus Webb, age 58, has a 401(k) with his employer containing $340,000 invested in a diversified mix of low-cost index funds with a blended expense ratio of 0.04%. His financial advisor, Rebecca Tomlinson of Pinnacle Wealth Partners, contacts Marcus after learning he is considering early retirement. Without conducting any comparative analysis of the fees, investment options, or services available in the existing 401(k) plan versus an IRA, Rebecca recommends Marcus roll over his entire balance into a proprietary IRA charging a 1.25% annual wrap fee and invested primarily in the firm's house-brand mutual funds with expense ratios averaging 0.72%. Rebecca receives a $5,100 commission on the rollover. She does not document why leaving the money in the 401(k) plan or rolling to a lower-cost IRA would be inferior alternatives.

QUESTION
--------
Did the financial advisor comply with PTE 2020-02's best interest standard when recommending the IRA rollover?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates PTE 2020-02's care obligation and documentation requirements for rollover recommendations.

PTE 2020-02 requires that investment advice fiduciaries act in the retirement investor's best interest and, specifically for rollover recommendations, conduct a documented comparative analysis of the existing plan's fees, investment options, and services against the recommended IRA. Rebecca failed to perform or document any such comparison. The dramatic increase in costs—from 0.04% to approximately 1.97% blended all-in fees—combined with the undisclosed financial incentive of the $5,100 commission, demonstrates a failure to satisfy the care obligation and the exemption's disclosure requirements.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
DOL PTE 2020-02 – Best Interest Standard; Section II(a) Care Obligation for Rollover Recommendations

KEY FACTOR
----------
The advisor failed to conduct and document the required comparative analysis of the 401(k) plan versus the recommended IRA rollover as mandated by PTE 2020-02.
