Topic: reg_bi_effectiveness
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A broker-dealer representative recommends a 45-year-old millennial client earning $175,000 annually with $410,000 in retirement assets and a high risk tolerance move $150,000 from a money market fund into a diversified portfolio of low-cost index ETFs (average expense ratio 0.06%) within a brokerage IRA. The representative's firm receives no additional transaction-based compensation on the ETFs beyond a $4.95 per-trade commission totaling $24.75 for five trades. The representative documented the client's investment objectives (long-term growth, 20+ year time horizon), compared the ETF portfolio to three alternatives including a target-date fund and a managed account, and noted that the client's existing money market allocation was generating negative real returns after inflation. The firm's Conflict of Interest Obligation disclosures were provided at account opening, and the representative's compensation grid does not incentivize proprietary products over third-party ETFs.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation satisfy Reg BI despite the client moving from a safe money market position into equity-heavy ETFs?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The recommendation satisfies all four Reg BI component obligations. The Care Obligation is met through documented analysis of the client's long time horizon, high risk tolerance, and comparison of reasonably available alternatives. The Disclosure Obligation is met through account-opening CRS and conflict disclosures. The Conflict of Interest Obligation is addressed by the firm's compensation structure that does not preferentially incentivize the recommended products. Moving from a negative-real-return money market to low-cost diversified equities is consistent with empirical research showing that younger investors with long horizons benefit from equity exposure, and the recommendation aligns with the client's stated growth objectives.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i)-(iv) – Reg BI Disclosure, Care, Conflict of Interest, and Compliance Obligations

KEY FACTOR
----------
The representative documented a thorough comparative analysis of alternatives, the recommendation aligned with the client's stated objectives and long time horizon, and the firm's compensation structure did not create preferential incentives for the recommended products.
