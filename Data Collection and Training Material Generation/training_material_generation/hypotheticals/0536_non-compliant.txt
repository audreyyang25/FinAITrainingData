Source Folder: 03_FINRA_AWC
Topic: finra_awc_supervision
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Ridgeway Financial Services, a FINRA member firm with 20 registered representatives and three branch offices, has been operating since Regulation Best Interest's compliance date of June 30, 2020 without adopting any written policies or procedures specifically addressing Reg BI. The firm continues to rely solely on its legacy FINRA Rule 2111 suitability policies and has not updated its supervisory system, training materials, or compliance manuals to incorporate the Reg BI Care, Disclosure, Conflict of Interest, or Compliance obligations. The firm's CCO acknowledges that no Form CRS has been filed or delivered to retail customers. A FINRA examination in 2024 identifies these deficiencies, and the firm is unable to produce any documentation showing Reg BI compliance efforts over the four-year period.

QUESTION
--------
Did Ridgeway Financial Services violate regulatory requirements by failing to establish written policies, procedures, and a supervisory system reasonably designed to achieve compliance with Reg BI since its June 30, 2020 compliance date?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is not compliant. This constitutes a violation of Exchange Act Rule 15l-1(a)(1), FINRA Rules 3110 and 2010.

Under Reg BI's Compliance Obligation, broker-dealers must establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with the rule's requirements. As seen in the Alexander Investment Services AWC (No. 2024080211301), FINRA has disciplined firms for failing to establish and maintain such policies and supervisory systems since the June 30, 2020 compliance date. The complete absence of Reg BI policies, procedures, training, and Form CRS delivery over a multi-year period demonstrates a systemic supervisory failure, not a minor gap.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Exchange Act Rule 15l-1(a)(1) (Regulation Best Interest – Compliance Obligation); FINRA Rules 3110 (Supervision) and 2010 (Standards of Commercial Honor)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm's total failure to adopt any Reg BI-specific written policies, procedures, or supervisory system since the June 30, 2020 compliance date is the pivotal violation.
