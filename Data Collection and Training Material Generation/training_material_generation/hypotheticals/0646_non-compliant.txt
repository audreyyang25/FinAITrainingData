ID: 646
Source Folder: 08_State_Fiduciary_Rules
Topic: state_fiduciary_annuity
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A licensed insurance producer in New York recommends a 15-year surrender period indexed annuity from Pacific Life to Margaret Donovan, a 79-year-old retired schoolteacher with $120,000 in liquid savings and $1,400/month in Social Security income. The producer earns a 7% commission on the sale. The producer documents that the client 'wants growth potential' but does not document any analysis of alternative products with shorter surrender periods, nor does the producer evaluate whether the client's liquidity needs over the next 15 years were considered. The transaction is completed in August 2020, after NY Reg 187's annuity provisions took effect.

QUESTION
--------
Did the producer satisfy the best interest standard under NY Reg 187 when recommending this long-surrender-period indexed annuity to the elderly client?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of NY Reg 187's best interest and care obligations.

NY Reg 187 requires that a recommendation be in the best interest of the consumer, considering the consumer's age, liquidity needs, financial situation, and the insurer product's features including surrender period. A 15-year surrender period for a 79-year-old client with limited liquid assets creates significant liquidity risk. The producer failed to document consideration of reasonably available alternatives with shorter surrender periods, violating both the duty of care (to evaluate suitability factors and alternatives) and the documentation requirements under Reg 187.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NY Reg 187 (11 NYCRR 224) – Best Interest Standard for Annuity Transactions, effective August 1, 2019

KEY FACTOR
----------
The producer failed to consider and document the client's age-related liquidity needs relative to the 15-year surrender period, violating NY Reg 187's duty of care and best interest standard.
