ID: 641
Source Folder: 08_State_Fiduciary_Rules
Topic: state_fiduciary_annuity
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A licensed insurance producer in New York recommends a 15-year deferred variable annuity with a 7% surrender charge to Margaret Chen, a 78-year-old retired schoolteacher with $180,000 in liquid savings and a stated need for income to cover monthly assisted-living costs. The producer earns a 6.5% commission on the sale. The producer documents the client's risk tolerance as 'moderate growth' but does not document any analysis of shorter-duration or lower-cost income alternatives such as single premium immediate annuities. The sale is completed in August 2019, after NY Reg 187's annuity provisions took effect.

QUESTION
--------
Did the producer comply with NY Reg 187's best interest standard when recommending the 15-year deferred variable annuity to this client?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates NY Reg 187's best interest standard.

NY Reg 187 requires that a recommendation be in the best interest of the consumer, considering the consumer's age, financial situation, insurance needs, and stated objectives. A 15-year deferred variable annuity with significant surrender charges is plainly unsuitable for a 78-year-old client who needs current income for assisted-living expenses. The producer's failure to evaluate or document consideration of more appropriate alternatives, such as an immediate annuity, violates the duty of care and documentation requirements under Reg 187.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NY Reg 187 – 11 NYCRR § 224.4(b) – Best Interest in Annuity Transactions (effective August 1, 2019 for annuity transactions)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Recommending a long-duration, illiquid deferred annuity to an elderly client with an immediate income need, without considering or documenting more suitable alternatives, violates NY Reg 187's best interest obligation.
