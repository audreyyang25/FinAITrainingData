Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Clearwater Financial Group, a broker-dealer, recommended that a 45-year-old software engineer named Priya Mehta roll over her $210,000 former-employer 401(k) into a new IRA at Clearwater rather than leaving assets in the plan. The representative, Thomas Abe, completed a written Best Interest Analysis documenting: (1) the plan's limited fund menu of 12 options versus 3,000+ options available in the IRA; (2) the plan's 0.45% administrative fee versus Clearwater's 0.30% platform fee for accounts over $100,000; (3) Mehta's stated goal of consolidating multiple accounts for simplified management; and (4) the loss of certain creditor protections under ERISA, which Abe explicitly disclosed to Mehta in writing. Clearwater's Form CRS was delivered at the initial meeting, and the rollover disclosure checklist required by the firm's WSPs was completed and signed by Mehta prior to the transfer.

QUESTION
--------
Did Thomas Abe and Clearwater Financial Group satisfy the Reg BI Care Obligation and disclosure requirements in recommending the IRA rollover to Priya Mehta?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

SEC staff guidance on rollover recommendations specifically requires that advisors document a comparison of costs, investment options, services, and features — including ERISA protections — before recommending a rollover. Abe's written Best Interest Analysis addressed each of these elements, demonstrated a quantifiable cost advantage in the IRA, and proactively disclosed the loss of ERISA creditor protections. The Form CRS delivery and signed rollover checklist further satisfy the disclosure and documentation requirements under Reg BI and the firm's own WSPs.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Staff Bulletin on Reg BI and IRA Rollover Recommendations (2021)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Complete written documentation of the rollover analysis, including disclosure of potential disadvantages, satisfied the Care Obligation for IRA rollover recommendations.
