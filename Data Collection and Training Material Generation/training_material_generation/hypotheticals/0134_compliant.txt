FACTS
-----
A 56-year-old participant with $550,000 in a 401(k) plan that imposes a $50 annual administrative fee and offers only three actively managed mutual funds (average 0.98% ER) requests to leave the employer. An advisor, acting as a fiduciary, documents a written analysis showing the rollover to a no-fee custodial IRA with access to low-cost index funds (0.04–0.12% ER) would save approximately $4,290 annually in aggregate fees. The advisor discloses all conflicts under PTE 2020-02 BICE, charges a flat $2,000 advisory fee (deferred until the participant separately engages the advisor for ongoing management), and provides written documentation that the rollover serves the participant's best interest. The participant has no stated annuity needs and moderate risk tolerance.

QUESTION
--------
Does this rollover recommendation comply with DOL ERISA fiduciary duty and PTE 2020-02 requirements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this recommendation satisfies DOL ERISA fiduciary duty and PTE 2020-02 standards.

The advisor satisfied ERISA § 404(a)(1)(A) by conducting a documented comparative analysis demonstrating that the rollover to a low-cost IRA is objectively preferable to remaining in the 401(k). The quantified annual savings ($4,290 in combined fees on $550,000) establish material economic benefit. The advisor complied with PTE 2020-02 by providing written disclosure of conflicts, a flat advisory fee that is not contingent on the rollover decision, and documentation that the rollover is in the participant's best interest. Critically, the advisor deferred the advisory fee until the participant separately engages for ongoing services, thereby severing any temporal link between the rollover recommendation and compensation. This structure demonstrates that the advice is not influenced by immediate financial gain. The participant's circumstances—no stated annuity need, moderate risk tolerance, and departure from employer—support the rollover as prudent.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
ERISA § 404(a)(1)(A) (prudence and loyalty); PTE 2020-02 § I.C (rollover advice BICE requirements); 29 CFR § 2550.404a-3(b)(1).

KEY FACTOR
----------
Written comparative fee analysis quantifying annual savings, coupled with deferred flat-fee compensation structure and full PTE 2020-02 BICE documentation without contingency-based incentive.
