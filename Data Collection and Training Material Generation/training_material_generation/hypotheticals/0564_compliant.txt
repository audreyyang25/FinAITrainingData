Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Westbrook Financial Services, a FINRA member firm with 45 registered representatives, receives revenue-sharing payments from three mutual fund families whose products appear on the firm's recommended list. The firm fully discloses the existence and nature of these revenue-sharing arrangements in its Form CRS, in a standalone Reg BI disclosure document provided to each retail customer before or at the time of any recommendation, and in its written supervisory procedures. The firm's compliance department maintains policies that require representatives to document, for each recommendation, why the specific fund selected is in the customer's best interest relative to reasonably available alternatives — including alternatives from fund families that do not pay revenue sharing. The firm conducts quarterly reviews of recommendation patterns to identify any statistical bias toward revenue-sharing fund families and has implemented a compensation structure that does not provide differential payouts based on which fund family is selected.

QUESTION
--------
Does the firm's receipt of revenue-sharing payments from fund families on its recommended list violate Reg BI's Conflict of Interest Obligation?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The Conflict of Interest Obligation does not prohibit all conflicts; rather, it requires firms to establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to identify, disclose, and mitigate or eliminate conflicts of interest. Westbrook has disclosed the revenue-sharing arrangement to customers, implemented policies requiring best-interest documentation for each recommendation, neutralized differential compensation incentives, and conducts ongoing surveillance for recommendation bias. These measures satisfy the Conflict of Interest Obligation's requirements to mitigate conflicts that create an incentive to place the firm's interest ahead of the customer's interest.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iii) – Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm disclosed the revenue-sharing conflict, mitigated it through neutral compensation and documented best-interest analysis, and monitored for bias — satisfying Reg BI's conflict mitigation requirements.
