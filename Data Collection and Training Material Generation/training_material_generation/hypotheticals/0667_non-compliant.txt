Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Apex Capital Partners, a SEC-registered investment adviser managing $2.1 billion across twelve private funds, grants a large sovereign wealth fund investor preferential redemption rights allowing quarterly liquidity with 15-day notice, while all other limited partners in the same fund are subject to a two-year lock-up with 90-day notice. The adviser discloses these preferential terms only to the sovereign wealth fund itself via a side letter but does not provide any notice to other current or prospective investors in the fund regarding the existence or nature of these preferential redemption terms. The fund's PPM states that 'the adviser may enter into side letter arrangements with certain investors' but does not describe the specific material economic terms granted. Compliance was last reviewed in early 2022 before the SEC's Private Fund Adviser Rules were finalized.

QUESTION
--------
Does Apex Capital Partners' failure to disclose the specific preferential redemption terms granted via side letter to all other investors in the fund comply with the SEC's Preferential Treatment Rule under the Private Fund Adviser Rules?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates the SEC's Preferential Treatment Rule requiring disclosure of preferential redemption and information rights.

Under the SEC's Private Fund Adviser Rules, preferential treatment regarding redemption rights and portfolio information that has a material, negative effect on other investors is prohibited unless disclosed. For preferential terms not outright prohibited, the adviser must provide written notice to all current and prospective investors describing the specific material economic terms of the side letter arrangement—not merely a generic statement that side letters may exist. Apex's vague PPM disclosure is insufficient to satisfy the rule's specific written notice requirement to all investors in the fund.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Private Fund Adviser Rules – Rule 211(h)(2)-3 (Preferential Treatment Rule); Investment Advisers Act Section 206

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser failed to provide specific written disclosure of material preferential redemption terms to all other investors in the fund, relying instead on a generic PPM statement.
