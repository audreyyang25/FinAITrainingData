Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Rachel Simmons is a dually registered representative at Meridian Financial Group, which operates both a broker-dealer and a registered investment advisory business. She recommends that David and Linda Reyes, both age 48 with a combined household income of $285,000, a $750,000 taxable brokerage account, and a 15-year growth-oriented time horizon, allocate $150,000 (20% of their portfolio) to a new interval fund focused on private credit. The fund charges a 2.5% annual management fee and offers quarterly liquidity limited to 5% of net assets. Rachel provides the Reyes family with a detailed written disclosure that specifically identifies the interval fund's liquidity limitations, the higher fee structure relative to comparable public fixed-income funds, and the $4,200 in trail compensation Rachel will receive annually. She documents that the recommendation is based on the clients' long time horizon, their existing liquidity in the remaining 80% of the portfolio, their desire for yield enhancement and diversification into private markets, and their stated ability to tolerate reduced liquidity on a portion of their assets. Meridian's compliance department reviews and approves the recommendation under its Reg BI policies before execution.

QUESTION
--------
Did Rachel satisfy Regulation Best Interest's Care, Disclosure, and Conflict of Interest Obligations when recommending the interval fund to the Reyes family?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Although interval funds present heightened liquidity and cost concerns, Regulation Best Interest does not prohibit recommendations of complex or higher-cost products. The Care Obligation requires that the recommendation have a reasonable basis considering the client's investment profile. Here, the clients have a long time horizon, adequate remaining liquidity, and a growth objective, supporting the allocation of 20% to an illiquid private credit strategy. The Disclosure Obligation is satisfied by the written, transaction-specific disclosure of the fund's liquidity terms, fee differential, and Rachel's trail compensation. The Conflict of Interest Obligation is addressed by the specific compensation disclosure and the firm's pre-trade compliance review. Reg BI requires a process-based evaluation, and each element is documented and substantiated.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i), (ii), and (iii) – Reg BI Care Obligation, Disclosure Obligation, and Conflict of Interest Obligation; Release No. 34-86031

KEY FACTOR
----------
The recommendation of a complex, higher-fee product was compliant because it was proportionate to the clients' profile, fully disclosed as to conflicts and limitations, and supported by documented analysis and compliance review.
