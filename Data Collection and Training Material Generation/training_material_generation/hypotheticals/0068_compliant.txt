Topic: ia_fiduciary_duty
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
An RIA managing $200M AUM recommends that a 62-year-old client with $1.2M in investable assets and conservative risk tolerance invest in a limited partnership focused on real estate income with a 1.5% management fee, 20% performance fee, and a 7-year lockup. The adviser discloses in writing the conflict of interest (the adviser is a 15% limited partner in the fund), the fee structure, liquidity restrictions, and risks. The adviser documents that the investment is suitable based on the client's stated goal of generating income from a portion (20%) of investable assets and documents why the adviser believes the expected return justifies the fees for this specific client.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation satisfy the Investment Adviser Act fiduciary duty of loyalty and care?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this recommendation complies with the fiduciary duties under the Investment Adviser Act.

The adviser satisfies the fiduciary duty of loyalty through full, transparent disclosure of the material conflict (15% limited partner ownership) and the opportunity cost (the adviser has economic incentive to recommend the fund). Critically, the adviser documented the business purpose: allocating a portion of the client's portfolio to an income-producing alternative investment aligned with the client's stated objective. The adviser also satisfied the duty of care by analyzing suitability (the investment is appropriate for a conservative 62-year-old seeking income in a portion of her portfolio) and ensuring the client understood the liquidity and fee structure before committing. The disclosure-and-consent framework of §206 permits otherwise conflicted transactions if the client is fully informed and can make a rational decision. The adviser's own investment in the fund (rather than profit from placing client assets) further demonstrates alignment with the client's interests.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Adviser Act §206 (fiduciary duty with ability to disclose and obtain consent to conflicts); SEC Rule 206(4)-2 (adviser may engage in principal transactions if client consents after full disclosure); SEC guidance on adviser conflict management and disclosure; SEC Release 33-10919 (on adviser duties and conflicts).

KEY FACTOR
----------
Comprehensive written disclosure of all material conflicts (adviser's LP ownership), fees, and risks, plus documented analysis of suitability for this specific client's stated income objective and risk profile.
