Source Folder: 03_FINRA_AWC
Topic: finra_awc_suitability
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Maria Delgado, a registered representative at a full-service broker-dealer, manages the account of Thomas Briggs, a 68-year-old retired engineer with a $1.4 million portfolio, a moderate risk tolerance, and a growth-and-income investment objective. In September 2023, Briggs tells Delgado he wants to allocate approximately 15% of his portfolio to individual equities in the technology sector, understanding the sector's volatility. Delgado documents the conversation in detail, provides Briggs with a written Reg BI disclosure explaining the risks of sector concentration and the commissions she will earn, and updates his customer profile to reflect his desire for targeted growth exposure. She selects five large-cap technology stocks and spreads the $210,000 allocation across them. Over the next 12 months, Delgado makes only two additional rebalancing trades in the position, generating $2,800 in total commissions.

QUESTION
--------
Did Delgado's recommendation of a 15% technology sector allocation to a retired client with a moderate risk tolerance and growth-and-income objective comply with Reg BI's Care Obligation?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

A 15% allocation to large-cap technology equities is not inherently unsuitable for a client with a $1.4 million portfolio, moderate risk tolerance, and a growth-and-income objective that contemplates equity exposure. Delgado exercised reasonable diligence by documenting the client's informed request, updating his profile, providing Reg BI disclosures including commission information, and selecting diversified large-cap positions rather than speculative securities. The minimal subsequent trading and low commission generation demonstrate she did not place her financial interest ahead of the customer's. This represents a permissible accommodation of an informed client's articulated investment preference.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation; FINRA Rule 2111 – Suitability

KEY FACTOR
----------
The thorough documentation of the client's informed preference, updated customer profile, Reg BI disclosures, and the reasonable size of the allocation relative to total portfolio value are the pivotal compliance factors.
