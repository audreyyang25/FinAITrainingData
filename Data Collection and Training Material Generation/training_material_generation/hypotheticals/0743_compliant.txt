Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
The Robert and Carol Henning Family Trust, managed by trustee Robert Henning (age 71), held a $1.8 million UBS Financial Services account overseen by advisor Jonathan Pratt. Henning filed a FINRA arbitration claim alleging breach of fiduciary duty and unsuitability related to a 22% allocation to municipal bond closed-end funds during a period of rising interest rates, which resulted in a $96,000 unrealized loss. However, discovery revealed that Henning had extensive investment experience as a retired CFO, had signed an investment policy statement expressly authorizing up to 30% in fixed-income closed-end funds, and had received quarterly performance reports documenting the allocation. The parties settled the underlying customer claim, and UBS subsequently requested an expungement hearing before an all-public arbitration panel, presenting evidence that the claims were factually impossible and clearly erroneous.

QUESTION
--------
Was the advisor's 22% allocation to municipal bond closed-end funds in the trust account compliant with suitability requirements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The allocation fell within the limits expressly authorized in the signed investment policy statement, and the trustee — a sophisticated former CFO — was provided regular performance disclosures. Municipal bond closed-end funds are a recognized fixed-income strategy appropriate for trust accounts with moderate risk tolerance. The settlement of the underlying claim and the panel's evaluation of whether the claim was factually impossible or clearly erroneous (consistent with expungement proceedings such as Weitzel Trust v. UBS and Kimura v. LPL Financial) further supports the conclusion that the advisor acted within the scope of the customer's documented investment objectives.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability — Customer-Specific and Reasonable-Basis Obligations); FINRA Rule 12805 (Expungement Standards)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The allocation was within the express limits of a signed investment policy statement held by a sophisticated investor, and the unrealized loss resulted from market conditions rather than unsuitable advice.
