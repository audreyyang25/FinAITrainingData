FACTS
-----
A 58-year-old executive with $2.8 million in investable assets, $450,000 of which is in a non-qualified brokerage account, pursues a deferred variable annuity (B-share) with 5% annual surrender charges (declining over 5 years), 1.2% M&E fee, and $13,500 commission (3%). The advisor documents: (1) client's objective to defer taxes on the non-qualified account and reduce annual tax liability over 15 years; (2) comparison to alternative taxable mutual fund platform showing variable annuity tax deferral would save $8,000-$12,000 over 10 years in deferred capital gains taxes; (3) client is comfortable with liquidity constraints given existing liquid emergency reserves of $200,000; (4) the advisor's fees as a percentage of total client assets are 0.50%, and the B-share structure was specifically selected to avoid level-load L-share charges (0.70% per year) given the client's stated 15-year holding period.

QUESTION
--------
Does this variable annuity recommendation comply with FINRA Rule 2330 and Reg BI?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this recommendation is compliant with FINRA Rule 2330(b) and Reg BI.

This recommendation satisfies Rule 2330(b) suitability because: (1) the documented investment objective (tax-deferred growth on non-qualified account to reduce annual tax liability) is clear and specific, and the variable annuity product directly addresses this objective—tax deferral within a non-qualified account is not available through mutual funds; (2) the advisor conducted and documented a comparative cost analysis showing the B-share structure's 5-year surrender charge schedule and 1.2% M&E fee would result in net tax savings of $8,000-$12,000 over 10 years relative to taxable mutual funds, offsetting the $13,500 commission; (3) the client's liquidity needs are satisfied by separate $200,000 emergency reserves (40% of non-qualified funds), so the 5-year surrender charge does not materially constrain the client's financial flexibility; (4) the client is a sophisticated investor (executive, $2.8M in assets) capable of understanding the trade-offs; and (5) the B-share structure is specifically justified by the client's 15-year holding period, making the surrender charge schedule more favorable than the L-share's perpetual 0.70% annual charge. The advisor's documentation of this comparative analysis and client acknowledgment of surrender charge terms demonstrates compliance with Rule 2330(d).

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2330(b)(1)(A) — investment recommendation must be suitable based on customer's financial situation, investment experience, investment objectives, and any other information obtained; Rule 2330(d) — documentation requirement; Reg BI Rule 15l-1(b)(2)(i)(A) — recommendation must be in client's best interest, considering available alternatives and compensation.

KEY FACTOR
----------
The advisor documented a specific tax-deferral objective tied to the non-qualified account, compared the variable annuity to the taxable mutual fund alternative with quantified tax savings, verified client liquidity needs were met separately, and justified the B-share structure by the client's long holding period.
