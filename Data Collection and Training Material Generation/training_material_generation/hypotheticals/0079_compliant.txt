Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A 45-year-old client with $200,000 liquid net worth, $1.2 million in employer 401(k), and stated objective of "retirement in 20 years with moderate growth" is placed in a diversified portfolio: 55% U.S. stock index funds (expense ratio 0.08%), 20% international stock index (0.12%), 15% bonds (0.06%), 10% alternatives. The adviser completes a comprehensive risk tolerance questionnaire documenting moderate risk capacity and moderate risk tolerance. The portfolio is rebalanced annually. The adviser documents the recommendation, explaining how the allocation aligns with the 20-year horizon and moderate growth objective.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation satisfy the Investment Adviser Act fiduciary duty of care?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this is compliant.

The adviser conducted a documented risk tolerance assessment and created a written client profile documenting the client's 20-year horizon, moderate growth objective, and moderate risk tolerance. The allocation (55/20/15/10) is proportionate to these circumstances and represented through low-cost index vehicles. The adviser documented the rationale linking the recommendation to the client's specific objectives and circumstances. Annual rebalancing demonstrates ongoing duty of care. The reasonable basis is evidenced by written suitability documentation, appropriate diversification, and low fee structure that minimizes conflicts of interest. This satisfies the investigative and documentation requirements of the duty of care under the Advisers Act.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940, Section 206(2); SEC Compliance Rule 206(4)-7; duty of care requires a reasonable basis for recommendations based on investigation of client circumstances, risk tolerance, and time horizon.

KEY FACTOR
----------
Documented risk tolerance assessment, written suitability analysis, and clear alignment between allocation and client's stated objectives, time horizon, and risk profile.
