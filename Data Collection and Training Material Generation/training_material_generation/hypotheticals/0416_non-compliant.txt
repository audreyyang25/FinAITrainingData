Source Folder: unknown
Topic: esg_sustainable_investing
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Sarah Chen, age 52, held a $680,000 taxable brokerage account with 25 years to retirement. Her advisor, without any discussion of ESG preferences, replaced her diversified portfolio (60% US large-cap index at 0.05% expense ratio, 40% international at 0.08%) with three ESG-focused funds: Vanguard ESG U.S. Stock ETF (0.09% ER), iShares MSCI USA ESG Select ETF (0.25% ER), and a high-conviction ESG emerging markets fund (1.10% ER). The advisor documented only: 'Client approved portfolio change.' No written disclosure of the 0.40–1.05 percentage point fee increase, no ESG preference was expressed by the client, and no discussion of potential performance drag (ESG screens historically underperform in energy/materials sectors). The advisor later stated in a compliance interview that ESG was 'an emerging trend' and recommended it to all clients in her age cohort.

QUESTION
--------
Did the advisor comply with Reg BI's care obligation and duty of loyalty in steering this client to higher-cost ESG products without documented preference or performance impact disclosure?

ANSWER
------
Non-compliant. Under Reg BI 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i), the advisor must conduct a reasonable investigation into the costs and benefits of each recommendation and ensure the recommendation is in the client's best interest, considering the client's investment profile and the material differences in cost. Here, the advisor (1) failed to investigate ESG performance impact; (2) increased costs by 40–105 bps without justification; (3) documented no ESG preference from the client; and (4) applied ESG to a cohort, suggesting predetermined sales practice rather than individualized assessment. This mirrors FINRA enforcement pattern where advisors upgraded client portfolios to higher-fee ESG products without documented suitability (similar to 2022–2023 sweeps). The lack of disclosure of fee increase and performance-screening consequences violates the duty of loyalty under the Investment Adviser Act §206(1).

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Reg BI Care Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i); Investment Adviser Act §206(1) (duty of loyalty); SEC IA-5653 Marketing Rule §206(4)-1(a)(1) (making misleading statements about ESG criteria).

KEY FACTOR
----------
Advisor recommended ESG products with materially higher fees to an unscreened cohort without client-expressed ESG preference, documented investigation of ESG impact, or disclosure of cost differential.
