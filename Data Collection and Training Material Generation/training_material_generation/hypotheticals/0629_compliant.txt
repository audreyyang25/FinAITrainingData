Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A dually-registered firm delivers its Form CRS to all new retail investors at the time of account opening and updates it within 30 days whenever material changes occur, as required. The Form CRS includes plain-language descriptions of the differences between brokerage and advisory services, fee structures for each, and the applicable standard of conduct. It prominently includes the question 'Who is my primary contact person? Is he or she a representative of an investment adviser or a broker-dealer?' and links to investor.gov/crs as required. A 72-year-old retired teacher, Mrs. Delgado, opens a new advisory account and receives the Form CRS along with a supplemental conversation guide. She asks her financial professional whether the firm has any disciplinary history. The professional discloses that one associated person received a FINRA letter of caution three years ago for a recordkeeping deficiency and directs Mrs. Delgado to BrokerCheck and IAPD for additional details. The firm's Form CRS separately discloses reportable legal and disciplinary history as required by the instructions.

QUESTION
--------
Does the firm's Form CRS delivery and content satisfy the Form CRS requirements, including the disclosure of disciplinary history and the inclusion of required conversation starters?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The Form CRS overview requires firms to inform retail investors about the types of relationships and services offered, fees and costs, conflicts of interest, the applicable standard of conduct, and whether the firm or its financial professionals have reportable legal or disciplinary history. The firm delivered the Form CRS at the beginning of the relationship, included all required content sections with conversation-starter questions, linked to investor.gov/crs, and affirmatively disclosed disciplinary history both in the document and verbally when asked. Providing a supplemental conversation guide and directing the client to BrokerCheck and IAPD for further information exceeds the baseline obligation and reflects industry best practices.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Form CRS (Form ADV Part 3); 17 CFR § 275.204-5 and 17 CFR § 249.319 – Form CRS Relationship Summary Requirements

KEY FACTOR
----------
Timely delivery of a complete Form CRS with all required content elements, including disciplinary history disclosure and the investor.gov/crs link, at the inception of the client relationship.
