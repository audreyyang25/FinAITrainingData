Source Folder: 14_NASAA_State_Enforcement
Topic: state_fraud
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Kuber Wealth Strategies, LLC, a state-registered investment adviser in Georgia with CRD No. 312088, manages $14 million for 38 clients. The firm's sole principal, Raj Mehta, maintains custody of six clients' funds totaling $2.3 million in a single omnibus bank account under the firm's name without segregating individual client holdings. Mehta has not notified the state administrator of having custody via Form ADV Item 9, has not engaged a qualified custodian to hold the assets in separate accounts for each client under the client's name, and has not arranged for an annual surprise examination. Mehta periodically transfers funds between the omnibus account and his firm's operating account to cover business expenses, later replenishing the amounts.

QUESTION
--------
Does the firm's handling of client funds comply with the NASAA Model Custody Rule 102(e)(1)-1 and applicable state custody requirements?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct constitutes multiple violations of NASAA Model Rule 102(e)(1)-1, including failure to notify the administrator of custody, failure to use a qualified custodian maintaining separate client accounts, and commingling client funds with firm operating funds.

Under NASAA Model Rule 102(e)(1)-1(a), it is deemed a fraudulent, deceptive, or manipulative act for a registered investment adviser to have custody of client funds unless the adviser (1) promptly notifies the administrator in writing via Form ADV, (2) uses a qualified custodian to maintain those funds in separate accounts for each client under the client's name or in accounts containing only clients' funds under the adviser's name as agent or trustee, and (3) provides required notice to clients. Mehta failed all three requirements and further commingled client assets with firm operating funds, which is a classic misappropriation pattern frequently cited in state enforcement actions.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NASAA Model Rule 102(e)(1)-1 – Custody of Client Funds or Securities by Investment Advisers, subsections (a)(1), (a)(2)(A)-(B), and (a)(3)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Maintaining custody of client funds in an unsegregated omnibus account without notifying the state administrator, without using a qualified custodian, and commingling those funds with firm operating capital.
