Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Sandra Okafor, a registered representative at Broadfield Investments, recommends that a 48-year-old software engineer with $950,000 in investable assets, a 15-year investment horizon, and a stated aggressive growth risk tolerance purchase $75,000 in a technology-sector ETF (expense ratio 0.35%) alongside a $25,000 position in a leveraged 2x S&P 500 ETF. Sandra's recommendation file includes a documented risk tolerance assessment, a written explanation noting that the leveraged ETF represents only 2.6% of the client's total portfolio and is consistent with the client's explicitly stated speculative allocation goals, a cost comparison to alternative sector funds, and a conflict disclosure confirming she receives no differential compensation between the two products. The client signed a contemporaneous acknowledgment that he understands the daily rebalancing risks of leveraged ETFs. Dodd-Frank §913 and the Reg BI legislative record confirm that best interest does not prohibit high-risk recommendations to sophisticated investors when the advisor's process is documented and conflicts are disclosed.

QUESTION
--------
Did Sandra Okafor's recommendation of a 2x leveraged ETF to this client satisfy Reg BI's Care and Disclosure Obligations?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Reg BI's Care Obligation requires that a recommendation be in the retail customer's best interest based on the customer's investment profile, not that it be the lowest-risk available option. Sandra documented the client's aggressive risk tolerance, quantified the leveraged ETF as a small speculative allocation (2.6% of portfolio), performed a comparative cost analysis, and obtained a client acknowledgment of leveraged ETF risks consistent with FINRA's Notice 09-31 guidance. The absence of differential compensation eliminates the primary conflict concern flagged in the Dodd-Frank §913 legislative record. The documented process and proportionate allocation satisfy the Care Obligation's reasonable-basis and customer-specific requirements.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation; FINRA Regulatory Notice 09-31 (Leveraged ETF Guidance)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The advisor's documented risk-tolerance assessment, proportionate allocation, cost comparison, conflict disclosure, and client acknowledgment collectively satisfy Reg BI's process-based best interest standard even for a high-risk product.
