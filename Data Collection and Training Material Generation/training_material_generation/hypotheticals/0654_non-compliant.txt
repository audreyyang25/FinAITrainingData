Source Folder: 08_State_Fiduciary_Rules
Topic: state_fiduciary_annuity
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Robert Simmons, a licensed insurance producer in New York, facilitates a 1035 exchange for his client, Gloria Davis (age 62, $380,000 in existing deferred annuity with no remaining surrender charges), into a new indexed annuity with a 10-year surrender schedule and 8% first-year surrender penalty. The new product offers a 3% premium bonus but has an annual spread charge of 2.5% and a participation rate cap of 50%. Robert documents Gloria's risk tolerance as 'moderate' and her objective as 'accumulation' but does not prepare a written comparison of the existing annuity's benefits, costs, and features against the replacement product. His notes state only that 'client wanted higher potential returns.' Robert earns a 6.5% commission on the new annuity versus no compensation on the existing contract. The insurer's market conduct examination file, similar to those reviewed in the Pacific Life & Annuity Company examination, shows no evidence of supervisory review of this replacement transaction.

QUESTION
--------
Does this 1035 exchange recommendation comply with NY Reg 187's best interest standard and replacement transaction requirements?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates NY Reg 187's best interest standard and replacement transaction documentation requirements.

NY Reg 187 requires that when recommending an annuity replacement, the producer must document a comparison of the existing and proposed products' benefits, costs, and features and demonstrate the replacement is in the client's best interest. Robert failed to prepare any written comparison, despite moving Gloria from a surrender-free contract into one with significant new surrender charges and restrictive indexed crediting features. The 3% premium bonus is substantially offset by the 2.5% spread charge and 50% participation cap, suggesting the exchange primarily benefited the producer's compensation. The absence of supervisory review, as would be flagged in a market conduct examination similar to the Pacific Life examination, compounds the compliance failure.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NY Reg 187 – Best Interest Standard (11 NYCRR 224); NY Insurance Regulation 60 – Replacement of Life Insurance and Annuities

KEY FACTOR
----------
Failure to prepare a written comparison of existing versus replacement annuity benefits and costs, combined with a commission-motivated exchange into a more restrictive product, violates NY Reg 187's replacement documentation and best interest requirements.
