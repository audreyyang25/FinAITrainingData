Source Folder: 23_International_Comparative
Topic: fca_consumer_duty_price_value_outcome
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A UK retail investment platform charges a 2.5% annual management fee to elderly, low-income retail customers holding low-risk cash ISA products that generate an average net return of 1.8% per annum. Internal management information shows that the cost of servicing these accounts is materially lower than the fee charged, and a competitor analysis conducted by the firm's own compliance team confirms the fees are significantly above market rates. Senior management is aware of the findings but takes no corrective action, citing commercial pressures.

QUESTION
--------
Does the firm's continued charging of disproportionate fees to vulnerable retail customers, despite internal evidence of poor value, satisfy the FCA Consumer Duty price and value outcome?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of FCA Consumer Duty (PRIN 12) and the Price and Value Outcome under PRIN 2A.4, which requires firms to ensure the price consumers pay is reasonable relative to the overall benefits of the product or service.

The FCA Consumer Duty requires firms to deliver good outcomes for retail customers, including ensuring that products and services represent fair value; here, fees that exceed net returns and are demonstrably above market rates cannot constitute fair value, particularly for a low-risk product. The firm's knowledge of the adverse findings through its own compliance review and subsequent inaction elevates this from an oversight to a conduct failure. The vulnerability of the affected customers, a factor PRIN 2A specifically highlights, further aggravates the regulatory exposure.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FCA PRIN 12; PRIN 2A.4 (Price and Value Outcome); FCA Consumer Duty Final Rules PS22/9

KEY FACTOR
----------
Knowingly maintaining fees that erode customer returns and exceed market norms for low-risk products, without remedial action, directly contradicts the Consumer Duty price and value outcome.
