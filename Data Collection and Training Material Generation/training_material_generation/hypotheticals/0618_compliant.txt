Topic: erisa_plan_fiduciary
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Rachel Kim is the HR Director and named ERISA fiduciary for a small employer's 401(k) plan with $8.2 million in assets and 74 participants. The plan is terminating because the company is shutting down. After extensive efforts to locate all participants, the plan has $47,000 in account balances belonging to 6 participants who cannot be found despite certified mail, email outreach, a commercial locator service, and searching the Social Security Administration's letter-forwarding program. Rachel, after consulting with the plan's ERISA counsel, elects to transfer the missing participants' balances to the PBGC's Missing Participants Program for terminating defined contribution plans. She documents each search step taken and the decision rationale in the plan's termination file. No distributions are made to an IRA rollover provider with whom the firm has a revenue-sharing arrangement.

QUESTION
--------
Did the plan fiduciary act consistently with ERISA fiduciary duties when transferring missing participants' balances to the PBGC Missing Participants Program upon plan termination?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

DOL FAB 2021-01 establishes a temporary enforcement policy under which the Department will not pursue fiduciary breach claims against plan fiduciaries of terminating defined contribution plans who transfer missing participants' balances to the PBGC's expanded Missing Participants Program, provided the fiduciary has conducted reasonable, documented search efforts. Rachel conducted multiple search methods, documented each step, consulted with ERISA counsel, and chose the PBGC program rather than a self-interested IRA rollover arrangement. This conduct falls squarely within the safe harbor and the DOL's temporary enforcement policy, satisfying ERISA §404(a)(1)'s prudence standard.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
ERISA §404(a)(1) – Duty of Prudence; 29 CFR 2550.404a-3 – Safe Harbor for Distributions from Terminated Individual Account Plans; DOL Field Assistance Bulletin 2021-01

KEY FACTOR
----------
The fiduciary conducted thorough, documented search efforts for missing participants and utilized the PBGC Missing Participants Program consistent with DOL FAB 2021-01's temporary enforcement policy for terminating defined contribution plans.
