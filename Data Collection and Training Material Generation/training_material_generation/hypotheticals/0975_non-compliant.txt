ID: 975
Source Folder: 23_International_Comparative
Topic: fca_consumer_duty_poor_product_outcome
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A retail bank in the UK offers a fixed-term savings product marketed to customers aged 70 and above with low financial literacy. The product contains a significant early withdrawal penalty equivalent to six months' interest, disclosed only in paragraph 14 of a 22-page terms and conditions document. The bank's internal testing showed that fewer than 10% of target customers understood the penalty feature, yet the product was launched without modification. The FCA's Consumer Duty came into full effect prior to the product launch.

QUESTION
--------
Does the bank's product design and disclosure approach satisfy the FCA Consumer Duty's requirements regarding consumer understanding and good outcomes for retail customers?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of FCA Consumer Duty (PRIN 12), specifically the consumer understanding outcome and the products and services outcome.

PRIN 12 requires firms to act to deliver good outcomes for retail customers, including ensuring that communications support customer understanding and that products meet the needs of the identified target market. Internal evidence demonstrating that fewer than 10% of target customers understood a material penalty feature is a direct indicator that the consumer understanding outcome is not being met. Proceeding with launch without remediation constitutes a foreseeable harm that the Duty requires firms to prevent.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FCA Consumer Duty (PRIN 12); FCA PS22/9 Consumer Duty Final Rules, Chapter 4 (Consumer Understanding Outcome)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Firm launched a product with a material feature demonstrably not understood by the target market, breaching the consumer understanding outcome under FCA PRIN 12.
