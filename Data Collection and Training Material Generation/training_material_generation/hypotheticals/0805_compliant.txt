ID: 805
Source Folder: 15_FINRA_Regulatory_Notices
Topic: senior_investor_protection
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Patricia Nguyen, age 74, with a $1.1 million portfolio and a moderate growth investment objective, requests that her advisor at Bayside Securities allocate $165,000 (15% of her portfolio) to the Goldman Sachs Absolute Return Tracker Fund, an alternative mutual fund using derivatives-based replication strategies. Her advisor, Lisa Chen, conducts a thorough suitability analysis noting Nguyen's 15-year investment experience including prior holdings in options-writing strategies, her $78,000 annual pension income that covers her living expenses, a $200,000 emergency reserve in CDs, and her stated 10-year time horizon. Chen provides Nguyen with the fund prospectus, a firm-prepared Alt Fund risk disclosure supplement explaining derivatives exposure and potential for tracking error, and documents a detailed conversation in which Nguyen confirms she understands the product may underperform in certain market conditions. The branch manager reviews the recommendation under the firm's enhanced Alt Fund supervisory procedures, confirms the allocation is consistent with the client's risk profile and overall portfolio diversification, and approves the trade.

QUESTION
--------
Did the advisor and firm comply with suitability and supervisory obligations in recommending an alternative mutual fund to a 74-year-old investor?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

While the client is a senior investor, age alone does not render a complex product recommendation unsuitable. Regulatory Notice 22-11 highlights effective practices including product-specific training, enhanced supervisory review, and providing customers with clear risk disclosures beyond the prospectus. Here, the advisor documented the client's substantial investment experience with similar strategies, confirmed independent income sources and liquidity reserves, ensured the allocation was a moderate percentage of overall assets, and provided layered disclosures. The firm's enhanced Alt Fund supervisory review—distinct from generic mutual fund approval—aligns with the effective practices FINRA identified in Notice 22-11.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rules 2111 (Suitability), 3110 (Supervision); SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation; FINRA Regulatory Notice 22-11 (Alternative Mutual Funds)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The recommendation was supported by documented suitability analysis reflecting the senior client's relevant experience, independent income, adequate liquidity, and moderate allocation size, with enhanced product-specific supervisory review consistent with FINRA Regulatory Notice 22-11 effective practices.
