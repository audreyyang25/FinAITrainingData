Source Folder: 08_State_Fiduciary_Rules
Topic: state_fiduciary_bd
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Kevin Huang is an investment adviser representative registered in a state that has adopted the NASAA Model Rule 102(a)(4)-1. He manages a $2.3 million portfolio for Sandra Bell, a 58-year-old executive with a high risk tolerance and a 10-year investment horizon. Bell asks Huang to invest 8% of her portfolio ($184,000) in a private REIT that Huang's advisory firm also recommends to other qualified clients. Huang discloses in writing that the firm receives a one-time placement fee of 1.5% from the REIT sponsor, provides Bell with the private placement memorandum detailing risks including illiquidity and lack of a secondary market, documents that Bell meets accredited investor qualifications, and confirms in his file notes that the allocation is consistent with Bell's stated objectives of portfolio diversification and real estate exposure. The remaining 92% of the portfolio is diversified across equities, fixed income, and liquid alternatives.

QUESTION
--------
Does Huang's recommendation of the private REIT comply with his fiduciary obligations under the NASAA Model Rule 102(a)(4)-1?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Under the NASAA Model Rule, an investment adviser representative is a fiduciary who must act primarily for the benefit of clients. While recommending a product from which the firm receives compensation creates a conflict, the rule does not prohibit such recommendations outright. Huang disclosed the placement fee in writing, ensured the client met suitability and accreditation requirements, documented the rationale for the allocation within the broader portfolio context, and limited the illiquid position to a reasonable proportion of total assets. This conduct satisfies the fiduciary duty of care and loyalty by prioritizing transparency, suitability, and client objectives.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NASAA Model Rule 102(a)(4)-1 – Unethical Business Practices of Investment Advisers, Investment Adviser Representatives, and Federal Covered Advisers

KEY FACTOR
----------
Full written disclosure of the placement fee conflict, documented suitability analysis including accredited investor verification, and a reasonable allocation size within a diversified portfolio satisfy the fiduciary obligations under the NASAA Model Rule.
