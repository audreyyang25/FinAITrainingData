Source Folder: 22_PIABA_Materials
Topic: expungement_standard_factually_impossible
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Broker James Whitfield settled a customer complaint for $45,000 related to unsuitable variable annuity recommendations. Following settlement, Whitfield filed an expungement request in a new FINRA arbitration. A single arbitrator granted expungement solely on the basis that Whitfield was a long-tenured broker with no other complaints, without making any finding that the claim was factually impossible, clearly erroneous, or that Whitfield was not involved.

QUESTION
--------
Does granting expungement based solely on the broker's tenure and clean record history, without making a required statutory finding, comply with FINRA Rule 2080?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of FINRA Rule 2080's mandatory expungement findings requirement.

FINRA Rule 2080 requires that before a court orders expungement, the arbitration panel must find that the claim is factually impossible or clearly erroneous, the registered person was not involved in the alleged investment-related sales practice violation, or the claim is false. An arbitrator's reliance on a broker's tenure and lack of prior complaints is not among the three permissible statutory grounds and constitutes a legally insufficient basis for expungement.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2080; FINRA Code of Arbitration Procedure Rule 12805

KEY FACTOR
----------
Expungement was granted without any of the three required factual findings mandated by FINRA Rule 2080.
