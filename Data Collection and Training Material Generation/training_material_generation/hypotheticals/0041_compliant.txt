Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A broker-dealer firm offers both brokerage and advisory services to a 70-year-old client with $2 million in investable assets. The firm provides a Form CRS that clearly delineates two distinct relationship tracks: (1) brokerage services with transaction-based commissions (0.5%–1.5%) and (2) advisory services with a tiered asset-based fee (1% on first $500,000, 0.75% on next $750,000, 0.50% above $1 million). The Form CRS explicitly states that in the advisory model, the client pays no transaction commissions, and lists the specific conflicts: the firm's revenue from fund sponsors when their funds are recommended, and the higher revenue generated by advisory accounts (vs. brokerage accounts) for the firm. The Form CRS includes a detailed fee calculator and is updated annually to reflect any changes in fee schedules or conflicts.

QUESTION
--------
Does this multi-service disclosure satisfy Reg BI's transparency requirements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this is compliant.

The firm has provided a comprehensive, transparent Form CRS that clearly distinguishes between two fundamentally different business models (brokerage vs. advisory), with specific fee tiers for each. The form discloses material conflicts transparently: the firm's economic incentive to recommend the advisory model (higher revenue) and the potential conflicts from fund sponsor payments. The inclusion of a fee calculator allows the client to understand the actual economic impact of each model for their specific asset level. The tiered advisory fee structure is clearly explained, allowing the client to see exactly what the firm earns at each asset level and whether consolidation of assets would reduce fees. The annual update requirement ensures that if fee schedules or conflicts change, the client receives current information. This approach demonstrates good-faith compliance with Reg BI's core purpose: enabling retail investors to make informed decisions by understanding the relationship and economic incentives.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Regulation Best Interest (17 CFR 240.15l-1), Form CRS Item 2 (fees and compensation) and Item 4 (conflicts of interest), and SEC guidance on dual agency disclosure and clear fee comparison.

KEY FACTOR
----------
Clear, itemized disclosure of distinct fee structures for different service models, specific conflict disclosure (fund sponsor payments and firm revenue differential), tiered fee schedule with concrete examples, and commitment to annual updates.
