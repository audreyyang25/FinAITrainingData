Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Sarah Kim, a New York-licensed insurance producer, recommends a 7-year surrender period fixed annuity with a 3.25% guaranteed rate from a highly-rated carrier to David Rothstein, a 67-year-old recently retired engineer with $1.2 million in retirement assets, $280,000 in liquid savings outside the annuity, and a pension providing $5,200/month. The annuity purchase is for $200,000, representing approximately 17% of his total retirement assets. Sarah documents that David wants a conservative, guaranteed-return component to complement his equity portfolio, that his liquidity needs are met by his pension, Social Security, and remaining liquid savings, and that she considered three alternative fixed annuity products with comparable surrender periods but lower guaranteed rates. Sarah's commission on this product is 4%, which is in line with industry norms for this product type. David's investment profile form indicates a moderate risk tolerance with a desire for some guaranteed income components.

QUESTION
--------
Does this annuity recommendation satisfy the best interest standard under NY Reg 187 despite the commission-based compensation?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

NY Reg 187 does not prohibit commission-based compensation; rather, it requires that the recommendation be in the best interest of the consumer considering the consumer's financial situation, needs, age, and objectives. Here, the producer documented a thorough analysis of the client's overall financial picture, considered reasonably available alternatives, and selected a product with a competitive guaranteed rate and a surrender period proportionate to the client's age and liquidity position. The 4% commission is within industry norms and the allocation represents a modest portion of total assets, ensuring the client retains substantial liquidity. The documentation requirements of Reg 187's duty of care are satisfied.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NY Reg 187 (11 NYCRR 224) – Best Interest Standard for Annuity Transactions

KEY FACTOR
----------
The producer documented a thorough needs analysis, considered alternatives, and recommended a proportionate allocation that preserved client liquidity, satisfying NY Reg 187's best interest standard despite commission compensation.
