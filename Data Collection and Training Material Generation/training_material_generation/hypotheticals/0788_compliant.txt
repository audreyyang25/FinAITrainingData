ID: 788
Source Folder: 15_FINRA_Regulatory_Notices
Topic: reg_bi_implementation
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A broker-dealer recruits a top-producing advisor from a competing firm with a recruitment package valued at $1.2 million over five years. The firm delivers an educational communication to all 215 of the advisor's transferring clients, disclosing the recruitment compensation, explaining that the advisor has a financial incentive to recommend they transfer their accounts, and detailing that certain proprietary products held in their prior accounts may need to be liquidated—potentially triggering tax consequences and surrender charges. The communication also explains differences in fee structures between the old and new firms. The firm implements a 90-day enhanced supervision period for the recruited advisor's recommendations and requires documented suitability reviews for every account transfer recommendation.

QUESTION
--------
Does the firm's handling of the recruitment compensation and account transfer recommendations comply with Reg BI and FINRA guidance on recruitment practice conflicts?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

FINRA Regulatory Notice 15-19 specifically addresses the conflict of interest posed by large recruitment compensation packages, noting that six-, seven-, and even eight-figure signing bonuses create serious conflicts between clients and the recruited advisors. The firm here mitigates this conflict through full written disclosure of the recruitment compensation, explanation of material impacts on proprietary product holdings, enhanced supervisory oversight during the transition period, and individualized suitability documentation for each transfer recommendation—all consistent with both Reg BI's disclosure and care obligations and the effective practices contemplated in RN 15-19.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) and (ii) – Reg BI Care and Disclosure Obligations; FINRA Regulatory Notice 15-19 (Recruitment Practices)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm proactively disclosed the recruitment compensation conflict, delivered the educational communication to all affected clients, and implemented enhanced supervision—directly addressing the conflict of interest concerns identified in FINRA's recruitment practices guidance.
