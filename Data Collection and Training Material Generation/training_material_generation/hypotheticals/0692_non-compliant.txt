Source Folder: 10_Academic_Articles
Topic: reg_bi_effectiveness
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A dually-registered broker-dealer representative recommends a 62-year-old Gen X client with $340,000 in retirement savings roll over her 401(k) into a variable annuity with a 7-year surrender charge and 2.1% total annual expenses. The representative's firm receives a 5.25% upfront commission on the annuity sale. The client expressed anxiety about Gen X retirement confidence gaps, stating she was 'not confident' about retiring on her own terms and wanted low-cost, flexible options. The representative's firm had a lower-cost advisory IRA rollover option available that would have charged 0.85% annually with no surrender period. The representative documented only that the annuity provided 'guaranteed income features' without comparing it to the advisory alternative or analyzing whether the guarantee rider justified the significantly higher cost given the client's stated preference for flexibility.

QUESTION
--------
Did the representative satisfy the Reg BI Care Obligation by recommending the variable annuity rollover without evaluating reasonably available lower-cost alternatives that matched the client's stated preferences?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates the Reg BI Care Obligation.

The Reg BI Care Obligation requires that a broker-dealer exercise reasonable diligence, care, and skill to understand the potential costs, risks, and reasonably available alternatives before making a recommendation. The SEC's 2018 proposed interpretation (Release No. IA-4889) and final Reg BI rule emphasize that cost is a critical factor and that higher-cost products require heightened justification, particularly when the client expressly requested flexibility and low cost. Failing to document a comparison to the lower-cost advisory IRA that matched the client's stated objectives, while recommending a product with a 7-year surrender charge and substantially higher fees, demonstrates inadequate care. The empirical literature on retirement planning confidence gaps among Gen X investors further underscores the vulnerability of this demographic to unsuitable recommendations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Release No. 34-86031 (requiring evaluation of reasonably available alternatives)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The representative failed to evaluate and document consideration of a reasonably available lower-cost alternative that aligned with the client's expressly stated preference for flexibility and low cost.
