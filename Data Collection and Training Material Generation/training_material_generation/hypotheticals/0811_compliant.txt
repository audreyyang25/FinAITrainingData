ID: 811
Source Folder: 15_FINRA_Regulatory_Notices
Topic: reg_bi_implementation
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A mid-size FINRA member firm's registered representative recommends that a 62-year-old pre-retiree, David Nguyen, with $520,000 in investable assets and a moderate-to-aggressive risk profile, allocate 20% of his portfolio to a diversified basket of exchange-traded funds (ETFs) that include leveraged and inverse ETF positions totaling no more than 5% of the overall portfolio. Prior to the recommendation, the representative completes a detailed Reg BI-compliant account profile documenting David's 8-year investment horizon, his stated desire for growth with some tactical hedging, his prior experience trading options and leveraged products, and his acknowledgment that he does not need to access this portion of his savings for at least 5 years. The firm's compliance department reviews the recommendation under its complex products supervisory procedures, documents that the leveraged/inverse ETF allocation is limited and appropriate given the client's experience and objectives, and files the relevant retail communication materials with FINRA's Advertising Regulation Department consistent with FINRA Rule 2210, as amended following Regulatory Notice 15-16. The representative provides David with a written disclosure of all costs, risks of leveraged ETFs including compounding risk, and the firm's conflict disclosures under Reg BI's Disclosure Obligation.

QUESTION
--------
Does the representative's recommendation of a small allocation to leveraged and inverse ETFs within a broader diversified portfolio satisfy Reg BI's Care and Disclosure Obligations and FINRA complex product suitability standards?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Although leveraged and inverse ETFs are complex products that trigger heightened scrutiny, the representative satisfied Reg BI's Care Obligation by documenting the client's experience with similar products, his long time horizon, growth-oriented objectives, and the limited 5% allocation to leveraged positions within a diversified strategy. The Disclosure Obligation was met through written cost, risk, and conflict disclosures provided before the transaction. The firm's compliance review under its complex products procedures and proper filing of retail communications with FINRA under Rule 2210 — streamlined pursuant to the amendments supported in the ICI comment letter on Regulatory Notice 15-16 — demonstrate reasonable supervisory diligence. The small, experience-appropriate allocation distinguishes this from cases where complex products dominate an unsuitable portfolio.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i)–(ii) – Reg BI Care and Disclosure Obligations; FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability); FINRA Rule 2210 (Communications with the Public, as amended per Regulatory Notice 15-16); FINRA Regulatory Notices on Complex Products

KEY FACTOR
----------
The limited 5% leveraged ETF allocation was supported by documented client experience, a thorough Reg BI care analysis, written risk disclosures, and supervisory review under complex product procedures — making the recommendation appropriate despite the inherent complexity of the products.
