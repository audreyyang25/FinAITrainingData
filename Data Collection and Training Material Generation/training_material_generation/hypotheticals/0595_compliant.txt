ID: 595
Source Folder: 06_DOL_Fiduciary
Topic: pte_2020_02
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Apex Insurance Solutions is a licensed insurance agency whose agents provide annuity recommendations to IRA owners. Following the DOL's April 23, 2024, Retirement Security Rule expanding the definition of investment advice fiduciary, Apex acknowledges fiduciary status for its annuity recommendations to IRA clients. Agent Laura Simmons recommends a fixed indexed annuity with a 7-year surrender period and 6% declining surrender charge to Patricia Owens, a 67-year-old retiree with $280,000 in IRA savings, moderate risk tolerance, and a need for guaranteed lifetime income. Simmons earns a 5.5% commission ($15,400). However, Apex has adopted comprehensive written policies and procedures under PTE 2020-02, including: (1) a documented best interest analysis comparing three annuity products from different carriers, showing the recommended product offers the highest guaranteed withdrawal benefit for Patricia's age and premium; (2) full written disclosure to Patricia of all compensation, fees, surrender charges, and material conflicts; (3) a mitigation framework ensuring that compensation does not vary based on the specific product recommended within the annuity category; and (4) an annual retrospective review conducted by an independent compliance consultant. Apex is currently within the one-year transition period for certain PTE conditions following the September 23, 2024 effective date and has documented its good-faith compliance efforts.

QUESTION
--------
Can Apex Insurance Solutions rely on PTE 2020-02 to receive conflicted compensation for the annuity recommendation despite the high commission and the agent's fiduciary status under the 2024 Retirement Security Rule?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

PTE 2020-02 was specifically designed to permit investment advice fiduciaries to receive otherwise-prohibited compensation, including commissions on annuity sales to IRA owners, provided all conditions are met. Apex's documented product comparison demonstrates that the recommendation reflects care and is in Patricia's best interest given her income needs. Full disclosure of compensation and conflicts satisfies the exemption's transparency requirements. The level compensation structure within product categories mitigates the incentive to recommend based on compensation rather than client need. The annual retrospective review by an independent consultant satisfies the oversight requirement. The firm is operating within the one-year transition period referenced in the April 2024 Retirement Security Rule amendments to the PTEs, and its documented good-faith compliance efforts are consistent with the DOL's temporary enforcement posture described in FAB 2021-02.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
DOL PTE 2020-02 – Sections I and II (Conditions for Relief); Retirement Security Rule (April 23, 2024); Amended PTE 84-24

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm satisfied all PTE 2020-02 conditions — including documented best interest analysis, full disclosure, conflict mitigation, and retrospective review — allowing it to receive commission-based compensation on the IRA annuity recommendation despite fiduciary status.
