ID: 851
Source Folder: 17_SEC_Staff_Bulletins
Topic: disclosure_requirements
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Meridian Brokerage Services onboarded 47 new retail customers in August 2020 — six weeks after Reg BI's June 30, 2020 effective date — without delivering Form CRS to any of them. The firm's compliance officer acknowledged in an internal email that the firm's client intake system had not been updated to generate Form CRS delivery, and that the oversight affected accounts with aggregate assets of approximately $3.2 million. Representatives at Meridian continued opening commission-based brokerage accounts and making buy/sell recommendations during this period without providing the required relationship summary. When regulators inquired, the firm argued that because the accounts were technically 'existing' prior to the delivery mandate, no new delivery was required.

QUESTION
--------
Did Meridian Brokerage Services violate its Form CRS delivery obligations under Reg BI by failing to provide Form CRS to new retail customers onboarded after the June 30, 2020 effective date?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of Form CRS delivery obligations under Reg BI.

Form CRS must be delivered to all new retail customers at or before the earliest of account opening or a recommendation. The firm's argument conflating 'new accounts' with 'existing accounts' is incorrect — the FAQ guidance from Cambridge/CIRA and SEC staff bulletins clarify that Form CRS is required for every new account opened after the effective date, regardless of prior firm relationship. Failure to deliver Form CRS prior to or at account opening constitutes a per se violation for each affected customer.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 17a-14 and Reg BI Form CRS Obligation – effective June 30, 2020

KEY FACTOR
----------
Form CRS was not delivered to new retail customers at or before account opening, as required from June 30, 2020 onward.
