ID: 926
Source Folder: 21_FINRA_OHO_Decisions
Topic: undisclosed_outside_business_activity
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Senior registered representative Jonathan Adler formed a private LLC, Adler Capital Ventures, through which he solicited five of his brokerage customers to invest a combined $550,000 in a private real estate syndication deal. Adler did not provide written notice to his member firm prior to engaging in this activity and did not disclose on his annual compliance certification that he had any outside business activities. The customers believed they were investing through Adler's firm due to his use of the firm's email domain in initial communications. Adler received a $42,000 referral fee from the syndicator, which he also failed to disclose.

QUESTION
--------
Did Adler's conduct in soliciting customers through his undisclosed private LLC satisfy FINRA Rules 3270 and 2010?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of FINRA Rule 3270 (outside business activities) and FINRA Rule 2010 (standards of commercial honor).

Rule 3270 requires registered persons to provide prior written notice to their member firm before engaging in any outside business activity for compensation, enabling the firm to evaluate and potentially restrict the activity. Adler violated this rule on multiple dimensions: he failed to provide prior written notice, actively solicited firm customers through the undisclosed entity, misrepresented the activity's affiliation by using firm email infrastructure, and concealed a $42,000 referral fee on compliance certifications. This conduct also rises to a Rule 2010 violation because using a firm's communications infrastructure to deceive customers about the source of investment solicitations reflects a fundamental failure of commercial honor.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 3270 (Outside Business Activities of Registered Persons); FINRA Rule 2010 (Standards of Commercial Honor and Principles of Trade); FINRA Rule 4511 (Books and Records)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Adler's failure to provide prior written notice of a compensated outside activity involving firm customers, compounded by active concealment and use of firm resources, constitutes both a Rule 3270 violation and dishonest conduct under Rule 2010.
