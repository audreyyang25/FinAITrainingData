Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Cornerstone Wealth Advisors, a registered investment adviser, manages $180 million in client assets. During its annual compliance review, the firm's Chief Compliance Officer identifies that six client accounts totaling $14 million have been invested in a private real estate fund managed by the CCO's spouse, generating approximately $210,000 in annual management fees to the spouse's entity. The CCO did not disclose this conflict to the firm's managing partners or to the affected clients, and the firm's Form ADV Part 2A does not reference the arrangement. The CCO contends the investment was suitable for each client and that the private fund has outperformed its benchmark.

QUESTION
--------
Does the CCO's failure to disclose the personal conflict of interest involving the spouse's private fund violate the investment adviser's fiduciary duty?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of the Investment Advisers Act fiduciary duty, specifically the duties of loyalty and full disclosure of material conflicts.

Under the Investment Advisers Act fiduciary duty, an adviser has an obligation of full and fair disclosure of all material facts, particularly conflicts of interest, so that clients can provide informed consent. The CCO's spousal relationship with the private fund manager is a material conflict that must be disclosed in Form ADV Part 2A and directly to affected clients. Performance of the underlying investment does not cure the failure to disclose; the fiduciary duty requires informed client consent before the conflicted transaction, not post-hoc justification based on returns.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940 §§ 206(1), 206(2) – Anti-fraud provisions; SEC Release IA-5248 (Commission Interpretation Regarding Standard of Conduct for Investment Advisers, 2019)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The CCO failed to disclose a material personal conflict of interest—the spousal relationship with the fund manager—to clients and in the firm's Form ADV, violating the adviser's fiduciary duty of loyalty and full disclosure.
