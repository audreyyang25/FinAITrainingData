Source Folder: 21_FINRA_OHO_Decisions
Topic: compliant_supervision_red_flag_escalation
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Branch manager Theodore Kwan received an automated exception report flagging registered representative Samuel Ortega's account activity for a turnover rate of 5.8 over a six-month period, just below the firm's WSP review threshold of 6. Rather than dismissing the report, Kwan proactively initiated a supervisory review, interviewed Ortega, reviewed correspondence with the affected client, and determined that the elevated activity was attributable to a documented tax-loss harvesting strategy explicitly requested by the client in writing. Kwan memorialized his review findings in the firm's supervisory log, noted the client's written authorization for the strategy, and scheduled a follow-up review for the next quarter. The client subsequently confirmed satisfaction with the account management approach in a routine customer service call recorded and retained by the firm.

QUESTION
--------
Did Branch Manager Kwan's supervisory response to the exception report satisfy FINRA's supervision requirements under Rule 3110?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Rule 3110 requires member firms to establish and maintain a supervisory system reasonably designed to achieve compliance with applicable securities laws and FINRA rules, including meaningful review of exception reports rather than perfunctory acknowledgment. Kwan's decision to investigate an account that fell just below the formal trigger threshold, corroborate the trading rationale with client-originated documentation, and memorialize findings in the supervisory log reflects the kind of substantive, good-faith supervision that OHO panels have distinguished from mere procedural compliance. Retention of the client confirmation call and written authorization also satisfies the firm's recordkeeping obligations under Rule 4511.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 3110 (Supervision); FINRA Rule 4511 (Books and Records)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Proactively reviewing activity below the formal WSP threshold, corroborating the trading rationale with client documentation, and creating a complete supervisory record demonstrates supervision that exceeds the minimum standard under Rule 3110.
