Topic: undisclosed_conflict
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A broker-dealer's investment advisory subsidiary recommends a $1.5M allocation in a variable annuity (5.4% annual insurance cost + 1.2% M&E charge) to a 62-year-old client with $3.2M investable assets and moderate-to-conservative risk tolerance. The broker-dealer receives a 5.25% sales commission (approximately $78,750) on the variable annuity purchase. The client's account statement and Form CRS clearly disclose (1) the broker-dealer's receipt of the commission, (2) the specific dollar amount, (3) the insurance costs embedded in the VA, (4) the conflict of interest created by commission-based compensation, and (5) that lower-cost alternatives exist (indexed annuities, direct mutual funds). The firm conducted a written suitability analysis documenting the client's understanding of the costs, acceptance of the guarantees and income riders, and need for tax-deferred growth.

QUESTION
--------
Does this variable annuity recommendation and disclosure satisfy Reg BI's Conflict Obligation and FINRA Rule 2330 (VA sales rules)?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this recommendation satisfies Reg BI's Conflict Obligation and FINRA Rule 2330.

Although the broker-dealer receives substantial commission compensation (a material conflict), the disclosure is comprehensive, specific, and front-loaded: the client received the exact dollar amount of the commission ($78,750), the annual insurance costs (5.4%), the M&E charges (1.2%), and explicit notice that lower-cost alternatives exist. Under Reg BI § 211(a)(1)(A), a conflict may be mitigated by 'disclosure in a manner that would be understood by a reasonable investor.' The firm also conducted a full suitability analysis (satisfying FINRA Rule 2330(b)(1)) documenting the client's need for tax deferral, acceptance of costs, and understanding of the income riders. The client's age (62), moderate-conservative risk tolerance, and $3.2M portfolio are consistent with annuitization strategies. While the 5.25% commission is high, it is disclosed and the recommendation passes the suitability test given the client's articulated needs for guaranteed income. The combination of clear disclosure + suitability + acknowledgment of alternatives satisfies both Reg BI and Rule 2330.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Regulation Best Interest § 211(a)(1)(A) (broker-dealers must disclose material conflicts of interest 'in a manner that would be understood by a reasonable investor'); FINRA Rule 2330(b)(1) (prior written receipt of suitability information required for VA sales); FINRA Rule 2111 (suitability); SEC IA Guidance (IA Release No. 2759) establishing that disclosure + suitability analysis + comparative analysis can satisfy Reg BI for commission-based recommendations.

KEY FACTOR
----------
Full, specific disclosure of the commission amount, embedded costs, and availability of lower-cost alternatives, combined with documented suitability analysis, allows a commission-based recommendation to satisfy Reg BI's Conflict Obligation.
