Topic: compliance_program_design
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A dual-registered broker-dealer and investment adviser firm missed the June 30, 2020 Reg BI compliance date without establishing any written procedures or training programs for its 45 associated persons on the best interest standard. The firm continued making recommendations to retail customers using its legacy suitability framework. The firm's CCO acknowledged in an internal email that 'we'll get to Reg BI when things settle down' and no Form CRS was filed or delivered to any retail investor. During an OCIE examination in September 2020, examiners found zero documentation of any Reg BI compliance efforts, no updated supervisory procedures, and no evidence that any associated person had received training on the four component obligations (Care, Disclosure, Conflict of Interest, and Compliance).

QUESTION
--------
Did the firm satisfy its obligations under the Reg BI Compliance Obligation by continuing to operate under its pre-existing suitability framework after the June 30, 2020 compliance date?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of the Reg BI Compliance Obligation and Form CRS requirements.

The Reg BI Compliance Obligation requires broker-dealers to establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with the best interest standard, including training for associated persons. As the FINRA Reg BI Checklist explicitly states, firms must have 'procedures and training in place to assess recommendations using a best interest standard' by the June 30, 2020 compliance date. The firm's complete failure to implement any compliance infrastructure, coupled with its failure to file and deliver Form CRS to retail investors, constitutes a fundamental violation of both requirements.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iv) – Reg BI Compliance Obligation; SEC Rule 17a-14 – Form CRS Delivery Obligation

KEY FACTOR
----------
Total absence of written Reg BI policies, procedures, training, and Form CRS filing by the mandatory June 30, 2020 compliance date.
