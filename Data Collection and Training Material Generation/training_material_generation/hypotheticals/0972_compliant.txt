Topic: singapore_faa_know_your_client_suitability
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A Singapore-licensed financial adviser representative meets with a 45-year-old client seeking to invest SGD 200,000 in unit trusts. Before making any recommendation, the representative collects a fully completed fact-find covering the client's financial objectives, investment horizon of ten years, risk tolerance assessed as 'balanced,' existing assets and liabilities, and investment knowledge. The representative recommends two diversified unit trusts aligned with the balanced risk profile, provides a written Financial Needs Analysis, and documents the basis for the recommendation in a contemporaneous file note. The client receives a copy of the product highlights sheet and a clear explanation of all fees before signing.

QUESTION
--------
Does the financial adviser representative's conduct satisfy the know-your-client and suitability obligations under the Singapore Financial Advisers Act?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Section 27 of the Financial Advisers Act requires a licensed adviser to have a reasonable basis for any recommendation by collecting and assessing information about the client's financial objectives, risk tolerance, financial situation, and investment experience, which the representative comprehensively discharged through the fact-find and Financial Needs Analysis. The written documentation of the recommendation basis and provision of product disclosure materials prior to execution demonstrates full compliance with MAS Notice FAA-N16 requirements for a reasonable basis and proper disclosure.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Singapore FAA §27; MAS Notice FAA-N16 (Recommendations on Investment Products); MAS Guidelines FAA-G11

KEY FACTOR
----------
Comprehensive fact-find, documented suitability rationale, and pre-execution product disclosure collectively satisfy FAA §27 reasonable basis requirements.
