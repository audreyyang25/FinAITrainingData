Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Apex Derivatives Advisors LLC, a CFTC-registered CTA, manages a discretionary commodity trading program trading S&P 500 futures and natural gas options on behalf of a commodity pool with 12 participants, all of whom are verified QEPs with portfolios exceeding $2,000,000 in securities and commodity interests. One participant, a 45-year-old hedge fund principal named Marcus Thibodeau, has a net worth of approximately $14,000,000 and a documented history of actively trading commodity futures in his personal accounts. Apex prepared a comprehensive disclosure document for the pool consistent with NFA's CPO Disclosure Document Guide (December 2025 revision), including a detailed Risk Disclosure Statement, a table of contents, complete fee and conflict-of-interest disclosures, and a five-year performance track record with the required past-performance disclaimer. When Thibodeau requested that Apex execute a large speculative natural gas position on his behalf exceeding the position limits set in the pool agreement, Apex declined, documented the request refusal, and instead allocated a proportionate position consistent with the pool's stated investment strategy and risk parameters disclosed in the offering documents.

QUESTION
--------
Did Apex Derivatives Advisors LLC's management of the commodity pool and handling of Thibodeau's out-of-scope trading request comply with applicable CTA and CPO regulatory obligations?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Apex satisfied its disclosure obligations by preparing a disclosure document consistent with NFA's CPO Disclosure Document Guide, including the required Risk Disclosure Statement, performance history with appropriate disclaimers, and full fee and conflict disclosure, ensuring all pool participants received material information necessary to make an informed investment decision. The verification of all participants as QEPs with portfolios exceeding applicable financial thresholds satisfied CFTC Rule 4.7 eligibility requirements. Apex's refusal to execute Thibodeau's speculative out-of-scope request and its documented adherence to the pool's stated investment strategy reflects sound fiduciary practice consistent with the CTA's obligation to act within the scope of its disclosed trading program and to treat all pool participants equitably. Maintaining written records of the declined request further demonstrates the supervisory and compliance infrastructure expected of registered CTAs under NFA rules.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
CFTC Regulations 4.31 and 4.34 – CTA Disclosure Requirements; NFA Compliance Rule 2-29; NFA CPO Disclosure Document Guide (December 2025); CFTC Rule 4.7 QEP Eligibility Standards

KEY FACTOR
----------
Apex's compliant conduct turned on its full adherence to NFA disclosure document requirements, verified QEP eligibility documentation, and disciplined refusal to deviate from the pool's disclosed investment strategy in response to a single participant's speculative request.
