Source Folder: 04_RegBI_Compliance_Guidance
Topic: reg_bi_compliance
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Murray Ridge Securities, a small FINRA member firm in East Texas with four registered representatives, has been operating since June 30, 2020 without establishing any written supervisory procedures addressing Regulation Best Interest. The firm conducts a general securities business primarily with retail customers, many of whom are retirees investing modest portfolios of $50,000 to $200,000. The firm's chief compliance officer acknowledges that the firm never updated its WSPs to incorporate Reg BI requirements, relying instead on the prior suitability standard under FINRA Rule 2111. No Reg BI training was provided to any registered representatives, and the firm had no process to evaluate whether recommendations were in customers' best interest.

QUESTION
--------
Did the firm satisfy its obligations under the Reg BI Compliance Obligation by continuing to rely on its pre-existing suitability-based supervisory framework after June 30, 2020?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of the Reg BI Compliance Obligation.

The Compliance Obligation under Reg BI requires broker-dealers to establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with Reg BI as a whole. As seen in the Murray Securities AWC (No. 2021069350301), FINRA has sanctioned firms that failed to establish and maintain supervisory systems and written policies and procedures to comply with Reg BI after its June 30, 2020 effective date. Simply retaining prior suitability-based WSPs without updating them to address the Care, Conflict of Interest, and Disclosure Obligations is insufficient.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iv) – Reg BI Compliance Obligation; FINRA Rules 3110(a) and 3110(b) – Supervisory System and Written Supervisory Procedures

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm's complete failure to establish, maintain, and enforce written supervisory procedures addressing Reg BI after the rule's June 30, 2020 compliance date constitutes a violation of the Compliance Obligation.
