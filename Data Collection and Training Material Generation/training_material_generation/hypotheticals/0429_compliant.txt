FACTS
-----
Registered investment adviser David Liu manages $2.8M for client Robert Torres, age 52, a software engineer with 25 years of tech industry experience and $1.2M in liquid net worth. Torres has held Bitcoin directly since 2017, manages his own crypto trading account (holds $120K in ETH and smaller positions), and has explicitly requested exposure to Bitcoin as part of his portfolio allocation. In 2024, Liu recommends a 4% allocation ($112,000) to the newly SEC-approved iShares Bitcoin ETF (IBIT), citing: (1) Torres's stated preference; (2) his demonstrated crypto expertise; (3) the ETF's regulatory approval and lower custody risk vs. direct holdings; (4) the allocation's modest size relative to Torres's existing crypto exposure and overall portfolio. Liu documents: Torres's prior crypto experience, his explicit request, the ETF's prospectus (including fees of 0.19% annually), market risk disclosures, and the rationale. The recommendation is approved by Liu's CCO. Torres accepts and holds the position for 18 months, earning 35% return.

QUESTION
--------
Did Liu comply with Reg BI and the Investment Adviser Act fiduciary duty in recommending IBIT to Torres?

ANSWER
------
Yes. (1) Reg BI Care Obligation (17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i)): Liu documented Torres's financial situation (sophisticated investor, $1.2M liquid, tech industry expertise), investment experience (direct Bitcoin holdings since 2017, active crypto trading), and explicit investment objectives (requested Bitcoin exposure). The 4% allocation aligns with these facts. (2) Reg BI Best Interest Standard: The recommendation serves Torres's interests by providing regulated ETF exposure aligned with his stated preferences and expertise level, with lower custody/counterparty risk than direct holdings. (3) Conflict Disclosure: Liu's advisory fee structure was pre-disclosed; no hidden conflicts. (4) Fiduciary Duty (Duty of Care & Loyalty): Liu exercised reasonable care by documenting Torres's experience and preference, and the recommendation furthers Torres's stated objective. This is compliant because the facts support the recommendation and documentation is thorough.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Reg BI Care Obligation, 17 CFR §240.15l-1(a)(2)(i); Investment Adviser Act §206 fiduciary duty (duty of care and loyalty)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Adviser documented sophisticated client's prior crypto expertise, explicit Bitcoin request, and modest allocation (4%) to SEC-approved ETF; no undisclosed conflicts.
