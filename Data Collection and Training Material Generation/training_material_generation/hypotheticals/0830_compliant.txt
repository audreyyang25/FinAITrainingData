Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Trentwood Advisory Group, a registered investment adviser with $2.1 billion AUM, manages accounts for approximately 1,400 retail clients. Following a 2023 GAO report finding that many RIAs lacked adequate written policies for identifying and mitigating conflicts in model portfolio programs, Trentwood proactively retains outside compliance counsel to audit its model portfolio recommendation process. The audit reveals that Trentwood's affiliated broker-dealer clears all client trades, generating $0.03/share in clearing revenue that flows back to Trentwood's parent company. Trentwood's ADV Part 2A already disclosed this affiliated-clearing arrangement in plain-English terms with dollar-range estimates, but the firm further responds to the audit by adding a transaction-level disclosure on client statements, implementing a best-execution committee that quarterly benchmarks clearing costs against three unaffiliated custodians, and documenting that the affiliated-clearing cost is within 0.4 basis points of best-execution benchmarks. GAO's Reg BI and investment adviser oversight reports have recognized proactive compliance remediation combined with ongoing best-execution monitoring as a model for satisfying fiduciary and conflict-of-interest standards.

QUESTION
--------
Does Trentwood Advisory Group's affiliated-clearing arrangement, as managed through its proactive disclosure and best-execution monitoring program, satisfy its investment adviser fiduciary duty?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

An investment adviser's fiduciary duty under Advisers Act §206 permits affiliated-service arrangements provided the conflict is fully and fairly disclosed and the adviser acts in the client's best interest after disclosure, as confirmed in the SEC's 2019 fiduciary interpretation release. Trentwood satisfied the disclosure prong through plain-English ADV disclosure with dollar-range estimates and augmented it with transaction-level statement disclosures. The firm satisfied the best-interest prong by establishing a quarterly best-execution committee that documented the affiliated-clearing cost is within 0.4 basis points of unaffiliated alternatives, meeting the standard that best execution does not require the lowest possible cost but the most favorable terms reasonably obtainable. GAO reports have specifically cited this type of proactive audit-and-remediate compliance model as consistent with robust fiduciary practice.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940 §206 – Fiduciary Duty; SEC Release IA-5248 (Commission Interpretation of Fiduciary Standard); FINRA Rule 5310 – Best Execution (as applied through affiliated broker); Dodd-Frank Act §913 (uniform fiduciary standard legislative history)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Trentwood's combination of plain-English conflict disclosure, transaction-level notice, and a documented quarterly best-execution benchmarking process that confirms costs are within 0.4 basis points of market alternatives satisfies both prongs of the investment adviser fiduciary duty despite the affiliated-clearing conflict.
