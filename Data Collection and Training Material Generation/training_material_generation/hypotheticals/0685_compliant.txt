Topic: reg_bi_effectiveness
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A broker-dealer representative recommends that a 58-year-old Boomer client with $750,000 in retirement savings, moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance, and a 10-year investment horizon allocate 15% of her portfolio ($112,500) to a Class A share growth equity mutual fund with a 4.25% front-end load, a 0.72% expense ratio, and breakpoint pricing that reduces the load to 3.50% based on rights of accumulation. The representative also presented a no-load institutional share class available only through an advisory account with a 1.0% annual wrap fee, and documented in writing that the Class A shares would result in lower total cost over the client's 10-year horizon due to the absence of the ongoing wrap fee. The client chose the brokerage account after reviewing a written comparison. The firm's Reg BI conflict disclosure identified the commission incentive, and the representative's compensation does not include differential payouts based on product type.

QUESTION
--------
Does the representative's recommendation of Class A shares over the advisory wrap account satisfy the Reg BI Care and Conflict of Interest Obligations?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The representative satisfied the Care Obligation by performing a documented cost comparison between the commission-based Class A shares and the fee-based advisory alternative, demonstrating that the Class A shares were lower total cost over the client's stated 10-year horizon. The recommendation matched the client's risk tolerance, time horizon, and portfolio size. The Conflict of Interest Obligation was met through specific written disclosure of the commission incentive, and the firm's uniform compensation grid eliminated differential financial motivation to recommend one product over another. The client made an informed choice after reviewing the comparison.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iii) – Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation

KEY FACTOR
----------
The representative documented a genuine cost comparison demonstrating that the recommended share class was lower cost over the client's time horizon and ensured full conflict disclosure, satisfying both the Care and Conflict of Interest Obligations.
