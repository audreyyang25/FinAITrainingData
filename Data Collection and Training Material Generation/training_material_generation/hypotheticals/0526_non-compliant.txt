ID: 526
Source Folder: 03_FINRA_AWC
Topic: finra_awc_supervision
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Meridian Financial Group, a FINRA member firm headquartered in Columbus, Ohio with 18 registered representatives, has been operating since 1978. Since the June 30, 2020 compliance date for Regulation Best Interest, the firm has not updated its written supervisory procedures to address Reg BI's four component obligations (Care, Disclosure, Conflict of Interest, and Compliance). The firm's compliance manual still references only FINRA Rule 2111 suitability standards. The firm's CCO acknowledged in an internal email in March 2021 that 'we still need to get around to updating the Reg BI stuff' but no action was taken. The firm continued to make recommendations to approximately 340 retail customers using only its legacy suitability framework through the present date, and no Form CRS was filed or delivered to retail customers.

QUESTION
--------
Did the firm violate its obligations by failing to establish written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with Regulation Best Interest since June 30, 2020?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is not compliant. This constitutes a violation of Exchange Act Rule 15l-1(a)(1), FINRA Rules 3110, and 2010.

Reg BI's Compliance Obligation under Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iv) requires broker-dealers to establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with Reg BI. The firm's complete failure to update its supervisory system and written procedures since the June 30, 2020 compliance date mirrors the Alexander Investment Services AWC (No. 2024080211301), where FINRA censured and fined the firm $25,000 for this same deficiency. The failure to file or deliver Form CRS compounds the violation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Exchange Act Rule 15l-1(a)(1) – Reg BI Compliance Obligation; FINRA Rules 3110 (Supervision) and 2010 (Standards of Commercial Honor)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm entirely failed to establish and maintain written policies, procedures, and a supervisory system to achieve compliance with Reg BI since the June 30, 2020 compliance date.
