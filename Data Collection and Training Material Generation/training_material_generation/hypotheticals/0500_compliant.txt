Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Beacon Securities, a FINRA-member broker-dealer, dually registers its financial advisors as investment adviser representatives of Beacon Advisory Services, an affiliated RIA. When Beacon's advisor, Carla Nguyen, recommends that her retail client, David Park (age 55, $620,000 in investable assets, growth-oriented risk profile), roll over his 401(k) into an IRA managed by Beacon Advisory Services, she provides David with Form CRS detailing the differences between brokerage and advisory services, delivers a written disclosure of the fee differential (the advisory account charges a 1.10% wrap fee versus his 401(k)'s 0.45% all-in expense), explains the services he will receive in exchange (ongoing monitoring, quarterly rebalancing, comprehensive financial planning), and documents in her file a cost-benefit analysis showing the net value proposition over a 10-year horizon. Carla also discloses that Beacon Securities receives a one-time $1,200 transition fee for the rollover.

QUESTION
--------
Did Carla Nguyen satisfy Regulation Best Interest requirements when recommending the 401(k) rollover to an advisory IRA despite the higher cost?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Regulation Best Interest, as articulated in Release No. 34-86031, requires that a broker-dealer exercise reasonable diligence, care, and skill when recommending an account-type change such as a 401(k) rollover. The rule does not mandate the lowest-cost option but requires that the recommendation be in the retail customer's best interest considering the customer's investment profile and the reasonably available alternatives. Carla conducted a documented cost-benefit analysis, disclosed all material conflicts including the transition fee and fee differential, delivered Form CRS, and explained the additional services justifying the higher cost. This demonstrates compliance with the Care, Disclosure, and Compliance Obligations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iv) – Reg BI Compliance Obligation; Release No. 34-86031

KEY FACTOR
----------
The advisor documented a thorough cost-benefit analysis, disclosed all material conflicts including the transition fee and fee differential, and demonstrated that the additional advisory services justified the higher cost relative to the existing 401(k).
