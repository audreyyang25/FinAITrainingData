ID: 885
Source Folder: 19_CFTC_Guidance
Topic: cta_cpo_suitability
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A registered CTA, TradeEdge Advisors LLC, solicits a 68-year-old retired schoolteacher with $210,000 in total investable assets and no prior derivatives experience to participate in a managed futures trading program focusing on crude oil and agricultural commodity futures contracts. The client's stated investment objective is capital preservation for retirement income. TradeEdge places the client into the program without conducting any formal suitability analysis, collecting no documentation of her financial situation, investment experience, or risk tolerance. The client sustains losses of $47,000 within her first four months, consistent with the CFTC's own research showing that retail futures traders characteristically experience gains or losses of a few hundred to several thousand dollars per trading event and frequently exit markets permanently after large initial losses.

QUESTION
--------
Did TradeEdge Advisors LLC fulfill its suitability obligations under NFA rules before placing the retired teacher into the managed futures program?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of NFA suitability requirements for commodity trading advisors.

NFA Compliance Rule 2-30 requires CTAs to make reasonable efforts to obtain information about a customer's financial situation, investment experience, and investment objectives before providing commodity interest trading advice. TradeEdge collected no such information and placed a capital-preservation-oriented retiree with limited assets into a high-risk speculative futures program. The CFTC's own research confirms that retail futures participants frequently suffer large losses on initial trades and exit markets permanently, underscoring the materiality of upfront suitability screening. Failure to document any suitability determination is itself an independent violation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NFA Compliance Rule 2-30 – Know Your Customer / Suitability Obligations for CTAs

KEY FACTOR
----------
The CTA failed entirely to conduct or document a suitability analysis before placing a risk-averse retiree into speculative commodity futures trading.
