Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Summit Ridge Securities, a FINRA member broker-dealer, recommends that David Kowalski, a 55-year-old engineer earning $180,000 annually with a $1.2 million portfolio and a moderate risk tolerance, allocate 15% of his portfolio to a diversified alternative investment fund with a five-year lock-up period. Before making the recommendation, the registered representative documents a thorough analysis of David's investment profile including his existing holdings, time horizon of 10+ years to retirement, liquidity needs (noting $200,000 in emergency savings outside this portfolio), and investment objectives emphasizing portfolio diversification. Summit Ridge's Reg BI policies require that all alternative investment recommendations undergo a supervisory pre-approval review, which was completed by the branch manager. The firm's Form CRS and a specific pre-trade disclosure document provided to David detail the fund's fee structure (1.5% management fee plus 20% performance allocation), the lock-up period, and the fact that Summit Ridge receives a 2% selling concession.

QUESTION
--------
Did Summit Ridge Securities satisfy Reg BI's Care, Disclosure, and Conflict of Interest Obligations in recommending the alternative investment fund to Mr. Kowalski?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Under Reg BI's Care Obligation, the representative exercised reasonable diligence and skill by analyzing the customer's full investment profile, confirming adequate liquidity outside the portfolio, and ensuring the allocation was a reasonable percentage given the client's overall assets and long time horizon. The Conflict of Interest Obligation was addressed through written supervisory policies requiring pre-approval of alternative investment recommendations, and the 2% selling concession was specifically disclosed rather than buried in general language. The Disclosure Obligation was met through the Form CRS and a detailed pre-trade disclosure document covering fees, lock-up terms, and compensation conflicts, consistent with Release 34-86031's emphasis that material facts about conflicts must be 'fully and fairly' disclosed.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Conflict of Interest Obligation; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iii) – Reg BI Disclosure Obligation; Regulation Best Interest Release No. 34-86031

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm conducted a thorough, documented suitability analysis proportionate to the product's complexity, implemented supervisory pre-approval procedures, and provided specific pre-trade disclosure of all material compensation conflicts and product risks.
