Topic: variable_annuity_rule
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Registered representative James Calloway recommends that his client, Sandra Okafor, age 61, exchange her existing deferred variable annuity — which has a remaining surrender period of 18 months and a current account value of $310,000 — for a new deferred variable annuity with a 5-year surrender schedule, lower annual mortality and expense charges (1.10% vs. 1.55%), a broader selection of subaccounts, and an optional guaranteed lifetime withdrawal benefit rider. Sandra has a moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance, a retirement time horizon of 5-7 years, $920,000 in total liquid assets, a pension income of $4,800/month, and no anticipated need for the annuity funds within the surrender period. James prepares a detailed written comparison documenting the surrender charge differential, the fee savings over the projected holding period, the additional rider benefits, and the tax implications of the exchange. The firm's designated principal, after reviewing the full comparison document, Sandra's updated investment profile, and the exchange rationale, approves the transaction three business days before it is processed, and documents her independent analysis in the firm's supervisory review log.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommended variable annuity exchange comply with the suitability, documentation, and principal review requirements of FINRA Rule 2330?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

FINRA Rule 2330 requires that an exchange recommendation be based on a reasonable belief that the transaction is suitable given the customer's age, liquidity needs, investment objectives, time horizon, existing benefits, and cost differentials. Here, the representative conducted and documented a thorough comparative analysis showing meaningful fee reduction, enhanced features, and suitability alignment with the client's profile — the client has ample liquidity, no near-term need for the funds, and a time horizon well beyond the new surrender period. Under Rule 2330(c), a registered principal reviewed and approved the transaction before processing, examining the full comparison, the client's updated profile, and the exchange rationale, and documented her independent assessment. This sequence satisfies the rule's substantive suitability, documentation, and prior-principal-approval requirements.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2330(a), (b), and (c) – Suitability, Disclosure, and Principal Review for Deferred Variable Annuity Exchanges

KEY FACTOR
----------
The representative's detailed comparative analysis demonstrating net client benefit, combined with the principal's documented pre-transaction substantive review and the client's ample liquidity and appropriate time horizon, collectively satisfied Rule 2330's heightened exchange requirements.
