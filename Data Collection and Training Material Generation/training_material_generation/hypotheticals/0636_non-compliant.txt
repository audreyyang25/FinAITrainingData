ID: 636
Source Folder: 08_State_Fiduciary_Rules
Topic: state_fiduciary_annuity
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A licensed insurance producer in New York recommends a 15-year deferred variable annuity with a 7% surrender charge to Margaret Chen, an 82-year-old retired widow living on Social Security and a small pension totaling $28,000 per year. The annuity requires a $150,000 premium, which represents virtually all of Margaret's liquid savings from a recently matured CD. The producer documents only Margaret's stated desire for 'growth' but does not document her liquidity needs, existing insurance coverage, or risk tolerance. The producer earns an 8% commission on the sale.

QUESTION
--------
Does this annuity recommendation comply with New York Regulation 187's best interest standard?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates NY Reg 187's best interest and suitability requirements for annuity transactions.

NY Reg 187 requires that a recommendation of an annuity be in the best interest of the consumer, considering the consumer's age, liquidity needs, financial situation, and existing insurance coverage. Recommending a long-duration, illiquid variable annuity with steep surrender charges to an 82-year-old client whose liquid savings would be entirely consumed fails the care obligation. Additionally, the producer's failure to document key suitability factors such as liquidity needs, risk tolerance, and existing coverage violates Reg 187's documentation requirements.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
NY Reg 187 – Best Interest Standard for Annuity Transactions (11 NYCRR 224)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The producer recommended an illiquid, long-surrender-period annuity to an elderly client without documenting essential suitability factors, directly contravening NY Reg 187's best interest and documentation requirements.
