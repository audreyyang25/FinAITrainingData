ID: 591
Source Folder: 06_DOL_Fiduciary
Topic: dol_rollover
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Margaret Chen, a 58-year-old school administrator, has $340,000 in her employer's 403(b) plan invested in a diversified mix of low-cost index funds with an all-in expense ratio of 0.08%. Upon attending a retirement seminar hosted by Horizon Financial Group, advisor Derek Walsh recommends she roll the entire balance into an IRA at his firm, where he places her in a suite of proprietary mutual funds carrying an average expense ratio of 1.35% and generating a 5% front-end sales load. Walsh earns a $12,750 commission on the rollover. He does not document any comparison of the 403(b) plan's investment options, fees, or services against the proposed IRA, nor does he provide Margaret with a written explanation of why the rollover is in her best interest.

QUESTION
--------
Does the advisor's rollover recommendation comply with DOL PTE 2020-02's best interest standard and documentation requirements?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of PTE 2020-02's care obligation and documentation requirements for rollover recommendations.

PTE 2020-02 requires that investment advice fiduciaries providing rollover recommendations must act in the retirement investor's best interest, which includes evaluating the alternatives available in the existing plan — such as fees, investment options, and services — against the proposed IRA. The advisor failed to perform or document any such comparative analysis. Additionally, the significant increase in costs (from 0.08% to 1.35% plus a front-end load) strongly suggests the recommendation was driven by the advisor's compensation rather than the client's interest.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
DOL PTE 2020-02 – Improving Investment Advice for Workers & Retirees; Section I(c) Care Obligation; ERISA §404(a)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The advisor failed to document a comparative analysis of the existing 403(b) plan versus the proposed IRA rollover, as required by PTE 2020-02's care and documentation conditions.
