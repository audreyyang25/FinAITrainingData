Topic: ia_fiduciary_duty
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
An adviser managing $500K for a 42-year-old client with a 25-year time horizon and moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance recommends replacing the client's existing broad-market index fund (0.08% expense ratio, $300K) with the adviser's proprietary actively managed equity fund (1.2% expense ratio). The proprietary fund has underperformed its benchmark by an average of 0.8% annually over the past 5 years. The adviser provides no written disclosure that she manages the proprietary fund or earns advisory revenue from it, and no performance comparison.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation violate the fiduciary duty of loyalty?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this violates the fiduciary duty of loyalty and duty of care under the Investment Adviser Act.

This is a clear-cut breach of the duty of loyalty because the adviser has failed to disclose a material conflict of interest (her ownership stake in and compensation from the proprietary fund) and has made a recommendation that prioritizes her revenue over the client's interests. The facts are particularly egregious: (1) the proprietary fund has chronically underperformed the index fund by 0.8% annually—a 1.28% relative underperformance when including the fee differential; (2) the client's 25-year horizon and moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance support index fund investing; (3) no disclosure of the conflict or comparative analysis provided; and (4) the recommendation directly increases adviser revenue at the client's expense. Under Capital Gains, even if the adviser disclosed the conflict, the underperformance would likely render the recommendation unsuitable. The failure to disclose compounded with an unfavorable recommendation is a textbook fiduciary breach.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Adviser Act §206(2) and §207 (disclosure requirements); SEC v. Capital Gains Research Bureau, 375 U.S. 180 (1963) (material conflicts must be disclosed); SEC Rule 206(4)-1 (adviser must maintain compliance procedures regarding disclosure of conflicts)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Undisclosed conflict of interest combined with a recommendation to shift to an underperforming proprietary fund is a clear violation of the duty of loyalty.
