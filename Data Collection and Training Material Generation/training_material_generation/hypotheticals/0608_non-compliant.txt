Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A registered investment adviser managing $4.2 billion in private fund assets files Form PF as a large hedge fund adviser. In response to the SEC and CFTC's proposed amendments to Form PF requiring enhanced reporting of investment strategies, exposures, and master-feeder arrangements, the adviser's compliance team reviews the new requirements but determines they are too burdensome and elects to continue filing under the legacy framework without adopting the amendments. The adviser also fails to report two qualifying events—a significant drawdown of 20% in a flagship fund and a large margin increase from a prime broker—within the required current reporting timeframes. The SEC Division of Examinations, which has identified Form PF reporting as a 2026 examination priority, discovers these omissions during a sweep exam of large hedge fund advisers.

QUESTION
--------
Is the investment adviser's decision to disregard amended Form PF reporting requirements and failure to file current reports for qualifying events compliant with its regulatory obligations?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of Form PF reporting requirements under SEC Rule 204(b)-1 and the adviser's duty of compliance under the Investment Advisers Act.

Registered investment advisers that meet the large hedge fund adviser threshold are required to file Form PF and comply with all applicable reporting requirements, including current reporting of qualifying events such as significant losses and extraordinary margin increases. The adviser's unilateral decision that amended requirements are too burdensome does not excuse noncompliance; regulatory obligations are not optional. The SEC Division of Examinations has specifically identified Form PF reporting accuracy and timeliness as a 2026 examination priority, indicating heightened scrutiny of exactly this type of deficiency. Additionally, the adviser's compliance program failed in its fundamental obligation under the Advisers Act compliance rule to ensure adherence to applicable securities laws.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 204(b)-1 – Form PF; Investment Advisers Act Section 204; SEC Release No. IA-6083 (Proposed Form PF Amendments)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The adviser unilaterally elected to ignore mandatory Form PF reporting amendments and failed to file required current reports for qualifying events, reflecting a compliance program that does not reasonably ensure adherence to regulatory obligations.
