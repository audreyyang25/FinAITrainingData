Topic: ia_fiduciary_duty
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
An RIA with $280M AUM manages accounts for 35 clients. The RIA's engagement letter specifies a tiered fee schedule: 1.00% on the first $500K, 0.75% on assets $500K–$2M, and 0.50% on assets above $2M. A new client with $2.5M signs the agreement. After one year, the client's assets grow to $3.2M through investment gains. The RIA's fees automatically adjust down to 0.50% on the additional $700K in growth, as specified in the written agreement. The fee change is prospective and the client receives quarterly statements showing the fee reduction.

QUESTION
--------
Does this tiered fee schedule arrangement comply with the RIA's fiduciary duty?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this is compliant.

The RIA's tiered fee structure is disclosed upfront in the written engagement letter, so there is no hidden conflict or surprise. The client consented to the specific fee schedule before the relationship began. The fee reduction for assets above $2M is transparent and benefits the client directly. There is no self-dealing because the fee schedule is applied uniformly to all clients in the same asset tier and is based on a pre-agreed formula, not adviser discretion. The RIA is not favoring itself over the client; rather, the fee decreases as the client's assets grow, which is a common industry practice that SEC guidance permits when disclosed in advance.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Adviser Act § 206; SEC Rule 206(4)-1; SEC guidance on fee arrangements

KEY FACTOR
----------
Clear written disclosure of fee schedule in advance of the relationship, uniform application across all clients, and alignment of fee reduction with client asset growth.
