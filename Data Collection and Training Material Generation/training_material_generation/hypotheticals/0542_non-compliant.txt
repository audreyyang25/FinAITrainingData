Source Folder: 03_FINRA_AWC
Topic: finra_awc_supervision
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Pinnacle Financial Services, a FINRA member firm since 1998 headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina with 22 registered representatives, failed to establish or maintain any written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with Regulation Best Interest after its June 30, 2020 compliance date. The firm continued to evaluate representative recommendations using its pre-existing FINRA Rule 2111 suitability framework without updating its supervisory system to incorporate Reg BI's Care, Disclosure, Conflict of Interest, and Compliance obligations. The firm did not file a Form CRS with the SEC or deliver relationship summaries to retail customers. A FINRA examination in March 2024 revealed the deficiency had persisted for nearly four years.

QUESTION
--------
Did Pinnacle Financial Services violate its regulatory obligations by failing to implement Reg BI policies and procedures and a reasonably designed supervisory system after the June 30, 2020 compliance date?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is not compliant. The firm violated Exchange Act Rule 15l-1(a)(1) (Reg BI Compliance Obligation) and FINRA Rules 3110 and 2010.

Under Reg BI's Compliance Obligation, broker-dealers must establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with the best interest standard. The firm's complete failure to update its supervisory system mirrors the Alexander Investment Services AWC, where the firm was censured and fined $25,000 for identical deficiencies persisting from June 30, 2020 onward. Continuing to apply only the pre-Reg BI suitability standard is insufficient to satisfy the heightened best interest obligations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Exchange Act Rule 15l-1(a)(1) – Regulation Best Interest (Compliance Obligation); FINRA Rule 3110 – Supervision; FINRA Rule 2010 – Standards of Commercial Honor

KEY FACTOR
----------
Complete failure to establish written policies, procedures, and a supervisory system for Reg BI compliance for nearly four years after the June 30, 2020 compliance date.
