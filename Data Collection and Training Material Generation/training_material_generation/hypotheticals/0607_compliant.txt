Topic: industry_best_practices
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A dual-registrant broker-dealer/investment adviser makes a recommendation to a sophisticated family office with $850 million in assets, governed by a board of directors with three CFA charterholders, to invest $50 million in a private credit fund managed by an affiliate. Relying on the SEC staff's no-action guidance in its December 23, 2020 letter to SIFMA regarding institutional family offices, the broker-dealer treats the family office as a non-retail customer and does not apply Reg BI's Care, Disclosure, or Conflict of Interest obligations to the recommendation. The firm documents its analysis confirming that the family office meets the conditions outlined in the staff letter: the family office qualifies under SEC Rule 202(a)(11)(G)-1 or its equivalent exclusion, has sufficient sophistication and bargaining power, and has opted into institutional treatment. The firm maintains records of this determination and the family office's written acknowledgment.

QUESTION
--------
Is the broker-dealer compliant in not applying Reg BI protections to its recommendation to this institutional family office?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The December 23, 2020 SEC staff letter to SIFMA confirmed that staff would not recommend enforcement action against broker-dealers that do not treat qualifying institutional family offices as 'retail customers' for purposes of Reg BI, provided specified conditions are met. The family office here meets the relevant criteria: it has sufficient assets, sophistication, and bargaining power, and has affirmatively opted into institutional treatment. The firm's thorough documentation of its analysis and the family office's written acknowledgment demonstrates a well-reasoned compliance determination consistent with the staff's guidance.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Staff No-Action Letter to SIFMA re: Institutional Family Offices and Reg BI (December 23, 2020); SEC Rule 15l-1 – Regulation Best Interest

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm properly documented its reliance on the SEC staff's no-action position regarding institutional family offices, confirming the family office met all specified conditions for exclusion from Reg BI's retail customer protections.
