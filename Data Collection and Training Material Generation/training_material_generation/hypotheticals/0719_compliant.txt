Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Clearwater Advisory Group, an SEC-registered investment adviser with $520 million under management, allocates certain client accounts into a private real estate fund managed by a separate entity in which Clearwater's founder holds a 12% limited partnership interest. Clearwater's Form ADV Part 2A specifically identifies the private fund by name, discloses the founder's 12% ownership stake, states the exact management and incentive fees charged by the fund (1.5% management fee and 20% carried interest above an 8% hurdle), explains that the founder benefits financially when client assets are allocated to the fund, and quantifies the estimated annual economic benefit to the founder at approximately $185,000. Each affected client receives a separate written conflict disclosure and signs an informed consent acknowledgment before any allocation is made. Clearwater's compliance department maintains a log of all consents and reviews each allocation for investment suitability relative to the client's stated objectives and risk tolerance.

QUESTION
--------
Does Clearwater's allocation of client assets to a private fund in which its founder has a financial interest comply with the Investment Advisers Act's fiduciary duty requirements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The Investment Advisers Act's fiduciary duty, as articulated in Capital Gains Research Bureau and interpreted in Robare Group, requires full and fair disclosure of material conflicts of interest sufficient to obtain informed client consent. Unlike the deficient generic disclosures at issue in Robare Group, Clearwater provides specific, quantified disclosure identifying the exact fund, the founder's precise ownership percentage, the fee structure, the estimated dollar benefit to the founder, and the nature of the conflict. Each client provides individualized written informed consent, and the firm independently evaluates suitability. This level of specificity and process represents the type of disclosure that satisfies the adviser's fiduciary obligations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940 §§ 206(1), 206(2); SEC v. Capital Gains Research Bureau, Inc., 375 U.S. 180 (1963); Robare Group, Ltd. v. SEC, 922 F.3d 468 (D.C. Cir. 2019)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Specific, quantified disclosure of the exact conflict — including the fund name, ownership percentage, fee structure, and estimated economic benefit — combined with individualized written informed consent satisfies the adviser's fiduciary disclosure obligation under the Investment Advisers Act.
