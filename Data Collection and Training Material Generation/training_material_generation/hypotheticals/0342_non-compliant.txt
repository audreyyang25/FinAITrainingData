Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Branch manager Jennifer L. (supervising 12 representatives, branch assets $850 million) received customer complaint in March 2023 from 71-year-old retiree Robert K. alleging unsuitable variable annuity (VA) recommendation by rep Daniel F. The VA was sold in January 2023 for $125,000 with 7% surrender charge (declining 0.75% annually), 1.40% annual insurance cost, and nested 0.85% expense ratio on equity subaccounts. Robert's stated objectives were income and stability; he had $380,000 liquid assets, $65,000 annual pension, and expressed age-appropriate moderate risk tolerance. Jennifer reviewed the file in April 2023, noting Daniel's fact-finding form showed Robert checked 'long-term growth' but was marked 'income-oriented.' Jennifer did not investigate further, did not request suitability documentation from Daniel, and closed the complaint internally without reporting to FINRA. No record of supervisory approval of the VA sale predating the complaint.

QUESTION
--------
Did Jennifer L. comply with FINRA Rule 3110 (supervision) and Rule 4530 (complaint handling) in responding to the customer complaint?

ANSWER
------
No. Rule 3110(b)(5) requires supervisors to establish and enforce procedures for receipt, filing, and review of complaints and to take appropriate action. Rule 4530 mandates reporting of customer complaints to FINRA within prescribed timeframes when they allege suitability violations. Jennifer violated both rules by: (1) failing to investigate the complaint thoroughly—she did not request suitability files, obtain Daniel's contemporaneous recommendation rationale, or analyze whether the 7% surrender charge and 1.40% annual cost were disclosed and justified for a 71-year-old income-focused client; (2) internally closing the complaint without escalation or FINRA report; and (3) not reviewing pre-sale supervisory approval. FINRA pattern enforcement (2020-2023) shows $200K–$1.2M fines for complaint handling failures combined with inadequate VA supervision. The 'income vs. growth' mismatch and absence of documented suitability were red flags requiring escalation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 3110(b)(5) (supervisory complaint procedures); FINRA Rule 4530 (complaint reporting requirement); FINRA Rule 2330 (variable annuity suitability)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Supervisor failed to investigate customer complaint of unsuitable VA sale and did not report to FINRA, violating complaint-handling and supervisory escalation obligations.
