Topic: reg_bi_effectiveness
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A broker-dealer representative recommends that a 45-year-old millennial client with $410,000 in retirement assets and a moderate-aggressive risk tolerance roll over her former employer's 401(k) ($195,000) into an IRA invested in a diversified portfolio of index ETFs with a total expense ratio of 0.08%. The former 401(k) plan offered only 12 target-date funds with expense ratios ranging from 0.35% to 0.72% and limited asset class options. The representative earns a one-time $750 account setup fee for the IRA. The representative documents a rollover analysis comparing the 401(k) plan's limited fund menu, higher costs, and lack of customization against the IRA's broader investment options, lower costs, and ability to tailor the allocation. The client's CRS and Reg BI disclosures are provided at account opening, including the account setup fee.

QUESTION
--------
Does the rollover recommendation from a higher-cost 401(k) with limited options to a lower-cost IRA satisfy Reg BI requirements, even though the representative receives compensation for the rollover?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The SEC has emphasized that rollover recommendations are subject to Reg BI's Care Obligation and require documented analysis of whether the rollover is in the client's best interest. Here, the representative performed and documented a thorough comparison showing the IRA provides meaningfully lower costs (0.08% vs. 0.35%-0.72%), greater investment flexibility, and customization benefits. The $750 one-time fee is modest, disclosed, and does not create an outsized incentive relative to the client's account size. Empirical research on retirement planning, including findings consistent with the 2025 State of Retirement Planning data on millennial confidence, supports that enabling lower-cost, more flexible investment options can materially improve long-term retirement outcomes.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Staff Bulletin on Account Type Recommendations (Rollovers)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The documented cost comparison demonstrating material savings and expanded investment options in the IRA, combined with full fee disclosure, is the pivotal compliance factor supporting the rollover recommendation.
