ID: 924
Source Folder: 21_FINRA_OHO_Decisions
Topic: leveraged_etf_suitability_retiree
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Registered representative Marcus Chen recommended a 3x leveraged S&P 500 ETF to a 74-year-old retired schoolteacher with a fixed income of $2,200 per month and a stated investment objective of capital preservation. The customer had no prior experience with leveraged products and a moderate-conservative risk tolerance documented in her new account form. Chen placed the recommendation without reviewing the customer's investment profile and allocated 40% of her $180,000 portfolio to the leveraged ETF. The customer lost $38,000 within six months due to volatility decay inherent in the product.

QUESTION
--------
Did Marcus Chen's recommendation of a 3x leveraged ETF to this retired customer satisfy FINRA Rule 2111 suitability requirements?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of FINRA Rule 2111 (suitability).

Rule 2111 requires that a recommendation be suitable based on the customer's investment profile, including age, financial situation, risk tolerance, and investment objectives. A 3x leveraged ETF carries daily reset risk and amplified volatility that is fundamentally incompatible with a capital preservation objective for a retiree on fixed income. Allocating 40% of the portfolio to such a product further compounds the suitability failure by creating dangerous concentration risk.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability); FINRA Regulatory Notice 09-31 (Leveraged and Inverse ETFs)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The product's risk profile was irreconcilable with the customer's documented capital preservation objective and fixed-income financial situation.
