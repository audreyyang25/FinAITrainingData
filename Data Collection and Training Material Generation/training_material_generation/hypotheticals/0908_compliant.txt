Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Crestwood Fiduciary Advisors, LLC, a registered investment adviser, managed a $4.2 million discretionary portfolio for the Della Fontaine Family Trust, a sophisticated institutional-equivalent client. Crestwood's Form ADV Part 2A disclosed that it received revenue-sharing payments averaging 0.15% annually from its affiliated custodian, Crestwood Custody Services, and that this arrangement created a conflict of interest that was reviewed annually by an independent compliance consultant. The adviser's investment policy statement for the trust required maintaining at least 40% of assets in investment-grade fixed income. In Q3 2023, Crestwood rebalanced the portfolio into a suite of iShares Core bond ETFs and Vanguard Short-Term Corporate Bond ETF, generating $12,400 in revenue-sharing credits to the firm, all of which were fully disclosed in the quarterly client report sent to the trust's independent trustee within 15 days of quarter-end.

QUESTION
--------
Does Crestwood Fiduciary Advisors' receipt of revenue-sharing payments from its affiliated custodian in connection with portfolio rebalancing, where the conflict was pre-disclosed in the Form ADV and the specific credit amount was disclosed in the quarterly report, comply with the Investment Advisers Act?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Under §206(2) of the Advisers Act, an investment adviser does not commit fraud if it makes full and fair disclosure of a material conflict of interest and obtains informed consent from the client. Crestwood's dual-layer disclosure — prospective conflict disclosure in Form ADV Part 2A and specific transaction-level disclosure in the quarterly report — satisfies the informed consent standard articulated in Advisers Act administrative proceedings. The rebalancing itself was consistent with the trust's written investment policy statement and used low-cost, broadly diversified ETFs, demonstrating that the adviser acted in the client's best interest notwithstanding the affiliated custodian relationship. The involvement of an independent trustee reviewing quarterly disclosures further supports the adequacy of the consent mechanism.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Advisers Act of 1940 §206(2); Advisers Act Release IA-6896 – conflict of interest disclosure and informed consent framework

KEY FACTOR
----------
Dual-layer disclosure of the affiliated custodian conflict — both in the Form ADV and in a specific post-transaction quarterly report to an independent trustee — satisfies the Advisers Act informed consent standard and defeats a §206(2) fraud claim.
