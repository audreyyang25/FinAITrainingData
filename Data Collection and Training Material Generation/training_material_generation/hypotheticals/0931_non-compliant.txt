ID: 931
Source Folder: 21_FINRA_OHO_Decisions
Topic: undisclosed_outside_business_activity
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Registered representative Donovan Hatch began serving as a compensated advisory board member for a private real estate crowdfunding startup, FinancialNest LLC, receiving $2,500 per month and equity warrants representing a potential 1.5% ownership stake. Hatch did not disclose this arrangement to his member firm, believing it qualified as a passive investment rather than an outside business activity because he was not a named officer or director. Over the following year, Hatch referred eleven of his brokerage clients to FinancialNest, three of whom invested a combined $215,000 in an unregistered offering. Hatch never disclosed the conflict of interest to the referring clients and did not obtain prior written approval from his firm.

QUESTION
--------
Did Hatch's failure to disclose his compensated advisory board role and client referral activity to his firm violate FINRA rules governing outside business activities?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a violation of FINRA Rule 3270 (Outside Business Activities) and Rule 2010 (Standards of Commercial Honor).

Rule 3270 requires registered persons to provide prior written notice to their member firm before engaging in any outside business activity for compensation, regardless of title or perceived passivity; receipt of monthly payments and equity warrants constitutes compensation triggering mandatory disclosure. Hatch's referral of clients to an entity in which he held a financial interest, without disclosing that conflict, further violates Rule 2010's just and equitable principles standard and implicates Rule 2111 by compromising the objectivity of his recommendations. The undisclosed equity stake and referral pattern created a material, undisclosed conflict of interest that the firm's supervisory system never had an opportunity to detect or remediate.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 3270 (Outside Business Activities of Registered Persons); FINRA Rule 2010 (Standards of Commercial Honor); FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Receipt of monthly cash compensation and equity warrants from FinancialNest LLC unambiguously triggered Rule 3270's prior written notice requirement, which Hatch deliberately circumvented by mischaracterizing the role as passive.
