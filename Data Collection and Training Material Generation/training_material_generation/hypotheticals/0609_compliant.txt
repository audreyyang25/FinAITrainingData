Source Folder: 07_Practitioner_Commentary
Topic: industry_best_practices
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A broker-dealer with 500 registered representatives implements a comprehensive Reg BI compliance program that includes: (1) annual in-person training modules covering the Care, Disclosure, Conflict of Interest, and Compliance Obligations; (2) quarterly assessments where representatives must demonstrate, using recent client case studies, how they evaluated reasonably available alternatives before making recommendations; (3) an automated surveillance system that flags recommendations of high-cost products to clients over age 65 or with conservative risk profiles for supervisory review; and (4) an enhanced conflict-of-interest inventory updated semi-annually that maps revenue-sharing arrangements, proprietary product incentives, and compensation differentials. A 68-year-old retiree with a moderate risk tolerance is recommended a B-share mutual fund. The automated system flags the recommendation, a supervisor reviews it, and after confirming that the B-share's lower upfront cost and the client's stated 8-year holding period make it cost-effective compared to A-share and C-share alternatives, the supervisor approves the transaction with documented rationale.

QUESTION
--------
Does the broker-dealer's compliance program and the specific supervisory review of the B-share recommendation satisfy Reg BI's Compliance and Care Obligations?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The firm's multi-layered compliance program—featuring training, assessments, automated surveillance, and a detailed conflict-of-interest inventory—is reasonably designed to achieve compliance with all four Reg BI component obligations. The specific supervisory review of the B-share recommendation demonstrates the Care Obligation in action: the supervisor evaluated reasonably available alternatives (A-shares, C-shares) and documented why the B-share was in the client's best interest given her 8-year holding period and preference for lower upfront costs. This reflects the kind of robust compliance infrastructure that industry guidance from SIFMA and examination priorities from the SEC Division of Examinations envision.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i) – Reg BI Care Obligation; SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iv) – Reg BI Compliance Obligation; FINRA Rule 3110 – Supervision

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm's layered compliance infrastructure, including automated surveillance and documented supervisory review of flagged recommendations with comparison of reasonably available alternatives, demonstrates a compliance program reasonably designed to achieve Reg BI compliance.
