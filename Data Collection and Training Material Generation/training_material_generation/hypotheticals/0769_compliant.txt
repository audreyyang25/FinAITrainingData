ID: 769
Source Folder: 14_NASAA_State_Enforcement
Topic: state_registration_violations
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Sierra Peak Advisors, LLC, a newly formed investment advisory firm based in Sacramento, registered with the California DFPI by completing all six required steps: enrolling in the IARD system, funding the daily account, completing Form ADV Parts 1 and 2 (including the brochure supplement), submitting the form through IARD, registering its two investment adviser representatives, and submitting the complete application package. The firm disclosed on Form ADV Item 9 that it would have custody of client assets through its authority to deduct advisory fees directly from client accounts held at Charles Schwab, a qualified custodian. Sierra Peak also arranged for an independent public accountant to conduct an annual surprise examination. A competing firm questioned whether Sierra Peak could legally begin accepting clients within three weeks of filing, suggesting the timeline was suspiciously fast.

QUESTION
--------
Did Sierra Peak Advisors comply with California state registration requirements and NASAA custody rules despite the rapid timeline from filing to accepting clients?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Sierra Peak followed every step prescribed by the California DFPI's registration process, including IARD enrollment, Form ADV completion and submission, and registration of investment adviser representatives. There is no minimum waiting period mandated beyond the DFPI's processing and approval of the application; once approved, the firm may lawfully begin advisory activities. The firm also properly disclosed custody on Form ADV, uses a qualified custodian (Charles Schwab) maintaining separate client accounts, and arranged for a surprise examination—satisfying all prongs of NASAA Model Rule 102(e)(1)-1(a). The speed of registration does not, by itself, indicate any deficiency.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
California Corporations Code Section 25230; California DFPI Registration Steps (IARD, Form ADV); NASAA Model Rule 102(e)(1)-1(a)(1)-(3) – Custody Safekeeping Requirements

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm completed all required California DFPI registration steps and fully complied with NASAA custody safekeeping requirements, and the rapid timeline alone does not constitute a violation.
