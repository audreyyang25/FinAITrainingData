Source Folder: unknown
Topic: reg_bi_disclosure
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A broker-dealer's Form CRS states that the firm recommends individual securities and provides a 'standard fee schedule' ranging from 0% to 1.5% commission depending on product type. A 65-year-old client nearing retirement with $1.2 million in assets inquires about alternative investments. The advisor verbally tells the client that the firm will earn a 3% transaction fee on any private equity fund placement but does not disclose this in writing or update the Form CRS. The advisor also does not disclose that the firm receives an annual 0.50% referral fee from the private equity sponsor or that the advisor personally receives a $15,000 completion bonus if the client invests at least $250,000 in the fund.

QUESTION
--------
Does the advisor's oral disclosure and reliance on the outdated Form CRS satisfy Reg BI's disclosure obligations?

ANSWER
------
No, this violates Regulation Best Interest.

Reg BI requires clear, concise written disclosure of all material facts about the relationship and compensation before providing advice. Here, multiple material conflicts exist: the 3% transaction fee (substantially higher than the disclosed 0%–1.5% range), the 0.50% annual referral fee from the sponsor, and the $15,000 completion bonus that creates a personal incentive for the advisor. Oral disclosure does not satisfy the requirement for written, documented disclosure. The Form CRS that lists only the standard 0%–1.5% commission range is affirmatively misleading when the advisor is about to recommend a product carrying a 3% transaction fee plus undisclosed ongoing and completion-based compensation. The advisor's personal bonus of $15,000 is a material conflict that must be disclosed in writing before, not after, the recommendation. The SEC has emphasized in no-action letters and FAQs that Reg BI requires affirmative written disclosure, not reliance on oral explanations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Regulation Best Interest (17 CFR 240.15l-1(b)(2) – required disclosures) and SEC Reg BI FAQ guidance on written disclosure of material conflicts and compensation structures; compliance with updating Form CRS when material changes occur.

KEY FACTOR
----------
Failure to provide written, pre-recommendation disclosure of the 3% transaction fee, 0.50% annual referral fee, and $15,000 completion bonus, coupled with reliance on an outdated Form CRS that does not reflect the actual fee structure of the recommended investment.
