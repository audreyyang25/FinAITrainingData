Source Folder: 10_Academic_Articles
Topic: reg_bi_effectiveness
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A dual-registered broker-dealer representative recommends a 62-year-old Gen X client—who reports low confidence in retiring on her own terms and has $185,000 in retirement savings—roll over her 401(k) into a variable annuity with a 7-year surrender period and total annual costs of 3.15%. The representative's firm pays a 5.25% upfront commission on variable annuity sales versus 1.0% on advisory IRA accounts. The representative documents the recommendation as 'suitable' based on the client's stated desire for 'guaranteed income,' but performs no comparative cost analysis of lower-cost alternatives such as a fixed indexed annuity or a managed IRA portfolio. The client's risk tolerance questionnaire indicates moderate-conservative, and her primary goal is capital preservation through retirement.

QUESTION
--------
Did the representative satisfy the Reg BI Care Obligation by recommending the high-cost variable annuity without performing a comparative analysis of reasonably available alternatives?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct violates the Reg BI Care Obligation.

The Care Obligation under Reg BI requires a broker-dealer to exercise reasonable diligence, care, and skill to understand reasonably available alternatives and to have a reasonable basis for the recommendation. The representative failed to perform any comparative cost analysis between the variable annuity and lower-cost alternatives such as a fixed indexed annuity or managed IRA. Given the client's moderate-conservative risk tolerance, capital preservation goal, and proximity to retirement, the 7-year surrender period and 3.15% annual cost structure require heightened scrutiny. Academic research on Reg BI effectiveness highlights that without meaningful comparative analysis, the Care Obligation is reduced to a pre-existing suitability standard and fails to protect investors from conflicted recommendations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(ii)(B) – Reg BI Care Obligation: reasonable diligence, care, and skill in evaluating reasonably available alternatives

KEY FACTOR
----------
The representative's failure to evaluate and document any reasonably available lower-cost alternatives before recommending the highest-commission product is the pivotal violation of the Care Obligation.
