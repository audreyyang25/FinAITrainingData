Source Folder: 23_International_Comparative
Topic: singapore_faa_needs_analysis_compliant
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A Singapore-licensed financial adviser meets with a 35-year-old client seeking life insurance coverage. Before making any recommendation, the adviser conducts a comprehensive fact-find covering the client's income, existing coverage, dependants, liabilities, and long-term financial goals. The adviser documents that the client's existing group term policy lapses upon employment change and recommends a portable individual whole-life policy with a specified sum assured, providing a written explanation of why this product best addresses the identified needs gap. The client acknowledgement form and needs analysis are retained on file.

QUESTION
--------
Does the adviser's process of conducting and documenting a full needs analysis before recommending a life insurance product comply with the Singapore Financial Advisers Act requirements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Section 27 of the FAA requires licensed financial advisers to have a reasonable basis for any recommendation, which under MAS Notice FAA-N16 necessitates a documented needs analysis covering financial objectives, existing coverage, and risk profile before recommending a life policy. The adviser's process satisfies all prescribed elements: comprehensive fact-finding, identification of coverage gaps, product-specific rationale, and written documentation retained on file.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Singapore Financial Advisers Act §27; MAS Notice FAA-N16 (Recommendations on Investment Products); MAS Guidelines FAA-G11

KEY FACTOR
----------
Comprehensive needs analysis fully documented with written rationale linking client circumstances to the specific product recommended.
