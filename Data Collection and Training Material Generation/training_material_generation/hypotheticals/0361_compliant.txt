FACTS
-----
Pinnacle Securities, a 120-person FINRA member firm with 4 branches, implemented a tiered supervisory approach for variable annuity (VA) sales. FINRA Rule 3110 and Rule 2330 require enhanced supervision of VA recommendations. Pinnacle's WSPs required that all VA recommendations to customers age 55+ receive documented pre-approval by a VA-certified supervisor before execution. The procedures also mandated annual suitability certification signed by each registered rep and annual compliance sampling of 20% of VA files per branch. In 2023, registered rep Nathaniel P., age 34, recommended a $280,000 VA (surrender charge 8%, annual M&E 1.35%) to Mrs. Huang (age 63, $520,000 portfolio, stated risk tolerance 'moderate'). Nathaniel P. documented the suitability analysis, noting that Mrs. Huang had a 20-year time horizon, existing non-VA assets of $240,000 (liquid), and understood the surrender charge terms. He submitted the recommendation to VA-certified supervisor Carlos M., who reviewed the file within 3 business days. Carlos M. confirmed the suitability analysis was complete, the customer's long time horizon justified the VA, and the surrender charge was reasonable given the guaranteed income rider selected. Carlos M. issued written approval, which was preserved in the customer file. The branch's 2023 compliance sampling reviewed Mrs. Huang's file and found it compliant.

QUESTION
--------
Did Pinnacle Securities comply with FINRA Rule 2330 and Rule 3110 regarding variable annuity supervision?

ANSWER
------
Yes. Pinnacle complied with both rules. FINRA Rule 2330(d)(1) requires member firms to establish and maintain WSPs for VA recommendations, including supervisory review of suitability. Rule 3110(b) requires written procedures reasonably designed to supervise the activities of each registered person. Here, Pinnacle's procedures explicitly addressed VAs, required pre-approval by a certified supervisor, and mandated documentation of the suitability analysis. The facts show: (1) Nathaniel P. documented a complete suitability analysis including time horizon (20 years), liquidity needs ($240,000 non-VA assets), and customer understanding of terms; (2) Carlos M., a VA-certified supervisor, reviewed and approved the recommendation within 3 business days; (3) the file was preserved and later sampled during the firm's annual compliance review. The recommendation itself appears suitable: an 8-year surrender charge is reasonable for a customer with a 20-year horizon and adequate liquid reserves. Pinnacle's tiered supervisory structure, pre-approval requirement, and sampling methodology exemplify compliant practice under Rules 2330 and 3110.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2330(d)(1) — Variable Annuity Supervisory Procedures; FINRA Rule 3110(b) — Written Supervisory Procedures

KEY FACTOR
----------
Written VA procedures required pre-approval by a certified supervisor, which was completed timely with documented suitability analysis; file was preserved and sampled in annual compliance review.
