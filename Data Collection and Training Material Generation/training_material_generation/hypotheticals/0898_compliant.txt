Source Folder: 19_CFTC_Guidance
Topic: cta_cpo_suitability
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A registered CPO operates a commodity pool that holds interests in a global asset management holding company structure, which in turn owns operating subsidiaries across real estate and infrastructure sectors. The CPO's compliance team receives an inquiry questioning whether the intermediate holding companies within the structure—which do not themselves solicit commodity pool participants or pool funds for trading—must independently register as CPOs. The CPO obtains an interpretive letter from the CFTC Division of Swap Dealer and Intermediary Oversight, consistent with the analytical framework of CFTC Letter No. 17-68, confirming that intermediate holding companies that do not independently satisfy the definition of 'commodity pool' under CEA Section 1a(10) and CFTC Regulation 4.10(d)(1) need not register as CPOs.

QUESTION
--------
Was the CPO's reliance on an interpretive no-action letter from the CFTC Division of Swap Dealer and Intermediary Oversight to determine that intermediate holding companies in its fund structure need not independently register as CPOs consistent with applicable CFTC requirements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

CFTC Letter No. 17-68 establishes the Division's interpretive framework that entities within a multi-tier holding structure do not independently constitute commodity pools merely by virtue of owning interests in an entity that does qualify as a pool, where those holding entities do not themselves pool participant funds for commodity trading purposes. Reliance on a formal interpretive letter from the Division of Swap Dealer and Intermediary Oversight provides a recognized safe harbor from enforcement action under CFTC policy. The CPO's proactive approach of seeking and obtaining written interpretive guidance—rather than unilaterally assuming the exemption applied—represents a best practice in regulatory compliance.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
CEA Section 1a(10) – Definition of Commodity Pool; CFTC Regulation 4.10(d)(1); CFTC Letter No. 17-68 – Interpretive Guidance on Commodity Pool Definition

KEY FACTOR
----------
Intermediate holding companies that do not independently satisfy the CEA Section 1a(10) commodity pool definition need not register as CPOs, and reliance on a formal CFTC interpretive letter confirms the permissibility of this structural approach.
