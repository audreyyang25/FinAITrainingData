FACTS
-----
A 35-year-old software engineer with $80,000 in savings, a 25-year investment horizon, and moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance opens an account with a broker-dealer. After completing a detailed risk tolerance questionnaire and discussing the client's goals (retirement savings), the advisor recommends a 90/10 portfolio of diversified equity index funds (0.10% expense ratio) and bond index funds (0.05% expense ratio), with no load and no commission tiers. The advisor documents the discussion and the rationale for equity-heavy allocation given the long time horizon.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation satisfy the Reg BI care obligation?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this recommendation complies with the Reg BI care obligation.

The recommendation complies because: (1) the 90/10 equity/bond allocation is appropriate for a 35-year-old with a 25-year horizon and moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance; (2) the chosen index funds charge 0.10% and 0.05% annually—well below industry averages and appropriate for a cost-conscious analysis; (3) there is no load, front-end or back-end charge, and no commission structure incentivizing a higher-cost recommendation; (4) the advisor documented the risk tolerance assessment and the rationale; and (5) for a young, long-horizon investor, equity exposure is aligned with the reward/risk profile. The advisor satisfied the Reg BI obligation by considering costs (minimal), risks (appropriate for age/horizon), rewards (equity growth potential), and implicitly considering alternatives through the no-load, low-cost structure.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i); Reg BI care obligation—broker-dealer must recommend an investment in the customer's best interest considering costs, risks, rewards, and alternatives.

KEY FACTOR
----------
Low-cost, conflict-free recommendation with documented risk tolerance assessment and appropriate asset allocation for the client's age and time horizon.
