FACTS
-----
Cornerstone Capital Advisors, a 28-person FINRA member firm ($1.8B AUM), maintains comprehensive written supervisory procedures (WSPs, last updated September 2022) that require documented suitability reviews of all securities recommendations to clients age 65+ or with account balances exceeding $250K. Between June 2023 and January 2024, registered rep David Kim recommended a diversified portfolio shift for Helen Martinez, age 68, with a $580K account. Kim's recommendation: reduce equity concentration from 72% to 52%, shift 15% into intermediate-term municipal bonds, maintain 8% in high-yield corporate bond fund, and allocate 25% to stable value/cash equivalents. The transaction generated $8,400 in commissions. Supervisor Janet Walsh documented a comprehensive 45-minute suitability review on June 8, 2023, reviewing Helen's risk questionnaire (updated May 2023), account history (12-year client), income sources (Social Security $2,100/month, pension $3,400/month, part-time consulting $800/month), and existing holdings. Walsh's written memo documented the analysis: Helen's moderate risk tolerance aligned with the 52% equity allocation; municipal bond ladder matched her tax bracket (32%); high-yield allocation appropriate given her stated need for income ($4,200/month). A documented supervisory file was maintained and filed per firm procedures. No customer complaint was filed.

QUESTION
--------
Did Cornerstone Capital Advisors comply with FINRA Rule 3110 supervisory obligations for this recommendation?

ANSWER
------
Yes. Cornerstone satisfied all prongs of Rule 3110 supervision: (1) Written supervisory procedures existed and explicitly required documented suitability review for clients 65+ with $250K+ accounts (Helen triggered both); (2) the procedures were implemented—Walsh conducted a thorough, documented supervisory review documenting Helen's age, account size, risk tolerance, income sources, time horizon, and existing holdings; (3) the review was timely (conducted immediately before recommendation, not retroactively); (4) the recommendation was justified—52% equity allocation and income-focused bond strategy were appropriate for Helen's moderate risk profile and stated income needs; (5) documentation was comprehensive and filed per firm procedures. The 45-minute review depth and written analysis demonstrate reasonable supervision. No red flags (e.g., unsuitable concentration, excessive risk for age, poor income match) were present. This represents the compliant supervision model FINRA expects.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 3110(a) and (b) — Establishment and Implementation of Written Supervisory Procedures; Rule 2111 — Suitability

KEY FACTOR
----------
Timely, documented supervisory review by qualified supervisor with written analysis of suitability factors (age, risk tolerance, income, time horizon) demonstrates reasonable supervision compliant with Rule 3110(b).
