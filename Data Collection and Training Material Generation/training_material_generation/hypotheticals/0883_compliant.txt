Source Folder: 19_CFTC_Guidance
Topic: cta_cpo_suitability
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Ridgeline Capital Management, a registered CPO, operates a 4.7 Fund that employs a diversified managed futures strategy across equity index, fixed income, and agricultural commodity futures. A prospective limited partner is a 52-year-old physician with $4.2 million in investable assets, a $600,000 annual income, and an existing portfolio consisting of $1.8 million in exchange-listed securities and $900,000 in other financial instruments, satisfying the Portfolio Requirement under the current Rule 4.7 QEP threshold. Ridgeline provides the investor with a concise disclosure document identifying the fund's trading strategy, fee structure (2% management / 20% performance), material risks including leverage and margin calls, and the CTA's five-year performance record. The investor allocates $400,000 (approximately 9.5% of liquid net worth) to the fund after signing a QEP eligibility questionnaire.

QUESTION
--------
Does Ridgeline Capital Management's onboarding of this investor into its 4.7 Fund satisfy CPO suitability and disclosure obligations under CFTC Rule 4.7?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

The investor satisfies the QEP Portfolio Requirement under current Rule 4.7 thresholds, and Ridgeline properly verified this through a signed eligibility questionnaire. Although Rule 4.7 permits reduced disclosure obligations for QEP-only pools, Ridgeline voluntarily provided a substantive disclosure document addressing strategy, fees, risks, and performance history, exceeding minimum obligations. The 9.5% allocation is proportionate to the investor's overall financial profile, and the suitability analysis is documented, satisfying NFA Compliance Rule 2-30.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
CFTC Rule 4.7 – Conditional Exemptions for CPOs and CTAs; NFA Compliance Rule 2-30 – Suitability

KEY FACTOR
----------
The investor met verified QEP eligibility thresholds, the CPO provided voluntary meaningful disclosures exceeding Rule 4.7 minimums, and the allocation was proportionate to demonstrated net worth.
