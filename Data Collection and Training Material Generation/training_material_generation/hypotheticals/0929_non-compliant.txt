ID: 929
Source Folder: 21_FINRA_OHO_Decisions
Topic: leveraged_etf_suitability_retiree
Difficulty: easy
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Registered representative Marcus Delano recommended a 3x leveraged inverse ETF to a 71-year-old retired school teacher with a fixed income, moderate risk tolerance documented in her new account form, and a stated investment objective of capital preservation. Delano made the recommendation after the client expressed general concern about inflation but without conducting any updated suitability analysis. The client lost 42% of her invested principal within six months due to volatility decay inherent in the leveraged product. Delano had no prior training on leveraged ETF mechanics and had not reviewed the firm's internal guidance restricting such products to aggressive investors.

QUESTION
--------
Did Delano's recommendation of a 3x leveraged inverse ETF to a conservative retiree satisfy FINRA Rule 2111 suitability obligations?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a suitability violation.

Rule 2111 requires that a recommendation be suitable based on the customer's investment profile, including age, financial situation, risk tolerance, and investment objectives. A 3x leveraged inverse ETF carries extreme short-term volatility and decay risk that is fundamentally inconsistent with a capital preservation objective and moderate risk tolerance. Delano failed to conduct reasonable-basis and customer-specific suitability analysis before making the recommendation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability); FINRA Regulatory Notice 09-31 (Leveraged ETFs)

KEY FACTOR
----------
Recommending a high-risk leveraged product to a conservative retiree without an updated suitability analysis directly contravenes Rule 2111's customer-specific suitability requirement.
