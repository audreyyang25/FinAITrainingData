Source Folder: 06_DOL_Fiduciary
Topic: pte_2020_02
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Samantha Reyes, a registered representative at Clearwater Financial Group, provides investment advice to IRA holders. Clearwater receives varying levels of compensation depending on the products recommended, creating a potential prohibited transaction under IRC §4975. However, Clearwater has adopted comprehensive written policies and procedures requiring advisors to act in clients' best interest. Samantha recommends that her client, David Park (age 62, $520,000 IRA), purchase a fixed indexed annuity from National Guaranty Life that pays Clearwater a 4.5% commission. Before making the recommendation, Samantha documents a thorough analysis showing David has no pension, limited Social Security income, significant longevity risk, and an expressed desire for guaranteed lifetime income. She compares three annuity products and two non-annuity alternatives, discloses all compensation including the commission and a 15-basis-point trail, and provides David with a written explanation of the conflicts of interest. Clearwater's compliance department reviews and approves the recommendation under the firm's retrospective compliance review program.

QUESTION
--------
Does Samantha's annuity recommendation to an IRA holder comply with PTE 2020-02 despite the conflicted compensation arrangement?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

PTE 2020-02 permits investment advice fiduciaries to receive conflicted compensation provided they satisfy all exemption conditions: rendering advice in the client's best interest reflecting care, skill, and prudence; charging no more than reasonable compensation; making required disclosures of conflicts and compensation; and maintaining written policies and procedures subject to retrospective review. Samantha satisfied each condition by documenting a thorough needs-based analysis, comparing multiple alternatives, fully disclosing all compensation, and operating under a firm compliance framework with retrospective review. The annuity recommendation was objectively suited to David's income needs and longevity risk.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
DOL PTE 2020-02 – Improving Investment Advice for Workers & Retirees; Section II(a)-(d) Conditions

KEY FACTOR
----------
The advisor satisfied all PTE 2020-02 conditions—best interest analysis, comparative evaluation, full disclosure of conflicted compensation, and firm-level retrospective compliance review—making the otherwise prohibited transaction exempt.
