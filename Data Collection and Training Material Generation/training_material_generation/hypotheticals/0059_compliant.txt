Topic: ia_fiduciary_duty
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
An RIA receives a $15,000 quarterly referral fee from a mutual fund company for directing client assets into the fund family's products. Before engagement, the RIA provides each client a Form ADV Part 2A and Part 2B that explicitly discloses the referral fee arrangement, the dollar amounts paid in the prior year, and the potential conflict. The RIA documents that the recommended fund lineup (0.45% net expense ratio) offers competitive performance and costs compared to alternatives. The client signs acknowledgment of the disclosure.

QUESTION
--------
Does this referral fee arrangement satisfy the RIA's fiduciary duty?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this is compliant.

While the referral fee creates a potential conflict of interest, the RIA has satisfied the duty of loyalty through full, clear, and conspicuous disclosure in the ADV and acknowledgment by the client. The RIA has also satisfied the duty of care by documenting that the recommended funds are competitive on performance and expense ratios relative to alternatives, showing that the recommendation is based on client benefit, not just the fee incentive. SEC guidance permits compensation arrangements—including referral fees—when properly disclosed and when the adviser acts in the client's best interest. The RIA's documentation of competitive analysis supports the reasonableness of the recommendation.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Adviser Act § 206(2) and Rule 206(4)-1; SEC Form ADV Instructions; SEC guidance on conflicts of interest

KEY FACTOR
----------
Complete, documented disclosure of the conflict combined with evidence that the recommendation is suitable based on competitive fund performance and cost analysis.
