ID: 177
Source Folder: unknown
Topic: concentration
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
A 45-year-old client with $2 million in investable assets, 25+ years to retirement, and aggressive risk tolerance owns 40% of her portfolio ($800,000) in a concentrated position in her employer's publicly traded stock (Tech Mega Corp, symbol: TMC). The stock trades 15+ million shares daily and represents only 8% of her total net worth including real estate and other assets. Her advisor reviews the position quarterly, discusses diversification, and recommends a gradual 3-year tax-loss harvesting strategy to reduce concentration, while the client voluntarily elects to maintain the position for tax deferral reasons.

QUESTION
--------
Does the advisor's handling of this concentrated position comply with fiduciary duty and suitability standards?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this is compliant.

While the concentration is substantial (40% of investable assets), the advisor has satisfied the duty of care by: (1) regularly monitoring and reassessing the position; (2) recommending diversification strategies that are tax-efficient (3-year harvesting); (3) documenting the client's informed decision to retain the position despite recommendation; and (4) noting that the position is only 8% of total net worth and highly liquid (15M+ daily volume). The client's aggressive risk tolerance, long time horizon, and ability to bear concentration risk make the recommendation reasonable under FINRA Rule 2111. The advisor's proactive engagement and documented recommendations distinguish this from passive acceptance of concentration.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111 (suitability); Investment Adviser Act § 206 (fiduciary duty of care and loyalty); SEC Reg BI Rule 15l-1(a)(1) (duty of care includes identifying concentration risk).

KEY FACTOR
----------
Documented quarterly review, specific diversification recommendation, and informed client consent to retain the position based on tax considerations transforms an otherwise high-risk concentration into compliant advice.
