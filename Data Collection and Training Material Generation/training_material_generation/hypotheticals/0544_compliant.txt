Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Heartland Wealth Advisors, a FINRA member firm with 45 registered representatives, implemented a comprehensive Reg BI compliance framework effective June 28, 2020, two days before the compliance deadline. The firm's program included updated written supervisory procedures addressing all four Reg BI component obligations (Care, Disclosure, Conflict of Interest, and Compliance), mandatory representative training with documented completion records, a Form CRS filed with the SEC and delivered to all existing retail customers, and an enhanced conflict of interest inventory identifying revenue-sharing arrangements with three mutual fund families. During a 2024 FINRA examination, examiners noted that two registered representatives had failed to document their cost-basis analysis on 8 out of 340 recommendations, though the firm's supervisory principal had flagged and addressed those deficiencies within 30 days through follow-up reviews and supplemental documentation requirements.

QUESTION
--------
Did Heartland Wealth Advisors satisfy its Reg BI compliance and supervisory obligations despite the documentation gaps identified in 8 out of 340 recommendations?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Reg BI's Compliance Obligation requires firms to establish, maintain, and enforce reasonably designed written policies and procedures—not to achieve perfection in every instance. The firm implemented a comprehensive framework addressing all four component obligations before the compliance date, trained representatives, filed Form CRS, and maintained a conflict inventory. The fact that 8 out of 340 recommendations had initial documentation gaps is not a systemic failure, particularly where the firm's supervisory system detected the deficiency and the principal remediated it within 30 days. This demonstrates a functioning, reasonably designed supervisory system consistent with FINRA Rule 3110 expectations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Exchange Act Rule 15l-1(a)(1) – Regulation Best Interest (Compliance Obligation); FINRA Rule 3110 – Supervision

KEY FACTOR
----------
A firm's supervisory system is judged on whether it is reasonably designed, not whether it catches every deficiency in real time—prompt detection and remediation of isolated documentation gaps demonstrates an effective system.
