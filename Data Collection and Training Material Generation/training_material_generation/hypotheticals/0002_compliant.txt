FACTS
-----
A 45-year-old employed client with a $150,000 401(k) balance and moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance meets with a registered representative. The client explicitly states interest in equity exposure. The advisor recommends a diversified portfolio of 80% low-cost equity index ETFs (0.05–0.12% expense ratio) and 20% bond index ETFs (0.05% expense ratio), generating minimal commission (12 bps total on a trail basis). The advisor documents the discussion, considers the client's 20+ year time horizon, and presents no-load alternatives during the recommendation process.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation satisfy the Reg BI care obligation?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this recommendation complies with the Reg BI care obligation.

The advisor's recommendation satisfies the Reg BI care obligation because: (1) the recommended portfolio of low-cost index ETFs is aligned with the client's stated moderate-to-aggressive risk tolerance and 20+ year time horizon; (2) the costs are minimized (0.05–0.12% expense ratios far below industry averages); (3) the advisor explicitly considered alternatives (presented no-load options) and documented the rationale; (4) the compensation structure (minimal trail commissions) does not create a material conflict incentivizing a higher-cost recommendation; and (5) the risk/reward profile is appropriate for the stated objectives. Under Reg BI, the broker-dealer has satisfied its obligation to act in the customer's best interest by recommending low-cost, diversified products suitable to the customer's profile.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(i); Reg BI care obligation—broker-dealer must recommend an investment in the customer's best interest considering costs, risks, rewards, and alternatives.

KEY FACTOR
----------
Combination of low-cost products, alignment with customer's risk tolerance and time horizon, documented consideration of alternatives, and minimal conflicts of interest.
