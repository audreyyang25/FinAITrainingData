Source Folder: 12_FINRA_Arbitration_Awards
Topic: arbitration_liability
Difficulty: medium
Verdict: NON-COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Fortune Financial Services, a FINRA member firm with approximately 280 registered representatives across 200 branch offices, permitted its registered representative to execute 14 deferred variable annuity exchanges for a 72-year-old retired schoolteacher over a 3-year period from 2017 to 2020. Each exchange triggered new surrender charge periods of 7 years and generated approximately $4,200 in commissions to the representative. The firm's written supervisory procedures required a principal to review and approve variable annuity exchanges, but the branch office principal routinely approved the exchanges based solely on the representative's verbal representation that the client requested the switches, without reviewing the client's existing annuity surrender schedules or conducting a comparative analysis of the old and new products. The client's account records showed total surrender charges of $38,000 incurred across the exchanges, while the new annuities offered substantially similar subaccount options and death benefit riders as the annuities being replaced.

QUESTION
--------
Did the firm's supervisory system satisfy FINRA requirements for oversight of variable annuity exchange transactions?

ANSWER
------
No, this conduct is a supervisory failure in violation of FINRA Rules 3110 and 2330.

FINRA Rule 2330(b) requires that a registered principal review and determine whether each deferred variable annuity transaction is suitable, specifically considering whether the customer would incur a surrender charge, whether the customer would lose existing benefits, and whether the new product offers materially different features. The principal's approval based solely on the representative's verbal representations, without reviewing surrender schedules or performing a comparative product analysis, constitutes a failure to implement a reasonable supervisory system under Rule 3110. This pattern closely mirrors the Fortune Financial Services AWC findings regarding supervisory system deficiencies from July 2016 through July 2021.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 3110 (Supervision) and FINRA Rule 2330 (Members' Responsibilities Regarding Deferred Variable Annuities)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm's principal approved variable annuity exchanges without conducting any comparative analysis of the existing and replacement products, rendering the supervisory review a rubber stamp rather than a substantive suitability check.
