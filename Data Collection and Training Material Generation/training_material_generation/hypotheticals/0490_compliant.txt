Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Pinecrest BDC Inc. and Pinecrest Private Credit Fund LP are both managed by Pinecrest Capital Management LLC. A limited partner of Pinecrest Private Credit Fund LP, Westbrook Capital Partners, which holds a 3% limited partnership interest, proposes to co-invest alongside the BDC in a $22 million senior secured loan to a mid-market technology company. Pinecrest Capital Management identifies Westbrook as a second-tier affiliate of the BDC. After conducting a thorough affiliate-status analysis with outside counsel, the firm concludes that Westbrook qualifies as a 'remote affiliate' under the 1940 Act because it is not an officer, director, or employee of the adviser, does not own more than 5% of the adviser's voting securities, and has no other close affiliate nexus. The co-investment transaction is presented to the BDC's board of directors and approved by a required majority of independent directors, with the terms documented as fair and on market terms comparable to recent arm's-length transactions.

QUESTION
--------
Did Pinecrest BDC Inc. properly comply with the Investment Company Act of 1940's affiliate transaction provisions in permitting the co-investment with Westbrook Capital Partners?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

IM Guidance Update 2014-12 clarifies that a second-tier affiliate of a BDC — such as a limited partner of a partnership under common control with the BDC — may be treated as a remote affiliate depending on factors such as the nature and extent of the affiliate relationship. Pinecrest obtained outside counsel analysis confirming Westbrook's remote affiliate status, and the co-investment was approved by a required majority of the BDC's directors as specified in the 1940 Act. The documentation of fair, market-rate terms further satisfies the 1940 Act's protective framework designed to prevent undue influence and overreaching.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
Investment Company Act of 1940, Sections 57(a) and 57(f); IM Guidance Update No. 2014-12 – BDC Transactions with Certain Second-Tier Affiliates

KEY FACTOR
----------
The BDC properly classified the second-tier affiliate as a remote affiliate through documented legal analysis and obtained the required majority board approval before permitting the co-investment transaction.
