Topic: variable_annuity_rule
Difficulty: hard
Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Jennifer Alvarez, age 62, has a $750,000 investment portfolio and is five years from retirement. She holds an existing deferred variable annuity with a cash value of $195,000 that is outside its surrender period and offers no living benefit rider. Her registered representative, Mark Torres at Summit Wealth Advisors, recommends exchanging into a new deferred variable annuity with a guaranteed lifetime withdrawal benefit (GLWB) rider, a 5-year surrender period, and a combined annual insurance charge of 1.85% (including the GLWB rider cost of 0.95%). Mark documents in the client file that Jennifer's primary concern is guaranteed retirement income and that Social Security and a small pension will cover only 55% of her projected expenses. He obtains Jennifer's acknowledgment of the new surrender period and fee structure. Before processing, Mark submits the exchange to the branch's designated registered principal, Lisa Grant, who reviews the client profile, compares the features of both annuities, confirms that the GLWB rider addresses a documented income gap, and documents her approval rationale in the firm's supervisory system within two business days.

QUESTION
--------
Does the recommended variable annuity exchange comply with FINRA Rule 2330's suitability and principal review requirements?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

FINRA Rule 2330(b) requires that a recommended exchange of a deferred variable annuity be suitable considering the customer's age, income needs, existing annuity benefits, and the new product's features including surrender charges. Here, the exchange provides a materially beneficial GLWB rider that directly addresses a documented retirement income gap, the client is 62 with a 5-year surrender period aligning with her retirement timeline, the existing annuity is past its surrender period, and the annuity represents approximately 26% of her total portfolio — preserving adequate liquidity. Rule 2330(c) requires prior registered principal review and approval with documented rationale, which Lisa Grant completed within two business days after a substantive comparative review of both products and the client's profile.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2330(b) – Suitability Considerations for Deferred Variable Annuities; FINRA Rule 2330(c) – Principal Review and Approval

KEY FACTOR
----------
The exchange provided a materially beneficial living benefit rider addressing a documented retirement income gap, the surrender period aligned with the client's timeline, and the registered principal completed a substantive pre-transaction review with documented rationale.
