Verdict: COMPLIANT

FACTS
-----
Ahead of the June 2020 Reg BI compliance date, a regional broker-dealer with 85 registered representatives implemented a comprehensive compliance program. The firm required all representatives to complete a 12-hour training module covering Reg BI's four component obligations — Disclosure, Care, Conflict of Interest, and Compliance. The firm also updated its Form CRS to a two-page relationship summary clearly distinguishing its brokerage services from affiliated advisory services, including prescribed conversation-starter questions such as 'Given my financial situation, should I choose a brokerage service? Why or why not?' The Form CRS was filed with the SEC via EDGAR and delivered to all existing retail customers by June 30, 2020, and is now provided to every new retail customer at or before the earliest of a recommendation, the opening of an account, or the placement of an order. Supervisory procedures were updated to require branch managers to review a sample of recommendations monthly against each customer's investment profile, and the firm's written supervisory procedures explicitly address how conflicts of interest arising from compensation differentials between proprietary and third-party products are identified, disclosed, and mitigated.

QUESTION
--------
Does the broker-dealer's implementation of Reg BI training, Form CRS delivery, and supervisory procedures satisfy applicable compliance program design standards?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this conduct is compliant.

Reg BI's Compliance Obligation requires broker-dealers to establish, maintain, and enforce written policies and procedures reasonably designed to achieve compliance with Reg BI's component obligations. The firm's 12-hour training program, updated Form CRS conforming to the SEC's prescribed format and conversation starters as described in industry FAQs, timely delivery protocol, monthly supervisory reviews of recommendations, and explicit conflict-of-interest policies collectively demonstrate a reasonably designed compliance program. The firm's approach aligns with SIFMA and industry guidance emphasizing that firms should train representatives on all four Reg BI obligations and update supervisory procedures to monitor ongoing compliance.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
SEC Rule 15l-1(a)(2)(iv) – Reg BI Compliance Obligation; SEC Rule 17a-14 – Form CRS Delivery Requirements; FINRA Rule 3110 – Supervision

KEY FACTOR
----------
The firm established a comprehensive, multi-layered compliance program — including training, Form CRS delivery, supervisory review, and conflict mitigation policies — that was reasonably designed to achieve compliance with all four Reg BI component obligations before the compliance date.
