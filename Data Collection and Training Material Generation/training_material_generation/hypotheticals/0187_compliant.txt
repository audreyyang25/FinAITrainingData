FACTS
-----
A 76-year-old widowed client with $800,000 in investable assets, RMD obligations of $32,000 annually, and a stated goal of 'income preservation with modest growth' meets with her advisor. The advisor recommends a diversified portfolio of 60% investment-grade corporate bonds and preferred stock ETFs yielding 4.5%, 30% dividend-paying large-cap equities (yield 2.2%), and 10% short-term bond funds. All holdings are liquid, trading on major exchanges with low bid-ask spreads. The advisor documents: (i) the client's income needs and RMD obligations; (ii) her moderate liquidity needs; (iii) her stated aversion to complex products; and (iv) her prior 25-year history of stable, diversified stock holdings. The fee is 0.75% AUM, disclosed and reasonable.

QUESTION
--------
Does this recommendation comply with FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability), Rule 2010 (General Conduct Standards), and SEC senior investor guidance?

ANSWER
------
Yes, this recommendation complies with applicable standards.

This recommendation satisfies suitability under Rule 2111 because the advisor has documented the client's financial situation (income needs, RMD obligations), age (76), investment experience (25 years of diversified holdings), and explicitly matched the portfolio to her stated goals (income preservation with modest growth). The 60/30/10 allocation is conservative and appropriate for a senior with liquidity needs and income focus. All recommended securities are liquid, plain-vanilla instruments with transparent pricing, addressing the SEC's concern (in its 2013 senior investor guidance) that seniors should not be steered toward opaque or complex products. The advisor's documentation demonstrates compliance with Rule 2010's fair dealing standard by showing due diligence and a reasoned basis for the recommendation. The 0.75% fee is disclosed and not excessive for a managed account. The absence of unsuitable leverage, derivatives, or illiquid holdings, combined with the explicit suitability analysis, demonstrates best practices under the SEC's framework for senior investor recommendations.

APPLICABLE STANDARD
-------------------
FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability Rule); FINRA Rule 2010 (General Conduct Standards); SEC senior investor risk alert guidance (2013); Investment Adviser Act duty of care (Section 206)

KEY FACTOR
----------
The comprehensive documentation linking the product selection (income-focused, liquid, diversified) to the client's documented needs (RMDs, income, risk aversion) and experience (25-year history), with transparent fees and no complex or illiquid instruments.
