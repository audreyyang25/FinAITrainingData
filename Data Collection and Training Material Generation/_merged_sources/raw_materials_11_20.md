## Folder 11: Case Law (179 files)

### Overview
- **Files:** 179 (mix of PDFs and .txt files; many PDFs have companion .txt extractions)
- **Estimated % relevant to suitability/best interest:** ~85%
- **File types:** Federal court opinions, SEC releases, academic papers, practitioner articles

### Key Topics & Legal Standards Found
1. **Investment Adviser Fiduciary Duty (Advisers Act §206):** SEC v. Capital Gains Research Bureau (1963) — the foundational Supreme Court case establishing that investment advisers owe fiduciary duties under the Advisers Act. Extensively cited across multiple documents.
2. **SEC IA-5248 (June 2019):** The Commission Interpretation Regarding Standard of Conduct for Investment Advisers — the final interpretation clarifying duty of care and duty of loyalty. Full text present.
3. **SEC IA-6941 (Jan 2026):** FamilyWealth Advisers enforcement action — hedge clause violations, demonstrating that advisers cannot contractually limit fiduciary duties with retail clients.
4. **5th Circuit DOL Fiduciary Rule Vacature (Chamber of Commerce v. DOL, 2018):** Landmark decision vacating the DOL's 2016 Fiduciary Rule on grounds of statutory overreach. Critical to understanding ERISA fiduciary standards for retirement accounts.
5. **SEC v. Jarkesy (SCOTUS 2024):** Supreme Court decision on 7th Amendment right to jury trial in SEC enforcement — affects enforcement mechanism for all suitability/fiduciary violations.
6. **Northstar Financial Advisors v. Schwab Investments (9th Cir. 2015):** Fiduciary duty of mutual fund trustees to shareholders — breach of duty by failing to adhere to fundamental investment objectives.
7. **Duty of Care article (Investment Lawyer, Feb 2023):** Detailed analysis of how SEC enforces the duty of care post-IA-5248, covering both retail and institutional clients.
8. **Fiduciary Duty Paper (Lorna Schnase, 2010):** Comprehensive overview of common law vs. federal fiduciary duty for investment advisers — agency law, trust law, and Advisers Act.

### Particularly Important Documents
- `case_Spotlight_Investment_Advisers_Act_of_1940_Fiduciary_Duty.pdf` — Complete enumeration of all fiduciary sub-duties (best interest, suitability, competence, supervision, best execution, proxy voting, allocation, confidentiality)
- `case_ia-5248.pdf` — The definitive SEC interpretation of adviser standard of conduct
- `case_ia-6941.pdf` — Recent (Jan 2026) enforcement action on hedge clauses
- `FifthCir_ChamberCommerce_v_DOL_Vacated_2018.pdf` — ERISA fiduciary rule vacature
- `case_Investment-Adviser-Duty-of-Care-SEC-Enforcement-and-Policy.pdf` — How duty of care is being enforced

### AI Training Value
**HIGH.** This folder provides the authoritative legal framework for fiduciary duty. An AI model would learn: (1) the two-component structure of fiduciary duty (care + loyalty); (2) how courts interpret suitability obligations; (3) the relationship between the Advisers Act standard and Reg BI; (4) how enforcement actions are structured; (5) the ERISA fiduciary framework for retirement accounts.

## Folder 13: FINRA Rulebook (56 files)

### Overview
- **Files:** 56
- **Estimated % relevant to suitability/best interest:** ~90%
- **Content type:** FINRA rules, FAQs, regulatory notices, compliance guides, practitioner white papers

### Key Topics & Legal Standards Found
1. **FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability):** Complete FAQ documents covering all three suitability obligations:
   - **Reasonable-basis suitability:** Product must be suitable for at least some investors
   - **Customer-specific suitability:** Product must be suitable for the particular customer based on investment profile
   - **Quantitative suitability:** Series of transactions must not be excessive
2. **FINRA Rule 2090 (Know Your Customer):** Requirement to gather and retain "essential facts" about customers throughout the relationship.
3. **FINRA Rule 2330 (Variable Annuities):** Members' responsibilities regarding deferred variable annuities — specific suitability requirements for annuity recommendations including tax-deferred growth benefits, death/living benefits, and subaccount allocation.
4. **Reg BI Checklist:** FINRA's operational checklist for broker-dealer Reg BI compliance — 20+ items covering disclosure, care, conflict of interest, and compliance obligations.
5. **Regulatory Notice 22-08 (Complex Products):** FINRA guidance on sales practice obligations for complex products and options, reminding firms of Reg BI application.
6. **Investment profile factors:** Age, other investments, financial situation/needs, tax status, investment objectives, investment experience, investment time horizon, liquidity needs, risk tolerance.
7. **IRI Rule 2330 analysis (2020):** Dynamic annuity concentration limits — argues that historical 2330 concentration rules are outdated given shift from variable to fixed indexed annuities.

### Particularly Important Documents
- `CombinedSuitabilityFAQs_12-10-12.pdf` — Consolidated FINRA Rule 2111 FAQ (comprehensive, 27+ pages)
- `faq-finra-rule-2111-suitability.pdf` — Morrison & Foerster summary of Rule 2111
- `reg-bi-checklist.pdf` — FINRA's Reg BI compliance checklist
- `FMC_FINRA_2090_2111.pdf` — NICE Actimize white paper on FINRA 2090/2111 compliance solutions
- `Regulatory-Notice-22-08.pdf` — Complex products and options guidance
- `IRI-Rule2330-2020.pdf` — Annuity concentration limits analysis

### AI Training Value
**VERY HIGH.** This is the operational rulebook. An AI model would learn: (1) the exact text and interpretation of FINRA suitability rules; (2) what information must be gathered from customers; (3) how to evaluate whether a recommendation is suitable; (4) specific requirements for variable annuities, complex products, and options; (5) how Reg BI's care obligation builds on and enhances FINRA suitability.

### Key Legal Standards Identified Across All Folders

1. **SEC Regulation Best Interest (Reg BI, Rule 15l-1):** Four component obligations — Disclosure, Care, Conflict of Interest, Compliance. Applies to broker-dealers recommending to retail customers. Effective June 30, 2020.

2. **Investment Advisers Act §206 Fiduciary Duty:** Duty of care + duty of loyalty. Applies to all investment advisers (registered and unregistered). Confirmed in SEC v. Capital Gains Research Bureau (1963), codified in IA-5248 (2019).

3. **FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability):** Three-part test — reasonable-basis, customer-specific, quantitative suitability. Applies to FINRA member recommendations. Now supplemented by Reg BI for retail customers.

4. **FINRA Rule 2330 (Variable Annuities):** Enhanced suitability requirements for deferred variable annuity recommendations.

5. **FINRA Rule 2090 (Know Your Customer):** Obligation to gather and maintain "essential facts" about customers.

6. **ERISA Fiduciary Duty:** Duties of loyalty and prudence for retirement plan fiduciaries. Governed by DOL; partially vacated 5th Circuit decision on 2016 fiduciary rule.

7. **OCC 12 CFR 9.12:** Self-dealing and conflicts of interest rules for bank fiduciaries.

8. **CFTC CTA/CPO Conduct Rules:** Disclosure, conflict of interest, and risk disclosure obligations for commodity advisers.
