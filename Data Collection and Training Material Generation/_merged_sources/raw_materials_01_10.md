## Folder 01: SEC Guidance, No-Action Letters & Interpretive Releases
**Total files:** 135 | **Estimated % relevant to suitability/best interest:** ~70%

### Key Topics & Legal Standards Found
- **IA-5248 (2019):** The foundational SEC Interpretation of the fiduciary standard for investment advisers under the Advisers Act. Defines duty of care (best interest advice, best execution, monitoring) and duty of loyalty. Cites SEC v. Capital Gains Research Bureau, 375 U.S. 180 (1963).
- **Reg BI Final Rule (34-86031, 2019):** The core broker-dealer best interest standard. Establishes four component obligations: Disclosure, Care, Conflict of Interest, and Compliance. Explicitly states it "enhances the broker-dealer standard of conduct beyond existing suitability obligations."
- **Staff Bulletin on Care Obligations:** SEC staff guidance on how both broker-dealers and investment advisers should implement care obligations in practice.
- **Chairman Clayton speech:** Policy rationale for the dual-standard approach (Reg BI for BDs, fiduciary for IAs).
- **Form CRS (IA-5249):** Customer Relationship Summary requirements for retail investor disclosure.
- **SIFMA comment letters on Reg BI implementation**
- **IM Guidance series (2013–2023):** Division of Investment Management staff guidance on various adviser conduct issues.
- **Proposed rules IA-5955, IA-6240, IA-6383:** Private fund adviser rules with fiduciary implications.

### Particularly Important Documents
1. `IA_FiduciaryInterpretation_IA-5248_2019.pdf` — THE defining document for IA fiduciary duty
2. `RegBI_FinalRule_34-86031_2019.pdf` — THE defining document for BD best interest standard
3. `noaction_staff-bulletin-standards-conduct-broker-dealers-investment-advisers-care-obligations.txt` — Practical care obligation guidance
4. `noaction_exams-reg-bi-alert-13023.pdf` — Exam-focused Reg BI risk alert

### Training Value
**HIGH.** This folder contains the primary source documents that define what "best interest" and "fiduciary duty" mean under federal securities law. Essential for grounding the model in authoritative legal standards. The dual IA-5248/Reg BI framework is the backbone of all suitability/best interest analysis.

## Folder 02: SEC Enforcement Actions
**Total files:** 378 | **Estimated % relevant to suitability/best interest:** ~55%

### Key Topics & Legal Standards Found
- **Reg BI Care Obligation violations:** LifeMark Securities (34-100616) — BD recommended GWG L Bonds (high-risk, illiquid corporate bonds) without reasonable diligence. Shows both "reasonable basis" and "customer-specific" prongs of Care Obligation in action.
- **Advisers Act Section 206(2) violations:** Summit Planning Group (34-104022) — IA invested 293 client accounts in VXX (volatility ETN) without reasonable basis, inconsistent with product prospectus. Demonstrates breach of fiduciary duty of care.
- **Fiduciary Duty Recommendation (2013):** SEC Investor Advisory Committee recommendation that broker-dealers providing personalized investment advice should be subject to a fiduciary duty standard. Key historical document showing evolution toward Reg BI.
- **Regulatory Impact Analysis (2015):** Analysis of the economic effects of applying fiduciary standards to investment advice.
- **AARP Report (2011):** "Protecting Investors—Establishing the SEC Fiduciary Duty Standard" by Mercer Bullard, covering suitability vs. fiduciary duty, the broker exclusion, and Dodd-Frank Section 913.
- **Oaktree Capital (34-101163):** Beneficial ownership reporting violations (not directly suitability-relevant, showing not all enforcement actions are on-topic).

### Key Legal Standards
- Exchange Act Rule 15l-1 (Reg BI General Obligation and Care Obligation)
- Advisers Act Sections 206(1), 206(2), 206(4) and Rule 206(4)-7
- FINRA Suitability Rule 2111 (pre-Reg BI)
- Dodd-Frank Section 913

### Particularly Important Documents
1. `34-100616.txt` (LifeMark Securities) — Textbook Reg BI Care Obligation enforcement case
2. `34-104022.txt` (Summit Planning Group) — IA fiduciary duty breach, VXX suitability
3. `fiduciary-duty-recommendation-2013.txt` — Investor Advisory Committee fiduciary recommendation
4. `091311aarpreport.txt` — Comprehensive overview of fiduciary duty vs. suitability debate

### Training Value
**HIGH.** Enforcement actions show how legal standards apply to real conduct. The LifeMark and Summit cases are exemplary for teaching a model to identify specific violations of the care obligation. The dataset includes both Reg BI-era (post-June 2020) and pre-Reg BI enforcement. Note: many files cover topics beyond suitability (reporting violations, insider trading, etc.), so filtering is needed.

## Folder 03: FINRA AWC (Acceptance, Waiver & Consent) Cases
**Total files:** 645 | **Estimated % relevant to suitability/best interest:** ~45%

### Key Topics & Legal Standards Found
- **FINRA Suitability Rule 2111:** Pre-Reg BI suitability standard with three obligations: (1) reasonable-basis suitability, (2) customer-specific suitability, (3) quantitative suitability (churning).
- **Reg BI transition cases:** AWC documents covering the changeover from suitability to Reg BI for broker-dealers.
- **FINRA Annual Conference materials (2015, 2017, 2019):** Detailed panel discussions on suitability compliance, including James Wrona's analysis "The Best of Both Worlds" comparing IA fiduciary duty and BD suitability.
- **2019 Reg BI Obligations panel:** SEC Chief Counsel Emily Westerberg Russell explaining Reg BI's disclosure, care, conflict of interest, and compliance obligations.
- **Named AWC cases against firms and individuals:** Spartan Capital, Cetera Advisors, LPL Financial, Credit Suisse, Network 1 Financial, etc. Cases cover churning, unsuitable recommendations, excessive trading, failure to supervise.
- **Senior investor protections:** 2017 FINRA guidance on suitability for senior investors.

### Key Legal Standards
- FINRA Rule 2111 (Suitability)
- FINRA Rule 2090 (Know Your Customer)
- Reg BI Exchange Act Rule 15l-1
- FINRA Rule 3110 (Supervision)

### Particularly Important Documents
1. `awc_2015_AC_Suitability_What_You_Need_to_Know_2.txt` — Comprehensive FINRA conference panel on suitability compliance
2. `awc_2019_RBI_Reg_BI_Obligations.txt` — FINRA/SEC panel on Reg BI implementation
3. `awc_2017_AC_Suitability_Considerations.txt` — Suitability considerations and exam findings
4. AWC cases against specific firms (Spartan Capital, LPL, Cetera, etc.)

### Training Value
**HIGH.** AWC cases provide the most granular, fact-specific examples of suitability and Reg BI violations. The combination of FINRA conference materials (explaining the rules) with actual AWC disciplinary cases (showing violations) creates excellent training pairs. The model can learn both the standard and how it's applied to specific conduct patterns (churning, unsuitable complex products, failure to supervise, etc.).

## Folder 06: DOL Fiduciary Rule & Retirement Guidance
**Total files:** 112 | **Estimated % relevant to suitability/best interest:** ~60%

### Key Topics & Legal Standards Found
- **DOL 2016 Fiduciary Rule (Conflict of Interest Rule):** Expanded definition of "fiduciary" for retirement investment advice under ERISA. Replaced the 1975 five-part test with a broader functional test. Created the Best Interest Contract Exemption (BIC Exemption).
- **Key concepts:** ERISA fiduciary standards (prudence, loyalty, exclusive purpose), prohibited transaction rules, the distinction between ERISA fiduciary advice and incidental sales activities.
- **PTE 2020-02:** "Improving Investment Advice for Workers & Retirees" — prohibited transaction exemption requiring fiduciary advisers to act in retirement investors' best interest.
- **2024 Retirement Security Rule:** DOL's latest attempt to update the fiduciary definition (subject to litigation).
- **DOL FABs (Field Advisory Bulletins):** Enforcement guidance on fiduciary rule compliance.
- **Rollover advice:** Treatment of 401(k)-to-IRA rollover recommendations as fiduciary advice.
- **Note:** Some DOL .txt files contain "Page Not Found" errors (failed web scrapes), reducing usable content.

### Key Legal Standards
- ERISA Section 3(21)(A)(ii) (fiduciary investment advice definition)
- Internal Revenue Code Section 4975 (prohibited transactions)
- PTE 2020-02 conditions (Impartial Conduct Standards)
- DOL 2016 Fiduciary Rule (vacated by 5th Circuit, 2018)
- DOL 2024 Retirement Security Rule (proposed/final)
- Best Interest Contract Exemption (BIC)

### Particularly Important Documents
1. `DOL_2016_Fiduciary_ConflictInterest_Rule_FR.pdf` — Complete 2016 fiduciary rule
2. `6_D_retirement-security-rule-and-amendments-to-class-pte-fo.pdf` — 2024 Retirement Security Rule
3. `DOL_FAB_2018-02_FiduciaryRule_Enforcement.pdf` — Enforcement guidance
4. `dol_brave_meeting-your-fiduciary-responsibilities-booklet-2021.pdf` — Plain-language fiduciary guide
5. `dol_brave_fiduciary-investment-advice-regulatory-impact-analysis-2015.pdf` — Economic analysis

### Training Value
**HIGH.** The DOL fiduciary framework is the second major axis of fiduciary duty (alongside SEC Reg BI). For advisers handling retirement accounts, both SEC and DOL standards apply simultaneously. The interplay between these regimes — especially for IRA rollovers — is a critical area where suitability analysis becomes complex. The 2016 Rule's BIC Exemption concepts influenced Reg BI's design.

## Folder 08: State Fiduciary & Best Interest Rules
**Total files:** 145 | **Estimated % relevant to suitability/best interest:** ~55%

### Key Topics & Legal Standards Found
- **New York Regulation 187:** Best interest standard for life insurance and annuity recommendations. Effective Aug 2019 (annuities), Feb 2020 (life insurance). Requires that only consumer's interests influence recommendations; compensation cannot drive product selection.
- **Nevada NRS 628A:** Statutory fiduciary duty for financial planners. Requires disclosure of compensation/commissions. Duty of diligent inquiry into client circumstances.
- **Maryland fiduciary standard developments** (primarily AG consumer protection focus in sampled file).
- **Colorado HB181 (2019):** Fiduciary standard for advisers.
- **Minnesota SF2253 (2023):** Proposed state fiduciary duty.
- **NASAA Fiduciary Duty Letter (2013):** North American Securities Administrators Association urging SEC to extend fiduciary duty to broker-dealers under Dodd-Frank Section 913. Supports uniform standard "no less stringent" than Advisers Act Sections 206(1) and 206(2).
- **NASAA Model Rules:** State-level investment adviser conduct rules, including prohibited conduct provisions.
- **Massachusetts 950 CMR 12.207 fiduciary standard**

### Key Legal Standards
- NY Insurance Regulation 187 (11 NYCRR 224)
- NRS 628A (Nevada Financial Planners Act)
- Massachusetts 950 CMR 12.207
- NASAA Model Rules for state investment adviser regulation
- Dodd-Frank Section 913 (federal-state interplay)

### Key Themes for AI Model Training
1. **The suitability-to-best-interest evolution:** How BD standards moved from FINRA Rule 2111 to Reg BI
2. **Dual standard framework:** IA fiduciary duty vs. BD Reg BI — differences and similarities
3. **Care obligation mechanics:** Reasonable basis + customer-specific analysis + quantitative (no excessive trading)
4. **Conflict identification and management:** Disclosure alone is insufficient; must mitigate or eliminate certain conflicts
5. **Retirement account special rules:** SEC/DOL dual jurisdiction for IRA rollovers and retirement advice
6. **State regulatory overlay:** Additional fiduciary requirements in NY, MA, NV, CO, etc.
7. **Product-specific suitability:** Complex products (L Bonds, VXX, leveraged ETFs, annuities) require heightened diligence
8. **Robo-adviser compliance:** Fiduciary duty applies equally to automated/algorithmic advice platforms
9. **Documentation requirements:** How to document compliance with care obligation
10. **Supervision and compliance programs:** Firm-level obligations to prevent suitability violations
